'''Social drugs''' are [[drug]]s that are relatively safe to use; they can never cause or contribute to [[overdose]] and require a certain amount of tolerance to cause [[addiction]]. This categorization is also used to determine acceptability for the [[Drug use|Drug use]] [[precept]].{{IdeologyIcon}}
