The '''persona core''' is a highly valuable component required to build a [[ship]] capable of leaving the planet.

== Acquisition ==
Persona cores can be found in an [[Quest#Item Stash|item stash world quest]]. Once the [[Starflight basics]] research project has been completed, you may also purchase the location of one from allied [[factions]] for the price of {{Icon Small|silver||1500}} [[silver]] at a [[comms console]]. You do not need to actually receive the [[Events/Incidental#Persona_Core_Offer|Persona Core Offer]] event to do this. It can also be earned as a reward for completing randomly generated [[quest]]s.

Alternatively, it's possible to go to the [[ship]] found in the [[Ship to the stars|journey offer]], deconstruct its [[ship computer core]], and bring it to a colony. There is a chance for the persona core to be lost when deconstructing.

Alternatively, deconstructing the [[destroyed cerebrex core]]{{OdysseyIcon}} provides a persona core.

== Summary ==
A single persona core is used to build the [[ship computer core]]. It has no other purpose, but sells for a high price.

== Analysis ==
As you need only one core to win the game, additional cores can be traded away without fear of setting yourself back. Alternatively, if another victory condition will be performed, or none will be, then they can also be sold without consequence.  

=== Profit ===
Due to the [[Trade Price Improvement|natural trade disadvantage]], the profit from continually farming persona cores isn't very high. At 20 Social skill in [[difficulty|Strive to Survive]], you would get {{Icon Small|silver}} {{#expr:{{P|Market Value Base #}} * 0.78 round 0}} from selling a persona core, a profit of {{Icon Small|silver}} {{#expr: ({{P|Market Value Base #}} * 0.78) - 1500 round 0}}. At 10 Social, this drops to a profit of {{Icon Small|silver}} {{#expr: ({{P|Market Value Base #}} * 0.69) - 1500 round 0}}. However, this does not consider the time cost of a [[caravan]] or the material costs of using a [[transport pod]] or [[passenger shuttle]].{{OdysseyIcon}}

Usually, it is easier, safer, and more profitable to grow [[psychoid plant|psychoid]] and [[cotton plant|cloth]], [[deep drill|drill]] for ore, take the mood penalty for [[organ harvesting]], and so on. These methods do not require pawns to venture outside the colony (potentially leaving the base underdefended) or fight enemies, making them safer overall.

With a [[passenger shuttle]]{{OdysseyIcon}}, the travel time is almost nonexistent, the cost of travel is cheap, and if the main colony gets raided, any attacking pawns can come back quickly. However, farming persona cores still requires manual player interaction and presents some danger to the attacking party.
