'''Glow pods''' are temporary, bioluminescent light source that spawns with [[hive]]s.

== Occurrence ==
They spawn from [[hive]]s, both prexisting in caves and from [[infestation]]s.{{Check Tag|Detail needed}}

== Summary ==
As of game version 1.2.2753, they cannot be uninstalled and reinstalled upon being spawned, but after a few hours it should be movable. They last for 20 in game days.{{Check Tag|Verify entire paragraph}}

=== Lighting ===
[[File:Glow pod illumination area.png|300px|thumb|left|The white area (2 tiles out) is 50% illumination, required for best [[Surgery Success Chance Factor|surgery success chance]]; the yellow (3rd tile) represents 30% or better, adequate for all general labor, avoiding [[mood]] penalties, and for growing [[Decorative Plants|decorative plants]] indoors in [[plant pot]]s.]] A glow pod produces [[light]] in a 5 tile radius, with the nearest {{P|Light Radius}} tiles at or above 30% light level and considered "[[Light|Lit]]", to a maximum of 50%. The provided light in this area is bright enough to prevent work speed penalties, grow decorative plants, and prevent the debuff [[Thoughts#In darkness|"In darkness"]]. However, glow pods are not bright enough to grow most [[crop]]s indoors, which require 51% illumination.
