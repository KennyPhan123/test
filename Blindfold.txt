'''Blindfolds''' are a form of [[clothing]] added by the [[Ideology DLC]] that reduces [[Sight]] and pleases [[ideoligion]]s that venerate blindness.

== Acquisition ==
Blindfolds can only be crafted by a pawn with the [[Blindsight]] meme. {{Acquisition}}

== Summary ==
Wearing a blindfold inflicts the "Blindfolded" hediff, setting [[Sight]] to a maximum of {{Bad|20%}}. Note that this only sets a maximum bound, it does not set the actual value - if other conditions would force wearer's Sight below {{Bad|20%}}, they will still do so while wearing the blindfold. 

While pawns normally get a bonus to [[psychic sensitivity]] if their [[Sight]] drops below 50% this isn't the case for blindfolds as this bonus will ignore off sets and post modifiers to the pawn's eye sight.

Wearing a blindfold will satisfy a pawn with a [[Blindsight]] ideology. Pawns who believe in the blindsight meme will get a {{+|2}} [[mood]] offset from wearing a blindfold.

The blindfold is also unique in that it occupies the [[Apparel layers|eye layer]], which no other apparel does. This allows it to stack protection with Headgear layer items that also cover the eyes, such as [[power armor]] helmets. As the Eye layer is considered to be over the Headgear layer, the blindfold will attempt to [[Apparel#Protection|mitigate or negate]] damage, and thus take damage to its {{HP}}, before headgear items do.

== Analysis == 
Blindfold will not get much use outside of colonies using the blindsight meme. The {{Bad|-80%}} sight offset is crippling in battle and significantly impairs a pawn's ability to work. Psycasters will not benefit from it either as it doesn't affect a pawn's [[psychic sensitivity]]. Beyond using the blindsight ideology, only colonists with less than 20% sight (ex: [[blindness|blind pawns]]) will benefit from blindfolds due to the additional if unremarkable armor and cold insulation.

=== Prisoner management ===
While working [[slaves]] would be hindered greatly by a blindfold, blindfolds can be given to [[prisoner]]s intended for recruitment, sale, or [[hemogen]],{{BiotechIcon}} as it will render them largely ineffective without reducing their value, or needing future replacement parts to restore function.
