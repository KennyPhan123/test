The '''mini-slugger''' is a [[turret]]-only ranged [[weapon]]. It is the main armament of the [[mini-slugger turret]]. It has low damage output and shot count, but is made up for the number of turrets that will spawn.

This weapon cannot be independently obtained in normal play. It is provided here for comparisons sake.

== Acquisition ==
Mini-sluggers spawn on all [[mini-slugger turret]]s. However, it is not possible to obtain it directly in normal play. Buildings do not drop it upon destructions and pawns carrying it will not drop it on [[death]], [[downing]], or complete loss of [[manipulation]] like other weapons. It can be spawned in by the [[Development mode#Debug actions menu|Debug actions menu]] but, like all [[unobtainable items|unobtainable weapons]], will disappear after being dropped.

== Analysis ==
With relatively low damage and a long cooldown, the mini-slugger has poor overall {{DPS}} which makes [[mini-slugger turret]]s a low priority when facing [[mech cluster]]s.{{RoyaltyIcon}} When combined with their abysmal accuracy at long range, a long range sniper duel a becomes valid tactic when [[blind smoke]] is unavailable or untenable. 

That said, their lack of warm-up means that turrets will shoot at pawns with even the shortest target presentations, and can do so at extreme ranges. As such, they can't and shouldn't be ignored totally, and ideally smoke should be used to prevent them firing at all.
