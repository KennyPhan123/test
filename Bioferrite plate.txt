'''Bioferrite plate''' is a buildable [[floor]] type added by the [[Anomaly DLC]]. Its primary purpose is for strengthening [[containment]] of nearby [[holding spot]]s and [[holding platform]]s.

== Acquisition ==
{{Acquisition}}

== Summary ==
Bioferrite plates improves the [[containment]] of a room that is directly proportional to the percentage of the room covered by the plates, up to a maximum of {{+|15}} containment strength with the entire room covered. For example, a 30-tile room with 20 tiles floored with bioferrite plate would receive {{+|10}} containment strength.

Bioferrite plates boost [[cleanliness]] by {{+|{{P|Cleanliness}}}}, meaning an entire room of bioferrite plate will have {{P|Cleanliness}} Cleanliness. In addition, they only take {{Good|{{%|{{P|Cleaning Time Factor}}}}}} of the time to clean, but have {{Bad|{{P|Beauty Base}}}} [[beauty]] per tile. 

Bioferrite plate is flammable and it leaves behind [[burned bioferrite plate]] when it burns.

== Analysis ==
Bioferrite plates are designed specifically for strengthining [[containment]] cells. It is not an immediate priority for every containment cell to have bioferrite plates, as bioferrite is in limited supply early on, and level 1 entities are generally easy to contain. It is advisable to upgrade to such as soon as is convenient, however, as a fully plated room provides a bonus that perfectly offsets the decrease in strength from using a [[Bioferrite harvester]] in the cell.

Bioferrite plates are also clean, sharing the same cleanliness bonus and clean time as [[steel tile]]. Compared to steel tile:
* Bioferrite can be easier to farm if the colony is focused on Anomaly content rather than standard progession.
* Bioferrite plate is worth {{Good|x{{%|{{P|Market Value Base}} / {{Q|Steel tile|Market Value Base}} round 3}}}} as much [[wealth]] as steel tile. [[Concrete]] is still cheaper, however.
* Bioferrite plate is flammable and has a [[beauty]] penalty. 

Overall, bioferrite plate can be used for laboratories, hospitals, and kitchens if faster cleaning is desired, at the cost of flammability and the beauty penalty. Even if all pawns are [[inhumanized]] and don't care about beauty, the beauty will impact [[room]] impressiveness. Therefore, [[steel tile]] or [[sterile tile]] will be better choices once the cost can be afforded.
