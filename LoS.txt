{{Stub|reason=Better characterization of LoS blockers needed.}}'''LoS''' is a abbreviation and means "'''L'''ine '''o'''f '''S'''ight": anything in your LoS is visible. This is a common mechanic of [[combat]] to determine if a pawn armed with a ranged weapon can fire on a target.

In RimWorld, while many items provide [[cover]], line of sight is typically only broken by [[walls]] and objects with similar properties such as ores, natural stone and nutrient paste dispensers.
