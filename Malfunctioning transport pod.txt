The '''malfunctioning transport pod''' is a structure added by the [[Odyssey DLC]]. Its contents are ejected when opened.

== Occurrence ==
The malfunctioning transport pod is only found in orbit. It has a 50% chance of spawning instead of an [[ancient transport pod]] in the orbital transport rooms found in orbital ancient platforms, orbital settlement platforms, abandoned platform quest sites, orbital wreck quest sites, and orbital item stash quest sites.

== Summary ==
{{Loot Container Note}}

A malfunctioning transport pod randomly drops resources, drugs, food, a corpse or a non-hostile live space pirate when opened.
