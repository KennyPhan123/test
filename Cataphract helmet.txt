A '''Cataphract helmet''' is a type of advanced [[armor]] introduced by the [[Royalty DLC]] that can effectively block gunshots, cuts, blunt trauma, or burns. It forms a full set along with the [[Cataphract armor]].

== Acquisition == 
{{Acquisition}}

Alternatively, the helmet can be received as a quest reward, purchased from traders, or found on [[Empire#Cataphract|Imperial Cataphracts]].

== Summary == 
{{Apparel Suppression Note|-0.10}}

A cataphract helmet increases the [[vacuum resistance]]{{OdysseyIcon}} of its wearer by {{+|{{%|{{P|Vacuum Resistance Offset}}}}}}. [[Quality]] does not affect this resistance.

== Analysis == 
Cataphract helmets provide the best protection of any helmet in the game, especially against blunt damage. Like [[recon helmet|recon]] and [[marine helmet]]s, and unlike the [[Simple helmet|simple]] or [[flak helmet]]s, it covers the entire head, including the Jaw, Nose, Ears and Eyes. It provides roughly the same protection as a Marine Helmet of one quality level above its own, or of Recon Helmet two quality levels above its own. A legendary cataphract helmet reaches the cap of 200% Sharp protection.

Cataphract helmets only cost {{Icon Small|plasteel}} 10 [[plasteel]] more than a [[marine helmet]] to create. However, it requires the [[Research#Cataphract armor|Cataphract Armor]] research, which itself requires 2 rare and expensive [[techprint]]s to complete. Acquiring helmets through fighting the Empire is also difficult due to their ubiquitous [[death acidifier]] implants and the general difficulty of killing Cataphract-level soldiers. But, once researched, there is little reason to create marine helmets over cataphract as unlike the body armors, there is no movement penalty, and the upgrade is inexpensive. If a cataphract helmet is acquired through other means, then it will be a straight upgrade to almost any other available helmet of similar quality, and often of a number of quality tiers above.

[[Prestige cataphract helmet]]s provide identical protection and cost significantly more resources and time in exchange for satisfying the apparel requirements of [[noble]]s and slightly enhancing [[psycast]]ing. If there is no particular need for these benefits, then it is better to produce the base model and only craft the prestige variant as needed. However, if you already have the prestige version, then there's no reason not to use it.

In the end-game, a cataphract helmet should be equipped on every colonist that does not require some other specialized headwear, such as the prestige variant, [[social impact]] hats, or a [[mechlord helmet]].{{BiotechIcon}}

== Trivia ==
Cataphracts were heavy armored cavalry used in ancient warfare by people in Eurasia, including the Byzantines that inspired the Empire.

Every cataphract helmet found on an [[Empire#Cataphract|Imperial Cataphract]] will be white.
