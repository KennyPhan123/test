'''Brainwipe''' is a [[psychic ritual]] that removes all allegiances (including [[Unwaveringly loyal|unwavering loyalty]]) and social memories of a pawn at the cost of putting them into a coma. It can also undo the effects of [[Inhumanized|Inhumanization]]. With the [[Ideology DLC]]{{IdeologyIcon}} enabled, it will also reset their [[Certainty]] and [[Will]], making it trivial to enslave or convert them.

== Analysis ==
Brainwipe is the only way to remove [[unwavering loyalty]]. If a particularly useful pawn is captured but is unwavering, this ritual can be well worth the cost of a shard and significant downtime.

In most cases, it is not worth using brainwipe for general recruitment, but it can make sense in certain situations. If all colonists have [[Inhumanizing|Inhumanizing: Required]],{{IdeologyIcon}} and become [[inhumanized]], their social stats will be poor and recruitment will be slow. [[Noble]]s{{RoyaltyIcon}} and [[sanguophage]]s{{BiotechIcon}} have especially high resistance and recruiting them can take a very long time.

Brainwipe can also be used as a [[conversion]] tool.{{IdeologyIcon}} For example, an [[iron-willed]] pawn with [[Apostasy|Apostasy: Abhorrent]] has a {{Down|×10%}} conversion multiplier, making regular conversion extremely slow. While a masterful [[conversion ritual]] will instantly convert such pawns, and should be attempted first, conversion rituals have a long cooldown and so brainwipe will be faster than repeated ritual attempts.
