'''Sandstone''' are a [[stony]] [[material]] and are the quickest to build and craft with out of the five types of [[stone blocks]].

== Acquisition ==
Sandstone blocks are cut from [[sandstone chunk]]s at a [[{{P|Production Facility 1}}]]. It takes {{Ticks|{{P|Work To Make}}}} of work to cut a single chunk into {{P|Product Amount}} blocks.

== Usage ==
Sandstone blocks can be used as a material for [[stuff]]able items with the [[Stony]] stuff tag.

== Analysis ==
=== Comparison to other materials ===
Sandstone blocks don't have any unique recipes and are only used in stuffable items and buildings. As such, they must be considered in comparison to the other stone types, which share all the same recipes.

Sandstone has mostly middling stats in comparison to the other stone blocks, but they are the fastest to work with. The difference is slight at about 15%, but that can add up when building or crafting in large quantities, such as for large defensive projects, or bulk-crafting sculptures for sale in [[trade]].

While sandstone blocks do not have as high of a "Max Hit Points" factor as that of [[granite blocks|granite]] or [[limestone blocks]], walls built from all three have enough {{HP}} to survive one explosion from [[frag grenade]]s and [[high-explosive shell]]s, and can survive the same amount of hits from a [[thump cannon]], giving them identical performance against [[breacher]]s and certain other scenarios. The HP still matters against [[sapper]]s and tribal [[breacher]]s, but as a defensive wall material, sandstone is an attractive choice. It can be the best choice for perimeter walls, areas with walls expected to be destroyed like with [[IED trap]]s, and other places where the difference between sandstone and granite HP is not likely to be relevant.
