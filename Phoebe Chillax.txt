'''Phoebe Chillax''' is a storyteller whose main characteristic is the long break between challenges, leaving the player with enough time to rest and recover after an event, and live without much to worry about. This gives new players a more relaxed experience to get familiar with the game. However, long breaks between raids can lead to surprising spikes of difficulty.

==Analysis==
Of the storytellers, Phoebe sends the fewest amount of [[Major threat|major threats]]. Her 16 day threat cycle means that she can never send 2 raids in quick succession, always taking anywhere from 8 to 24 days between major threats. While her description says she will "hit as hard as anyone", this is in the sense that major threats will be sent at the same [[raid points]] as Cassandra and the same ''average'' size as Randy.

Cassandra is a strictly more difficult storyteller. She sends major threats of the same size but on a more frequent 10.6 day cycle, and is able to send 2 major threats in a single on cycle.

Randy is more difficult on average. He sends major threats at a strictly higher frequency, though it is occasionally possible for Randy to generate a period of lower major threat intensity than Phoebe by rolling a weak raid.

Phoebe may be uniquely difficult as her less frequent major threats may cause overestimation of the colony's defenses: different types of major threat, even using the same threat calculations, may vary in practical difficulty. For example, a [[Manhunter pack|manhunter pack]] is generally much less difficult than a [[Raid#"Drop right on top of you" drop pods|center drop]].

When playing with Phoebe, in the beginning its always likely to gain one or two more colonists, typically a wanderer. A trade caravan or two shall also arrive before completing a full season (15 days).

== Code specifics == 
{{Stub|section=1|reason=Add table of, say, the first 10 or 20 phases with dates to serve as reference and worked example for on/of phases}} 
Major Threats

Phoebe's cycle begins on day 13 in her 8 day "On" phase. During the "On" phase there is always 1 major threat sent. Phoebe alternates between 8 day "On" phases and 8 day "Off" phases. The dates of these phases are always the same. Phoebe always begins the first "On" phase on day 13 and the first "Off" phase begins on day 21. Rimworld days begin at hour 6.

These On and Off cycles are the same after saving and reloading the game, on multiple map tiles, and when changing storytellers.
