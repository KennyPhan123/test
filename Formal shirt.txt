The '''formal shirt''' is a clothing item added in the [[Royalty DLC]]. [[Titles|Nobles]] of Acolyte rank and above require either one of these, or a [[eltex shirt]], to consider themselves properly dressed and avoid a negative moodlet.

== Acquisition ==
{{Acquisition}}

It can also be found on [[Empire#Pawns|Imperial Nobles]], for sale at [[traders]] and as a [[quest]] reward.

== Summary == 
{{Apparel Suppression Note|-0.10}}

== Analysis ==
Prior to the nerfs, the Formal Shirt was arguably the best craftable shirt, exchanging weight and crafting time for better cold resistance. Post nerf, it has a complicated value proposition. 
It has the decreased cold insulation and mass of the [[t-shirt]] but the superior coverage of the [[button-down shirt]], at the cost of a significantly longer crafting time and 44% more materials to craft. 

Should Noble Apparel already be researched, the Formal Shirt can take the place of the button-down. They are just as protective, weigh less and pawns won't require an apparel change when they gain titles. In colonies with nobles, this means production can be simplified to a single type of shirt for all pawns, reducing redundancy. However their significant material and time cost make this less attractive.
