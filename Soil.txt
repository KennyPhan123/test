'''Soil''' is a naturally occurring type of terrain.

== Summary ==
It is found in the following biomes:
* [[Boreal forest]]
* [[Tundra]]
* [[Cold bog]]
* [[Temperate forest]]
* [[Temperate swamp]]
* [[Tropical rainforest]]
* [[Tropical swamp]]
* [[Arid shrubland]]
* [[Desert]]
* [[Glacial plain]]{{OdysseyIcon}}
* [[Lava field]]{{OdysseyIcon}}
* [[Scarlands]]{{OdysseyIcon}}

It has the following terrain affordances:
* Light (Supports light structure)
* Medium (Supports medium structures (mainly walls))
* Heavy (Supports heavy structures)
* Growable (Things can grow here)
* Diggable (For graves)

It generates [[Filth#Dirt|dirt]] [[filth]].

Pawns have the ability to throw [[Hediffs#Dirt|dirt]] into an enemy's eyes as an available attack when on this terrain, impairing their vision momentarily.

[[Glowforest]]s{{OdysseyIcon}} and [[Grassland]]s{{OdysseyIcon}} have near-identical variants of '''soil''', which differ only by their textures.
