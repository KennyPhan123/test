'''Shotguns''' are [[Weapons#Ranged weapons|ranged weapons]] with high accuracy and [[stopping power]], but a short range and lower armor penetration.
* The [[pump shotgun]] fires single shots.
* The [[chain shotgun]] fires 3-shot bursts for higher DPS, with a slightly lower accuracy and range.
