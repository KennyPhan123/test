'''Ancient smoke vent''' are [[ruins]] added by the [[Odyssey DLC]].

Ancient smoke vents will periodically vent smoke blocking the sun. This lasts 3 days on average, with a downtime of 4 days on average. While active they produce 40C heat per second, as well as causing the Darkened Skies condition.
