The '''ceremonial hood''' is an item of [[clothing]] added by the [[Anomaly DLC]].

== Acquisition ==
{{Acquisition}}

== Analysis ==
Since the ceremonial hood is not [[stuff]]able with [[bioferrite]], but rather uses it as a set ingredient, it does not grant bioferrite's bonuses to [[psychic sensitivity]] and [[neural heat]]. Its function is limited to protection and style.

The hood can be set as an ideoligion's preferred apparel, granting a mood bonus while offering respectable protection. In this role, it's strictly better than a [[broadwrap]], granting more armor with a smaller wealth footprint.

Without being favored by an ideoligion, a [[devilstrand]] ceremonial hood can still be used as combat armor. It can't realistically protect a pawn's brain from more powerful weapons such as [[charge lance]]s, but it's much cheaper than [[power armor]] helmets that can, and still boasts full-face coverage. [[Death refusal]] is highly accessible and can absorb an occasional unlucky hit while the hood reduces most combat injuries. When considering coverage and stats, a devilstrand ceremonial hood offers superior sharp and heat protection to a [[plasteel]] simple helmet of the same quality, or inferior sharp in exchange for coverage and heat armor when compared to a [[steel]] [[flak helmet]]. In both cases, the blunt protection of the hood leaves something to be desired however.

As an alternative to the hood, the bioferrite could be fashioned into a simple or kid helmet, which lack full-face coverage but have bioferrite's psychic bonuses and an even smaller wealth footprint.
