'''Ghoul barbs''' are an [[artificial body part]] added by the [[Anomaly DLC]] that improves the melee damage of [[ghoul]]s at the cost of slowing [[Move Speed]].

== Acquisition ==
{{Acquisition}}

== Summary == 
When installed into the torso of a [[ghoul]], ghoul barbs multiply their [[Melee Damage Factor]] by {{Good|x150%}}, stacking multiplicatively with other increases to the factor. They also reduce the ghoul's base [[move speed]] by {{--|0.25}} {{CS}}. As offsets such as these apply before multiplicative effects such as from [[Moving]] capacity, the actual impact on move speed can be larger or smaller than this value. For example, a pawn with {{Good|125%}} Moving would have their speed decreased by {{--|{{#expr:1.25*0.25}}}} {{CS}}, while a pawn with only {{Bad|50%}} Moving would only have their speed decreased by {{--|{{#expr:0.5*0.25}}}} {{CS}}. See [[Move Speed]] for details.

=== Installation ===
Ghoul barbs can only be installed in [[ghoul]]s. Note that despite the description noting [[pain]] as the reason it cannot be installed in other pawns, methods of pain nullification such as [[painstopper]]s do not allow the barbs' installation into other pawns.

Installing the part requires {{ticks|2500}} of work. It doesn't [[Anesthetic|anesthetize]] the ghoul, requires no medicine, cannot fail and has no skill requirements.

== Analysis ==
While ghouls are fairly powerful by default, ghoul barbs are one of the key ways that they can compete with end-game human melee pawns, elevating [[body part weapons]], normally outclassed by traditional melee with [[quality]] multipliers, to a point where they are sufficent for the ghouls other advantages to make them the premier melee choice. Every ghoul should have ghoul barbs equipped, and besides a body part weapon and some select [[gene]]{{BiotechIcon}} options, it should be the prioritised over most other [[Ghoul#Analysis|upgrades]].

Because of the impact on [[move speed]], and because other upgrades will reduce it further, ways to counter the penalty should also be sought for ghouls, including [[bionic leg]]s and move speed genes.{{BiotechIcon}}
