A '''pyre''' is a consumable ritual focus added by the [[Ideology DLC]]. It serves as a focus for the [[Rituals#Symbol burning|Symbol Burning]] ritual.

== Acquisition ==
Pyres can only be constructed by pawns with an [[ideoligion]] that has selected them as a building option. Only ideoligions with the [[Rancher]] meme can select them. Each pyre requires {{Required Resources}} and {{Ticks|{{P|Work To Make}}}} of work.
