'''Compacted plasteel''' is a natural structure that can be [[mine]]d to obtain {{Icon Small|plasteel}} plasteel. 

== Summary ==
'''Compacted plasteel''' tiles have 8,000 health each, making it the slowest ore to mine and the toughest object in-game. They can be found in smaller veins, ranging from 2 to 8 tiles in size. Each mined block has a base yield of {{Icon Small|plasteel}} 40 plasteel, however this is modified by factors such as the [[Difficulty]] setting and the [[Mining_Yield|mining yield]] of the pawn.

== Analysis ==
Excellent for [[containment]]{{AnomalyIcon}} if you can hollow out a vein, even if parts of it have to be filled with less strong regular [[wall]]s.
