'''Prestige armors''' are specialized variants of the three base power armor sets. Each item improves the [[Psychic Sensitivity]] of the wearer by 5%, their [[Neural Heat Recovery Rate]] by 0.033 and also satisfies the [[Titles#Clothing requirements|apparel requirements]] of [[titles|nobles]]. This makes these sets ideal for Combat [[Psycast]]ers, as the armor sacrifices no protection while also buffing their abilities.

These advantages come at the cost of adding [[gold]] and extra [[plasteel]] to the crafting recipe of the base variant, and with an increased [[market value]] to reflect that change.

The current varieties of prestige armor are:
*[[Prestige cataphract armor]] - A variant of [[Cataphract armor]], and requiring an additional {{icon Small|Gold||180}} gold and {{icon Small|plasteel||40}} plasteel to craft.
*[[Prestige cataphract helmet]] - A variant of [[Cataphract helmet]], and requiring an additional {{icon Small|Gold||90}} gold and {{icon Small|plasteel||25}} plasteel to craft.
*[[Prestige marine armor]] - A variant of [[Marine armor]], and requiring an additional {{icon Small|Gold||100}} gold and {{icon Small|plasteel||20}} plasteel to craft.
*[[Prestige marine helmet]] - A variant of [[Marine helmet]], and requiring an additional {{icon Small|Gold||50}} gold and {{icon Small|plasteel||10}} plasteel to craft. This includes the [[samurai helmet]], which is a statistically style of the helmet with a different texture and name.
*[[Prestige recon armor]] - A variant of [[Recon armor]], and requiring an additional {{icon Small|Gold||90}} gold and {{icon Small|plasteel||20}} plasteel to craft.
*[[Prestige recon helmet]] - A variant of [[Recon helmet]], and requiring an additional {{icon Small|Gold||40}} gold and {{icon Small|plasteel||10}} plasteel to craft.
