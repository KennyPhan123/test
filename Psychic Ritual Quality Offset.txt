== Factors ==
* Ritual invokers add quality based on their [[Psychic Sensitivity]] ({{+|12.5%}} at 100% psychic sensitivity).
** Their psychic sensitivity is applied to a curve:

* Each chanter adds a fraction relative to the maximum number of chanters, up to a maximum of {{+|20%}} quality: At 4 max chanters for basic rituals, each adds {{+|5%}}, and at 6 max chanters for advanced rituals, each adds {{Good|≈+3.33%}}.
