'''Void structure''' is a structure that forms after awakening the [[monolith]], and must be activated in order to activate the [[void node]] and reach the Anomaly [[ending]].

== Occurance ==
After activating [[void monolith]] level 3, regular darkness will cover the map and two void structures will spawn somewhere on the map, surrounded by void metal floors and a few [[Void metal mass]]es, along with varying numbers{{Check Tag|Detail needed|Varying based on what factor, if any, and within what bounds?}} of [[metalhorror]]s that stick around.

After the two void structures are activated, three more will spawn after a delay.

== Summary ==
Initially unactivated, a colonist can be selected to activate the structure, taking 36 seconds to activate, changing the center of the structure from a dull gray to a glowing red, and prompting a horde of entities to spawn around the map edge to assault your colony after a short delay.

Once the two monoliths are activated, [[unnatural darkness]] will cover the map and will be harmful like the [[noctolith]] event. A countdown will begin before the next wave of three void structures arrive. After the three void structures have been activated, the [[void monolith]] may be activated to access the [[void node]] and end the event.

They will spawn with a foliage kill radius that gradually maxes out at 25 tiles.

== Trivia ==
When possible, the void structures will spawn in a roughly pentagonal shape around the edges of the map. This info can be used to prepare flood lights before the unnatural darkness occurs.
