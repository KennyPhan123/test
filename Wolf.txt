'''Wolf''' may refer to one of three [[animals]] using that name:
* [[Timber wolf]]
* [[Arctic wolf]]
* [[Greatwolf]]{{OdysseyIcon}}
All three provide [[Wolfskin]] when butchered. 

Not to be confused with:
* [[Warg]] -  a wolf-like creature that also provides [[Wolfskin]]
* [[Dog]]s - animals related to wolves, mechanically similar but completely distinct in-game.
