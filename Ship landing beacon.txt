The '''ship landing beacon''' tells [[imperial shuttle]]s where to land. Four of them must be placed on the corners of a 9x9 or larger clear area to create a landing pad. Shuttles will use the cleared area if possible. 

== Acquisition ==
{{Acquisition}} 

Ship landing beacons will also spawn, in the correct orientation and on a [[floor]]ed landing pad, in [[Empire]] [[faction base]]s, often with an [[imperial shuttle]] on it about to take off.

== Summary ==
When 4 beacons are placed in a rectangle of minimum size 9x9 and provided with power the area becomes a landing pad. [[Imperial shuttle]]s will land there if possible for dropoff and pickup of characters for [[quests]], including: the [[Quests#Noble Wimp|intro noble wimp quest]], [[Quests#Hospitality|hospitality]], [[Quests#Ferried bandit camp|ferried bandit camp]] and [[Quests#Ancient complex|ancient complex]],{{IdeologyIcon}} [[Quests#Pawn Lending|pawn lending]], and the ''rescue shuttle'' for the [[Quests#Shuttle Crash|shuttle crash]] quest. It does not work for the [[Quests#Shuttle Crash|crashed shuttle]]s themselves.

The landing pad must be clear of any objects you cannot build on top of such as trees, chunks and other buildings. Additionally, the interior area of the landing pad not including the rows and columns of tiles with the beacons themselves, i.e. a 7x7 area for the minimum size, must be [[roof|unroofed]] for the pad to function. The tiles in all 4 cardinal directions of each beacon, and the beacons themselves, can be roofed without issue.

It has no mechanical interactions with [[passenger shuttle]]s{{OdysseyIcon}} despite their similarity to imperial shuttles. It also does not interact with [[spacedrone]]s,{{IdeologyIcon}} [[crashed mechanitor ship]]s,{{BiotechIcon}} or [[grav ship]]s,{{OdysseyIcon}} despite its name and description.

== Analysis ==
Ship landing beacons can be useful to allow imperial shuttles to land in a safer and more convenient location, saving time as pawns will need to walk a shorter distance to reach the shuttle. However, shuttles don't land very often, and it's normally trivial to have pawns walk the extra distance, so they should only be built once steel and components are no longer a concern.

Cover the landing area in flooring, such as [[concrete]], to prevent tree growth from blocking the pad.

== Trivia == 
[[Ancient nav beacon]]s {{IdeologyIcon}} that can spawn as part of map generation bear a strong resemblance to ship landing beacons, though with visible damage. Similar to landing beacons, nav beacons will often spawn in the corners of partially incomplete, 11x11 areas of [[concrete]], with anywhere between 1 to 4 ancient nav beacons being present - presumably representing [[ship landing beacon|ship landing zones]].
