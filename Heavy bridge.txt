'''Heavy bridges''' are [[structures]] that can be built on [[Terrain|water]] or [[orbit|space]] to allow fast movement and the construction of any building and floor on top of them.
 
== Acquisition ==
{{Acquisition}} They can only be constructed on the following terrain types:
* [[Marshy soil]]
* [[Mud]]
* [[Marsh]]
* [[Shallow water]]
* [[Shallow ocean water]]
* [[Shallow moving water]]
* [[Chest-deep moving water]]
* [[Orbit|Space]]

== Summary == 
Heavy bridges occupy the foundation layer, which is above natural terrain, but below regular [[floor]]s. Unlike the terrain below them, heavy bridges do not penalize movement speed or cleanliness. Heavy bridges can also support all structures and floors.

Heavy bridges are treated as foundations and not floors, but do count as floored tiles for [[noble]]{{RoyaltyIcon}} purposes. Because they occupy the foundation layer, [[gravship substructure]] cannot be built on them. A [[gravship]] can land on a bridge, but will destroy the bridge in the process.

Unlike regular [[bridge]]s, heavy bridges cannot be destroyed by [[fire]] or explosions, effectively making them invincible. They can be removed manually by using the "Remove Foundation" gizmo under Architect/Structures.

Bridges and heavy bridges will contain [[weather|torrential rain]], preventing its tile and tiles beyond it from getting flooded.

== Analysis ==
Heavy bridges allow colonies to build any structure over water or the void of space, and unlike regular bridges, there is no risk of a heavy bridge getting destroyed. However, they are expensive and worth significant [[wealth]]. 

To save on resources when building in water, it is possible to use regular [[bridge]]s to cover most tiles, only building heavy bridges on tiles containing heavy/important buildings, tiles where regular bridges are expected to be destroyed, or as firebreaks to limit the spread of [[fire]]. Regular bridges are vulnerable to destruction, but natural fire is unlikely when surrounded by water (assuming a firebreak is installed at the entrance), meaning only raiders are a serious threat to them.

[[Moisture pump]]s cover a wide area, so they are more cost-effective than bridges and/or heavy bridges if a large plot of buildable area is needed. However, pumps are slower, and you cannot choose which tiles a pump converts. If you only need a few tiles of coverage per area, e.g., to cover up a swamp base, or a thin area, heavy bridges are more appropriate. Note that moisture pumps cannot convert space tiles.

[[Gravship substructure]] can be placed over water, allowing tiles of substructure to serve a similar role to heavy bridges. They can be placed freely by uninstalling and re-installing the [[grav engine]] wherever substructure is desired. However, substructure costs [[gravlite panel]]s, which are of limited supply before [[fabrication bench]]es are unlocked. In addition, they may cause unintended connection to an actual [[gravship]].
