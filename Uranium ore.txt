'''Uranium ore''' is a natural structure that can be [[mine]]d to obtain {{Icon Small|Uranium}} uranium . 

== Summary ==
'''Uranium ore''' tiles have 4,000 health, making them one of the slower ores to mine. They can be found in midsize veins, ranging from 6 to 12 tiles in size. Each mined block has a base yield of {{Icon Small|Uranium}} 40 uranium, however this is modified by factors such as the [[Difficulty]] setting and the [[Mining_Yield|mining yield]] of the pawn.
