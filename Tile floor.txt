'''Tile floor''' refers to a number of different floors in the game, and most use different amounts of different [[materials]]. The descriptions below are very simplistic; see the individual article or [[Floors]] for more information.

* '''[[Stone tile]]''' - can be made from granite, limestone, marble, sandstone or slate. Slight [[beauty]]
* '''[[Paved tile]]''' - actually made of [[steel]]
* '''[[Steel tile]]''' - slightly higher [[Cleanliness]]
* '''[[Silver tile]]''' - high beauty, similar moderate cleanliness to steel
* '''[[Gold tile]]''' - very expensive, highest beauty, similar moderate cleanliness
* '''[[Sterile tile]]''' - relatively slow and expensive to build, the highest cleanliness by far
* '''[[Fine stone tile]]'''{{RoyaltyIcon}} - more expensive than standard stone tiles, and with higher [[Beauty]]
** '''[[Morbid stone tile]]'''{{IdeologyIcon}} - [[Ideoligion|Ideological]] variant of fine stone tile
** '''[[Spikecore stone tile]]'''{{IdeologyIcon}} - [[Ideoligion|Ideological]] variant of fine stone tile
** '''[[Totemic stone tile]]'''{{IdeologyIcon}} - [[Ideoligion|Ideological]] variant of fine stone tile
