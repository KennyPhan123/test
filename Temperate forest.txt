'''Temperate forest''' is a temperate [[biome]] in RimWorld.

== Summary ==
One of the most forgiving biomes in the game, temperate forests are distinguished by their copious amounts of fertile soil, lack of extreme heat or cold, excellent access to trees to chop for wood, and large wildlife populations. Temperate forests tend to have very few areas of low [[Property:Terrain Affordance|terrain affordance]].

== Analysis ==
Temperate forest is one of the most hospitable biomes. It is dense in flora and fauna and often has a year-round growing season, without the [[disease]] frequency of a [[tropical rainforest]] or swamp. Therefore, it is recommended to new players, players wanting a casual experience, and/or players who want the challenge to come from [[raid]]s or other sources rather than from the biome.

* '''Growing and Food: Easy''' - Growing seasons are year-round or long. Many animals are present. Soil is common.

* '''Disease: Easy''' - Temperate forest has a relatively low disease rate for a biome with year-round growing. [[Wild healroot]] grows naturally, too, unlike the otherwise similar [[arid shrubland]].

* '''Temperature: Easy''' - Temperatures should be comfortable save for [[heat wave]]s and [[cold snap]]s, as well as winters in temperate forests without a year-round growing season. See the [[heat wave]] and [[cold snap]] sections for how to deal with these events.

* '''Fires: Moderate''' - There are plenty of trees and plants, meaning fires can spread. Firebreaks can be built around the colony to mitigate fire damage.

* '''Predators: Moderate''' - [[Grizzly bear]]s can show up, but will be rarely hungry enough to attack colonists unless they are already near.

Overall difficulty is easy - it has the least overall challenges of all the biomes.
