== Calculating Wastepacks Per Recharge ==
All Mechanoids have a base value of 5. This value is multiplied post process by a mech's '''Bandwidth Cost''' stat to produce the final value:

<code>[[Wastepacks Per Recharge]] = '''Base Wastepacks Per Recharge''' × '''[[Bandwidth Cost]]'''</code>

Simply put, just multiply bandwidth cost by 5.
