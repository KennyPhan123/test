'''Flesh sacks''' are organ structures dispersed among [[Fleshmass]] infested areas that drop valuable loot when destroyed.

== Occurrence ==
Flesh sacks are only generated within the [[Pit gate]]'s [[undercave]] and the [[Distress signal|Distress Signal quest]] map. In both cases, the number spawned depends on the number of [[Raid points|threat points]], resulting in 1-4 flesh sacks for the distress signal quest and 3-9 for the undercave. On distress signal maps, flesh sacks always spawn in proper rooms, with a clump of fleshmass generated in their proximity.

Items with quality, such as apparel, tome, or nerve spiker, range from awful to excellent.

Corpses spawn with the same gear as raiders of the same type. Inventory-wise, these particular corpses tend to carry:
* Meals: All three types of corpses carry 2-3 meals, with a 50% chance of being [[simple meal]]s, and 25% chance each of being [[fine meal]]s or [[packaged survival meal]]s. 
* Drugs: Corpses of addicts carry 2-5 of one drug they're addicted to. Pirate boss corpses have a 80% chance of carrying 1-2 combat drugs, i.e. [[go-juice]] or [[yayo]].
* Medicine: Pirate gunner corpses have a 10% chance of carrying 1 [[medicine]].

== Analysis ==
The [[Provoke pit gate]] ritual consumes {{Icon Small|bioferrite||75}} [[bioferrite]] and can be used to continuously generate undercaves which come with {{Icon Small|flesh sack||3-9}} flesh sacks. This means that, with a sufficient supply of bioferrite, pit gates can be farmed for archotech legs, arms and eyes in a late-game colony,making this a far more efficient method than trading for them. Always accepting distress signals can also help with this. Loot is generated when the pit gate is entered for the first time, so it can be be very easily save scummed if you have the patience to repeatedly clear the undercave.
