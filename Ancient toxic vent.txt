'''Ancient toxic vent'''s are [[ruins]] added by the [[Odyssey DLC]]. A map with toxic vents will have several of them scattered about the map surrounded by ancient megastructure. They will lie dormant for an average of 4 days, then they will activate and begin to spew green toxic gases into the atmosphere, causing [[toxic fallout]] lasting an average of 12 days as well as 40C heat per second. Standing on top of a vent while it is active will increase [[toxic buildup]] rapidly.
