'''Count-down activators''' are [[Mechanoid cluster]] buildings added by the [[Royalty DLC]]. They wake up the cluster after a set amount of time. The average count-down time is {{Ticks|267060}} (4.45 days), but could be as short as {{Ticks|600}} or as long as {{Ticks|1200000}} (20 days).

Attacking any part of the mechanoid cluster of which it is part will cause it to trigger immediately.

== Trivia ==
Like all mech cluster buildings, they not only have a [[Flammability]] of 0%, but also take no damage from attacks with the [[Damage Types|Flame]] damage type. This is in contrast to, for example, a [[stone]] [[wall]] which will not be ignited by [[molotov cocktails]] but will take the 10 flame damage from the initial attack.
