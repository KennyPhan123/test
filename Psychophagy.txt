'''Psychophagy''' is a [[psychic ritual]] that provides a long duration buff to the invoker's [[psychic sensitivity]] at the cost of rendering the target permanently [[psychically dead]].

== Summary ==

* Grants the "Psychophage" [[hediff]], which grants 50% psychic sensitivity to the invoker for the duration of the effect.
* 1 day of duration gained per 1% ritual quality.
* Casting the ritual again will stack the duration, but the psychic sensitivity boost is unaffected.
* Has a chance of:
** Liquefying targets brain, killing them in the process,.
** Applying a psychic scarring to the brain. {{Check Tag|Verify|Scar always applied if target survives? Different levels of scar (in small sample size its always 1-1.2hp / 2% pain)}}  
* Target becomes [[psychically dead]], permanently setting their psychic sensitivity to 0%. Unlike [[psychically deaf]], this is a multiplier, not an additive offset, so no source of psychic sensitivity can bring it above 0%. This effectively prevents consequent usages of Psychophagy (or any phage rituals) on same pawn. 
* Applies a stacking mood debuff for participants:

== Analysis ==
While psychophagy is a limited duration ritual, it can last a long time, can be repeated in order to increase the duration, and costs nothing other than a small amount of [[bioferrite]] and a prisoner which can be obtained through [[skip abduction]] or any other means.

[[Psycast]]ers{{RoyaltyIcon}} benefit the most from invoking psychophagy, as the boost improves their [[neural heat limit]] by {{+|40}} if at level 6 - this boost can then be multiplied by [[bioferrite]] gear, [[voidsight serum]]s, and [[void touched]]. It can also benefit [[psychic harmonizer]]{{RoyaltyIcon}} pawns, although the invoker must not be [[downed]], so [[psychic harmonizer#Comatose pawns|permanently unconcious]] pawns cannot benefit.

With Anomaly alone, the benefits of psychic sensitivity are much less pronounced. It improves the effectiveness of [[psychic ritual]]s, boosting the quality by a modest {{+|4%}} if the cap hasn't been reached, and it improves the mood buff from [[pleasure pulse]]. These buffs are fairly small, but since the duration of psychophagy is so high, it can make sense to apply in the late game.

=== Intentional psychically deadening ===
While the usual target for psychophagy will be prisoners, due to the brain damage and chance of death, psychophagy may be used in order to lower a colonist's psychic sensitivity to 0%. This makes them immune to [[psychic drone]]s and many [[psycast]]s{{RoyaltyIcon}} (thus preventing friendly fire). Brain damage can be reversed through a few means like [[unnatural healing]] and the [[bioregeneration cycle]],{{IdeologyIcon}} and death reversed (expensively) with [[death refusal]].

The [[psychically deaf]] [[gene]]{{BiotechIcon}} is much better for this purpose. It does not cause brain damage, boosts [[metabolic efficiency]], and psychically deaf pawns can wear sensitivity boosting gear in order to participate in psychic rituals, most importantly [[imbue death refusal]]. In addition, if wanting to make an intentionally poor ritual - such as for [[draw animals]] to create a manhunter swarm - a deaf pawn can reach a low sensitivity, while a dead pawn cannot participate at all. However, there is no guarantee that a suitable [[genepack]] can be found in a timely manner, while psychophagy is available for research from the get go.
