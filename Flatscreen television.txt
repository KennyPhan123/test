A '''flatscreen television''' is a [[recreation]] item that provides "{{P|Recreation Type}}" recreation to [[colonist]]s in an area in front of it. It is functionally similar to other types of [[television]].

== Acquisition ==
{{Acquisition}}

They can also be [[Trade|purchased]] from [[Outlander]]s caravans and [[faction base]]s, or found in [[ancient shrine]]s.

== Summary ==
{{Television Summary|6|5}}
[[File:Flatscreen tv.jpg|thumb|center|A flatscreen television and its area of effect.]]

== Analysis ==
Any television provides another form of recreation to your colony. It is useful for bed-ridden pawns in particular, as the only other option requires that another colonist talk to them.

In general, it is not worth it to actually research televisions for the purposes of recreation. [[Megascreen television]]s provide stronger recreation and are often carried by exotic goods [[trader]]s and [[faction base]]s. Both flatscreen and [[tube television]]s can also be bought from the same sources or received as a quest reward.

Flatscreen televisions can be used for resource compression, compressing 59.7 kg of 105 [[steel]] and 12 [[components]] into a mere 8 kilos. This allows you to carry 7 times the weight you normally would on a caravan, but means that you will lose 50% of the 140 steel and 16 components.
