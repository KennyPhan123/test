'''Tropical swamp''' is a hot and wet [[biome]] in RimWorld.
<!-- return 28f + (tile.temperature - 20f) * 1.5f + (tile.rainfall - 600f) / 165f + tile.swampiness * 3f -->
== Summary ==
Tropical swamp are famous for having many diseases, including [[Sleeping sickness]]: a slow-progressing, long-lasting disease that is exclusive to the tropics. Many trees make it difficult to clear land and rapid plant growth makes it difficult to keep it clear. 

In the lists below the number in brackets represents the relative commonality.
