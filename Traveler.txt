Occasionally, one or more members of a friendly [[Factions|faction]] will arrive, just passing through. They'll enter at one edge of the map and proceed across the map. They'll exit the map upon reaching another edge. 

A drafted colonist can be directed to capture (arrest) one of the travelers.  However, once the arresting colonist reaches the target, that entire faction will immediately turn extremely hostile, dropping to -76 [[Factions#Relationship_status|Goodwill]]. This shift includes any other members of the same [[Faction]] currently there. 
</onlyinclude>
