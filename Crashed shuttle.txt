'''Crashed shuttles''' are non-functional transport vehicles.

== Occurrence ==
Crashed shuttles most commonly spawn as a result of a quest - damaging or destroying an [[imperial shuttle]] does not create a crashed shuttle in its place. A crashed shuttle will land in a random spot on the map along with a few {{icon Small|Steel slag chunk}} [[Steel slag chunk]]s. Abandoned orbital platforms has a semi-uncommon chance to generate a crashed shuttle outside of the main complex.

Unlike normal shuttles, they do not respect or interact with [[ship landing beacon]]s in any way.

=== Quests ===
* [[Quests#Sanguophage_Transport|Sanguophage Transport]]{{BiotechIcon}}
* [[Quests#Shuttle_Crash|Shuttle Crash]]{{RoyaltyIcon}}

== Summary ==
Crashed shuttles are impassible and can act like a [[wall]] for cover. They have no other functional use beyond being being deconstructed for resources. Crashed shuttles will always yield 1 {{Icon Small|Shuttle engine}} [[shuttle engine]] in addition to the other yields if the [[Odyssey DLC]] is active.

== Analysis ==
Deconstructing a crashed shuttle is one of the only ways to get the rare [[shuttle engine]],{{OdysseyIcon}} and it is the easiest way as it does not require trading or losing any goodwill. However, both crashed shuttle quests involve combat. The player does not have to succeed in the shuttle crash quest to loot the shuttle regardless. Outside of [[Odyssey (DLC)]], a crashed shuttle does not have any notable uses besides some extra resources.
