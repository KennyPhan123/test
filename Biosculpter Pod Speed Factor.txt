== Calculating Biosculpter Pod Speed Factor ==
The factors for [[Biosculpter Pod Speed Factor]] are: '''Biosculpter Pod Speed Factor Base''' and '''Room cleanliness'''. This would be written out as:

<code>[[Biosculpter Pod Speed Factor]] = '''Biosculpter Pod Speed Factor Base''' × '''Room cleanliness'''</code>

The maximum would be:

<code>'''1''' × '''1.25''' = '''1.25'''</code>

For an effective limit of '''125%'''.

The minimum would be:

<code>'''1''' × '''0.7''' = '''0.7'''</code>

For an effective limit of '''70%'''.
