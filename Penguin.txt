A '''{{PAGENAME}}''' is an [[animal]] added by the [[Odyssey DLC]].

== Acquisition ==
{{PAGENAME}}s can be found in {{Habitats}}. They can either be tamed by a [[Work #Handle|handler]] or self-tame in a random event. 

{{PAGENAME}}s can be bought and sold in other [[Trade#Faction base|faction bases]] and from [[Trade#Exotic goods trader 2|exotic goods traders]]. {{PAGENAME}}s purchased from traders will be already tamed.

== Analysis ==
On extreme cold maps with fishable water, they have some niche uses for meat and/or fertilized eggs due to their cold tolerance and ability to 'graze' by fishing for food.
Penguins are not pen animals, so they will need to constantly be re-tamed.

== Trivia ==
The penguin appears to be based off both (currently) extant species in the [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aptenodytes Aptenodytes] genus. The juvenile penguin has the gray pattern of a juvenile [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emperor_penguin emperor penguin], but the adult penguin has the cheek patches of a [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_penguin king penguin].
