A '''panda''' is an [[animal]] added by the [[Odyssey DLC]].

== Acquisition ==
{{PAGENAME}}s can be found in {{Habitats}}. They can either be tamed by a [[Work #Handle|handler]] or self-tame in a random event. 

{{PAGENAME}}s can be bought and sold in other [[Trade#Faction base|faction bases]] and from [[Trade#Exotic goods trader 2|exotic goods traders]]. {{PAGENAME}}s purchased from traders will be already tamed.

== Summary ==
{{PAGENAME}}s are one of the few animals that can [[nuzzle]] colonists, giving a {{+|4}} ''Nuzzled'' moodlet to colonists.

== Analysis ==
Pandas have a low hunger rate and low wildness (0.32 nutrition/day; 40%) relatively to grizzly bears (0.56 nutrition/day; 80%) and polar bears (0.56 nutrition/day; 85%), in addition to being herbivorous - making them preferable for bearskin farms (60 bearskin vs 86 bearskin for other bears).
Unlike bears, however, pandas cannot be trained to rescue and haul - with the tradeoff that they can nuzzle colonists. They are also evidently inferior in combat: they have lower speed, lower damage per second and lower health scale.

In conclusion, pandas are cheaper to maintain and useful for bearskin farms, but individually inferior to grizzly or polar bears in terms of capabilities - with the exception of having the ability to nuzzle.

With a sentience catalyst, Pandas gain the ability to rescue and haul, their min handling skill drops to 4 and wildness drops to 15% making them slower, but much lower maintenance haulers than bears. Compared to dogs, they eat less (0.32 Pandas, 0.64 Labradors, 0.86 Huskies) and can graze but produce more filth (2 Vs 1) with slightly higher combat power and accompanying raid points (70 Labradors, 75 Huskies, 80 Pandas) plus require higher skill and more training to maintain. Pandas' hunger rate 0.32 is the same as Cougars but Pandas are herbivores, much less wild and are therefore easier to keep fed/trained and are much more combat power efficient than Cougars (80 Pandas Vs 120 Cougars). If you only want one or a few animal haulers this can make catalysted Pandas a competitive option for an animal hauler.
