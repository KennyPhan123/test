'''Ancient exostrider midsection''' are [[ruins]] added by the [[Biotech DLC]]. Upon destruction, it will explode into [[fire|flames]] and leave behind a [[mechanoid transponder]].
== Occurrence ==
{{Stub|section=1|reason=Disabled in scenario set up?}}
One ancient exostrider midsection is guaranteed to spawn in the starting map of a playthrough, this is the only instance where it will appear. The only exception is the [[Scenario system#The Mechanitor|Mechanitor]] starting scenario, where exostrider parts do not generate.

== Summary ==
The ancient exostrider midsection spawns with 1 [[ancient exostrider head]], 1 [[ancient exostrider cannon]], and 3 [[ancient exostrider leg]]s nearby.

Once a pawn walks within 4 tiles of it, a [[letter]] will appear:

{| class="wikitable"
| Passing near the remains of an ancient exostrider mechanoid, <span style="color:brown"><PAWN></span> noticed an <span style="color:darkorange">intact transponder</span> inside.<br/>If you could get the transponder, you could read valuable information from it. The massive mech is smashed and fused into a solid piece. You must destroy it to retrieve the transponder.<br/>Be careful - the incendiary weapon cells look <span style="color:red">unstable</span> and will likely detonate after you do some damage.
|}

[[File:Ancient exostrider midsection explosion radius.png|thumb|left|250px|The blast radius of the exostrider midsection. While [[Filth#Chemfuel_puddle|chemfuel puddles]] may extend beyond this range, fire will always spawn inside of it.]]

When damaged below 17% of its health, it will start leaking [[Filth#Chemfuel_puddle|puddles of chemfuel]] before exploding after {{Ticks|589}}, dealing [[Damage Types#Flame|Flame]] damage in a 5 tile radius around itself and setting [[fire]] to all affected tiles.

Destruction will yield 6 [[steel slag chunk]]s and a [[mechanoid transponder]]. The transponder can then be decrypted at a [[simple research bench]] or [[hi-tech research bench]]. Upon decryption, the transponder will be destroyed and a [[Quests#Ancient Transport|quest]] will trigger, allowing you to call a ship to land on the map. The ship will contain a few hostile [[mechanoid]] and a desiccated [[mechanitor]] corpse. The mechlink can then be extracted from the mechanitor corpse. For further details, see the [[mechanoid transponder]] page.

== Analysis ==
Destroying the ancient exostrider midsection to obtain its transponder is a quick and straightforward way to find a [[mechlink]] and become a [[mechanitor]].

One should exercise caution when destroying it however, as the resulting explosion could easily set your pawns or the surrounding environment ablaze, causing much destruction. Preferrably, one should keep multiple pawns on standby nearby to deal with the fires so they don't spread out of control. Be sure to zone a [[Area#Home area|home area]] around the fires to aid in [[firefighting]]. Alternatively, cutting nearby plants to create a 2-tile wide barrier will prevent fire from spreading uncontrollably, as fire will not jump to an flammable object more than 2 tiles away. Pawns will have enough time to escape the explosion if [[Weapons#Melee weapons|melee]] attacking the midsection as long as they move shortly after the fuse begins. But if wanting to minimize risk, [[Weapons#Ranged weapons|ranged weaponry]] should be used instead.
