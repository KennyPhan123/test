The '''club''' is a neolithic melee [[weapon]] that does a moderate amount of blunt damage and has a moderate-long cooldown. As a blunt weapon, it doesn't cause bleeding (unless a body part gets destroyed), and generally do more damage against armor when compared to sharp weapons.

== Acquisition ==
{{Acquisition}} 

They can also be purchased from any combat supplier or war merchant, or obtained from melee [[raider]]s drops.

== Analysis ==
Clubs are generated without a [[quality]] level - in essence, all clubs are "normal." This makes them worth considering if you have no competent crafters yet. They may also be useful in when starting the game in [[Naked Brutality]] if a [[short bow]] cannot be crafted. Otherwise, they have underwhelming damage output, so clubs only make sense as melee options in the early game. Once the colony has crafters capable of reliably producing better than normal quality weapons, there is little reason to ever use a club again.

Clubs are the only craftable items that can be made from regular [[stone blocks]], so they can have a niche for farming Crafting experience in [[wood]]-poor [[biome]]s. If wood is readily available, then [[short bow]]s or the larger bows will be better options.

Although [[uranium]] clubs are effective weapons for use against armored foes, [[mace]]s are clearly superior weapons, especially when high quality, for only 10 more material.
