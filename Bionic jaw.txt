{{Info|A '''bionic jaw''' is an [[artificial body part]] that acts as a replacement for a pawn's natural jaw, increasing the [[Talking]] and [[Eating]] capacities.}}

== Acquisition ==
{{Acquisition}}

They can also be found in [[ancient shrine]]s, offered as a [[quest]] reward, or [[Trade|bought]] from exotic traders, [[Empire]] traders, and at [[Outlander]] [[faction base]]s.{{Check Tag|Verify}}

== Summary ==
{{Stub|section=1|reason=Talking and Eating importance/caps are wrong on the capacity pages. The actual desc needs to be implemented there and explained here, }}
A Bionic jaw replaces an organic jaw and has a part efficiency of {{Good|125%}}, improving both [[Talking]] and [[Eating]] by {{Good|+25%}}. This increases the [[Eating speed]] by {{Good|+23.75%}}.

The following [[stats]] are also affected: {{#ask: [[Talking Importance::+]]|sort = Name}}. Note however that, {{Hover title|Version/1.5.4104|as of the time of writing}}, no stat is improved by a Talking capacity above 100%. However, a bonus will provide a buffer against stat and capacity penalties from other sources.

It also removes the [[Disfigured]] condition from pawns missing a jaw or a tongue. Installing a Bionic Jaw will replace any previously installed [[bionic tongue|Bionic Tongues]], and it is impossible to install a tongue on a colonist with a Bionic Jaw. This means that the Bionic Jaw is superior to a Bionic Tongue in every way. 

=== Installation ===
Installing the part requires {{Ticks|2500}} of work, 2x medicine of [[Herbal medicine|herbal]] quality or better, and a [[Skills#Medical|Medical skill]] of 5.

Removing the part requires {{Ticks|2000}} of work, 1x medicine of [[Herbal medicine|herbal]] quality or better, and has no [[Skills#Medical|Medical skill]] requirements.

If the operation fails, the part will be destroyed.

== Analysis ==
Bionic jaws are a very low priority implant to install on most pawns. Improving Talking past 100% has no game impact, so the only benefit of installing bionic jaw on a healthy pawn is {{+|23.75%}} Eating Speed, which is fairly minor due to how little time pawns spend eating, and somewhat better Talking when injured.

The best purpose of a bionic jaw is for wardens and Social pawns who would otherwise be missing a jaw or wearing a [[denture]]. In addition, a Social pawn with a [[joywire]] would appreciate a bionic jaw, as the increased Talking will counteract the [[Consciousness]] penalty from a joywire. The Talking stat is also used for animal handling, but the animal stats have an allowed defect of 20%, so the benefit is not much higher than a [[denture]].

For pawns with a missing jaw, then the bionic jaw must be compared to a [[denture]]. Both replacements will replace the jaw and remove the [[disfigured]] social penalty.
* Dentures are more cost efficient. A denture is virtually free, costing nothing except the medicine used to install it. In addition, dentures are better for [[wealth management]] purposes - a denture reduces pawn value by -30%, while a bionic jaw increases value by 785 silvers.
* A bionic jaw improves Talking, making it a better if more expensive option for Social pawns. Outside of Social and Animal skill pawns, the only benefit is a net {{Good|x148.43%}} eating speed, which is typically not worth the crafting cost.
