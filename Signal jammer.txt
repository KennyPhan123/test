The '''signal jammer''' is a buildable [[gravship]] upgrade building in the [[Odyssey DLC]].

== Acquisition ==
{{Acquisition}}

== Summary ==
A signal jammer must be placed on a [[gravship]] to land on and attack [[Traders guild]] stations and [[Endings#The_Mechhive|the Mechhive]].

== Analysis ==
Signal jammers have no other benefits outside of enabling a gravship to land on certain locations. Unless there's plans to eventually visit those locations, there is no reason to build one.
