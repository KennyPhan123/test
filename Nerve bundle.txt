'''Nerve bundles''' are an entity spawned by the [[fleshmass heart]], providing a way to learn about and eventually defeat the heart.

== Summary ==
One nerve bundle spawns with the initial growth spurt of the fleshmass heart, with additional bundles appearing one at a time at random intervals, possibly taking days for a sufficient quantity of nerve bundles to spawn. They drop a single [[Neural lump]] upon destruction, which you can order a colonist to analyze. It takes 3 neural lumps to be analyzed to find a way to destroy the fleshmass heart, by ordering a colonist to "Trigger Tachycardiac Overload" by direct contact with the heart.

== Analysis ==
As it may take days to spawn a new nerve bundle, it is better to have colonists continue their normal routines instead of waiting while drafted to destroy the next nerve bundle. The path to a nerve bundle may be blocked by flesh, so as few flesh blocks should be destroyed as possible to avoid triggering more fleshbeast spawns.
