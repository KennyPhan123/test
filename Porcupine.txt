A '''porcupine''' is an [[animal]] added by the [[Odyssey DLC]].

== Acquisition ==
{{PAGENAME}}s can be found in {{Habitats}}. They can either be tamed by a [[Work #Handle|handler]] or self-tame in a random event. 

{{PAGENAME}}s can be bought and sold in other [[Trade#Faction base|faction bases]] and from [[Trade#Exotic goods trader 2|exotic goods traders]]. {{PAGENAME}}s purchased from traders will be already tamed.

== Summary ==
When struck in melee, slaughtered, or when a porcupine attacks a creature, porcupines leave quills against their enemy. Attacking or slaughtering always leaves a quill in the attacker's torso, while being attacked by a porcupine leaves quills in whichever body parts were harmed. Quills cause {{++|5%}} [[pain]] each, but do not appear to have any other negative effects.{{Check Tag|Infections?}} Each body part can only receive one quill, although tiny parts like toes can each get a quill, and they can be removed through a [[Doctoring#Operations|medical procedure]].

== Analysis ==
Porcupines can be hunted safely using ranged attacks, and butchering them doesn't result in any quills.

Quills may be intentionally left in [[Masochist|masochistic]] or [[Pain (precept)|pain idealized]]{{IdeologyIcon}} pawns to improve mood, or to incapacitate [[prisoner]]s or [[entities]]{{AnomalyIcon}} that can feel pain.

=== Tamed animal ===
Porcupines make poor general tamed animals due to their high wildness, small size, and inability to be trained without a [[sentience catalyst]]. They have low HP, so they are ineffective in combat even when considering their quills. Instead, the main reason to tame a porcupine is to inflict quills onto a desired target. 

=== Nutrition ===
An adult porcupine consumes {{P|Real Hunger Rate}} nutrition per day, and a female creates up to {{#expr: 1/{{P|Gestation Period Days}} round 2}} offspring per day.
* When offspring are slaughtered as babies, a female porcupine will produce {{#expr:{{Meat Production|{{PAGENAME}}|baby}} round 2}} nutrition of meat per day, giving an optimal nutrition efficiency of {{%|{{Meat Production|{{PAGENAME}}|baby}}/{{P|Real Hunger Rate}} round 3}}.
* If the offspring are allowed to grow to adulthood, they will consume an additional {{#expr:{{Nutrition Consumption|{{PAGENAME}}|adult}} - {{P|Real Hunger Rate}} round 2}} nutrition per day, but will instead yield {{#expr:{{Meat Production|{{PAGENAME}}|adult}} round 2}} nutrition per day as they are slaughtered, resulting in a nutrition efficiency of {{%|{{Meat Production|{{PAGENAME}}|adult}}/{{Nutrition Consumption|{{PAGENAME}}|adult}} round 3}}.
When considering a population of equal numbers of males and females, these nutrition efficiencies fall to {{%|{{Meat Production|{{PAGENAME}}|baby|1|1}}/{{Nutrition Consumption|{{PAGENAME}}|baby|1|1}} round 3}} for baby slaughter and {{%|{{Meat Production|{{PAGENAME}}|adult|1|1}}/{{Nutrition Consumption|{{PAGENAME}}|adult|1|1}} round 3}} for adult slaughter. This is further reduced in practice by the [[overeating]] factor.

* In real life, porcupine quills are coated in mildly antibiotic fatty acids, which are believed to serve the purpose of protecting the porcupine from infections in the event of self-injury.
