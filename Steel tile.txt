'''Steel tile''' is a type of [[floor]]. It is inherently [[Cleanliness|clean]], non-[[flammable]], and neutral [[beauty]]-wise.

== Acquisition ==
{{Acquisition}}

== Summary ==
Steel tiles have a {{+|{{P|Cleanliness}}}} [[cleanliness]] value, and have a [[cleaning time multiplier]] of {{Good|{{%|{{P|Cleaning Time Factor}}}}}}. Unlike [[Silver tile|silver]] and [[gold tile]], steel tile is not considered a [[fine floor]] for [[noble]]s in the [[Royalty DLC]].

== Analysis ==
Unlike steel furniture and walls, steel tile will not burn. It can be constructed out of {{Icon Small|steel}} {{P|Resource 1 Amount}} [[steel]], making it the second most expensive steel-type flooring.

With its neutral beauty, cleanliness and being quick to clean, it is an effective general purpose floor that will retain impressiveness better than many other floors, however the high steel cost makes this untenable in many colonies.

=== Cleanliness ===
{{Stub|section=1|reason= Normal vs sterile vs steel for different room types with actual numerical stat comparisons (table maybe?). [[Sterile tile#Analysis]] has good detail}}
Being quick to clean and with an intrinsic [[cleanliness]] bonus, steel tile is a useful option for kitchens, [[Research]] and [[genetics]]{{BiotechIcon}} laboratories and hospitals.

Steel tile provides less cleanliness per tile and per wealth ({{#expr: {{P|Cleanliness}}/{{P|Market Value Base}} }}/ {{Icon Small|silver}} vs {{#expr: {{Q|Sterile tile|Cleanliness}}/{{Q|Sterile tile|Market Value Base}} }}/ {{Icon Small|silver}}) than [[sterile tile]] but it has a greater beauty ({{P|Beauty}} vs {{Q|Sterile tile|Beauty}}). More importantly, steel tile only requires [[Research#{{P|Required Research}}|{{P|Required Research}}]] which almost every colony will unlock already in order to progress, while sterile tile requires [[Research#{{Q|Sterile tile|Required Research}}|{{Q|Sterile tile|Required Research}}]] which both requires more research total but also is much less generally useful.
