'''Spots''' are a category of building that allow certain tasks to be performed at them, but do not require a [[colonist]] to construct or [[deconstruct]]. Similarly, there is no cost, build time, or skill requirements for placing them. Instead, they are simply selected from the menu and placed directly by the player.
