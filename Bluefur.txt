== Analysis ==
Bluefur is one of many [[textiles]] with stats almost identical to [[plainleather]], only diverging in having better cold insulation and slightly higher market value and [[Property:Beauty Factor|beauty factor]].

As it is 6-way tied for the 11th most protective textile in the game, it is largely irrelevant as protective [[clothing]] after the very early game, and even then there are many other options that are just as good or better. While its cold insulation is moderately good, there are several textiles that provide both better protection and equivalent or better insulation, including [[Wolfskin|wolf]] and [[bearskin]], [[heavy fur]], [[devilstrand]] and, of course, [[thrumbofur]]. Thus, like its use in protection, bluefur will be superseded in this role after the early game.  

For use in [[furniture]], it only has a slightly improved [[Property:Beauty Factor|beauty factor]] than other common textiles, but its rapid redundancy as clothing makes it a decent choice for improving furniture [[beauty]]. Its inferiority to [[wool]]s in this regard and their commonness compared to bluefur limits its long term utility however.
