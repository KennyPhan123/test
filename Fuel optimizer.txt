The '''fuel optimizer''' is a [[building]] added by the [[Odyssey DLC]] that reduces the amount of [[chemfuel]] consumed per world tile travelled by a [[gravship]].

== Acquisition ==
Fuel optimizers cannot be built. They can only be acquired through Odyssey-related [[quests]] and locations discovered with the [[orbital scanner]].

== Summary == 
Fuel optimizers can be placed anywhere within on the [[gravship]]. Each fuel optimiser reduces the [[chemfuel]] required per tile traveled by the gravship by {{Icon Small|Chemfuel}} {{---|3}}. Up to two optimizers can affect a gravship at one time, reducing the chemfuel cost per tile from {{Icon Small|Chemfuel||10}} to {{Icon Small|Chemfuel||4}}. Note that this does not affect the minimum take off cost of {{Icon Small|Chemfuel||50}}. In other words, the fuel consumption of a gravship can be stated as follows:

== Analysis == 
While fuel optimizers are not strictly necessary, as [[chemfuel]] is renewable and not difficult to farm, they can save significant amounts of chemfuel and thus colonist time. They primarily appear in "gravship upgrade" loot piles, so it is likely that fuel optimizers will be obtained if looking for gravship upgrades in general.

Two fuel optimizers is enough to reduce the fuel cost to the point that a single [[large chemfuel tank]] can support the maximum possible travel distance.
