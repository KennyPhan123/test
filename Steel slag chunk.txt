'''Steel slag chunks''' are a type of [[chunk]] created by the destruction of several different structures and objects. They provide [[cover]] and can be smelted at an [[Electric smelter]] for {{icon Small|steel||15}} [[steel]].

== Acquisition == 
{{Stub|reason=What structures and exactly which pods create them, in what proportions.}}
Steel slag chunks are created from destroying certain buildings, [[raider]]'s drop pods, and [[cargo pods]] event, but never the pods of [[orbital trader]]'s or [[quest]] reward delivery.

== Summary ==
Their primary use is to be smelted at an [[Electric smelter]] for {{icon Small|steel||15}} [[steel]]. This requires {{ticks|400}} of work.

When targeted by the [[transmute steel]]{{AnomalyIcon}} ability, a chunk will be transformed into 15 units of either [[twisted meat]],{{AnomalyIcon}} [[bioferrite]],{{AnomalyIcon}} [[gold]], [[plasteel]], or [[uranium]], determined randomly.

Steel slag chunks also provide 50% [[cover]].

== Analysis ==
Steel slag chunks are a free, renewable soure of steel that can be harvested as soon as an [[electric smelter]] is built. There are many other sources of steel, so building a smelter and smelting chunks should not be immediately focused on unless playing on a map without ore (e.g., [[sea ice]]).

Smelting a steel slag chunk would take {{ticks|400/15 round 1}} per 1 steel, which takes less time than mining or [[deep drill]]ing. However, this does not take into account hauling time. Since drop pods usually land far away, it takes a long time to haul slag chunks back. Due to this, as well as the cost of the smelter - {{Required Resources|Electric smelter}}, requiring 12 chunks to break even - smelters are not terribly efficient and should not be built in the very early game. Still, smelters are a renewable source of steel, which will convert junk weapons/armor and chunks into a steady stream of steel, so they should be built once there is enough smeltable material on the map.

If you have a [[mechanitor]],{{BiotechIcon}} then [[lifter]]s and/or [[fabricor]]s can be used to automate the hauling process.

=== Cover ===
As a [[chunk]], it provides 50% [[cover]], so should be cleared to a stockpile away from areas where enemies could use them. Conversely, they should be exploited when fighting field battles to provide cover to your own pawns. With its modestly high {{HP}} and non-flammability, it makes for more durable and rapidly deployable early cover than [[wood]]en [[barricades]] at the cost of providing 5% less cover.
