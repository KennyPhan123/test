{{Info|The '''aesthetic shaper''' is a [[Royalty (DLC)|royalty]] [[artificial body part]] that improves the [[pawn beauty|beauty]] of the implanted pawn.}}

== Acquisition ==
{{Acquisition}}

== Summary ==
The aesthetic shaper is a visceral implant that does not replace any natural [[body part]]. The aesthetic shaper increases a [[pawn beauty|pawn's beauty]] by {{+|1}}, which increases the pawn's market value, and also increases other pawns' opinion of them by {{+|20}}. Note that all pawn beauty modifiers are additive and that a net change in pawn beauty above +2 or below -2 will have no further effect. See [[pawn beauty]] for more details.

The aesthetic shaper does not add any stat offsets to the pawn other than beauty.

As an artificial part, aesthetic shapers will give a mood buff to anyone with the [[Body modder]] trait, and a mood penalty to anyone with the [[Body purist]] trait. They will also satisfy colonists with a [[transhumanist]] ideology.{{IdeologyIcon}}

=== Installation ===
[[Doctoring#Operations|Installing the part]] requires {{Ticks|2500}} of work, 2x medicine of [[Herbal medicine|herbal]] quality or better, and a [[Skills#Medical|Medical skill]] of 5.

Removing the part requires {{Ticks|2500}} of work, 1x medicine of [[Herbal medicine|herbal]] quality or better, and a [[Skills#Medical|Medical skill]] of 5.

If the operation [[Doctoring#Failure|fails]], the part will be destroyed.

== Analysis ==
The aesthetic shaper improves the beauty of the pawn, increasing [[opinion]] of others. It is useful for social harmony, as higher opinion makes pawns less likely to insult each other and start [[social fight]]s. It can help couples, fight traits like [[Annoying voice]], and counteract negative opinions from [[ideoligion]].{{IdeologyIcon}} It increases a warden's ability to reduce [[Prisoner#Recruitment|prisoner resistance]] by a small amount. 

It can also be particularly useful to compensate for reduced beauty: it can cancel out the opinion malus from the [[ugly]] trait or the beauty reduction from the [[armorskin gland]].{{RoyaltyIcon}} When combined with the [[aesthetic nose]],{{RoyaltyIcon}} they can compensate for the opinion malus from the [[staggeringly ugly]] trait or the beauty reduction from the [[stoneskin gland]].{{RoyaltyIcon}}

Increasing a pawn's beauty above 2 has no additional effect on opinion. For example, a [[beautiful]] pawn will have a {{+|40}} opinion boost with or without aesthetic implants being installed. Reaching pawn beauty above 2 merely results in "reserve beauty", allowing that pawn to carry some ugliness like an [[armorskin gland]]{{RoyaltyIcon}} or [[flesh tentacle]]{{AnomalyIcon}} without losing their beauty bonuses. Also, keep in mind that the opinion bonus is '''completely''' disregarded if the pawn is [[disfigured]] (e.g. due to injury on the eyes or ears). Beauty and disfigurement also never affect the opinions of pawns who have the [[Kind]] or [[Psychopath]] traits, or who are completely [[Sight|blind]].

On the negative side, increased beauty will also increase the chance of [[Social#Likelihood_of_success|romantic rebuffs]] from pawns already romantically committed (unless their [[ideoligion]]{{IdeologyIcon}} permits multiple spouses).

However, this implant costs [[advanced component]]s to make, which could otherwise be used for other [[bionics]]. The cost of aesthetic implants should be weighed against a [[bionic arm]] or [[bionic leg]], which have stronger, tangible impacts on pawn performance. While it is a cheaper implant which pleases [[body modder]]s all the same, the net {{+|1-2}} mood after having a single part like a [[denture]] is generally not worth the material cost. You can also reduce the effect of low opinion by separating pawns through their [[schedule]]; a pawn on the night shift won't interact with a daytime pawn nearly as much.

It stacks with the [[aesthetic nose]], though beauty has no impact beyond +2.  Note that if a pawn is [[disfigured]] (e.g. missing a nose, ears, etc.), then all beauty bonuses are ignored.  Beauty also never affects other pawns who have the [[Ascetic]], [[Kind]], or [[Psychopath]] traits.
