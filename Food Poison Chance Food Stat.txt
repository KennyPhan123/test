== Explanation ==
Food Poison Chance (Food Stat) acts as the direct chance to receive [[Hediffs#Food poisoning|food poisoning]] when eating raw food or corpses. It acts as a base value when these ingredients are cooked into [[meals]], using the [[Food Poison Chance|Food Poison Chance (Pawn Work)]] and [[Cleanliness|room cleanliness]] stats as factors.

Rotten food (i.e. corpses, since rotten food items disappear once rotten) have a 100% chance of causing food poisoning, although rotten corpses have no nutrition<ref>[[Nutrition_(Stat)#Is Corpse Fresh|Is Corpse Fresh]] Stat Part from [[Nutrition (Stat)]]</ref> and their ingestibility is very limited if not impossible{{Check Tag|Fact check needed}}.
