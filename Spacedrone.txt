A '''spacedrone''' is an ancient spacedrone ship added by the [[Ideology DLC]] and utilized by [[Biotech DLC]]. It acts as an existence for quest related to finding the relic, and for mechanitor quest. Unlike the [[crashed shuttle]], it has no further use and cannot be used as a transport despite it being a ship.

== Acquisition ==
Spacedrone will only land after a while once its specific quest has been initiated.

== Summary ==
(For Ideology quest)
45000 hacking defence. When self destruct explodes with bomb type in 6 tile radius for 50 damage after {{Ticks|1800}} wick time, sends messages at 30, 20, 10 seconds. Self-destruction provides the deconstruct yield.

(For Biotech)
Appears as deconstructable upon landing. Has no other use aside from its own quest.
