'''Dogfish''' are large cold saltwater [[fish]]. 

== Acquisition ==
Dogfish will sometimes inhabit large bodies of saltwater in the following biomes: {{#ask: [[Saltwater Uncommon Dogfish::Yes]]|format=ul}}
The dogfish population can be converted into raw food through the work of a fisher in a [[fishing zone]].

===Bad fishing outcomes===
Dogfish have a chance to either bite, dealing 4-6 [[bite]] damage to the fisher, or to escape.

== Summary ==
As a [[raw food]], dogfish can be eaten raw, with a chance of giving [[food poisoning]], however this chance half that of raw foods at {{Bad|1%}}. They can also be cooked into a [[meal]] with a food poisoning chance dependent on the skill of the cook and the [[cleanliness]] of the kitchen, with no additional malus when compared to other raw foods. [[Animals]] are immune to food poisoning from raw food but not from meals. When used in food recipes that require specific types of [[nutrition]], such as [[fine meal]]s, dogfish are classified as meat.

As with most raw food, eating dogfish directly results in a {{Thought|desc=I had to eat raw food. We should be cooking that kind of food before eating it. We're not animals.|label=ate raw food|value=-7|duration=1}}. This mood penalty is negated if the pawn has the [[robust digestion]] gene or the [[ascetic]] trait.
