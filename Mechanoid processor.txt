'''Mechanoid processors''' are big mechanoid buildings. They have no special mechanics other than occupying space and providing cover.
== Occurrence ==
Mechanoid processors appear in [[Mechanoid platform]]s. They cannot be built by the player.

== Summary ==
Mechanoid processors act as a wall blocking line of sight and providing 75% cover. They drop 6 [[steel slag chunk]]s when destroyed.
