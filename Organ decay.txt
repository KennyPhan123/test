'''Organ decay''' is a rare, [[pain]]ful, and untreatable [[disease]] that causes increasingly reduced function in a particular organ, until destroying it completely.

== Overview ==
Organ decay can affect the [[heart]], [[lung]]s (left and/or right), and [[kidney]]s (left and/or right); multiple organs can be afflicted simultaneously (at least 3, at most, 5). Organ decay causes [[pain]] and reduced [[Capacity|part efficiency]] (for example, [[breathing]]) in the afflicted organ with the associated knock-on effects. When the disease progress reaches 100% the affected organ will be destroyed- which is immediately fatal if the heart is destroyed. 

The decaying organ can be surgically removed, but it cannot be harvested.

Organ decay will reach full saturation after 30 to 40 days. Accelerated organ decay, which appears on [[creepjoiner]]s,{{AnomalyIcon}} will take only 10 to 20 days to reach maximum saturation. Surgical Inspection can reveal the presence of Organ Decay before the normal reveal. [[File:Diagnosed_Organ_Decay.png|thumb|600px|right|Organ Decay Diagnosed early via [[surgical inspection]].]]

The [[perfect immunity]] gene{{BiotechIcon}} will prevent the acquisition of this disease during disease events, but it does not prevent organ decay from occurring on pawns at generation, such as with [[Corrupted obelisk|duplicated pawns]]{{AnomalyIcon}}. In such cases, organ decay is treated as a pre-existing condition that the gene cannot intercept, and the disease will progress in the same way as in pawns without the perfect immunity gene.

== Stages ==
==== Minor ====
* {{++|5%}} [[Pain]]
* {{--|10%}} Part efficiency

==== Moderate ====
* {{++|10%}} [[Pain]]
* {{--|25%}} Part efficiency

==== Severe ====
* {{++|15%}} [[Pain]]
* {{--|50%}} Part efficiency

==== Extreme ====
* {{++|25%}} [[Pain]]
* {{--|75%}} Part efficiency
* Complete loss of function at 1 severity. Note that this will result in the pawn's death if the heart, both kidneys, or both lungs are affected and reach this severity.

== Progression ==
=== Stages ===
If obtained by a disease event, it is detected immediately and starts at 0 severity. Severity increases over time, at a rate of 1.66% to 3.34% per day for normal organ decay, and at a rate of 5% to 10% for the accelerated version.{{AnomalyIcon}}

Organ decay can appear as a downside on [[creepjoiner]]s or [[Corrupted obelisk|duplicates]],{{AnomalyIcon}} although it initially stays undiagnosed. After diagnosis, it starts at 0.02% to 15% severity.
* Minor - Severity: 0 - 0.199
* Moderate - Severity: 0.2 - 0.399
* Severe - Severity: 0.4 - 0.599
* Extreme - Severity: 0.6 - 0.999
* Loss of function - Severity: 1

== Treatment ==
Organ decay cannot be tended to or treated on its own, nor can it be prevented by [[penoxycyline]]. Instead, the organ must be replaced by any natural or artificial replacement, or the condition healed by [[healer mech serum]], [[luciferium]], the [[scarless]] [[gene]],{{BiotechIcon}} or [[unnatural healing]].{{AnomalyIcon}} The [[bioregeneration cycle]]{{IdeologyIcon}} will not work. Note that luciferium and scarless heal conditions on a 15 day interval, so it may not be enough to cure accelerated organ decay.

== Analysis ==
If only a single lung or kidney is affected, organ decay is not directly lethal. Still, the loss of an organ will reduce productivity, increase risk of death by destruction of the other organ, and cause pain until the organ is actually removed or replaced. Loss of a kidney will also make a pawn [[Immunity Gain Speed|more vulnerable]] to other [[disease]]s.

The simplest way to cure organ decay is to [[human resources|harvest replacement organs]] from a [[prisoner]], but this inflicts a [[mood]] penalty on standard colonists. It is also possible to use [[artificial body part|artificial counterparts]]:
* Heart: [[Prosthetic heart]], [[bionic heart]]. Note that installing an organic [[heart]] or prosthetic heart has an unavoidable chance to kill the pawn if the surgery fails, while a [[bionic heart]] does not have this chance. 
* Lungs: [[Detoxifier lung]],{{BiotechIcon}} [[fleshmass lung]].{{AnomalyIcon}} Note that a fleshmass lung can only be given by a [[corrupted obelisk]], which cannot target a specific part.
* Kidney: [[Detoxifier kidney]]{{BiotechIcon}}
