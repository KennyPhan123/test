The '''Caribou''', called '''caribou calves''' as babies, is a large [[Herbivore|herbivorous]] [[animal]] that provides [[meat]], [[plainleather]] and, if tamed, [[milk]].

== Acquisition ==
Caribou can be found in {{Habitats|{{PAGENAME}}|or}}. They can either be tamed by a [[Work #Handle|handler]] or self-tame in a random event.

Caribou can be bought and sold in other [[Trade#Faction base|faction bases]] and from [[Trade#Exotic goods trader 2|exotic goods traders]]. Caribou purchased from traders will be already tamed.

== Summary ==
{{Pen Animal Note}}

A caribou can be milked for up to {{Icon Small|milk}} {{P|Milk Amount}} units of milk {{#ifeq:{{P|Milking Interval Days}}|1|per day|every {{P|Milking Interval Days}} days}}, depending on your pawn's [[Animal Gather Yield]]. Milking an animal takes {{Ticks|400}} of work, regardless of the amount of milk produced, and only modified by the [[Animal Gather Speed]] of the handler.

== Analysis ==
Caribou are animals to be hunted in colder biomes, and serve little other purpose. They are one of the few animals to come in herds ''and'' that have a manhunter chance, meaning the entire herd can revenge on a bad shot.

As tamed animals, caribou are almost entirely inferior to [[yak]]s, as 2 caribou produce less milk and eat more than 1 yak. Yaks are also [[pack animal]]s while caribou aren't.

===Milking===
Female caribou produce {{Icon Small|milk}} {{P|Daily Milk Average}} per day, or {{#vardefineecho:milk_nutr_per_day|{{#expr:{{P|Daily Milk Average}}*0.05}}}} [[nutrition]] in milk per day. A caribou consumes {{P|Real Hunger Rate}} nutrition per day. This results in a nutrition efficiency of {{%|{{#var:milk_nutr_per_day}}/{{P|Real Hunger Rate}} round 3}} from milk alone (or {{Icon Small|milk}} {{#expr: {{P|Daily Milk Average}}/{{P|Real Hunger Rate}} round 2}} per day per 1 nutrition consumed).

Yaks produce {{%|(({{Q|Yak|Daily Milk Average}}/{{P|Daily Milk Average}})-1) round 2}} more milk than caribou on average, but eat only {{%|(({{Q|Yak|Real Hunger Rate}}/{{P|Real Hunger Rate}})-1) round 2}} more food.
