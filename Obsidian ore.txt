'''Obsidian (ore)''' is a rare natural [[structure]] that can be [[mine]]d to obtain {{Icon Small|Obsidian}} [[obsidian]].

== Occurrence ==
Mineral obsidian veins can only spawn in maps with the [[Landmarks#Obsidian_deposits|obsidian deposits]] feature, exclusive to the [[lava field]] biome. They never occur outside of obsidian deposits. Veins are fairly large, 20 to 40 tiles in size. 

=== Number of veins in map ===
The number of veins is a function of the hilliness and the size of the map. Standard size maps have 5 obsidian veins on small hills, 7 on large hills, and 9 on mountainous terrain. Lava field maps cannot have flat terrain.

== Summary ==
Obsidian tiles have 1,200 health each, making them one of the fastest ores to mine. Each mined block has a base yield of {{Icon Small|Obsidian}} 40 obsidian, modified by factors such as the Difficulty setting and the [[Mining yield]] of the pawn.

== Analysis ==
Mining obsidian ore is the best way to acquire obsidian, a great material for crafting sharp melee weapons like [[longsword]]s. Veins are large enough to yield the materials for a few longswords. With several veins spawning per map it's feasible to spam craft obsidian weapons to farm high quality weapons.
