'''Harbinger seed''' can be planted to become a [[Harbinger tree]].

== Acquisition ==
Harbinger seeds can be obtained through trading (Exotic Goods merchant ship or Tribal Shaman caravan merchant) or fleshsacks from [[Quests#Distress_signal|Distress signals]].

== Summary ==
Can be planted to grow a [[Harbinger tree]], allowing you to control the precise location of the trees, as opposed to the semi-random growth of new sprouts within the proximity of mature Harbinger trees.
