The '''vacsuit''' is an item of [[apparel]] added by the [[Odyssey DLC]]. It forms a set with the [[vacsuit helmet]].

== Acquisition == 
{{Acquisition}}

Alternatively, vacsuits can be received as a quest reward or obtained from raiders in orbit.

== Summary ==
A vacsuit increases the [[vacuum resistance]] of its wearer by {{+|{{%|{{P|Vacuum Resistance Offset}}}}}}. [[Quality]] does not affect this resistance.

{{Apparel Move Speed Note|1.25}}

== Analysis ==
===Vacuum use===
The vacsuit and [[vacsuit helmet]] set provides a complete {{+|100%}} Vacuum Resistance, enabling pawns to survive in the vacuum of [[orbit|space]] until the armor breaks. In addition, the vacsuit set is the cheapest and earliest unlocked set to allow safe orbit travel. However, the vacsuit has a whopping {{--|1.25}} {{CS}} penalty to move speed - over double that of [[cataphract armor]] {{RoyaltyIcon}}. Colonists should only equip vacsuits when needed - the prohibitive reduction to movement speed will otherwise greatly impact their productivity during regular activities in and around the colony.

Any of the [[power armor]] suits will have better protection and a lower movement penalty, while having a slightly lower {{+|30%}} Vacuum Resistance. This vacuum resistance is not enough to reach complete vacuum immunity without external support. Still, their resistance is usually ''enough'' to survive extended periods in orbit, especially if there is an oxygenated room available. 
* Even [[recon armor]] and a [[recon helmet]] will provide {{+|95%}} Vacuum Resistance when worn together, enabling airless survival for as long as {{ticks/gametime/days|60000}}. 
* Simply equipping a [[vacsuit helmet]] increases resistance to {{+|99%}}, granting an impressive {{ticks/gametime/days|300000}} of safety. 

However, power armor requires more research and is much more expensive, so vacsuits are useful before power armor is affordable. In addition, if there is no oxygenated room available, e.g., if using a [[drop pod]] or [[shuttle]] to enter orbit, then having 100% vacuum immunity is more valuable.

With the [[vacskin gland]],{{RoyaltyIcon}} power armor by itself is enough to reach 100% Vacuum Resistance. The [[vacuum resistant]] [[gene]]{{BiotechIcon}} gives enough resistance for a power armor + power helmet set to reach 100% immunity, and the [[Breathless]] gene{{BiotechIcon}} gives total immunity on its own.

===Combat===
Vacsuits are a poor choice for planetary combat. Compared to [[plate armor]], vacsuits offer significantly less Sharp/Blunt protection and have a greater move penalty. It is further outclassed by the flak set, as each flak piece ([[flak vest|vest]], [[flak jacket|jacket]], [[flak pants|pants]]) provides more Sharp protection on their own than the entire vacsuit. It does have better Heat protection, but [[devilstrand]] [[duster]]s + flak vests are a better combo for that.

In [[Scenario system#Default Scenarios|The Gravship starting scenario]], vacsuits can be a decent choice for combat before other armor is obtained or crafted. Even then, certain colonists might prefer the added movement speed of wearing no armor.
