{{Info|'''Rhinoceros''' are a large species of herbivore that tend to travel alone or in small packs.}}

== Acquisition ==
Rhinoceroses can be found in {{Habitats}}. They can either be tamed by a [[Work #Handle|handler]] or self-tame in a random event. 

Rhinoceroses can be bought and sold in other [[Trade#Faction base|faction bases]] and from [[Trade#Exotic goods trader 2|exotic goods traders]]. Rhinoceroses purchased from traders will be already tamed.

== Analysis ==
Rhinoceroses are easy to anger, whether you hunt or tame them. Their [[{{lc:{{P|Leather Name}}}}]] is highly protective, the fourth most protective [[textile]] in the game. This can make rhinos worthwhile to hunt early on, assuming you can take down a pack. It generally isn't worth to breed rhinoceroses for meat or leather, however, due to their slow reproduction and difficulty to tame. [[Devilstrand]] is a stronger material, and often easier to produce than rhinoceros leather.

Rhinoceroses are decent attack animals, possessing a fast movement speed combined with a tanky ability to take damage and deal more damage back in return. Unlike the others in this table, however, they cannot be trained to haul.
