The '''toxipotato plant''' is a domesticated [[plant]] added by the [[Biotech DLC]]. It notably '''must''' be planted on [[pollution|polluted terrain]]; Toxipotatoes cannot be grown on unpolluted terrain and will rapidly die if planted on a tile that is subsequently cleaned of pollution.

It is worth noting that toxipotato plants still die to [[toxic fallout]].

== Analysis ==
Toxipotato plants ({{Q|Toxipotato plant|Harvest_Yield_Per_Day_(100)}} base yield/day) are strictly inferior in yield to both [[potato plant]]s ({{Q|Potato plant|Harvest_Yield_Per_Day_(100)}} yield/day) and [[rice plant]]s ({{Q|Rice plant|Harvest_Yield_Per_Day_(100)}} yield/day) in regular soil. While {{+|{{%|-1+{{Q|Toxipotato plant|Harvest Yield Per Work}}/{{Q|Rice plant|Harvest Yield Per Work}} round 4}}}} better in yield/work than rice, toxipotatoes take {{Bad|x{{%|{{Q|Toxipotato plant|Real Grow Days}} / {{Q|Rice plant|Real Grow Days}}round 3}}}} the time per plant to grow, so are a less reliable crop. If work ''is'' a concern, then regular potatoes ({{+|{{%|-1+{{Q|Potato plant|Harvest Yield Per Work}}/{{Q|Toxipotato plant|Harvest Yield Per Work}} round 4}}}}) or [[corn plant|corn]] ({{+|{{%|-1+{{Q|Corn plant|Harvest Yield Per Work}}/{{Q|Toxipotato plant|Harvest Yield Per Work}} round 4}}}}) are much better options for yield.

Toxipotatoes can be grown on polluted soil, but their abysmal yield in all aspects means that it would take a heavily polluted map, where little clean soil is nearby, to make it feasible. Even then, you could clear out a field to grow more traditional plants in. Cleaning can take a while, so toxipotatoes can be used in the earlier stages of an extremely polluted colony.

The extra [[food poisoning]] chance is negligible, as raw food should always be cooked anyways.
