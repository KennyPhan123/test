A '''vulture''' is an [[animal]] added by the [[Odyssey DLC]].

== Acquisition ==
Vultures can be found in {{Habitats}}. They can either be tamed by a [[Work #Handle|handler]] or self-tame in a random event. 

Vultures can be bought and sold in other [[Trade#Faction base|faction bases]] and from [[Trade#Exotic goods trader 2|exotic goods traders]]. Vultures purchased from traders will be already tamed.

== Summary ==
Despite being carnivores, vultures can only eat raw [[meat]] and fresh organic [[corpse]]s. Vultures '''cannot''' eat rotting corpses. {{Check Tag|Verify|Is this a bug?}}

A female vulture produces {{P|Eggs Per Clutch Average}} [[vulture egg (fert.)]] every {{P|Egg Laying Interval}} day when fertilized. Vultures will not lay unfertilized eggs.

== Analysis ==
In combat, they can be used as a sturdier alternative to [[crow]]s, but due to the long egg-laying interval and the meat requirement, holding a large number will require a good supply of meat.
