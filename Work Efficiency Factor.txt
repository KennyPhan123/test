}} This acts as a multiplier on the products of recipe bills performed at the work table. For example, a value of 50% would result in bills returning only half of the products they would produce with a value of 100%.

The default value is {{#expr:{{P|Default Base Value}}*100}}%. The minimum value is {{#expr:{{P|Stat Minimum}}*100}}%

The only workstation with a non-default ''Work Table Efficiency Factor'' is the [[Butcher spot]] with a value of {{%|{{Q|Butcher spot|Work Efficiency Factor}}}}.
