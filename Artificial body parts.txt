{{imagemargin|[[File:artificial_preview.png|right]]|30px}}'''Artificial body parts''' are - as the name would imply - non-organic [[body parts]] that exist with the purpose of replacing a missing or damaged body part. There are a variety of different body parts that can be prosthetically replaced into/on pawns. Some serve to replace damaged / missing natural body parts, others are improvements over the original organ. Some offer implanted weaponry or additional capabilities. 

Most prostheses fall loosely under one of several categories based on [[tech level]] and [[research]]:

* Improvised: Crude, makeshift replacements fashioned on the spot, and usually out of [[wood]]. Weak, but very cheap and better than nothing.
* [[Prosthetics]]: [[Machining table|Machined]] industrial replacements formed out of [[steel]] and [[component]]s. Either functioning as passable replacements for lost body parts, or as specialized bionics that come with some drawback.
* [[Bionics]]: Spacer level futuristic prosthetics, [[Fabrication bench|fabricated]] from [[plasteel]] and [[advanced component]]s. Matches performance if not outright better than the original body part.
* [[Archotech]]: The ultimate bionic replacements, designed solely by incomprehensible archotech minds. Cannot be crafted, only bought through [[trade]] and received through [[quest]] rewards.

In many cases, there is not an equivalent part for every tech level - there is not always a primitive, prosthetic, bionic, and archotech version of a given part. In the case of implants, they are usually available in one form only.

== Comparison to Flesh ==
Artificial parts offer some benefits over regular, fleshy counterparts:

* Body part replacements are immune to certain [[Ailments|health conditions]]. Replaced body parts cannot be [[Infection|infected]], while health conditions like [[cataract]]s, [[bad back]]s, and [[heart attack]]s are instantly cured and prevented depending on the part.
* Injuries to replacement artificial body parts cannot [[Bleeding|bleed]] or [[scar]], and do not cause [[pain]]. Wounds to [[Twisted obelisk|fleshmass mutations]]{{AnomalyIcon}} still bleed but will not generate additional pain.
* A replacement arm or leg essentially 'compacts' the entire limb, including the hand, foot, fingers, and toes, into the installation site; for arms this is the shoulder, for legs it is the leg. As a result, the usually-exposed hands and feet are now protected fully by arm-and-leg-covering apparel.

Like organic body parts, artificial parts heal naturally over time, and will be destroyed if the part reaches 0 health.

== Body Modders and Purists ==
Pawns with the [[Body modder]] or [[Body purist]] [[Traits]] will either get a [[Mood]] bonus or penalty depending on how many artificial body parts they have, if any at all, while also gaining different [[opinion]]s of other pawns depending on if they also have artificial parts installed. Nearly every single artificial enhancement installed on a pawn is counted- from a peg leg, organ replacements, [[Royalty]]'s{{RoyaltyIcon}} glandular enhancements (healing enhancer, immunoenhancer), archotech body parts, a [[Mechlink]],{{BiotechIcon}} and [[Anomaly]]{{AnomalyIcon}}'s flesh limbs and [[Revenant vertebrae]]. The only enhancements not considered by either trait is any levels of [[Psylink]]{{RoyaltyIcon}} and upgrades used by a Mechanitor beyond the mechlink. No clothing or equipment item is counted, specifically [[Eltex]] gear{{RoyaltyIcon}} or any psychically active [[Persona weapon]].{{IdeologyIcon}} An implanted [[xenogerm]]{{BiotechIcon}} will be counted as one single enhancement no matter how complex the xenogerm is.

== Installation and Removal ==
There are two types of artificial parts: implants and replacements. Replacements entirely take over for the function of natural body parts, while implants can be freely installed inside body parts provided there isn't already mutually exclusive implants.

Installing artificial body parts requires [[medical]] skill, with a minimum of 3 for wooden parts up to a minimum of 5 for implants.  Parts can be installed regardless of if they have been [[research]]ed, as the research is only required to manufacture the parts.

Most replacement parts and implants can be uninstalled freely to retrieve the item back, with the exception of [[joywire]]s, [[mindscrew]]s, [[death acidifier]]s and [[Twisted obelisk|obelisk mutations]].{{AnomalyIcon}} Artificial parts cannot be uninstalled from corpses, with the exception of the [[mechlink]]{{BiotechIcon}} which can ONLY be removed from corpses.

Artificial body parts cannot be installed on [[babies]],{{BiotechIcon}} but can be installed on [[children]].{{BiotechIcon}}

=== Part Compatibility ===
Most implants are compatible with all other implants with the exception of skin glands. However, replacement parts cannot have implants or other replacement parts installed onto them, a [[bionic arm]] cannot have a [[field hand]]{{RoyaltyIcon}} replacement or an [[elbow blade]]{{RoyaltyIcon}} implant attached.
