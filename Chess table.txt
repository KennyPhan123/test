The '''chess table''' is a piece of [[recreation]] furniture that provides cerebral recreation when used by colonists, as well as small trickle of [[Intellectual|intellectual skill]] experience. 

It is the equivalent to the [[Tech levels|neolithic]] [[Game-of-Ur board]], and only available to factions of Medieval tech level or above.

== Acquisition ==
{{Acquisition}} Chess tables can only be constructed by [[Scenario_system#New Tribe vs New Arrivals|New Arrivals]]. [[Scenario_system#New Tribe vs New Arrivals|New Tribes]] can never construct chess tables, and instead have the exclusive ability to construct [[game-of-Ur board]]s.

They are also available from [[Outlander]] [[traders]] and [[faction base]]s, and as [[quest]] rewards.

== Summary ==
[[file:chess-seating.png|thumb|left|All valid chess seating - orientation has no impact - as long a seat is adjacent to a chess table it's valid (example in bottom right shows chairs facing 'wrong' way but still being used]]
Chess tables must have chairs directly adjacent to it to be used. Up to two colonists at a time can sit in these chairs to play at the chess table, and gain [[comfort]] from the chairs as normal. The rate at which colonists gain [[Recreation]] from playing chess depends on the [[quality]] of the table, but the rate of gain is constant as long as the game is played. Note that a colonist playing chess alone gives the same recreation as playing at the same time as another. 

Playing chess also improves the player's [[Intellectual|Intellectual skill]] at a meager rate of 0.002 XP per [[tick]], or {{#expr: 60*0.002}} XP per real-time second.

== Analysis ==
Chess tables are one of the early recreational items, built once the colony has outgrown the [[Horseshoe pin]] alone. They are relatively cheap and effective and should be built early to provide variety to your colonists.

Compared to other recreation items, the chess table uses twice the materials of it's tribal equivalent, the [[Game-of-Ur board|game-of-Ur board]], but has a significantly higher base recreation power ({{%|{{P|Recreation Power}}}} vs. {{%|{{Q|Game-of-Ur board|Recreation Power}}}}). It also provides 33% more [[Intellectual]] skill XP than the [[Game-of-Ur board]]. It is advisable to replace any game-of-Ur boards with a chess table as soon as possible, so long as the boards are not of a sufficiently higher quality to make up for the deficit. 

The chess table is a very cheap option for intellectual play and gives a significant amount of recreational power at higher crafting levels, but does not produce as much cerebral play value as a [[poker table]]. It does not require cloth, however, and is an acceptable substitute until cloth can be grown or traded for a poker table. Once poker tables are available however, they should replace chess tables entirely.
