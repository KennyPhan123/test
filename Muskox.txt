A '''muskox''' (pl. '''muskoxen'''), called a '''muskox calf''' as a baby, is an [[animal]] added by the [[Odyssey DLC]].

== Acquisition ==
{{PAGENAME}}s can be found in {{Habitats}}. They can either be tamed by a [[Work #Handle|handler]] or self-tame in a random event. 

{{PAGENAME}}s can be bought and sold in other [[Trade#Faction base|faction bases]] and from [[Trade#Exotic goods trader 2|exotic goods traders]]. {{PAGENAME}}s purchased from traders will be already tamed.

== Summary ==
{{Pen Animal Note|Muskoxs}}

{{PAGENAME}}s can be sheared for {{Icon Small|Muskox wool}} {{P|Wool Amount}} [[muskox wool]] ({{Icon Small|silver|16|{{#expr: {{Q|Muskox wool|Market Value Base}}*{{P|Wool Amount}}}}}}) every {{P|Shearing Interval Days}} days for {{Ticks|1700}} of work. Note that yield and shearing time are modified by the shearer's [[Animal Gather Yield]] and [[Animal Gather Speed]] respectively.
