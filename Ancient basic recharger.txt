'''Ancient basic recharger''' is a [[Ruins|ruined]], non-functional variant of the [[mech recharger]].{{BiotechIcon}} It is defined in Core but only appears when the [[Biotech DLC]] and/or the [[Odyssey DLC]] are enabled.

== Occurrence ==
If the Biotech DLC is enabled, they can be found in the [[mechanitor complex]] quest site.

It is currently unclear if and when it appears when the Odyssey DLC is enabled.{{Check Tag|Detail needed}}

If neither DLC is enabled, they will not appear in-game at all.
