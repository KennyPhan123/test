A '''Harpsichord''' is a [[musical instrument]] that is a source of High Culture [[recreation]] and is a requirement for specific [[titles]].

== Acquisition ==
{{Acquisition}}

== Summary ==
{{Stub|section=1|reason= 1) Has almost no information about unique mechanics 2) Titles information}}
Nobles can put on concerts with the harpsichord, similar to a [[Events#Party|party]], giving a [[mood]] bonus to the noble and attendees.
Anyone listening to or playing the harpsichord receives a {{+|4}} thought for as long as it is being played. It can be heard within a radius of 16 tiles. Pawns with {{Bad|0%}} [[Hearing]] do not get the mood bonus.
{{Recreation Power Quality Table}}

The harpsichord cannot be played if the pawn has a [[manipulation]] capacity of 0%.
