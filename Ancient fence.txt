'''Ancient fence''' are [[ruins]] that are defined in Core but only appear when the [[Ideology DLC]] and/or the [[Odyssey DLC]] are enabled.

Deconstructing an ancient fence yields no resources.

== Occurrence ==
If the Ideology DLC is enabled, they can be found among the debris scattered on maps.

If the Odyssey DLC is enabled, they can be found scattered on [[scarlands]] maps, in [[junkyard]]s, and in the following landmarks: [[ancient warehouse]], [[ancient chemfuel refinery]], [[ancient launch site]], [[Landmarks#Ruins|ancient ruins]].

If neither DLC is enabled, they will not appear in-game at all.
