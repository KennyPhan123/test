'''Television''' may refer to one of several [[recreation]]al [[building]] types that provide the "Television" recreation type:  
*[[Tube television]]
*[[Flatscreen television]]
*[[Megascreen television]]
Both tube televisions and flatscreen televisions may be built with the required [[research]], but megascreen televisions are only offered as [[quest]] rewards or purchased from [[trader]]s. 

A pawn must have a [[Sight]] capacity above 0% to watch television.
