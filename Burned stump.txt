A '''burned stump''' is what remains after a [[tree]] has been burned down by [[fire]]. It blocks planting a new tree until it's removed. For the necessary {{Ticks|{{P|Harvest Work}} }} of work it yields only comparably few {{P|Harvest Yield}} {{Icon Small|{{P|Harvest Product}} }}. Also it's quite ugly and provides only relatively low {{%|{{P|Cover Effectiveness}} }} [[cover]].

Cutting them does not give a mood penalty to those with a precept against [[cutting trees]].{{IdeologyIcon}}

Despite not growing, it has growdays and lifespanDaysPerGrowDays values set. It is unclear what effect, if any, this has.
