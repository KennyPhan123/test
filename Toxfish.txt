'''Toxfish''' are tiny [[fish]] found in [[polluted]] water.

== Acquisition ==
Toxfish will sometimes replace the normal catch while [[fishing]] in polluted bodies of water. The chance of replacement scales with the proportion of polluted water tiles.

== Summary ==
As a [[raw food]], toxfish can be eaten raw, with a chance of giving [[food poisoning]], however this chance is quadruple that of other [[fish]] and double that of other raw foods at {{Bad|4%}}. They can also be cooked into a [[meal]] with a food poisoning chance dependent on the skill of the cook and the [[cleanliness]] of the kitchen, with no additional malus when compared to other raw foods. [[Animals]] are immune to food poisoning from raw food but not from meals. When used in food recipes that require specific types of [[nutrition]], such as [[fine meal]]s, toxfish are classified as meat.

As with most raw food, eating toxfish directly results in a {{Thought|desc=I had to eat raw food. We should be cooking that kind of food before eating it. We're not animals.|label=ate raw food|value=-7|duration=1}}. This mood penalty is negated if the pawn has the [[robust digestion]] gene or the [[ascetic]] trait.

== Analysis ==
