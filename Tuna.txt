'''Tuna''' are large warm saltwater [[fish]]. 

== Acquisition ==
Tuna will sometimes inhabit large bodies of saltwater in the following biomes: {{#ask: [[Saltwater Uncommon Tuna::Yes]]|format=ul}}
The tuna population can be converted into raw food through the work of a fisher in a [[fishing zone]].

=== Bad fishing outcomes ===
Tuna have a chance to escape. This does no harm to the fisher, but the work spent fishing will be wasted. 

== Summary ==
As a [[raw food]], tuna can be eaten raw, with a chance of giving [[food poisoning]], however this chance half that of raw foods at {{Bad|1%}}. They can also be cooked into a [[meal]] with a food poisoning chance dependent on the skill of the cook and the [[cleanliness]] of the kitchen, with no additional malus when compared to other raw foods. [[Animals]] are immune to food poisoning from raw food but not from meals. When used in food recipes that require specific types of [[nutrition]], such as [[fine meal]]s, tuna are classified as meat.

As with most raw food, eating tuna directly results in a {{Thought|desc=I had to eat raw food. We should be cooking that kind of food before eating it. We're not animals.|label=ate raw food|value=-7|duration=1}}. This mood penalty is negated if the pawn has the [[robust digestion]] gene or the [[ascetic]] trait.
