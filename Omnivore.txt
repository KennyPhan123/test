'''Omnivores''' are creatures that will eat [[vegetables]] or [[meat]]. Unlike their [[Omnivorous grazer|grazing counterparts]], they cannot eat live plants. They usually rely on predation for sustenance but will eat any [[food]] left lying about like raw [[meat]], [[vegetables]], or prepared [[meals]]. [[Hay]] is classified as a [[Food types#Plants|plant]], and so cannot be eaten by non-grazing omnivores unless made into kibble. All omnivores happen to be able to eat [[animal products]] due to having a secondary diet category.
