{{Info|Snagroot trees are a plant that naturally occur in {{Habitats}}, but can't be sown in hydroponics. A mature snagroot tree yields 8 [[wood]].}}

== Growing ==
Snagroot trees have a base growing time of 52 days and need a minimum soil fertility of 50%. Compared to [[fibercorn]]{{IdeologyIcon}} or even [[witchwood tree]]s,{{BiotechIcon}} Snagroot is an inferior source of wood due to its extremely long growing time, meagre yield and low fertility sensitivity.
Like [[witchwood tree]]s,{{BiotechIcon}} snagroot can grow on [[polluted]]{{BiotechIcon}} terrain.
