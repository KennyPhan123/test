The '''slugthrower''' is an [[mechanoid]]-only ranged [[weapon]] added by the [[Biotech DLC]]. It was the main armament of the [[fabricor]] and [[constructoid]] but was removed from both of these mechs. 

This weapon cannot be independently obtained in normal play. It is provided here for comparisons sake.

== Acquisition ==
Slugthrowers currently spawn on no pawns in game. Even when it did, it was not possible to obtain it directly in normal play. 

Pawns carrying it will not drop it on [[death]], [[downing]], or complete loss of [[manipulation]] like other weapons. It can be spawned in by the [[Development mode#Debug actions menu|Debug actions menu]] but, like all [[unobtainable items|unobtainable weapons]], will disappear after being dropped.

== Analysis ==
With mediocre range and abysmal {{DPS}} and accuracy, the slugthrower was not intended to be a serious weapon. Even opportunity cost of placing a non-combat mechanoid on the firing line might be sufficient to disqualify it from use. While the melee DPS is significantly higher, it is still mediocre and the fragility of the mech it was used on made it a bad idea in all but the most dire emergencies. 

Ultimately its removal did little to affect the game balance beyond removing a potential "noob trap".
