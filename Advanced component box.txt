The '''advanced component box''' is a structure added by the [[Odyssey DLC]]. Its contents are ejected when opened.

== Occurrence ==
Advanced component boxes are only found in some orbital sites: on settlement platforms, the abandoned orbital platform [[Quests#Odyssey|gravship quest]] site, and the orbital wreck, satellite, and abandoned platform [[orbital scanner]] quest sites.

== Summary ==
{{Loot Container Note}}

=== Loot ===
The advanced component box drops {{Icon Small|Advanced component||1-2}} [[advanced component]]s when opened.
