Ideoligion Spread Chance only determines the likelihood a pawn will try to organically convert a pawn of a different [[ideoligion]] to their own respective ideoligion. This has a base value of 100% and is affected by factors such as the [[Proselytizer]] meme and it's respective [[Proselytizing]] precept.
