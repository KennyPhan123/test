Fleshbeast attack is a [[major threat]] from the [[Anomaly DLC]]. Fleshbeast attack can be fired at [[monolith]] tier 1 or higher. The earliest day it can occur is day 15.

== Summary ==
Fleshbeasts emerge from [[pit burrow]]s, which spawn in random pathable outdoor areas not under furniture. Fleshbeast attacks only spawn [[fingerspike]]s, [[trispike]]s, and [[toughspike]]s. [[Bulbfreak]]s are not possible.
