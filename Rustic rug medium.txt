A '''rustic rug (medium)''' is a floor covering added by the [[Ideology DLC]]. It is a smaller counterpart to the [[rustic rug (broad)]]. 

== Acquisition ==
{{Acquisition}}

== Summary ==
Rustic rugs significantly increases the dominance of the {{P|Style}} style to those who see it, and is also moderately [[Beauty|beautiful]]. As it is not an edifice, other buildings can be built on top of it. There are several possible varieties of texture available - the texture is determined randomly upon completion.

It has has no effect on [[move speed]] and [[cleanliness]]. For these, the stats of the [[floor]] or [[terrain]] beneath the rug is taken into account.

== Analysis ==
In a single ideoligion colony, style is trivial to establish - a [[table (1x2)]] + a [[standing lamp]]/[[torch lamp]] or their [[wall lamp|wall]] [[wall torch lamp|variants]] is enough to reach 10 style, which is enough to give the moodlets relating to style.

Rustic rugs could be built or bought in order to please diversity of thought ideoligions. For beauty, regular [[carpet]] will be more efficient for both resources and beauty, although rustic rugs do not require dedicated research.
