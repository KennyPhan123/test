'''Lab coats''' are an [[apparel]] item added by the [[Anomaly DLC]] that improves the capabilities of researchers.

== Acquisition ==
{{Acquisition}} 

Pawns in the [[Scenario system#The Anomaly|The Anomaly]] starting scenario will often generate wearing a [[cloth]] lab coat.

== Summary ==
A lab coat increases the [[Research Speed]] of the wearer by {{+|5%}}, increasing the rate at which the pawn researches as well as the decreasing the time to scan with the [[ground penetrating scanner|ground penetrating]] and [[long-range mineral scanner]]. See the [[Research Speed]] page for further details. 

The lab coat also increases [[Entity Study Rate]] by {{+|10%}}

Note that these buffs do not vary with [[quality]] or [[material]]. 

== Analysis ==
As an actual piece of clothing, the lab coat is weak. It is outclassed by the [[duster]] and [[jacket]], both of which provide more armor, and provide more insulation against heat and cold respectively. It should not be given to most pawns on a daily basis.

However, the lab coat offers an entirely unique bonus to [[Research Speed]] and [[Entity Study Rate]], making it potentially useful for researchers. Since it only increases [[Entity Study Rate]] and not the amount of research provided it is likely not worth it as entities are analyzed infrequently. The 5% research speed bonus is likely only useful on a pawn that is dedicated to researching, as you are compromising both insulation and protection.

If only weak textiles are available, and insulation is irrelevant, then the lab coat may be a decent option. The different in protection of [[cloth]] armor is not likely to be relevant. However, the lab coat then competes with the [[heavy bandolier]],{{BiotechIcon}} which offers a ranged combat bonus even if made from poor materials.
