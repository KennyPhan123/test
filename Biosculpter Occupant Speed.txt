== Factors ==
This stat determines how long a pawn will stay in a biosculpter pod in order to complete the cycle. This is affected by the [[transhumanist]] meme and it's respective [[Biosculpting (precept)|biosculpting]] precept, which will increase the occupant speed to {{Good|200%}}.
