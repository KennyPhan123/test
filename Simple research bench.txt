A '''simple research bench''' is a [[production]] bench at which your [[colonist]]s can [[research]] new technology. 

It is the less advanced version of the [[hi-tech research bench]], only allowing access to the basic technologies, including those which unlock its more advanced counterpart.

== Acquisition == 
{{Acquisition}}

== Summary ==
{{Stub|section=1|reason=Specific mechanical effect of [[cleanliness]] on research rate.}}
Once a project has been selected in the [[Research]] tab, [[colonist]]s assigned to [[Work#Research|Research work type]] will work at a simple research bench to complete the project. Multiple researchers can contribute toward a project at the same time, so long as sufficient research benches are available, and benches can be freely swapped between researchers. Simple research benches are incapable of researching projects beyond [[Research#Microelectronics|Microelectronics]]. 

The rate of research can be inspected after selection, depends on the following factors: 
* The [[research speed]] stat of the researcher. 
* The type of research bench and facilities used. Simple research benches have ×0.75 multiplier to this research rate.
* A ×0.7 work multiplier for being in "Bad Temperature" - below {{Temperature|10}} or above {{Temperature|35}} - like most production facilities. 
* A ×0.9 work multiplier for being outdoors. {{Check Tag|Verify|Selecting the research bench claims 90%. Clicking on the info tab gives a "Research Speed" of 0.75x}} 
* The [[Cleanliness]] of the room. The modifier is ×0.75 for ''very dirty'' (-5 or lower), ×1 for clean (0), and ×1.09 for ''sterile'' (0.6). Being outdoors gives a ×0.75 Cleanliness multiplier (in addition to the penalty for being outdoors).
* [[Reading bonus]] for books on bookshelves nearby.

Unlike the hi-tech research bench, simple benches can be uninstalled. Simple research benches cannot be linked to a [[multi-analyzer]].

== Analysis == 
The simple research bench should be one of the first things to be set up in a new base, after building rooms, beds, storage, and, possibly, a freezer. Multiple research benches can be used concurrently; as soon as you have the manpower to spare, you may want to have 2 researchers operating at once. If resources are limited, placing half of the available researchers on a [[Schedule|night shift]] allows them to share benches.

Once they are unlocked, hi-tech research benches are 33.33% faster at researching. As soon as [[Research#{{Q|Hi-tech research bench|Required Research}}|{{Q|Hi-tech research bench|Required Research}}]] is researched and the colony has sufficient [[power]] and resources to construct them, simple benches should be replaced with hi-tech benches, which also unlocks all projects beyond Microelectronics. 

If resources are tight but several researchers are available, simple research benches can be retained in order to research less complex technologies. Simple and hi-tech research benches may be used together, though pawns will automatically abandon any simple benches when hi-tech is required.

=== Laboratories ===
[[File:Compact_lab_simple.png|thumb|right|300px|Compact, 6x6 lab with 4 research benches. Can fit 2 [[hi-tech research bench|hi-tech benches]] later on; see its [[hi-tech research bench#Laboratories|Laboratories]] section for visualization.]]
Because research is a long term task, it is advised to please researchers:
* Increase a researcher's [[comfort]] with [[dining chair]]s or [[armchair]]s.
* Install [[sculpture]]s or other [[beauty|beautiful]] items to increase mood.
* Install [[floor]]s and keep the room clean, which also increases research speed.
** [[Steel tile]] and [[sterile tile]] provide a small boost to Cleanliness, and thus research speed, but are somewhat costly.
Research rooms can be kept small and out of major thoroughfares, to minimize the amount of cleaning time and sterile tile used. Alternatively, you can combine research rooms and hospitals, as both take full advantage of Cleanliness.

If investing any significant amount of resources into a laboratory, it is recommended to build it such that it will fit the larger, {{Q|Hi-tech research bench|Size}} tile, [[hi-tech research bench]]es and the {{Q|Multi-analyzer|Size}} tile [[multi-analyzer]] that will eventually be required.
