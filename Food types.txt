All ingestibles such as [[food]] belong to one or more '''food types'''. This determines which animals it may be eaten by if left accessible, depending on the animal's [[Animals#diet|diet]], and how it can be used to cook.

==Human edible==
===Processed===
{{Main|Processed}}
Eaten by all animals ''except'' for [[strict carnivore]]s such as [[warg]]s.
