}}{{Info|A '''prosthetic arm''' is an [[artificial body part]] that acts as a replacement for a pawn's natural arm. The least advanced of the arm replacements, prosthetic arms have a part efficiency of 50%, therefore one prosthetic arm and one healthy natural arm provides 75% [[manipulation]], while having two prosthetic arms provides 50% manipulation.}}

== Acquisition ==
{{Acquisition}}

They can also sometimes be purchased from [[traders]].

== Summary ==
=== Body part ===
Prosthetic arms replace the user's organic arm up to, and including, the shoulder. They are universal and can be installed on either the left or right. The prosthetic arm has a part efficiency of 50%. This partially replaces the functionality of a normal arm - when coupled with the arm body part having a [[Manipulation]] importance of 50%, it results in restoring 25% [[manipulation]] per missing arm replaced, or a loss of 25% when replacing a healthy arm. Manipulation, in turn, affects a great many [[stats]]. For a full list of affected stats, see [[Manipulation#Affected stats|Manipulation]].

Installing an arm replacement can affect a pawn's character quality by affecting their manipulation capacity. A replacement part with lower than 100% part efficiency will reduce the pawn's value if replacing a healthy part, but installing a prosthetic when the arm is entirely missing will increase their value.

=== Combat ===
A prosthetic arm replaces the natural "Fist" attack of the replaced arm with a completely identical punch.

=== Installation ===
Installing the part requires {{Ticks|2500}} of work, 2x medicine of [[Herbal medicine|herbal]] quality or better, and a [[Skills#Medical|Medical skill]] of 4.

Removing the part requires {{Ticks|2000}} of work, 1x medicine of [[Herbal medicine|herbal]] quality or better, and has no [[Skills#Medical|Medical skill]] requirements.

If the operation fails, the part will be destroyed.

== Analysis ==
The prosthetic arm should be used on essential pawns with missing arms or shattered bones before [[Research#Bionics|Bionics]] have been researched. Compared to no arm, the increase in manipulation provides a major increase to combat and virtually any work type (short of Hauling). If just the hand is missing, then a [[wooden hand]] offers more manipulation.

Unlike organic parts, prosthetic arms don't contain subparts - all damage is taken by the main, 30 HP arm, instead of spread throughout the bones, fingers, and shoulder. While a prosthetic arm won't lose stray fingers to a gun shot, this makes it easier for it to be completely destroyed. In addition, the artificial part never feels [[pain]] and never bleeds.
