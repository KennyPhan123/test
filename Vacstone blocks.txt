'''Vacstone  blocks''' are a [[stony]] [[material]] added by the [[Odyssey DLC]] that can be used to construct especially tough structures, and are needed to construct [[Large thruster|Large Thrusters]] on a gravship.

== Acquisition ==
Vacstone blocks are cut from [[Vacstone chunk]]s at a [[{{P|Production Facility 1}}]]. It takes {{Ticks|{{P|Work To Make}}}} of work to cut a single chunk into {{P|Product Amount}} blocks.

Vacstone chunks can only be found on asteroids in orbit. They spawn naturally in chunk form and rock form.

== Usage ==
Vacstone blocks can be used in a number of recipes as well as a material for [[stuff]]able items with the [[Stony]] stuff tag.

== Analysis ==
Walls constructed from vacstone blocks are not airtight and thus allow the intrusion of [[vacuum]], unlike naturally occurring and smoothed vacstone walls.

=== Comparison to other materials ===
Vacstone is more durable than even [[Granite blocks]], and is most similar to it. It takes the same time to build but has a slightly higher beauty factor. Due to its location in orbit, it's not easy to gather a large amount of it. It's a good material to make important defensive structures out of due to its high hit point modifier. 

It is the only type of stone block that can be used to create [[large thruster]]s and [[gravlite panel]]s.
