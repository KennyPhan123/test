'''Smooth stone''' is a type of [[terrain]] with some of the features of a [[floor]] that is created by smoothing either [[rough stone]] or [[rough-hewn stone]]. It has a faster walk speed, positive beauty, and a market value compared to the stone terrain from which it was made.

== Summary ==
This flooring does not require any materials to be constructed, but it needs to be placed on rough or rough-hewn stone and takes a greater amount of time to be completed than any artificial flooring placed on top. Note that smooth floor takes less time than its smoothed wall counterpart, {{Ticks|2800}} whereas the later takes {{Ticks|6500}}. 

Rate is affected by the [[smoothing speed]] stat rather than [[construction speed]], however the two stats have identical values for all [[construction]] levels, [[global work speed]]s, and [[manipulation]] values ''unless'' the pawn has a [[sight]] value below 100%.

It is recommended to be used as standard flooring whenever available, as it more easily provides the [[Thoughts list#Pleasant environment|pleasant environment bonus]] to your colonists, not to mention it's free. Perfect flooring for a "Dwarfy" colony, built into the side of a mountain.

Since rough stone floors are widely available in most biomes, an easy way to train colonists in [[Construction]] is to select large sections of rough stone and set them for smoothing.
