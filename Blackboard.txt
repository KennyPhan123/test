The '''blackboard''' is a [[furniture]] item added by the [[Biotech DLC]] that improves the rate a [[child]] learns at a [[school desk]].

== Acquisition ==
{{Acquisition}}

== Summary ==
When connected to a [[school desk]], each blackboard increases a student's Learning rate from [[Children#Learning Tasks|''Lesson Learning'']] by 20%. This effect can stack up to three times per desk. A blackboard can connect to multiple desks. It does not matter which way the blackboard is facing.

Blackboards also increase children's [[skill]] XP gain from learning with an adult by 20% per blackboard. Meanwhile adult's social XP gain remains unaffected.

== Analysis ==
Blackboards are technically unnecessary. Children can still reach growth tier 8 without blackboards attached to their school desk. Blackboards will make up for extended periods of non-Learning, such as when resting from [[disease]]. And, because they are so cheap, you might as well build them.
