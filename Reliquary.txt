A '''Reliquary''' is a building added by the [[Ideology DLC]]. It is used to house a [[relic]].

== Acquisition ==
{{Acquisition}}

== Summary ==
{{Stub|section=1|reason=General stub - how to use, its effects when housing a relic and when not, pilgrims, etc.}}
If your colony has an available [[relic]] it can be installed by selecting a pawn that can reach both the relic and the reliquary, then right clicking on the reliquary or on the relic itself. An installed relic can be retrieved by a colonist.

If a relic of a colonist's ideoligion is installed:
*Performing [[ritual]]s in the room will improve mood: {{+|2}} for one relic, {{+|3}} for two, and {{+|4}} for all three.
*Pilgrims will occasionally come to visit.
*A relic installed in a reliquary gives an additional bonus to certainty loss inflicted by conversion attempted by pawns from that ideoligion on the same map as the reliquary.{{Check Tag|Verify|Verify same map}} The [[Moral guide]]'s [[Moral guide#Convert|Convert]] ability gains a +10% multiplicative bonus. The size of the increase depends on the number of relics currently held. See [[Conversion]] for details. 

Reliquaries do not have quality levels.

== Analysis ==
Reliquaries are not an immediate priority to build, but should be built when afforded (and the colony has a relic with matching ideoligion) as they provide a free mood buff from any ritual. If a relic is currently being wielded as a weapon, it is possible to install the relic just before a ritual, then immediately take it out.

Role change rituals ''do'' count as rituals, do not have cooldowns, and always succeed. Therefore, it's possible to maintain the mood buff indefinitely by continuously removing and re-adding a pawn's role.
