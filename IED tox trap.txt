The '''IED tox trap''' is a building added by the [[Biotech DLC]] that explodes in a cloud of [[tox gas]] when walked over.

When triggered, the trap will begin to spark and emit a hissing sound for {{ticks|15}} before exploding, creating a cloud of [[tox gas]] in a 8.9-tile radius.{{Check Tag|Verify Radius|How big is the resulting cloud? Confirm or correct current value}} The explosion and resulting cloud deal no damage but the gas causes tox gas exposure as well as [[toxic buildup]] to those not immune. See [[Tox gas]] for details. 

Non-drafted pawns will attempt to run from an activated IED trap, even if it wouldn't affect them.{{Check Tag|Verify|verify this fact}}

== Analysis ==
IED tox trap can serve to punish any unfortunate enemy attempting to take cover without causing any unintentional damage that any regular IED traps would. RimWorld's AI will not attempt to move away from the tox gas, so raiders will continue trying to hide behind cover, enduring significant accuracy penalties with their sight and breathing stats being reduced, eventually succumbing to the toxic buildup or onslaught of bullets. Unlike [[spike trap]]s, this affects more targets at a time, and IED tox traps are cheaper than a steel spike trap.

Their true purpose shines in killboxes through gas mazes - a long and winding tunnel that slows down pawns via [[fence]]s, taking advantage of the tox trap's relative cheapness. However, toxic gas does not affect [[waster]]s, [[mechanoid]]s, or [[shambler]]s. In addition, the toxic fumes will rot bodies and can lead to [[Carcinoma|cancers]] and [[dementia]] to survivors. The rotting bodies will create [[rot stink]], although this can be negated by making the killbox a [[cooler|freezer]] as corpses do not create rot stink while frozen.

Tox traps are also great for containing vulnerable [[entities]],{{AnomalyIcon}} such as [[devourer]]s.{{AnomalyIcon}} The tox trap will automatically trigger when the entity tries to escape and the tox gas will non-lethally down them.

Despite vents being able to transfer tox gas, the rate that they do being about 3 tiles combined with raiders tending to attack them and set them on fire makes them very inefficient for use in killboxes.
