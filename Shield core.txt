A '''shield core''' is an uncraftable item used in the creation of [[low-shield pack]]s sourced from the deconstruction of [[mech high-shield]] and [[mech low-shield]] in [[mechanoid cluster]]s. 

== Acquisition ==
Shield cores cannot be crafted. Instead they can only be obtained by deconstructing [[mech high-shield]]s and [[mech low-shield]]s in [[mechanoid cluster]]s, or from [[orbital trader]]s. Note that the cluster must be defeated, with all defenses, [[condition causer]]s, and [[mech assembler]]s destroyed, before you can deconstruct the shields. Destroying the shields will not result in a shield core.
