The '''turkey''' is a large bird that lays eggs. Adult male turkeys can be distinguished by a red head and neck, blue body, and a large fanned tail while females and babies of both genders are largely brown with a downwards angled tail.

== Acquisition ==
Turkeys can be found in {{Habitats}}. They can either be tamed by a [[Work #Handle|handler]] or self-tame in a random event. 

Turkeys can be bought and sold in other [[Trade#Faction base|faction bases]], [[Trade#Bulk goods trader 2|bulk goods traders]] or [[Trade#Exotic goods trader 2|exotic goods traders]]. Turkeys purchased from traders will be already tamed.


== Summary == 
{{Pen Animal Note|Turkeys}}

A female turkey produces {{P|Eggs Per Clutch Average}} [[turkey egg]] every {{P|Egg Laying Interval}} day when fertilized. Contrary to real life, RimWorld turkeys cannot lay unfertilized eggs.

== Analysis ==
Turkeys are one of many egg-laying animals that are inferior to [[chicken]]s. 2 hens eat less food and each hen lays {{%|({{Q|Chicken|Eggs Per Clutch Average}}/{{Q|Chicken|Egg Laying Interval}}) / ({{P|Eggs Per Clutch Average}}/{{P|Egg Laying Interval}})-1 round 2}} more eggs than 1 turkey. While chicken eggs are half as large, 2 chickens still lay more food per day. Hens do not need to be fertilized to lay eggs, which adds a further advantage. [[Duck]]s are slightly less nutritionally efficient than chickens, but are still superior to turkeys. 

Many animals, such as [[horse]]s, are strictly better when slaughtered for meat. But eating turkey eggs won't offend [[ideoligion]]s{{IdeologyIcon}} that forbid meat-eating. As such, they're decent if they you either want or need egg-layers and they are all you can find to tame, however you can typically find superior birds by the mid-game. Otherwise, you can hunt turkeys for early game meat, and they won't go manhunter on you.

=== Nutrition ===
An adult turkey consumes {{P|Real Hunger Rate}} nutrition per day, and female turkeys produce {{#expr:{{P|Eggs Per Clutch Average}}/{{P|Egg Laying Interval}} round 2}} turkey eggs.

The optimal ratio of male : female turkeys is 1 male for every {{#vardefineecho:femaleratio|{{#expr: 2/3*24*{{P|Egg Laying Interval}}/{{P|Mate Mtb Hours}} round 2}}}} female turkeys.

* When eaten as eggs, turkeys produce {{#vardefineecho:egg_nutr_per_day|{{#expr:{{P|Eggs Per Clutch Average}}/{{P|Egg Laying Interval}}*{{Q|Turkey egg|Nutrition}} round 3 }}}} [[nutrition]] of eggs per day.
* When slaughtered as babies, turkeys produce {{#vardefineecho:meat_nutr_per_day|{{#expr:{{P|Eggs Per Clutch Average}}/{{P|Egg Laying Interval}}*{{Meat Production|{{PAGENAME}}|Baby}} round 3 }}}} nutrition of meat per day.

== Attacks ==
Turkeys have three attacks: scratch (feet), bite (beak) and less commonly used, blunt (head). In addition to the base damage, the scratch and bite attacks apply a stun for {{ticks|14}}.
