'''Lance''' may refer to one of several items using that name: 
* {{Icon Small|Charge lance|24}} [[Charge lance]] - a [[tech level|spacer tech]] ranged [[weapon]] used by both [[human]]s and [[lancer]] [[mechanoids]].
* {{Icon Small|Psychic shock lance|24}} Psychic lances - [[utility]] items with two charges that inflict certain effects on [[psychic sensitivity|psychically sensitive]] pawns.
** [[Psychic shock lance]] - which instantly downs the target with [[psychic shock]], but may set their brain on fire.
** [[Psychic insanity lance]] - which instantly turns the target [[berserk]], causing them to attack anyone nearby, friend or foe. May also set their brain on fire.
** [[Shard shock lance]]{{AnomalyIcon}} - a craftable, but shorter ranged version of the normal psychic version of the shock lance.
** [[Shard insanity lance]]{{AnomalyIcon}} - a craftable, but shorter ranged version of the normal psychic version of the insanity lance.
* [[Biomutation lance]]{{AnomalyIcon}} - a craftable [[utility]] item that turns the target into a random [[fleshbeast]] depending on the targets size
