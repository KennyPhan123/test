An '''Agarilux''', is a wild plant that only spawns inside caves and [[glowforest]]s{{OdysseyIcon}}. It cannot be grown by the player. It has a slow grow time, and harvesting a fully grown specimen produces up to 35 [[raw fungus]]. It reproduces by spores to nearby dark areas. After a certain growth period it emits 41% light from the spot where it grows. It is immune to [[blight]].
