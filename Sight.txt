}} Sight affects most colonist stats, with varying impact, though its importance will depend on the pawn's role in the colony. Sight contribution is capped at or near 100% for many stats, but some combat, medical, and cooking stats can go significantly higher.

{{Hover title|Version/1.3.3242|At the time of writing}}, the maximum possible Sight capacity in the core game is 250% with a healthy pawn high on [[go-juice]] and [[luciferium]] with two [[archotech eye]]s installed, while also being infected with [[sensory mechanites]]. With the [[Royalty DLC]] installed, it is possible to reach a Sight capacity of up to a maximum of 325% with the addition of the  [[Focus]] psycast.{{RoyaltyIcon}}

== Effects ==
{{Stub|section=1|reason=There are definitely more of these that aren't listed - some to be tested inc. eclipses with the relevant precept, etc}}
Sight has a number of unique interactions with other mechanics, beyond the traditional effects on [[stats]]. 

All pawns whose Sight's base efficiency is less than 50% gain [[psychic sensitivity]] according to the formula <code>0.5-Sight base efficiency</code>, for a maximum of {{Up|+50%}} psychic sensitivity when fully blind. '''Offsets''' and '''Post factors''' are completely ignored for this. Do note that previous to 1.5, this bonus was based on Sight efficiency instead.

Pawns with 0% Sight:
* Are not subject to the factor from low [[light]] levels on [[move speed]] or on [[global work speed]]. 
* Will not receive the "Observed corpse" or "Witnessed ally's death" moodlets.{{Check Tag|Other similar moodlets?|Wb the other witnessed death (inc outsider, family) and wb bloodlust?}}
* Do not experience the mood buff from [[aurora]]s, nor from [[eclipse]]s with the [[Eclipse (precept)|eclipse]]{{IdeologyIcon}} precept.
* Do not gain nor lose any [[opinion]] for any other pawn based on their [[Beauty (Pawn)|beauty]] stat.
* Do not lose any [[opinion]] for [[disfigured]] pawns.
* Have the need for [[Beauty#Beauty_need_level|beautiful environments]] locked at 50%, becoming a total non-factor to their [[mood]].
* Cannot [[recreation|recreate]] at a [[telescope]], [[poker table]], [[billiards table]], [[hoopstone ring]], [[books]] or any type of [[television]]. They also cannot skygaze or view art for recreational purposes.
* Gain {{Up|+30%}} [[psychic sensitivity]] if they also have the [[Blind psysense]]{{IdeologyIcon}} precept and both their eyes removed. This stacks with the aforementioned {{Up|+50%}} for 0% sight bonus.

== Formula ==
The formula for sight is:<br/>
<big><code>'''Sight''' = (Base efficiency + Offsets) × Post factors</code></big>

Base efficiency is the result of combining both eyes efficiencies, with a 75% weight to the strongest eye. There are many way of writing it, but for this section the following will be used:<br/>
<big><code>'''Base efficiency''' = (right eye + left eye)/4 + max(right eye, left eye)/2</code></big>

Offsets are additive with each other.


All factors that affect Sight will be listed below.

=== Base efficiency ===
* Eye efficiency.  100% importance, no allowed defect, no max.
** The game assigns 75% weight to the stronger eye and 25% weight to the weaker, so entirely losing a single eye reduces sight by 25% rather than 50%.
** [[Blindness]]: 0% part efficiency
** [[Cataract]]: 50% part efficiency. Results in {{--|12.5%}} sight for the first eye affected, {{--|37.5%}} for the second eye.
** [[Bionic eye]]:  125% part efficiency.  Results in {{+|18.75%}} sight for the first one installed, {{+|6.25%}} for the second one.
** [[Archotech eye]]:  150% part efficiency.  Results in {{+|37.5%}} sight for the first one installed, {{+|12.5%}} for the second one.

::{{Sight Part Efficiency Table}}

=== Offsets ===
The following offsets affect Sight.
* [[Sensory mechanites]]: {{+|50%}}
* [[Go-juice]]: {{+|35%}}
* [[Luciferium]]: {{+|15%}}
* [[Tox gas]]:{{BiotechIcon}}
** Mild: {{--|10%}}
** Moderate: {{--|25%}}
** Severe: {{--|40%}}

=== Post factors ===
* [[Blindfold]]{{IdeologyIcon}}: Sets maximum to 20%
* [[Focus]] psycast {{RoyaltyIcon}}: '''x130%'''

== Example == 
If we were to calculate the highest possible value in unmodded Rimworld, we would have:
*Base efficiency: 
** Right eye: '''150%''' ([[archotech eye]])
** Left eye: '''150%''' ([[archotech eye]])
*Offsets (total {{+|100%}}):
** [[Luciferium]]: {{+|15%}}
** [[Go-juice| Go-juice High]]: {{+|35%}}
** [[Sensory mechanites]]: {{+|50%}}
*Post factors:
** [[Focus]] psycast{{RoyaltyIcon}}: '''x130%'''


From this, Base efficiency would be:

<code>'''Base efficiency''' = (1.5 + 1.5)/4 + max(1.5, 1.5)/2 = 3.0/4 + 1.5/2 = 0.75 + 0.75 = 1.5 = '''150%''' </code>

Replacing values, the maximum Sight capacity is:

<code>
'''Sight''' = (1.5 + 1.0) * 1.3 = 3.25<br/>
<big>'''Sight''' = 325%</big>
</code>
