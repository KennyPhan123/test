The '''{{PAGENAME}}''' is a piece of [[apparel]] added by the [[Biotech DLC]] that is not obtainable by the player.

== Acquisition ==
The heavy shield unit is worn by some [[mechanoid commander]]s when summoned. All [[diaboli]] from the third summon onward wear it, as so do [[war queen]]s from the second, and [[apocriton]]s from the first. The heavy shield unit will not appear if the commanders appear as part of a normal mech raid.

== Summary ==
The heavy shield unit creates a shield, like the [[shield belt]], but allows the user to fire out.

It has 400 shield HP, compared to 110 HP from a normal [[quality]] shield belt. This results in taking 133.3 damage before it breaks. However, it recharges at 1.3% / second, over 10x slower than a normal shield belt.

It is broken by [[EMP]].

== Analysis ==
Much like most weapons wielded by [[Mechanoids]], the heavy shield unit cannot be made by the player, or obtained from the corpses of dead mechanoids. They boast a notable amount of HP, but take {{ticks|36000}} to reset. Because of this, you likely won't need to worry about the shield after you've broken it once. The easiest way to remove the shield is simply to use any kind of [[EMP]] weapon to destroy it instantly and stun the carrier.

== Notes ==
Heavy shield units have a Medieval tech level, unchanged from the parent ApparelNoQualityBase, despite being found on mechanoids.
