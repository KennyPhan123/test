'''Sea lions''', called '''sea lion pups''' as babies, are a type of aquatic [[animal]] added by the [[Odyssey DLC]]. Males of the species can be distinguished by their brown coloration and slightly larger size, while females are a tawny color and slightly smaller. Despite the visual size difference, adults of both genders have the same stats and attacks. 

== Acquisition ==
{{PAGENAME}}s can be found in maps with large bodies of water{{Habitats}}. They can either be tamed by a [[Work #Handle|handler]] or self-tame in a random event. 

{{PAGENAME}}s can be bought and sold in other [[Trade#Faction base|faction bases]] and from [[Trade#Exotic goods trader 2|exotic goods traders]]. {{PAGENAME}}s purchased from traders will be already tamed.
