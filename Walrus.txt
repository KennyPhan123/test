'''Walruses''', call '''walrus calves''' as babies, are aquatic [[animal]]s added by the [[Odyssey DLC]].

== Acquisition ==
Walruses can be found in maps with large bodies of water; {{Habitats}}. They can either be tamed by a [[Work #Handle|handler]] or self-tame in a random event. 

Walruses can be bought and sold in other [[Trade#Faction base|faction bases]] and from [[Trade#Exotic goods trader 2|exotic goods traders]]. Walruses purchased from traders will be already tamed.
