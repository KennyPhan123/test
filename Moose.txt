'''Moose''', called '''moose calves''' as babies, are a type of [[animal]] added by the [[Odyssey DLC]]. Adult males of the species can be distinguished by their large antlers, while females and calves of either gender lack them. Despite the visual differences, adults of both genders have the same stats and attacks. 

== Acquisition ==
Moose can be found in {{Habitats}}. They can either be tamed by a [[Work #Handle|handler]] or self-tame in a random event. 

Moose can be bought and sold in other [[Trade#Faction base|faction bases]] and from [[Trade#Exotic goods trader 2|exotic goods traders]]. Moose purchased from traders will be already tamed.
