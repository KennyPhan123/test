'''Twisted meat''' is a type of [[meat]] obtained from butchering [[entities]]. It can be eaten just like any other meat, but most humans dislike eating it even when cooked into meals.

== Acquisition ==
Twisted meat is produced when a [[Skills#Cooking|cook]] butchers any of a number of [[entities]] at a [[butcher table]] or [[butcher spot]]. The amount obtained depends on the [[Meat Amount]] of the butchered entity, as well as the [[Butchery Efficiency]] of the butcher and a number of other factors. {{Check Tag|Detail Needed|What's the base yield of various entities?}}

You can also receive twisted meat by chopping down [[harbinger tree]]s, providing 30 meat when fully grown.

Alternatively, [[creepjoiner]] pawns with the [[psychic slaughter]] power can fatally convert an organic target directly into piles of twisted meat, rather than their normal output when butchered.

{{Transmute Steel Acquisition Note}}

[[Fleshmass nucleus|Fleshmass nuclei]] kept on [[holding platform]] or [[holding spot]]s regularly produce twisted meat. It creates meat on average every {{Ticks/gametime|60000}}, with the amount influenced by activity level, producing an average of 96 meat each time at 0% activity and an average of 220 meat at 90%. It also yields 2 [[shard]]s and 100-200 twisted meat when destroyed.

== Summary ==
Twisted meat has no specific health effects, but consuming twisted meat in any form (raw, cooked, or paste) inflicts a {{Thought|desc=It was so gristly and stringy - like eating cancer. I want to throw up.|label=Ate twisted meat|value=-10|stack=1|duration=1}}. [[Ascetic]] and [[inhumanized]] pawns do not get this moodlet, but [[cannibal]]s do. Eating raw twisted meat does not inflict the normal mood debuff for eating raw food. 

As a [[raw food]], twisted meat can be be eaten raw, with a flat {{Bad|2%}} chance of giving [[food poisoning]], or cooked into a [[meal]] with a food poisoning chance dependent on the skill of the cook and the [[cleanliness]] of the kitchen. [[Animals]] are immune to food poisoning from raw food but not from meals. When used in food recipes that require specific types of [[nutrition]], such as [[fine meal]]s, twisted meat is classified as meat.

== Analysis ==
Twisted meat, while unpleasant to eat, can be used without penalty to feed [[ascetic]] and [[inhumanized]] pawns, [[ghoul]]s, and [[animal]]s. It can also be used in a [[biofuel refinery]], [[biosculpter pod]],{{IdeologyIcon}} or [[growth vat]]{{BiotechIcon}} like any other food, and is required to make certain serums and biomutation weapons. The serums in particular have unique and useful effects, making twisted meat valuable and worth the effort to acquire.

For trade purposes, it is best used with [[bioferrite]] to make [[juggernaut serum]], which is worth {{Icon Small|Silver||{{Q|juggernaut serum|Market Value Base}}}}, 11 times more than its ingredients.

If the colony has an excess of twisted meat, it is possible to make ([[carnivore lavish meal|carnivore]]) [[lavish meal]]s for a net +2 mood from regular simple meals. Gifting it via [[transport pod]] or letting it rot are other options for getting rid of the [[wealth]] quickly.

=== Acquisition analysis ===
The fastest stable way to farm twisted meat safely is from the [[fleshmass nucleus]]. If kept at a high activity level, you can expect roughly 200 meat per day, enough to feed {{#expr: 200 * {{P|Nutrition}}/{{Q|Ghoul|Real Hunger Rate}}}} default ghouls, or {{#expr: 200 * ({{P|Nutrition}}*1.8 <!--Robust digestion-->)/(({{Q|Ghoul|Real Hunger Rate}}*1.15<!--Adrenal heart-->)/(4 <!--Nuclear stomach-->*2 <!--Metabolic effeiciency of 5+-->)) round 2}} hunger-optimized ghouls with adrenal hearts. Or with a steady supply of bioferrite, the 200 meat can be used to craft [[juggernaut serum]]s valuing {{Icon Small|Silver||{{#expr:10*{{Q|juggernaut serum|Market Value Base}}}}}}.

[[Harbinger tree]]s are guaranteed to appear early on if the [[void monolith]] is enabled. These trees are safe and can be fed corpses, including human corpses and otherwise unusable rotten corpses, to reproduce, effectively converting corpses into a renewable source of twisted meat and bioferrite.
