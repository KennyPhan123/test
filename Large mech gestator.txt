The '''large mech gestator''' is a [[building]] added by the [[Biotech DLC]] that allows for the creation and resurrection of larger [[mechanoids]] by a [[mechanitor]]

== Acquisition ==
{{Acquisition}}

== Summary ==
The large mech gestator is used to build and revive all medium, heavy, and ultraheavy mechanoids. It cannot gestate light mechanoids. Consumes 500 W of power when gestating a mech, and 50 W otherwise. If power is lost during gestation, the cycle will pause, but no other adverse effects will occur. The gestator can only be worked by a [[mechanitor]], and that mechanitor that works the gestator will be bonded to the mechs created.

In between each [[gestation cycle]], the mechanitor must initiate the next one, which is considered Smithing work.

Resurrection bills are split into the classes and can only be used on dead friendly mechanoids. 
* '''Resurrect Medium Mech:''' Requires {{Icon Small|Steel||50}} [[steel]], and 1 [[gestation cycle]] taking {{Ticks|1800}} to initiate.
* '''Resurrect Heavy Mech:''' Requires {{Icon Small|Steel||100}} [[steel]], and 1 [[gestation cycle]] taking {{Ticks|1800}} to initiate.
* '''Resurrect Ultraheavy Mech:''' Requires {{Icon Small|Steel||150}} [[steel]], and 1 [[gestation cycle]] taking {{Ticks|1800}} to initiate.

== Analysis ==
Functions identically to the [[mech gestator]], outside of which [[mechanoids]] it can gestate, the building's size, and its power consumption.

Most large mechanoids are dedicated for combat. Only [[tunneler]]s are able to do work tasks, and even then, it can hold a fair fight. If you don't plan on using the heavier combat mechanoids, then the uses of the large mech gestator are limited.
