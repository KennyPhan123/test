'''Egg boxes''' are [[miscellaneous]] buildings that egg-laying animals will try to lay [[eggs]] in. They do not protect eggs from [[temperature]] spoilage, but do prevent [[deterioration]] from exposure.

== Acquisition ==
{{Acquisition}}

== Summary ==
{{Stub|section=1|reason=Generally stubby. Options/gizmos etc}}
Egg-laying animals will always lay their eggs in an egg box if one is accessible to them, available in their assigned [[pen]] if relevant, capable of receiving the egg/s, and within a 30 tile range of the animal. Egg boxes can hold up to 25 [[egg]]s and new eggs must be able to stack with any existing eggs in the box. Once the box contains 20 eggs it will be emptied by [[Work#Haul|haulers]].

Egg boxes prevent [[deterioration]] of the eggs from exposure but do not protect eggs from [[temperature]] spoilage. 

== Analysis ==
{{Stub|section=1|reason=Section is generally stubby}}
Egg laying animals will always lay egg in an egg box if one is available in their assigned pen and within a 30 tile range, therefore, egg boxes are extremely useful to control the environment in which the eggs are placed.

Egg boxes function as shelves preventing weather and outdoors degradation types e.g. "Outdoors", "Unroofed", "Rain", "Shallow Water", etc., however, they do not prevent eggs from rotting (rotting eggs vanish just like all other food stuffs). Additionally, egg boxes do not prevent fertilized eggs from being spoiled, however they can be used to force animals to lay eggs in a temperature controlled room, preventing temperature spoilage.

Strategically placing egg boxes can also minimize the amount of hauling work to be done, as it both prevents pawns from hauling eggs until the egg boxes egg count reaches 20 (or until they are manually tasked to do so) and collects all eggs in a single location rather then having them be spread across a room or pen, greatly reducing the distance traveled by the hauler.
