'''Ancient barrels''' are [[ruins]] that are defined in Core but only appear when the [[Ideology DLC]], the [[Biotech DLC]] and/or the [[Odyssey DLC]] are enabled.

== Occurrence ==
If the Ideology DLC is enabled, they can be found among the debris scattered on maps and the [[ancient complex]] quest site.

If the Biotech DLC is enabled, they can be found in the [[mechanitor complex]] quest site.

If the Odyssey DLC is enabled, they are found in a variety of locations:
* Landmarks, features and related quest sites: [[ancient garrison]], [[ancient warehouse]], [[Landmarks#Ruins|ancient ruins]], [[frozen ruins]], [[ancient chemfuel refinery]], [[infested settlement]], [[ancient launch site]], [[junkyard]]
* Biome ruins: [[scarlands]] ruins, [[glacial plain]] ruins
* Orbital sites: abandoned ancient platform (either from [[Quests#Odyssey|gravcore quest]], or found with the [[Orbital scanner#Quests|orbital scanner]])
* [[Quests#Odyssey|Ancient reactor]] quest site

If none of these DLCs is enabled, they will not appear in-game at all.
