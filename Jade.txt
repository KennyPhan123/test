{{Info|'''Jade''' is a rare [[materials|material]] with high [[beauty]], but with little other purpose.}}

== Acquisition ==
[[Jade ore|Jade veins]] occur rarely on most maps, and must be [[mine]]d before they can be used. Jade can occasionally be found in random cargo pods, and may be [[trade|purchased]] from [[Trader#Bulk Goods Trader|bulk goods traders]]. Once you've completed the [[Research#Deep drilling|research]] necessary to construct a [[deep drill]], it can also be found in small deposits underground.

Jade may occasionally be dropped by [[tribal]] [[raider]]s, as part of raid loot.

== Analysis ==

Jade is relatively scarce and hard to find. It is best used for beauty. Unlike other stone types, [[bed]]s made from jade suffer no penalty to [[Rest Effectiveness]].

=== Beauty ===
The most efficient uses of jade are:

* [[Sculpture]]s. Sculptures are very beautiful, and jade sculptures more so. A normal [[small sculpture]] costs {{Icon Small|Jade}} 50 jade for {{+|135}} beauty, then modified by [[quality]]. Jade sculptures allow for very high beauty in 1 place.
* [[Fence]]s. Each jade fence costs {{Icon Small|Jade}} 1 jade for {{+|10}} beauty. 50 jade fences would {{+|500}} beauty, meaning they'll beat anything short of masterwork sculptures. They also have the advantage of being low [[wealth]]. Therefore, fences are great at raising a ''room'''s beauty (and thus room [[Impressiveness]]).
** However, jade fences consume a lot of space; they are worse than sculptures at raising the ''pawn'' [[beauty|beauty need]]. Colonists check for beauty within an 8-tile radius, and you can't easily fit 50 or so fences into a colonist's sight. In addition, the actual space constraint can be an issue for many base setups.

With a surplus of jade, tt can be used for [[wall]]s, [[bed]]s and other furniture, though these are less effective for beauty. Jade structures are poor as defenses, as they have lower HP.

=== Weapon ===
Jade has a surprisingly high Blunt damage rating. It is better than all materials short of [[uranium]]. However, the only blunt weapon that can be made out of jade is the [[club]], which has no [[quality]]. Jade can be an acceptable material in the early game, as a jade club would be stronger than a normal quality [[steel]] [[mace]], but improved access to both uranium and higher quality maces make them irrelevant by the mid-game.

[[Scenario system#Lost Tribe|Lost Tribe]] scenarios also start with the [[jade knife]], which would otherwise be uncraftable. As an actual weapon, the jade knife is worse than even a wooden club.

== Trivia ==
Despite being unavailable for use in any [[Apparel]] in the current version, it has stats for armor and insulation.

Jade is the worst material in the game for the walls and doors of [[Containment]]{{AnomalyIcon}} cells.
