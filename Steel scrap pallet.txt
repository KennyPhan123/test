The '''steel scrap pallet''' is a structure added by the [[Odyssey DLC]]. Its contents are ejected when opened.

== Occurrence ==
Steel scrap pallets are only found in orbital wrecks, which are quest sites discovered with the [[orbital scanner]].

== Summary ==
{{Loot Container Note}}

=== Loot ===
Steel scrap pallets drop {{Icon Small|Steel||100-200}} [[steel]] when opened.
