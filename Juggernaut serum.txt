The '''juggernaut serum''' is [[serum]], a consumable item added by the [[Anomaly DLC]], that significantly increases a pawn's melee combat ability at the expense of a mood penalty.

== Acquisition ==
{{Acquisition}}

== Summary == 
Juggernaut serum is a powerful combat enhancement serum. Unlike drugs, serums do not have a severity-based high, have no addiction, tolerance, or withdrawal mechanics and do not count as any kind of drug for the purposes of the [[Chemical (need)|Chemical need]], the [[chemical interest]], [[chemical fascination|fascination]], or [[teetotaler]] traits, or the [[Drug use]] precepts.{{IdeologyIcon}}

=== Upon injection ===
{{Quote|"A serum is super-powering this person's musculature, making them stronger and faster, but reducing their mood due to discomfort."|Juggernaut hediff description}}
A pawn taking the serum will do so right where they stand without looking for a place to sit first. The serum is taken by injection, taking {{Ticks|{{P|Ingestion Time}}}}. Pawns can be ordered to inject the serum through the usual means, but it can also be ordered from the item itself. This option allows [[ghoul]]s to take serums without using an operation bill.

The serum applies the "'''juggernaut'''" hediff with the following effects for {{Ticks|600000}} or 10 in-game days:
* [[Move speed]]: {{Good|+0.50}}
* [[Melee damage factor]]: {{Good|x150%}}
* [[Injury healing factor]]: {{Good|x200%}}
* {{Thought|desc=This stuff messes with my head. I can't stop thinking the worse things. |label=juggernaut|value=-10|stack=1}}. This mood penalty is nullified by the [[inhumanized]] hediff.

== Analysis ==
Juggernaut serum is an incredibly powerful combat drug, specially for melee combatants, with no [[overdose]] risk whilst also being relatively cheap. It's also useful for ranged colonists, as the healing factor and move speed bonus can help with kiting and healing from stray shots, and for reducing the down time of injured colonist. It can also be combined with [[Go-juice]] or any other drug.

In a colony that is mass producing [[bioferrite]] and with large stockpiles of [[twisted meat]], whether from a captured [[fleshmass nucleus]] or from butchered [[entities]], it can be added to the list of combat drugs that all colonists take.

=== Mood mitigation ===
The mood debuff can be mitigated by taking [[psychite tea]] or any other safe drug, [[Fine meal|Fine]]/[[Lavish meal]]s, or paired with the mood buff from [[Go-juice]]. Others [[Mood#Mood management|mood management]] techniques also apply.

The [[inhumanized]] hediff will prevent the mood penalty. A [[mind-numb serum]] will also remove this mood penalty for 2 days, alongside all other [[thoughts]].

=== Misc ===
Besides combat, the increased moving speed can make pawns significantly faster at hauling, indirectly aiding other tasks. It also counters/mitigates the move speed penalty from other pieces of gear/implants. If the mood penalty can be managed and the required resources abundant, there's no reason to not use this serum on all colonist that do not remain stationary most of the time.
