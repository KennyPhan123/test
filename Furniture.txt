'''Furniture''' are objects which can be used to make rooms inhabitable or to decorate them.

Furniture are not subject to [[deterioration]] under "unroofed" or "outdoors" conditions, neither in installed nor minified form.

== Moving and storing furniture ==
* Constructing furniture will install it on the spot where the blueprint was placed, and make it immediately useable.  It can afterwards be ''uninstalled'' which puts it into ''minified'' form, and allows it to be transported and stored.
* A minified piece of furniture only takes one tile of storage space, no matter how large it is when installed.
* Minified furniture can be deconstructed without installing it first.  This will give the same amount of materials back as deconstructing the installed piece of furniture would.
* "Re-installing" furniture will minify it, haul it to the target blueprint, and install it there.

== Artwork ==
Furniture of excellent [[quality]] or above will bear artwork. A description is provided by the Art button on the inspect pane.
