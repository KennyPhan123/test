'''Smoke spewers''' are [[mechanoid cluster]] and quest [[condition causers]] that reduce light levels by 14% in a 10 world-tile radius.
==Analysis==
While mostly a disadvantage, Colonies with the [[Lighting (precept)|Lighting: Darklight preferred]] precept {{IdeologyIcon}} will receive some benefit as daylight is no longer considered Brightly Lit and will not cause the [[Thoughts#blinding light|Blinding Light]] moodlet, otherwise the decreased lighting causes crops to grow slower.
