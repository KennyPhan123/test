'''Parkas''' are items of [[apparel]] that provide exceptional cold [[insulation]].

== Acquisition ==
{{Acquisition}} 

Parkas can also be purchased from traders, crafted, or stripped from [[Outlanders]], [[Pirates]], or Tribals - this being the only garment that can be found worn by all existing human factions. When starting a tribal faction, there's a chance one of your starting tribespeople will spawn with a cloth parka equipped.

== Analysis ==
Parkas are the most insulating garment in the game, and the absurdly high amount of cold insulation they offer relative to all other clothing can be vital in cold biomes. This insulation comes at a cost though: less armor than both [[jacket]]s and [[duster]]s (20% vs. 30%), and less coverage of the body than dusters and [[robe]]s (which cover the legs). This means that parkas should only be used when the insulation is required.

If the biome is only moderately cold, a jacket (or robe) will be a better option. It is possible to change the skin layer to [[tribalwear]], or the head slot to a [[tuque]] or [[tailcap]]{{IdeologyIcon}} so that a jacket is wearable, which warrants comparison:
* Skin layer: If the climate is just a little too cold for a jacket/robe, it is possible to wear [[tribalwear]] for a small increase in cold insulation. A jacket + tribalwear will be more protective than a parka + shirt and pants.
* Headgear: Wearing a parka is a better choice than having a pawn wear a [[tuque]] or [[tailcap]]{{IdeologyIcon}}. Parkas can be worn with [[flak vest]]s for extra body protection, but tuques/tailcaps cannot be worn with helmets. Therefore, wear parkas if it would enable pawns to wear a helmet; a parka + helmet is better than a jacket + tuque.

Note that all three [[power armor]] sets have massive cold insulation, more than all but the best material parkas. [[Marine armor]] will be more protective and insulative than a parka + flak vest, although is much more expensive and has a move speed cost.
