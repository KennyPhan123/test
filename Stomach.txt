'''Stomach''' can refer to a number of different things in RimWorld:
* Stomach: An organic [[body part]] that controls a pawn's [[digestion]] alongside the [[liver]] for most, if not all, non-[[mechanoid]] pawns. Has no item equivalent.
* [[Bionic stomach]]: An [[artificial body part]] that can replace the organic stomach for various benefits.
* [[Nuclear stomach]]:{{RoyaltyIcon}} An [[artificial body part]] that can replace the organic stomach for various benefits.
* [[Reprocessor stomach]]:{{RoyaltyIcon}} An [[artificial body part]] that can replace the organic stomach for various benefits.
* [[Sterilizing stomach]]:{{RoyaltyIcon}} An [[artificial body part]] that can replace the organic stomach for various benefits.
* [[Strong stomach]]:{{BiotechIcon}} A [[gene]] that prevents a pawn with it from getting food poisoning. 
* [[Fleshmass stomach]]:{{AnomalyIcon}} An [[artificial body part]] that can replace the organic stomach for various benefits.
