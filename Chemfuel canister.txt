The '''chemfuel canister''' is a structure added by the [[Odyssey DLC]]. Its contents are ejected when opened. Like chemfuel, it is highly flammable.

== Occurrence ==
Chemfuel canisters are reliably found in a variety of Odyssey biomes, map features and quests:
* in the ruins that spawn in the [[scarlands]] and [[glacial plain]] biomes.
* in some man-made landmarks: [[ancient launch site]], [[ancient garrison]], [[ancient chemfuel refinery]], [[frozen ruins]]
* in some ancient structure discovery quests: launch site, garrison, chemfuel refinery
* in some [[Quests#Odyssey|gravship quest sites]]: ancient reactor, frozen terraformer
