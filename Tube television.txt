A '''tube television''' is a [[recreation]] item that provides "{{P|Recreation Type}}" recreation to [[colonist]]s in an area in front of it. It is functionally similar to other types of [[television]].

== Acquisition ==
{{Acquisition}} 

They can also be acquired through [[trade]].

== Summary ==
{{Television Summary|5|3}}
[[File:Tube TV.jpg|thumb|center|A tube television and its area of effect.]]

== Analysis ==
Any form of television provides another form of recreation to your colony. They are useful for bed-ridden pawns in particular, as the only other option requires that another colonist talk to them. [[Flatscreen television]]s and [[megascreen television]]s provide more recreation power, but take up more space and power.

In general, it is not worth it to actually research television. [[Megascreen television]]s provide stronger recreation, and are often carried by exotic goods [[trader]]s and [[faction base]]s. Tube televisions can also be bought from the same sources.
