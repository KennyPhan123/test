== Analysis ==
Pigskin is one of the least protective [[textiles]] in the game, at tied 21st most protective, or tied 8th least protective. Only [[lightleather]], [[patchleather]], [[cloth]] and [[wool]]s are worse. It has terrible insulation, being the 4th and 3rd worst for heat and cold respectively. It also has a fairly low [[Property:Beauty Factor|beauty factor]] and market value. Thus it should only be used when there is no alternative or where its stats have little effect on the utility of the resulting item, such as in clothing production [[quests]] or [[Armchair]]s whose [[beauty]] rarely significantly affects a [[room stats|room's quality]].

The pigskin textile has no connection with the [[pigskins|pigskin xenotype]].{{BiotechIcon}} The pigskin xenotype can wear pigskin apparel without any penalty.
