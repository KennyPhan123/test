'''Carpet''' is a type of [[floor]]ing that is [[beauty|beautiful]] and available in a range of colors, but is flammable and hard to clean.

== Acquisition ==
{{Acquisition}} Color is chosen when the blueprint is laid, and incurs no additional cost.

== Summary ==
When carpet burns it leaves [[burned carpet]] behind.

== Analysis ==
With a [[Beauty]] of 2, carpets can quickly upgrade a "mediocre" room to an "[[Impressiveness|impressive]]" one, perfect for those troublesome [[greedy]] colonists.

Carpet can be particularly useful in the desert where wood is hard to come by or when you might not want to use up all your precious metals. Starting a cloth farm is pretty easy, especially if you have hydroponics researched, and if you don't have the time to spare or the skill to smooth floors, it's a nice substitute. Players also use this to color code rooms, for their own benefit.

It is important to consider the cleaning time of {{bad|200%}}, which doubles the time needed to clean it.
