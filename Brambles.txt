Brambles are a type of wild plant found in {{Habitats}}. They can be consumed by herbivores for {{P|Nutrition}} nutrition. They have a walk speed of 57% when on normal soil, and provide no cover. They are automatically cut when something is built on it. It can be manually removed with the [[cut plants]] command in the [[architect]] tab.
