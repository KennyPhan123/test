The '''ship cryptosleep casket''' is used to contain a humanoid or animal in a state of suspended animation for space travel in addition to the other utilitarian purposes of its [[Cryptosleep casket|non-ship cousin]]. 

== Acquisition ==
{{Acquisition}}

They also appears as part of the ship featured in the [[ship to the stars]] quest.

== Summary ==
Ship cryptosleep caskets are required to build and launch a [[ship]]. At least one casket must be loaded in order for a ship to launch.

A pawn can enter or be carried into a cryptosleep casket to activate cryptosleep. Inside, the occupants biological functions are suspended; they will not age biologically, and most needs and health conditions like hunger, drug addiction, disease, are suspended. However, social standing and time-sensitive [[mood]] effects do tick down while in cryptosleep. While in a casket, a colony's pawn counts as 100% of their actual wealth, but only count as 30% of a colonist when determining [[raid points]]. This means that pawns ''do'' count for determining raid size, but at a lesser amount.

For colonists, simply select the colonist, right click the casket, and select "Enter ship cryptosleep casket". Other colonists can drag downed pawns by right clicking the pawn in question. Animals or prisoners can be put into cryptosleep caskets, but they need to be downed first. You can set an operation to apply anesthesia, which requires a unit of medicine. Alternatively, you can use violence, or, if they have a brain implant, they can also be [[EMP]]ed to cause [[brain shock]] to quickly and cheaply down them. 

When occupied, an 'Eject sleeper' button becomes available to allow the player to remove the colonist remotely. After waking from cryptosleep, a humanoid will have [[cryptosleep sickness]] for a short time, which reduces consciousness and manipulation and causes vomiting.

When a ship cryptosleep casket is severely damaged, below 20% HP, it will begin to spark and emit a hissing sound for 2.4 seconds before exploding, dealing 10 [[Flame]] damage in a 2.66-tile radius and potentially igniting [[Flammability|flammable]] pawns, buildings, and flooring in the area. For a full list of effects, see [[Flame]].

== Analysis==
Ship cryptosleep caskets are required to launch a [[ship]]. Outside of the ship, any cryptosleep casket can help relieve a colony during a crisis, such as an intense psychic wave or medicine shortage, by safely storing pawns until the problem is resolved.   Additionally, pawns addicted to a drug will not progress through withdrawal during stasis, making the casket a handy way of saving the life of a [[Luciferium]]-user, or decrease total demand over time. 

With the use of anesthetic, prisoners can also be stored temporarily to lessen the drain on colony resources while they await [[trade|sale]], [[human resources|harvesting]] or, if the [[Royalty DLC]] is installed, exchanged for [[Title|Honor]]. It is also useful to save enemies/animals for recruitment and taming until an [[Mental_inspiration|inspiration]], particularly - [[thrumbo]]s and enemies with high resistance.

Ship cryptosleep caskets are required to build a [[ship]], so are convienent once you get to that stage of the game. Ship cryptosleep caskets also have the advantage of being openable by the pawn inside, meaning that they can be opened if no conscious pawns are outside. However, they require much more research than regular caskets and ancient caskets (which require none at all). They also need a [[ship structural beam]] to build, which is both space and resource demanding.
