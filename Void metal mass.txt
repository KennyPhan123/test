'''Void metal mass''' and its variants are a structure that appears alongside [[Void structure]]s and within the [[Metal hell]].

== Summary ==
Several void metal masses of varying sizes generate scattered around Void structures. They appear in a greater number and density around the edges of the Metal hell.

== Analysis ==
They can be used as cover and for preventing encirclement in melee when activating the Void structures.
