The '''corrosive heart''' is an [[artificial body part]] added by the [[Anomaly DLC]] that acts as a replacement for a [[ghoul]]'s heart. It gives ghoul the ability to spit acid at the expense of lower part efficiency and thus [[blood pumping]]. 

== Acquisition ==
{{Acquisition}}

It can sometimes spawn on hostile [[ghoul]]s,{{Check Tag|Starter colonist?|Can they spawn on ghoul starter colonists?}} however they cannot be harvested from them.

Artificial hearts are also immune to [[heart attack]]s and [[artery blockage]]s, preventing future cases and curing existing ones.

By installing a bionic heart, the pawn's natural [[heart]], if perfectly healthy, is harvested without a penalty. This organic heart can then be sold or stored for later use.

The corrosive heart is a [[ghoul]] exclusive body part. Corrosive hearts have an 85% part efficiency, or a {{--|15%}} reduction compared to a healthy organic heart. This results in {{--|15%}} loss to [[Blood Pumping]]. 

The reduction to Blood Pumping lowers [[move speed]] by a total {{--|6%}} and [[Consciousness]] by {{--|3%}}. The reduction of Consciousness in turn reduces [[Manipulation]], [[Talking]], and [[Eating]]. For a full breakdown of the affected stats, see those pages. 

[[File:Corrosive_spray.png|thumb|left|Corrosive spray gizmo]]
Pawns with the heart installed have access to the "Corrosive spray" ability while [[draft]]ed. The spray must be manually initiated by the player through use of a gizmo available while the ghoul is drafted and selected. Colonist ghouls will never independently use the spray.{{Check Tag|Verify}} NPC ghouls will use the ability if they have access to it.

It is worth noting that you do not get a natural [[heart]] when installing a corrosive heart but you will get back any artificial heart already installed.

Spits a fast-moving corrosive fluid projectile in a 3x3 area around the target. This does 30 damage to buildings and items, and creates a harmless "spent acid" filth on each affected tile. Creatures in the affected area suffer a 30 damage burn (possibly spread over multiple body parts), but suffer no persistent damage over time. The attack has an {{AP}} of 80%{{Check Tag|Armor type?|Does this oppose heat armor? Sharp? blunt?}} and a [[stopping power]] of 1.

=== Installation ===
{{Stub|section=1|reason=Medicine item requirement, if any, and stats for removal}}
The corrosive heart can only be installed in [[ghoul]]s. Note that despite the description noting [[pain]] as the reason it cannot be installed in other pawns, methods of pain nullification such as [[painstopper]]s do not allow the heart's installation into other pawns.

Installing the part requires {{ticks|2500}} of work. It doesn't [[Anesthetic|anesthetize]] the ghoul, requires no medicine, cannot fail, but requires [[Skills#Medical|medical skill]] of 4.

Removing the part requires ?<!--{{ticks|2000}} Time I got with a stopwatch.--> of work, 1x medicine of any quality, and has no [[Medical]] skill requirements.

Unlike most operations, installing the heart cannot fail.

== Analysis ==
The attack itself is almost identical to [[Acid spray]]{{BiotechIcon}} but the warm up and cooldown times are different.

Acid damage is great for inflicting pain upon pawns, as chemical burn inflict much more [[Pain#Formula|pain]] than bruises or cuts. A corrosive spray followed by a few punches is enough to down most pawns from pain shock. Consider having a few ghouls with this ability to deal with escaping prisoners, entities, or to down fleshbeasts, noctols, or gorehulks for capture.

However, this heart also reduce move speed by the indirect effect of consciousness, causing your ghoul to be even slower.
