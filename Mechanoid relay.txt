'''Mechanoid relays''' are buildings that can be destablized for a [[gravcore]].

== Occurrence ==
Mechanoid relays appear as part of the [[Quests#Mechanoid_Relay|Mechanoid Relay]] [[gravship]] quest.

In addition, in the gravship [[starting scenario]], the starting platform will contain mechanoid relays, but these cannot be destabilized.

== Summary ==
The mechanoid relay starts out invincible and uninteractable. After all [[relay stabilizer]]s on the map are hacked or destroyed, the mechanoid relay will be destablized, causing it to crash into the ground after a short delay. The crashed relay can be destroyed to retrieve {{Icon Small|Gravcore}} 1 [[gravcore]], {{Icon Small|Gravlite panel}} 165 [[gravlite panel]]s, and {{Icon Small|Steel slag chunk}} 4 [[steel slag chunk]]s.
