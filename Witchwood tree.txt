'''Witchwood trees''' are a wild [[Trees|tree]] added by the [[Biotech DLC]] that can be found in {{Habitats}}. It can only grow in polluted terrain, and can either be found growing there naturally, or sown by colonists. Witchwoods can grow well in poor soil, and like [[Polux tree|Polux Trees]], are one of the few trees able to survive in heavily polluted terrain. On the other hand, they look ugly, grow slowly, and do not yield as much wood as other varieties.
