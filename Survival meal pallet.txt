The '''Survival meal pallet''' is a structure added by the [[Odyssey DLC]]. Its contents are ejected when opened.

== Occurrence ==
Survival meal pallets are generated in a variety of map features and Odyssey quests. On the planet they are found in ancient launch sites, ancient warehouses, ancient garrisons, ancient chemfuel refineries, infested settlements, ruins, and ancient stockpiles. In orbit they are found in orbital ancient platforms and orbital quest sites.

Survival meal pallets are mostly located in undefended living rooms, dining rooms and kitchens. However they are also found in pallet rooms in warehouses, which are guarded by a [[hunter drone]] and an [[ancient defender turret]]. In addition, kitchens in infested settlements have a small chance of containing a [[hive]].
