A '''bog hound''' is an [[animal]] added by the [[Odyssey DLC]].

== Acquisition ==
{{PAGENAME}}s can be found in {{Habitats}}. They can either be tamed by a [[Work #Handle|handler]] or self-tame in a random event. 

{{PAGENAME}}s can be bought and sold in other [[Trade#Faction base|faction bases]] and from [[Trade#Exotic goods trader 2|exotic goods traders]]. {{PAGENAME}}s purchased from traders will be already tamed.

== Summary ==
?

== Analysis ==
Bog hounds are wild [[dog]]s adapted to survive in a [[pollution|toxic]]{{BiotechIcon}} environment. They are much wilder than their domestic cousins, with a lower hunger rate and stronger bite, but are otherwise similar in their ability to take damage.
