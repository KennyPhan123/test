<big>Welcome to the Comparison Table!</big>

This is a tool designed to let you compare every traditional ranged [[weapon]] in the game, quality included. Area-of-effect weapons are not included, as their {{DPS}} depends on how many enemies you can hit at once and they typically have forced miss radii. 

==== Instructions ====
# {{Cross}} - Click this on the control panel to show a weapon and all its variants on the main table.
# {{Check}} - Click this on the control panel to hide a weapon and all its variants on the main table.

Please mouse over column headings with dotted underlines for further explanation including assumptions made. 

==== Sorting ====
The main table can be sorted by each column, and tier sorting is enabled - for example, you can sort by DPS and then by Quality to easily compare DPS at a given quality.
