There are some items in RimWorld that are impossible to obtain by standard means, but can be spawned in using [[development mode]]. 

==Mechanoid Weapons==
[[Mechanoids]] come equipped with their own class of weapons. While the [[Charge lance]] found on [[Lancer]]s is otherwise available and thus not listed here, no mechanoid will drop their weapon when killed and no other mechanoid weapon is obtainable.
