'''Ancient exostrider head''' are [[ruins]] added by the [[Biotech DLC]].

== Summary ==
The ancient exostrider head spawns with 1 [[ancient exostrider midsection]], 1 [[ancient exostrider cannon]], and 3 [[ancient exostrider leg]]s.

Destruction will yield 4 [[steel slag chunk]]s.
