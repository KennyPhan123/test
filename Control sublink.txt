'''Control sublinks''' are one of two related [[artificial body parts]] available to [[mechanitor]]s. Each sublink, regardless of type, increases the number of [[control group]]s available to a mechanitor by {{+|1}} and the work speed of their mechs by {{+|6%}} up to a maximum of {{+|6}} groups and {{+|36%}} work speed. These two types are:
* [[Control sublink (standard)]], can be installed by any mechanitor. Stacks up to 3 times. Each costs {{Required Resources|Control sublink (standard)}}.
* [[Control sublink (high)]], can be installed by any mechanitor that already has 3 implanted standard sublinks. Stacks up to 3 times. Each costs {{Required Resources|Control sublink (high)}}.
