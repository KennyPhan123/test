'''Torture crowns''' are a form of [[clothing]] added by the [[Ideology DLC]]. They add [[pain]], for those who want to suffer. Despite being called "crowns", they have no relation to [[noble]]s{{RoyaltyIcon}} or more traditional [[crown]]s. {{RoyaltyIcon}}

== Acquisition ==
{{Acquisition}} Torture crowns can only be crafted if you have the [[Guilty (meme)|Guilty]] or [[Pain is virtue]] memes.{{Check Tag|Detail needed|Does this mean the crafter has to be of the ideo, or just that a pawn with the ideo is in the colony?}}

They can also occasionally be [[Trade|bought]] from bulk good traders.

== Summary ==
Wearing a torture crown inflicts {{++|5%}} [[pain]] on the wearer. It does no damage, and the pain will be removed the instant the crown is no longer worn and the amount inflicted is independent of quality or material - all torture crowns will inflict the same amount of pain. With no other sources of pain, this is insufficient to inflict any [[Consciousness]] penalties but does inflict a {{--|5}}/{{+|5}}/{{+|3}} [[mood]]let for normal, [[masochist]], and [[Pain (precept)|Pain Idealizing]] pawns respectively. These thoughts do not stack, with the most positive taking precedent. 

Finally, for [[psycast]]ers,{{RoyaltyIcon}} pain also adds a multiplier of <code>100% + 3 × Pain percentage</code> to [[Neural Heat Recovery Rate]], or {{Good|x{{#expr:100 + (3*5)}}%}} assuming no other sources of pain besides the crown.

Note that the effects of pain on mood and consciousness are not strictly linear, and the values may be different for pawns with other sources of pain. See the [[Pain]] article for details. 

== Analysis ==
{{Stub|section=1|reason=1) Flesh out 2) directly compare Torture crown NH dissipation to other psycaster headgear.}}
The obvious and intended use is to provide a mood buff to [[masochist]] and [[Pain (precept)|Pain Idealizing]] pawns. The pain inflicted is minor that being closer to the [[pain shock threshold]] and a [[consciousness]] penalty matter less than the crown being incompatible with a protective helmet. While drop pod [[raids]] or [[infestation]]s mean attacks may occur on short notice, the crown can be easily and quickly swapped out for a protective helmet in most circumstances. Ideally, the crown will only be worn when a pawn requires the mood buff, such as when close to a mental break, with it being kept in storage and replaced by a helmet at other times. 

There is some merit to equipping [[slave]]s with the crown - while their mood debuff from the pain will make them rebel ever so slightly more often, they will also be slightly easier to safely down as they will be slightly closer to their [[pain shock threshold]].

=== Psycasters ===
{{Royalty|section=1}}
[[Psycast]]ers  will also multiply their rate of neural heat recharge by a factor of {{Good|x1.15}}. The fact that this can be removed easily makes this more attractive than other sources of pain. If used in combat, consider pairing it with a [[shield belt]] or some cannon fodder as the crown does not provide significant armor, and will often provide none after weapon {{AP}}. Even high [[quality]] [[plasteel]] crowns are not particularly protective - a legendary example offers only 31%, less than a poor [[steel]] [[simple helmet]] or a normal [[thrumbofur]] [[bowler hat]]. 

If [[bioferrite]]{{AnomalyIcon}} is available as a material for the crown, its psychic effects synergize well with the effects of pain, providing a higher [[Neural Heat Recovery Rate]] than an [[eltex helmet]] and almost as much [[psychic sensitivity]] for an otherwise unenhanced 6th level psycaster. However, as the effects of both bonuses are multiplicative while those of the eltex are additive, depending on the other bonuses available to a pawn, a bioferrite crown can even outperform an [[eltex skullcap]] while being far easier to acquire at high [[qualities]].
