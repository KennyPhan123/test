The '''small chemfuel tank''' is a [[building]] added by the [[Odyssey DLC]] that stores [[chemfuel]] and allows the fuel to be consumed by the [[gravship]] on which the tank is built.

It is the smaller of the two such tanks, with the larger being the appropriately named [[large chemfuel tank]].

== Summary ==
{{Stub|aection=1|reason=Mechanics including loading (who by, how, limits etc), unlowding, placmeent on ship, lack of plumbing needed etc. Ensure parity with [[large chemfuel tank]]}}
{{Image wanted|section=1|reason= Gizmos}}
Small chemfuel tanks hold 250 units of chemfuel for use in gravship travel. Fuel extends the range of a gravship.

Chemfuel can be removed from this container by use of the "eject fuel" gizmo.

Chemfuel tanks that hold chemfuel and are damaged to ~40% of their total [[hit points]] will hiss before dropping all their inventory as a chemfuel bomb. The size of the explosion is dependent on the amount of stored chemfuel.

== Analysis ==
{{Stub|section=1|reason=General- also specifically  placementand protection, how to decide which and how many to use, consider cribbing info/graphs/tables from [[fuel optimiser]] to show which you need when. ensure parity with [[large chemfuel tank]]}}
This tank contains 250 units of chemfuel with a footprint of 2x2, or 62.5 units per tile. Compared with the [[large chemfuel tank]] which is 3x3 and holds 83.3 units per tile (for a total of 750) it is significantly less dense, but it is easier to place in the crowded corridors of a gravship.
