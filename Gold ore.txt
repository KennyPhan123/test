'''Gold ore''' is a natural structure that can be [[mine]]d to obtain {{Icon Small|Gold}} gold. 

== Summary ==
'''Gold ore''' tiles have 1,500 health each, making them one of the faster ores to mine. They can be found in smaller veins, ranging from 2 to 8 tiles in size. Each mined block has a base yield of {{Icon Small|Gold}} 40 gold, however this is modified by factors such as the [[Difficulty]] setting and the [[mining yield]] of the pawn.
