A '''{{PAGENAME}}''' is an [[animal]] added by the [[Odyssey DLC]].

== Acquisition ==
{{PAGENAME}}s can be found in {{Habitats}}. They can either be tamed by a [[Work #Handle|handler]] or self-tame in a random event. 

{{PAGENAME}}s can be bought and sold in other [[Trade#Faction base|faction bases]] and from [[Trade#Exotic goods trader 2|exotic goods traders]]. {{PAGENAME}}s purchased from traders will be already tamed.

== Summary ==
{{PAGENAME}}s are not pen animals, and must have their tameness periodically upkept by a [[Work #Handle|handler]] of skill at or above 9.

=== Diet ===
Can eat raw meat, corpses, all animal products, meals, all processed foods, and kibble.

== Analysis ==
Tigers can be used for either hauling or as an attack animal. They are slightly more wild than [[panther]] and [[cougar]], meaning they will require more training; however, they are slightly faster, have more health, and produce half the [[filth]]. Biggest issue is actually recruiting them as they are more aggressive and 3 times less likely to appear compared to [[panther]].
