'''Comfortable temperature''' refers to the range between the two stats [[Minimum Comfortable Temperature]] and [[Maximum Comfortable Temperature]].

== Summary ==
If a pawn is experiencing temperatures +/- {{Temperature|10||delta}} from its comfortable temperature range, it will receive [[hypothermia]] or [[heatstroke]], for being too cold or too warm respectively.

Humans will also experience negative [[thought]]s for experiencing any temperatures outside their comfortable temperature range will also receive negative [[mood]]lets. Furthermore, sleeping in a room outside of their comfortable temperature range (''before considering apparel'') will receive negate moodlets for sleeping in the cold or heat.
