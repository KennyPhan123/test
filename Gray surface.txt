'''Gray surface''' is a terrain that appears exclusively in the [[Labyrinth]].

== Summary ==
Gray surface is the prominent terrain of the labyrinth, only accompanied by a small amount of [[Void metal]] surrounding the obelisk. It has a move speed factor of {{P|Move Speed Factor}}, the same as most natural terrain.

== Analysis ==
It is neutral in all accords, providing no up or downsides. (except for the 87% move speed modifier.)
