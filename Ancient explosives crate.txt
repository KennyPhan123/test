The '''ancient explosives crate''' is an explosive device found in Odyssey-related ruins. Unlike the smaller [[ancient mining charge]], ancient explosives crates cannot be uninstalled.

== Acquisition ==
Cannot be crafted. Found in Odyssey related sites:
* Ancient Chemfuel Refinery
* Ancient Garrison
* Orbital Platform
* Quarry
* Asteroids
* (Incomplete)

{{Check Tag|Verify|Missing locations. Absent in others}}

== Summary ==
[[File:Mining charge 2x2 AoE.png|thumb|left|The radius of the ancient mining charge]]

When detonated, an ancient explosives crate will begin to spark and emit a hissing sound for {{ticks|480}} before exploding, dealing 50 [[Mining Bomb]] damage in a 5.9-tile radius. Mining Bomb damage has additional damage multipliers depending on the target.

== Analysis ==
When encountered in ancient ruins, ancient explosives crates can be dangerous if detonated by another threat on the same map. Fuel nodes, turrets, hostiles, and especially hunter drone traps can trigger ancient explosives crates.

Unlike ancient mining charges, ancient explosives crates are not practical for mining because they cannot be uninstalled by the player.
