A '''research bench''' is a [[production]] bench at which your [[colonists]] can [[research]] new technologies. Currently, there are two types available:
*{{Icon Small|Simple research bench|50}} [[Simple research bench]]: Allows research of basic technologies. Available from the start of the game. 
*{{Icon Small|Hi-tech research bench|50}} [[Hi-tech research bench]]: Provides faster research and grants access to more advanced technologies. Can be linked to a [[Multi-analyzer]]. Requires [[Research#Microelectronics|Microelectronics]].
