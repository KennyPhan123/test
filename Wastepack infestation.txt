'''Wastepack Infestations''' are triggered by destroyed [[Toxic wastepack|Toxic Wastepacks]]. When a wastepack infestation triggers, it creates [[Stasis cocoon|wastepack cocoon]]s. When these cocoons are disturbed, or when a human walks within a set radius, the [[Insectoids|insects]] will appear. These insects erupt hostile to all non-Insects, including wild animals and even some spawned map detritus like Ancient Car Frames. Wastepack Infestations generated an amount of cocoons based on [[Raid point|Raid Points]]. Each cocoon's raid point amount is the same as the insect inside: 150 for [[Megaspider]], 75 for [[Spelopede]] and 40 for [[Megascarab]]. 

== Wastepack spawned Wastepack Infestations ==

Anytime a Wastepack deteriorates or is destroyed on the map there is a ( 1.2% chance * Wastepack Cocoons Infestations difficulty option ) of a Wastepack Infestation. These Wastepack Infestations appear around the location of the wastepack being destroyed. 

== Storyteller fired Wastepack Infestations ==

The storyteller can no longer fire a Wastepack Infestation without any Wastepacks deteriorating. These Wastepack Infestations were fired like any other major threat like Raids, Manhunter Packs, or Defoliator Ships. 

These randomly fired wastepack infestations were considered a bug and removed in patch 1.5.
