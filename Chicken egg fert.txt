'''Chicken eggs''' are a type of [[Eggs|egg]] laid by female [[chicken]]s (hens).

[[Eggs#Fertilization|Unfertilized]] chicken eggs are displayed as "[[Chicken egg (unfert.)]]", while fertilized chicken eggs are displayed "Chicken egg (fert.)". Both fertilized and unfertilized eggs are edible to colonists and [[Ovivore|ovivorous]] [[animals]].

== Summary ==
As a [[raw food]], eggs can be be eaten raw, with a flat {{Bad|2%}} chance of giving [[food poisoning]], or cooked into a [[meal]] with a food poisoning chance dependent on the skill of the cook and the [[cleanliness]] of the kitchen. [[Animals]] are immune to food poisoning from raw food but not from meals. When used in food recipes that require specific types of [[nutrition]], such as [[fine meal]]s, eggs are classified as an animal product.

As with most raw food, eating eggs directly results in a {{Thought|desc=I had to eat raw food. We should be cooking that kind of food before eating it. We're not animals.|label=ate raw food|stack=1|value=-7|duration=1}}. This mood penalty is negated if the pawn has the [[ascetic]] trait or the [[robust digestion]] {{BiotechIcon}} gene.

Chicken eggs take 3.5 days to hatch.

== Analysis ==
When eaten or used in cooking, chicken eggs are worth {{P|Nutrition}} nutrition. If a fertilized egg is allowed to hatch and the chick is slaughtered, {{#vardefineecho:meat|{{Meat Leather Curve|{{Q|Chicken|Body Size}}*0.1*140}} }} [[Meat|{{Q|Chicken|Meat Name}}]] (worth {{#expr:{{#var:meat}}*0.05}} nutrition, modified by [[AI Storytellers#Economy|difficulty]] and [[butchery efficiency]]) will be produced, so if the butcher work is not a problem, it is better to allow eggs to hatch.
