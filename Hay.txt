'''Hay''' is the fastest produced food source, acquired by growing and harvesting [[haygrass]]. It's great for efficiently feeding [[animals]], and can be used for [[straw matting]]. It cannot, however be used for training animals.

It is inedible to humans unless prepared as an ingredient in [[kibble]], and doing so gives your colonists a brutal {{Thought|value=-12|stack=1|label=ate kibble|desc=I had to eat animal kibble. It's like I don't get to be human any more.|duration=1}}.
