The '''war mask''' is a tribal clothing item. It provides minor protection, as well as increasing [[pain shock threshold]], allowing the wearer to take more [[pain]] before being [[downed]]. 

== Acquisition ==
For [[New Tribe]]s, {{Acquisition}} [[New Arrivals]] can never craft war masks.

War masks can be purchased from traders, crafted (by tribal factions), or stripped from tribal raiders.

== Summary == 
In addition to minor head protection, a war mask increases the [[pain shock threshold]] of its wearer by {{+|10%}}, allowing its wearer to take more [[pain]] before being [[downed]].

== Analysis ==
War masks are a cheap source of armor for tribespeople in the early game, if you have a good craftsman. While they don't give much protection, the increase in pain shock threshold helps your pawns stay longer in the fight before giving in to pain.

Wood is reasonably easy to obtain in most biomes, so obtaining materials shouldn't be too much of an issue. Rather, the greater issue is having to divert labor to obtaining the wood and crafting it.

War masks can be equipped on [[Wimp]] colonists to allow them to function under certain illnesses which would normally surpass their pain threshold.
