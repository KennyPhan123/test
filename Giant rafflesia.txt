'''Giant rafflesia''' is a type of inedible [[plant]] found in {{Habitats}}. They provide a small amount of cover, so colonists can be stationed behind them in desperate circumstances. However their vile smell makes them very unbeautiful, making manually removing them when they grow in and around your base a good move.

They are based on a real-life holoparasite of the same name, the [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rafflesia Rafflesia arnoldii].
