'''Mastodon wool''' is a type of [[fabric]] gathered by [[Skills#Animals|animal handlers]] by shearing tamed [[mastodon]]s. Mastodon wool is a very good insulator, and is therefore recommended in clothing like [[parka]]s and [[tuque]]s.

== Acquisition ==
Mastodon wool is acquired by shearing tamed [[mastodon]]s - each mastodon can be sheared every {{Q|Mastodon|Shearing Interval Days}} days for {{Q|Mastodon|Wool Amount}} wool, for an average of {{Q|Mastodon|Daily Wool Average}} per day. Note that the amount of wool sheared scales with the [[Animal Gather Yield]] of the shearer. 

Shearing takes {{Ticks|1700}}, regardless of the amount of wool produced, and only modified by the [[Animal Gather Speed]] of the handler.

It can also be purchased from [[trade]]rs.

== Analysis ==
Along with the other [[wool]]s, mastodon wool has the second worst protective stats of any [[textile]] in the game, only beating [[cloth]] by virtue of heat armor. But like other wools, mastodon wool excels against the cold, offering the 4th best cold insulation. In comparison, its heat insulation is poor, at 20th of all textiles. While it is marginally better than other wools at insulating heat, it still isn't good when compared to other common textiles such as [[plainleather]] or [[cloth]].

Therefore, wool clothes are largely used in cold biomes, providing almost no physical protection. Considering you may keep some tamed for their caravan and combat abilities rather than for meat, you may passively earn some mastodon wool over time.

Mastodons are tied with [[megasloth]]s for the the fastest Per Day production of any wooly animal. Though it only has a somewhat improved [[Property:Beauty Factor|beauty factor]] over plain textiles, as clothes rapidly become redundant, it may be used to improve furniture [[beauty]] and provide bulk textiles for construction due its rapid production. Care should be taken, as like other wools in RimWorld, it is exceptionally flammable.

== Trivia ==
Contrary to wool in real life, wool in [[RimWorld]] is more flammable than [[Cloth|cotton]], rather than less.
