'''Repair probes''' are [[artificial body parts]] added by the [[Biotech DLC]] that increase the rate at which [[mechanitor]]s repair their [[mechanoids]].

== Acquisition ==
{{Acquisition}}

== Summary ==
Each repair probe increases base [[Mech Repair Speed]] of the implanted [[mechanitor]] by {{+|33%}}, before the effect of the multiplicative [[Crafting]] skill modifier. Repair probes have a [[max install count]] of six, meaning up to six repair probes can be installed in each mechanitor's brain. Each probe stacks additively as if it were {{+|33.3&#773;%}}, with the end result rounded down. For further detail on how [[Mech Repair Speed]] is calculated, see that page.

=== Installation ===
{{Stub|section=1|reason=Written from memory. Verification, expansion, and clarity needs work. also where installed }}
Unlike most other artificial body parts, the repair probe is not installed via an [[operation]]. Instead, the selected mechanitor can be selected to use the device on themselves, consuming the item and installing it.

== Analysis ==
{{stub|section=1|reason=Some numbers on how much time is saved repairing would be useful. Subsequently, a proper analysis of synergy with [[remote repairer]], if any}}
Repair probes use a valuable [[nano structuring chip]]. Making a repair probe should be weighed against making [[mechlord suit|mechlord]] [[mechlord helmet|armor]], a [[wastepack atomizer]], or your own [[war queen]]. Making a mechlord suit, in order to have 2 [[centipede]]s, is most likely better than having 1 centipede repaired 33% faster. The advanced components could also be used for [[bionics]]. If you have a surplus of chips and components, then repair probes are ultimately a buff to your mechanitor.

Usually the time taken to repair after battle isn't a big issue. Sees use with the [[remote repairer]], as this implant makes it easier to repair mechs during battle.
