The '''crib''' is a [[furniture]] item added by the [[Biotech DLC]]

== Acquisition ==
{{Acquisition}}

== Summary ==
[[Babies]] spend most of their time in a crib. Nursing mothers or pawns with the Childcare labor enabled will remove them to engage in breastfeeding or play, replacing them when done. Babies receive a [[mood]] bonus from being inside a [[Quality|high-quality]] crib. Cribs do not turn a bedroom into a [[barracks]].

When a child outgrows their crib, it will automatically be unassigned, causing them to seek a more suitably sized bed when they next rest.

Unlike normal beds, cribs don't have owner types and don't change when other beds in the room change.

== Analysis ==
Cribs are cheaper than [[bed]]s, are 1 tile smaller, and do not turn a bedroom into a barracks. They also provide a [[mood]] boost - since babies have few [[mood]]lets available, a high-[[quality]] crib can have a great impact on their mood, and thus a whole colony's mood. While a baby will eventually outgrow a crib, they are not expensive to create and thus should be made if planning to raise a baby naturally.

Note that babies can also sleep in any other type of bed. If a crib is not at least of good [[quality]], then using a regular bed would have no difference on the baby. {{Check Tag|Verify|Mechanics unclear. Also check stats of a crib}} Therefore, if there's a spare regular bed available, that can be used in place of building a crib. It is also better to use a [[hospital bed]] if your baby gets a [[disease]].

Lastly, a crib will heal the baby on it by 4 {{HP}} per day.
