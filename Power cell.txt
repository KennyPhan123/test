'''Power cells''' are a type of building that generate [[power]] from nothing and produce no waste. These are:

* [[Gravcore power cell]] {{OdysseyIcon}} - Constructed using [[gravcore]]s. Provides {{Q|Gravcore power cell|Power Consumption}}W, but is limited to [[gravship]] use.
* [[Unstable power cell]] {{RoyaltyIcon}} - Arrives with [[mechanoid cluster]]s. Provides {{Q|Unstable power cell|Power Consumption}}W and explodes with sufficient damage taken.
* [[Vanometric power cell]] - A [[quest]] reward that provides {{Q|Vanometric power cell|Power Consumption}}W.
