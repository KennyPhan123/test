'''Rat palm trees''' are wild [[Trees|trees]] added by the [[Biotech DLC]] that can be found in {{Habitats}}. They can only grow in polluted terrain, and can either be found growing there naturally, or sown by colonists. In this way they are much like [[Witchwood tree|witchwood trees]], [[Gray pine tree|gray pine trees]], and [[Pebble cactus|pebble cactus]]. Besides being pollution-resistant, rat palms can grow well in poor soil. On the other hand, they look ugly, grow slowly, and do not yield as much wood as other tree varieties.
