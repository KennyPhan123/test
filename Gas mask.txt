The '''gas mask''' is a piece of [[apparel]] added by the [[Biotech DLC]]. 

== Acquisition ==
{{Acquisition}}

== Summary ==
Gas masks increase [[Toxic Environment Resistance]] by {{+|80%}}, causing [[rot stink]], and [[Toxic buildup]] from [[tox gas]], [[toxic fallout]], and [[pollution]] to be 20% effective. Separate to its effect on toxic buildup, it also prevents the user from being affected by [[tox gas exposure]].  It does not protect from [[disease]]s nor does it prevent [[toxic buildup]] from attacks such as the [[cobra]]'s bite. [[Quality]] does not affect this resistance.

{{Apparel Nudity Note}}

== Analysis ==
The gas mask can be useful as a situational item when needing to handle a lot of rotting [[corpse]]s or survive toxic conditions. However, it cannot be worn with a helmet and provides minimal Sharp protection, so it should not be worn on a regular basis. It has surprisingly decent Blunt armor, on par with a [[steel]] [[flak helmet]] or [[plasteel]] [[simple helmet]], but the mask's blunt protection is too small to make a meaningful difference.

[[Face mask]]s are generally a better alternative: their 50% environmental protection is ''enough'' in many cases, and they do not cover the head, meaning they can be worn with a simple or flak helmet for superior Sharp protection. However, face masks do not provide tox gas immunity, which can debuff pawns over time.

The [[vacsuit helmet]]{{OdysseyIcon}} outclasses the gas mask, providing the same Toxic Environmental resistance and tox gas immunity, with better Sharp armor and insignificantly worse Blunt armor. However, the vacsuit helmet is unlocked later and is more expensive.
