'''Climate adjusters''' are [[mechanoid cluster]] and quest [[condition causer]]s added by the [[Royalty DLC]] that change the [[temperature]] of the world around them.  

== Occurrence ==
?

May come included with a mech cluster. They can also spawn as an off-map condition causers as part of a quest or random spawn.

== Summary ==
It affects a 10 [[world]]-tile radius, and the effects of multiple climate adjusters can stack.

A climate adjuster will increase or decrease temperature by 10 degrees, randomly. The direction is determined when the adjuster spawns and does not change afterwards.
