== Analysis ==
The assault rifle is the jack-of-all trades of RimWorld's firearms. Single-target damage is respectable, with accuracy and warmup time being good overall. Range is amazing - tied for fourth of all non-consumable weapons and only beaten by weapons with considerably less {{DPS}}. Statistically, the assault rifle's only significant deficiency is its [[stopping power]], as it's unable to stagger humans. Another consideration is its expense, though not as expensive as a charge rifle. You won't see raiders use assault rifles until the midgame somewhat limiting the chance to use looted rifles before the higher qualities available to colony-produced weapons take precedence.

In terms of actual {{DPS}}, the assault rifle is consistently lower than [[chain shotgun]], [[heavy SMG]], and [[LMG]] within the short range band (12 tiles). It is also beaten by the [[charge rifle]] at any range it can hit. The assault rifle is moderately skill friendly, but this weapon really excels at long-range combat, which is best done by skilled pawns.

The assault rifle can be used to good effect in virtually any use case, whether in close-quarters, out hunting, or on the field. Therefore, they make a great general purpose weapon until high quality [[charge rifle]]s can be mass produced. Charge rifles have 3 tiles shorter range, but have better {{DPS}} at any range (after accuracy, pre-armor) and better {{AP}} on top, thus making them better in most situations except [[kiting]].

=== Stats ===
Against an unarmored human, an assault rifle can kill with 4 hits to the torso, 3 hits to the head or neck, or 1 hit to the brain; cripple or destroy limbs in 3 hits; and cripple or destroy eyes and digits with a single hit. It'll generally take around 6 hits (~10.1 seconds average) to incapacitate that human from pain shock.
