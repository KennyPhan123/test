The '''Psylink neuroformer''' is an item added by the [[Royalty DLC]] that increases the [[psylink]] level of its user by one. 

== Acquisition ==
By gaining [[Honor]] with the [[Empire]] faction, one may call a [[Empire#Bestower|Bestower]] to the colony to provide a Psylink to a pawn after it ranks up in titles. Alternatively, you may steal the Psylink neuroformer themselves from the Bestower, making your colony an enemy of the Empire.

Two Psylink neuroformers can be obtained through the quest [[Quests#Royalty|The Deserter]].

Psylink neuroformers can also be found as occasional [[quest]] rewards. These rewards are generated by special logic: the game keeps track of each time a psylink level is "available" to the player. This includes the following events:{{Check Tag|Confirm?|Based on occurrences of Notify_PsylinkAvailable in 1.5.4297 game code, not confirmed ingame}}
* Completing an [[anima tree]] linking ritual to gain a psylink level
* Obtaining a psylink level as a title reward
* Obtaining a psylink neuroformer as a quest reward
If more than 45 days have passed since the last such event, it starts being possible to get a psylink neuroformer as a quest reward. This chance raises up to 100% at 60 days.{{hover title|curve QuestTuning.DaysSincePsylinkAvailableToGuaranteedNeuroformerChance in Rimworld v.1.5.4297|<sup>[Source]</sup>}} Hence, at most one neuroformer per 45 days can be obtained from quest rewards. This route is the only renewable source of psylink neuroformers that doesn't require gaining [[Honor]] with the [[Empire]], although there are [[Psycasts#Acquisition|some other ways]] of acquiring psylink levels.

Psylink neuroformers can not be acquired through [[trade]] (except trade quests) or surgery as they could in past versions.


== Summary ==
When used, a psylink neuroformer increase the [[psylink]] level of the pawn by one. This turns a pawn into a psycaster if they weren't already. This increases the neural heat capacity, neural heat regeneration rate, and the level of psycasts available. Upon gaining a new level of psylink, a random [[psycast]] of the new level will be granted, unless one has already been learned via [[psytrainer]]. The psylink levels cannot be removed.

== Analysis == 
The Empire only gives psylink levels to the level "owed" - e.g. if you already have a level 2 psylink, becoming an Acolyte will not increase psylink any further. If you plan on raising a pawn up the royal ranks, you may want to use any spare neuroformers on a different pawn.

The best pawns to give psycasting powers are those with [[psychic sensitivity]] boosts, and/or those with [[trigger-happy]] (which lowers psycast warmup time). If a pawn has the Natural [[meditation focus]], they can use the [[anima tree]] in conjunction with neuroformers with no penalty. Because gathering many neuroformers can take a long time, you may want to combine the anima tree with neuroformers to raise psylink faster.
