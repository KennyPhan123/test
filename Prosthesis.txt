'''Prostheses''', also commonly referred to as '''prosthetics''', may refer to one of several different [[body parts|body part]] replacements unlocked by the [[Research#Prosthetics|Prosthetics]] research. These are:
* [[Prosthetic arm]]
* [[Prosthetic heart]]
* [[Prosthetic leg]]
* [[Cochlear implant]]

They are inferior to normal body parts, but provide a bridge between the worse-still simple replacements, such as [[peg leg]]s, and the superior-to-natural [[bionics]]. Not all body parts are represented in every tier.

Prostheses may also refer to all [[artificial body parts]] in general.
