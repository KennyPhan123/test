== String Format ==
[[Repair Energy Cost]] is written out ingame as:

<code>'''Base Value''' × 100</code>

Which is then styled as an integer.

=== Worked Examples ===
# '''0.333''' (Default Base Value):
## 0.333 × 100 = ''33.3''
## ''33.3'' to integer = '''33'''
# '''0.33''' (BaseMechanoid):
## 0.33 × 100 = ''33''
## ''33'' to integer = '''33'''
# '''0.2''' (MechCentipede and HeavyMechanoid):
## 0.2 × 100 = ''20''
## ''20'' to integer = '''20'''
# '''0.66''' (LightMechanoid):
## 0.66 × 100 = ''66''
## ''66'' to integer = '''66'''
# '''0.15''' (SuperHeavyMechanoid):
## 0.15 × 100 = ''150''
## ''150'' to integer = '''150'''
