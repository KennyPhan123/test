The '''pilot assistant''' is an [[artificial body part]] added by the [[Odyssey DLC]] that improves the implanted pawn's [[Piloting Ability|ability]] to pilot [[gravship]]s.

== Acquisition ==
{{Acquisition}}

== Summary == 
[[Piloting ability]] {{+|45%}}.

The pilot assistant also renders a pawn vulnerable to [[EMP]]. Taking EMP damage from any source will inflict [[brain shock]] on the pawn, capping their [[consciousness]] at {{Bad|10%}} and [[down]]ing them for between {{ticks|2500}} and {{ticks|3500}}.
