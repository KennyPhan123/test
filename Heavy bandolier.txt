The '''heavy bandolier''' is a piece of [[apparel]] added by the [[Biotech DLC]]. 

== Acquisition ==
{{Acquisition}}

== Summary ==
Offsets the [[Ranged Cooldown Multiplier]] of the wearer by {{---|20%}}, meaning that weapons cooldown in 80% of the time they otherwise would.<!-- I know its phrased weirdly but its a negative value that is added to a multiplier, confusing but important for Order of Operations. Keep this in mind when rewording - Harakoni --> This reduction is not affected by [[quality]], though the usual apparel stats are affected as normal.

The exact {{DPS}} gain depends on the weapon. Each weapon has an Aiming Time, Burst Time, and Cooldown Time. The heavy bandolier reduces cooldown only. Therefore, the longer a weapon's cooldown is relative to its aiming and burst time, the greater impact the bandolier has. For the same reason, reducing the pawn's [[Aiming Time]], such as with [[trigger-happy]] or [[shooting specialist]],{{IdeologyIcon}} gives the bandolier a proportionally greater effect.

== Analysis ==
Heavy bandoliers are an important piece of gear for all shooters. They provide almost no insulation or protection, but bandoliers offers a valuable ranged {{DPS}} boost, and they can be worn with a [[flak vest]] to provide a good level of protection. Its efficacy depends on the weapon selected and provides the highest boost for weapons with largest ratio of cooldown time/cycle time, such as [[revolver]]s, but any ranged weapon will benefit, as will [[utility]] items with a cooldown and [[psycast]]s.{{RoyaltyIcon}}

Bandoliers are worn in the Outer layer, taking the place of [[duster]]s, [[parka]]s, [[flak jacket]]s, and the like. This introduces a number of considerations. First, it precludes their use in [[biomes]] with temperature extremes that necessitate certain apparel. 

Second and more broadly relevant is the impact on protection. Bandoliers do not have good coverage nor do they provide significant protection, but they compete with dusters and flak jackets which do. The bulk of protection offered by typical [[flak vest]] and duster/flak jacket set up comes from the flak vest itself. Bandoliers can also be worn over [[flak vest]]s, meaning a colonist can have significant protection while wearing one. The supplementary protection offered by the outer layers typically only becomes relevant once either flak jackets or [[devilstrand]] or [[thrumbofur]] become available. A [[duster]] made out of [[cloth]] is unlikely to meaningfully protect from bullets, a cloth heavy bandolier will let your colonists kill the enemy faster. As such, before those protective options are available, heavy bandoliers are a clear option for ranged colonists on temperate biomes.

Once you have access to [[devilstrand]] dusters, [[flak jacket]]s, or equivalent, the argument for heavy bandoliers starts to weaken and it depends on your playstyle and choices. Damage causes [[pain]], which lowers pawn efficiency, and the loss of a pawn means more than just their loss of their combat effectiveness.

Regardless of game stage, bandoliers remain useful in situations where pawns don't get hit, such as [[kiting]]. 

Any effect that reduces the [[Aiming Time]] of the pawn also increases the relative impact of the heavy bandolier. A [[trigger-happy]] or [[shooting specialist]]{{IdeologyIcon}} pawn will see a greater relative increase in {{DPS}} than another pawn, and therefore have a larger incentive to retain the bandolier when others may have consider abandoning it.

The protection and insulation offered by the bandolier is almost negligible outside of high qualities and high tier materials. But the effect on cooldown is not affected by material or quality. As such, if only the {{DPS}} buff is required, the worst quality and cheapest material can be used to [[wealth management|minimize wealth]] and cost while still being fully functional.
