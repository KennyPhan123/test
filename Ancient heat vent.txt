'''Ancient heat vent''' are [[ruins]] added by the [[Odyssey DLC]].

The ancient heat vent will periodically vent large amounts of heat into the surrounding area. It will last for 12 days on average with an average of 4 days of downtime. While active the heat vent produces 200C a second, ramping up from 0 over its first 5 hours active per cycle.
