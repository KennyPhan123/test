'''Wildness''' is a [[stat]] of [[animals]]. It is used to determine the [[Minimum Handling Skill]] stat, and combines with the [[Tame Animal Chance]] and [[Train Animal Chance]] stats to determine the final tame/train chance. 

Additionally, animals need over 10.1% wildness in order to be able to lose the tamed status. [[Pen animal]]s are excluded, and will never become wild again (may still wander off map if left outside a pen).

Finally, the degradation interval of [[Animal training|training]]/[[Taming|tamed status]] varies between {{ticks/gametime|720000}} and {{ticks/gametime|360000}}, inversely related to the '''wildness''' stat. [[Dryads]] and pen animals are exceptions, and will never lose training nor tame status due to decay.

Wildness can be lowered by 25% with a [[sentience catalyst]]{{OdysseyIcon}}.

Do note that some events are also related with this stat. For instance, the [[Events#Animals join|Farm Animals Wander In]] incident can only spawn animals with at most 35% wildness.
