The '''bioferrite shaper''' is a building added by the [[Anomaly DLC]] that allows for the production of a variety of items from that DLC.

== Acquisition ==
{{Acquisition}}

== Summary ==
{{Stub|section=1|reason=Work type, any special bills, verify content etc.}}
Pawns will do Smithing work at a bioferrite shaper. If a job is interrupted for any reason, the crafter will drop the piece, usually on the workbench, and it will be in an unfinished state until the crafter finishes it. A crafter cannot work on another crafter's piece. The piece will indicate the name of the crafter and how much work is remaining. The stockpile filter includes an entry for unfinished items which allows the player to control where they are stored. 

Up to two nearby [[tool cabinet]]s can be connected to a bioferrite shaper to increase work speed.
