The '''electric stove''' is a [[production]] bench used for preparing [[meals]] and [[psychite tea]]. It is the [[power]]ed equivalent to the [[fueled stove]].

== Analysis ==
Some sort of meal production is practically essential for standard pawns, due to the [[mood]] penalty and [[food poisoning]] chance of raw food, as well as the fact that cooking food increases nutritional efficiency. Unlike [[campfire]]s and [[fueled stove]]s, electric stoves do not require constant [[wood]]. Even if using a [[wood-fired generator]] for power, an electric stove will typically be more efficient overall, since the generator produces an extra 650 W that can be used for any other appliance.

Any colonist that prepares a meal has a chance to contaminate it and cause food poisoning. That chance is determined by the colonist's stat for [[Food Poison Chance]], which is determined by their cooking skill level. A level 0 cook has a 5% chance. A level 9 cook and above has a 0.1% chance, while extremely rare, it can still happen. A player would likely want to use the best cooker available to minimize food poisoning chances.

Vegetarian simple meals can be forced by removing all meat options from the simple meal bill. Vegetarian fine/lavish meals are their own separate bill, which require more resources than their regular counterparts.

=== Compared to nutrient paste ===
[[Nutrient paste dispenser]]s require no cook, have better nutrition efficiency, and have zero food posioning chance, but [[nutrient paste meal]]s provide {{--|4}} mood to standard colonists. Therefore, using nutrient paste is recommended for colonies with no good cook, or at the start of the game when both food and time are of short supply. They can also help control the spread of [[metalhorror]]s,{{AnomalyIcon}} which can spread through cooked meals.

Nutrient paste dispensers will also prevent pawns from grabbing meals and eating away from a dining room. This will ensure that colonists get the mood boost from an impressive dining room and avoid eating without a [[table]], at the cost of work efficiency when traveling far away.

The [[Eating nutrient paste|Eating nutrient paste: Don't mind]] [[precept]]{{IdeologyIcon}} will prevent the mood buffs from eating nutrient paste, although pawns will still lose out on the mood buff from eating [[fine meal]]s or [[lavish meal]]s.
