'''[[Lung]] rot''' is a long-lasting [[disease]] that can be [[Death|fatal]] if left [[#Treatment|untreated]].__TOC__

==Overview==
Lung rot appears after prolonged exposure to [[rot stink]], and affects both lungs. It can't be acquired from an [[event]].

The higher the "rot stink" hediff severity, the more likely the disease is. At 0.5 severity, there is an {{MTB}} of 8 days at 0.5 severity to get this disease. This goes up to a {{MTB}} of 0.5 days at 1.0 severity.{{Check Tag|Mechanics?|How does this scale? Linear or a curve?}}

Lung rot will kill if either lung reaches maximum lung rot severity. Each afflicted lung must be treated separately. As long as every treatment is of 30% [[tend quality]] or higher, the disease will not progress. After 3-4 treatments over 6-8 days the disease will disappear.

=== Progression ===
*Severity increases by 0.300 per day.
*Treatment slows progression by a maximum of 1.000 per day.
**This means the disease will regress by 0.700 per day at 100% tend quality.
**Progression will pause at 30% tend quality or higher.
**Note that good treatment will not cause the disease to go away on its own.

The disease resolves after 4-8 days, independent of the patient's immune functioning. <!--The XML for lung rot specifies disappearsAfterTicks, same as a mechanite disease-->

=== Prevention ===
Prevent colonists from inhaling rot stink [[gas]], which is done by avoiding rotten [[corpse]]s; a rot stink severity of at least 0.5 is required to contact lung rot. Try to avoid being in melee with [[scaria]]-infested [[animal]]s, as they will have a habit of immediately rotting on death. Otherwise, haul, [[grave|bury]], [[fire|burn]], or [[electric crematorium|cremate]] corpses in a timely manner, or quarantine them until they finish rotting.

[[Face mask]]s, [[gas mask]]s,{{BiotechIcon}} and other sources of [[Toxic Environment Resistance]] all reduce the severity gain of rot stink exposure.

[[Detoxifier lung]]s{{BiotechIcon}} and [[fleshmass lung]]s{{AnomalyIcon}} are immune to lung rot infections. Each replaced lung also increases Toxic Environment Resistance, increasing a colonist's resistance to acquiring lung rot even with only one replaced lung.

The [[perfect immunity]] gene{{BiotechIcon}} will prevent the acquisition of this disease, but will not cure pre-existing conditions.

=== Treatment ===
Treatment can be done using [[Medicine (disambiguation)|medicine]] which suppresses the disease, preventing it from reaching fatal stages. Pawns do not need to [[rest]], as no [[Immunity Gain Speed|immunity]] is gained.

As long as it is tended by a decent doctor, lung rot isn't particularly threatening to a pawn's life. The real downside is the time and medicine consumed for treatment, especially if many pawns get lung rot at the same time. It is possible to reach >30% [[tend quality]] even without medicine, if you have a great doctor and hospital setup.
