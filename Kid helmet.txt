The '''kid helmet''' is a headgear [[apparel]] added by the [[Biotech DLC]]. 

== Acquisition ==
{{Acquisition}}

== Summary ==
The kid helmet is a [[Children|child]]-sized version of the regular [[simple helmet]], meaning it can only be worn by pawns under 13.

== Analysis ==
Kid helmets are one of the few head [[armor]] options available for children, and [[steel]] kid helmets are fairly cheap to make, so they are a good choice for preventing nasty [[Death|booboos]]. While a helmet is unlikely to actually save a child from dying, due to their reduced body part HP, any protection is better than none. The kid helmet is not the most protective option if all DLC is installed, but it's cost-effective.

Alternatives for combat include:
* A [[devilstrand]] [[ceremonial hood]]{{AnomalyIcon}} provides better Sharp and Heat protection than a [[steel]] kid helmet while covering the entire head. It provides less Blunt protection, but even the kid helmet's blunt armor is barely relevant. It is a relatively cheap upgrade provided Anomaly content is active.
* A [[vacsuit helmet]]{{OdysseyIcon}} provides better combat protection than a steel kid helmet. It provides worse protection than a [[plasteel]] kid helmet, but is cheaper. The vacsuit helmet also provides Toxic Environmental resistance and [[Vacuum Resistance]],{{OdysseyIcon}} and like the ceremonial hood, covers the entire head.
* A [[hyperweave]] [[ceremonial hood]]{{AnomalyIcon}} is the best child helmet in the game, beating even a plasteel kid helmet, but hyperweave is prohibitively expensive.

In addition, if temperature is a concern, an insulating armor like a [[cowboy hat]] or [[tuque]] may be a better choice.
