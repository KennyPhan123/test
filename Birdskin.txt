== Analysis ==
Birdskin is one of the least protective and least insulative [[textiles]] in the game. It also has a fairly low [[Property:Beauty Factor|beauty factor]]. Thus it should only be used when there is no alternative or where its stats have little effect on the utility of the resulting item, such as in clothing production [[quests]] or [[armchair]]s whose [[beauty]] rarely significantly affects a [[room stats|room's quality]].
