'''Archean seeds''' can be planted in many types of terrain to grow [[archean tree]]s.

== Acquisition ==
Archean seeds can rarely be acquired from [[sealed crate]]s and [[ancient safe]]s. Regular sealed crates have 1.96% chance to drop 1-2 seeds, while crates that appear next to an archean tree on asteroids will always drop them.

== Summary ==
Archean seeds can be used in order to grow archean trees. Archean seeds can be planted in [[broken asphalt]], [[packed dirt]], [[soil]], [[lichen-covered soil]], [[marshy soil]], [[rich soil]], [[stony soil]], [[sand]], [[soft sand]], [[rough stone]], and [[rough-hewn stone]]. Archean trees transform nearby terrain that its seeds could be planted on into rich soil. The tree will also produce oxygen if enclosed in a pressurized room. Archean seeds can be planted in any level of [[pollution]],{{BiotechIcon}} but will not remove pollution.

== Analysis ==
Archean seeds grow into archean trees, which can be used to provide oxygen in the place of [[oxygen pump]]s and turn other terrain it can be planted on into rich soil. See [[archean tree]] for more specific analysis.
