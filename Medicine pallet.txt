The '''Medicine pallet''' is a structure added by the [[Odyssey DLC]]. Its contents are ejected when opened.

== Occurrence ==
Medicine pallets are generated in a variety of map features and Odyssey quests. They are found in ancient warehouses, infested settlements, ruins, frozen ruins, and ancient stockpiles.

Medicine pallets are usually protected by either insects or ancient defence systems. They always spawn in a [[hive]] room in ruins and stockpiles. There is also a small chance for a hive to be present in the room in infested settlements. In ancient warehouses, they are defended by a [[hunter drone]] and an [[ancient defender turret]]. In frozen ruins the room is undefended.
