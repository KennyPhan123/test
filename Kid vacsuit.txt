The '''kid vacsuit''' is an item of [[apparel]] added by the [[Odyssey DLC]] and only present with the [[Biotech DLC]] enabled. It forms a set with the [[vacsuit helmet]].

== Acquisition == 
{{Acquisition}}

== Summary ==
The kid vacsuit is a [[Children|child]]-sized version of the regular [[vacsuit]], meaning it can only be worn by pawns under 13.

A kid vacsuit increases the [[vacuum resistance]] of its wearer by {{+|{{%|{{P|Vacuum Resistance Offset}}}}}}. [[Quality]] does not affect this resistance.

{{Apparel Move Speed Note|1.25}}

Also note that [[children]] have a reduced move speed based on their age.{{Check Tag|Stacking?|confirm how this stacks with the penalty (it should make the quantitative penalty smaller but verify and add}}

== Analysis ==
The [[vacsuit helmet]] and kid vacsuit set provides {{+|100%}} Vacuum Resistance, enabling children to survive in [[orbit]]. These are the only kid apparel to provide Vacuum Resistance, as kids don't have access to [[power armor]]. Without a [[vacskin gland]]{{RoyaltyIcon}} or a vacuum-related [[gene]],{{BiotechIcon}} they are a practical necessity for children in space. 

In addition, kid vacsuits are the highest protection body armor available to kids, even beating [[thrumbomane]] [[kid parka]]s, so they have some merit while on the planet. However, since children have lower HP and should be avoiding combat anyway, upgrading their combat armor is a very low priority. It also provides a hefty movement penalty. Still, once the resource cost is no longer an issue, kid vacsuits can provide extra safety to a child. The movement speed penalty isn't as much of an issue as children are limited in their work, and they should still comfortably hit the max growth tier despite the slowdown.

When a child grows into an adult, the kid vacsuit is removed but the vacsuit helmet remains. Therefore, while such pawns won't die instantly, they are still in heavy danger if in a vacuum.
