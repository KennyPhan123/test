==Summary==
Foraged Food Amount is a colonist stat that determines the amount of nutrition that a pawn generate while on the world map. This stat is affected by 7 factors; the biome, growing season, the colonist's manipulation, sight, plants skill, whether the caravan is moving and whether the colony is a tribe vs outlander.

==Factors==

* [[Skills#Plants|Plants]][[Skill::Plants| ]]: [[Skill Base Factor::0.0|0]] plus [[Skill Bonus Factor::0.09|0.09]] nutrition per skill level.
* [[Manipulation]]: [[Manipulation Importance::0.5|50%]] importance, [[Manipulation Limit::1|100%]] max.
* [[Sight]]: [[Sight Importance::0.9|90%]] importance, [[Sight Limit::-|No]] max.
* Pawns belonging to a [[New Tribe]] faction forage {{Good|x170%}} as much nutrition as other pawns.
* Below is the foraged food amount multipliers for each biome.
** Grassland = 1x
** Glowforest = 1x
** Temporate Forest = 1x
** Tropical Rainforest = 1x
** Boreal Forest = 0.75x
** Temperate Swamp = 0.75x
** Tropical Swamp = 0.75x
** Arid Shrubland = 0.5x
** Lava Field = 0.5x
** Tundra = 0.5x
** Cold Bog = 0.5x
** Scarlands = 0.25x
** Desert = 0.25x
** Glacial Plains = 0x
** Extreme Desert = 0x
** Ice Sheet = 0x
** Sea Ice = 0x

==Trivia==
* The amount of pollution on a tile has no effect on foraged food amount
* A pawn in a tribal-start-colony forages more food than it consumes while stationary on a tile of a 100% forageable biome during growing season from Plants 8 at 100% Manipulation & Sight.
* Slaves in a tribal colony can be safely added to a foraging caravan without fearing rebellion, as long as they forage more food than they consume.
