== Analysis ==
Fabrication is often a highly sought-after technology, as it allows the player to create the ever-useful [[component]]s and [[advanced component]]s without relying on trade limits. It is recommended to plan ahead and gather the 2 advanced components required to build the fabrication bench before you research Fabrication. If you need advanced components, then [[trade]] with exotic goods traders, [[faction base]]s, and [[orbital trader]]s is the most consistent. [[Quest]]s can offer them from time to time.

In the late game, demand for advanced components can be very high, especially if you are constructing the [[ship]]. They take a long time to make, so it is recommended to have multiple fabrication benches with multiple crafters for their production. [[Deep drill]]s + [[ground-penetrating scanner]] or the [[long-range mineral scanner]] can help your colony get the minerals needed on its own. Fabrication itself can be used in many valuable projects, like [[charge rifle]]s, [[power armor]], and [[bionic]]s.

=== Skill training ===
With a great demand for components in most colonies, component assembly is a great way to train colonists' crafting [[skills|skill]], though both types of component have a minimum crafting requirement. Good smiths provide quality [[weapon]]s and [[armor]] for your colony and are valuable to any colony.
