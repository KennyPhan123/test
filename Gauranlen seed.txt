'''Gauranlen seed''' can be planted to become a [[gauranlen tree]].

== Acquisition ==
Gauranlen seeds are obtained by harvesting a [[gauranlen pod]]. These pods appear once every 60 days for regular ideoligions, once every 30 days for those with the [[Tree connection]] meme, and can also be obtained as a [[ritual]] reward if tree connection is selected.

Each pod gives a yield of 1 seed. However, this is affected by [[Plant Harvest Yield]], meaning you have a chance to get 2 seeds at high Plants skill, or 0 seeds at low skill.

== Summary ==
Can be planted to grow a [[gauranlen tree]]. This tree can be connected to a pawn to produce [[dryad]]s, assuming it is pruned.
