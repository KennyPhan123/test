The '''jade knife''' is a [[knife]] that's made out of [[jade]]. This weapon is unique because it can only be obtained via the [[Scenario system#Lost Tribe|Lost tribe]] scenario; it cannot be crafted nor purchased.

Owing to jade's properties as a material that favors blunt damage over sharp, the jade knife's handle is actually the strongest attack tool that it has, though its overall damage output is still lackluster. The jade knife performs similarly to the [[wood]]en [[club]] in combat despite its minor DPS deficit though due to how cutting attacks work.
