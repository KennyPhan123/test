The '''school desk''' is a [[furniture]] item added by the [[Biotech DLC]]

== Acquisition ==
{{Acquisition}}

== Summary ==
{{Stub|section=1|reason=How much skill XP from learning? Can multiple children use the same desk?}}
School desks allow children to do the [[Children#Learning Tasks|''Lesson Learning'']] task. Children sit behind the desk and constantly gain Learning. Adults can sit beside the children to increase skill XP in a random, valid skill. An awake adult capable of teaching is required for children start learning, even when that adult doesn't directly teach the child.

Desks can connect to up to 3 nearby [[blackboard]]s. Each blackboard increases Learning rate by 20% per blackboard, for a total of +60% Learning rate. When being taught by Adults, children's skill XP gain rate is also modified by the desk's Learning rate, with 3 blackboards making them learn at 160% speed. Meanwhile Adult's social XP gain rate remains unchanged. Multiple desks can connect to the same blackboard. Which directions the desk or blackboards are facing has no impact.

== Analysis ==
A no-brainer to build when raising any child, as it is inexpensive and gives the child access to more learning types. Lessons can provide a significant amount of skill XP, so long as an adult is available for childcare. Even without an adult, a desk will increase the rate of learning. Adults will also gain social XP by teaching children.
