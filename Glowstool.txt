A small brown mushroom typically found in caves and {{Habitats}}. It smells like an old, dirty rag pulled from the stomach of a rotting muffalo - but it is edible. Dies when exposed to light.

The term "edible" varies depending on the variation of RimWorld that you are playing. Without the [[Ideology (DLC)|Ideology DLC]], fungus is the same as any other vegetable. However, ''with'' that DLC, fungus becomes ''either'' a loved ''or'' hated food substance. See [[raw fungus]] and the [[Tunneler (meme)|Tunneler meme]] for more information.

A single glowstool{{Check Tag|Of What Age?|Does it need to be mature to provide the full light?}} provides a dim 28% [[light]] in orthogonally adjacent squares and 24% in diagonally adjacent, dropping into the teens at 2-3 squares and single-digits of light at 4 squares, none of which are enough to make any significant difference for your colonists. However, with ''multiple'' glowstools whose illumination (within 2 tiles) overlaps, those areas immediately jump to 50% light, which is enough to count as "lit" in those few tiles; often enough for a workbench or [[Recreation#Relaxing socially|gather spot]].

Once harvested, their glow disappears, so they are not a "movable" source of light.
