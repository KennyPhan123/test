A '''metalblood heart''' is an [[artificial body part]] added by the [[Anomaly DLC]] that acts as a replacement for a [[ghoul]]'s heart. It gives a ghoul the ability to become more resistant to damage for a short period of time at the cost of [[move speed]] and a vulnerability to fire damage.

== Acquisition ==
{{Acquisition}}

It can sometimes spawn on hostile [[ghoul]]s,{{Check Tag|Starter colonist?|Can they spawn on ghoul starter colonists?}} however they cannot be harvested from them.

== Summary == 
[[File:Metalblood injection gizmo.png|thumb|left|Metalblood injection gizmo]]

{{Quote|Use the metalblood heart to release metalblood serum into the bloodstream, reducing incoming damage for a short period of time.|Metalblood injection description}}

The metalblood heart is a [[ghoul]] exclusive body part. It has a part efficiency of 100%. This fully replaces the functionality of a normal heart but provides no statistical enhancement - when coupled with the heart body part having a [[Blood Pumping]] importance of 100%, it results in a directly proportional increase in blood pumping when replacing a heart with impaired efficiency. Blood pumping, in turn, affects [[Moving]]. Moving, in turn, affects the following [[stats]]: {{#ask: [[Moving Importance::+]]}}. For further details, see those pages.

Pawns with the heart installed have access to the "Metalblood injection" ability. The injection must be manually initiated by the player through use of a gizmo available while the ghoul is selected. Colonist ghouls will never independently use the injection. NPC ghouls will use the ability when engaged in combat.

The injection lasts for {{Ticks|2400}} and provides the same "Metalblood" effect as [[metalblood serum]], namely:
* {{Good|x50%}} [[Incoming Damage Multiplier]], stacking multiplicatively with other sources such as the [[Tough]] trait.
* {{Bad|x400%}} [[Flame]] damage
* {{Bad|x400%}} [[Burn]] damage
The injection has a cooldown of {{Ticks/gametime|15000}}. 
Metalheart metalbood injection does not stack with other sources of the metalblood hediff. If the ghoul has already taken metalblood serum, the timer will be reset to the {{Ticks|2400}} duration.

The metalblood heart inflicts a {{--|0.20 {{CS}}}} penalty to [[move speed]] - this applies at all times, not just while the metalblood injection ability is active.

=== Installation ===
{{Stub|section=1|reason=Stats for removal}}
The metalblood heart can only be installed in [[ghoul]]s. Note that despite the description noting [[pain]] as the reason it cannot be installed in other pawns, methods of pain nullification such as [[painstopper]]s do not allow the hearts installation into other pawns.

Installing the part requires {{Ticks|2500}} of work, and a [[Skills#Medical|Medical skill]] of 4. Unlike most operations, this operation cannot fail and the pawn is not [[Anesthetic|anesthetised]]. Like other ghoul body parts, the replaced basic ghoul heart is lost.

Removing the part requires ? of work, 1x [[herbal medicine]] or better, and has no [[Skills#Medical|Medical skill]] requirements. This will kill the ghoul. This operation can fail.{{Check Tag|Verify}}

== Analysis ==
Before [[metalblood serum]] is available, the metalblood heart is better than the [[adrenal heart]], if you don't need the move speed bonus.

When [[metalblood serum]] is available, another heart upgrade should be used instead, because the effect of the metalblood heart doesn't stack with that of the serum. This makes the metalblood heart only necessary if you still don't have a steady supply of [[bioferrite]] or [[twisted meat]] to make the [[metalblood serum]].

The alternative method to get metalblood, a dose of metalblood serum costs {{Icon Small|Bioferrite||20}} [[bioferrite]], {{Icon Small|Twisted meat||10}} [[twisted meat]], and lasts 5 days, for an average of {{Icon Small|Bioferrite||4}} bioferrite and {{Icon Small|Twisted meat||2}} twisted meat per day. This can be improved by only taking the serum right before combat.
