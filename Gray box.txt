'''Gray boxes''' are loot containers added by the [[Anomaly DLC]]. They are only found within the [[Labyrinth]] and eject its contents nearby when activated.

== Summary ==
Gray boxes are found exclusively within the Labyrinth housed within a [[Warped obelisk]]. Two are guaranteed to spawn within the final room of the maze, with other rooms containing between 1-16 opened or unopened boxes. Gray boxes cannot be destroyed.

{{Loot Container Note}}

== Loot ==
There are two types of gray boxes with different loot pools, which spawn in different conditions:
* Obelisk room: always contains 2 high value boxes.
* Room containing a single gray box: always high value.
* Room containing a single unopened gray box and up to 5 empty ones: always high value.
* Room containing multiple unopened gray boxes: always contains exactly two high value boxes. The rest are either empty or low value.
