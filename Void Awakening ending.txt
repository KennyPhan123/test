'''Void Awakening''' is an [[endings|ending]] available as part of the Anomaly DLC. Your colonists confront the darkness within the [[void monolith]].

This ending is only available if you have '''NOT''' selected the "Ambient horror" mode of Anomaly in your storyteller settings.

{{TOCright}}

== Briefing ==
'''Requirements:'''
* Unlock [[void monolith]] level 3. This requires the player to attune to the monolith (level 1), encounter 7 [[Entities#Basic|basic entities]] (level 2), and then 12 [[Entities#Advanced|advanced entities]] (level 3). 
* Interact with the monolith to awaken it, beginning the endgame event.
* The monolith will spawn two [[void structure]]s that you must activate. Each structure is guarded by [[metalhorror]]s, and a wave of entities will attack as each structure is activated.
* After a few days, the monolith will cause [[unnatural darkness]] to descend, with its usual damage mechanics. Three more structures will spawn that you must activate. The fifth and final wave of entities is given a 6 hour delay.
* After all five structures are activated, investigate the monolith to enter the [[metal hell]]. Activate the [[void node]] with a chosen colonist to select your reward.

'''Failure conditions:'''
* Once the monolith is awakened, the ending can't be canceled on the current map. At least one colonist must survive.
* You can abandon the map if you are able to leave on a [[caravan]], [[shuttle]], or gravship.{{OdysseyIcon}} You can awaken the monolith on a different map to try again.

== The Monolith ==
{{Main|Monolith|Entity}}

Your map begins with a [[monolith]] structure which is initially dormant. If you have moved to another map or added the Anomaly DLC after starting the game, a "Strange Signal" quest will appear which optionally spawns a monolith on your map.

A colonist approaching the monolith will cause an ominous letter notification. Having a colonist attune to the monolith begins the Anomaly quest in full. You will gain an [[entity]] codex, and the Anomaly [[research]] tree will unlock. If you chose the [[Scenario_system#The_Anomaly|Anomaly starting scenario]], the monolith will be dormant for several days after landing, but then twist and become active ''automatically'' without approaching it.

In order to advance, you must encounter and defeat [[entities]] in the codex. Entities will also reveal new research options, and can be captured and studied. The [[Work|Work tab]] calls this job "Dark Study". After researching the [[Void provocation]] ritual, you can optionally summon an entity event on your schedule rather than waiting for the storyteller to do it. The ritual prioritizes sending entities you have not encountered.

Encountering '''7 basic entities''' (out of 8) allows the monolith to be attuned, then after encountering '''12 advanced entities''' (out of 17), the monolith becomes attunable again. Attuning this last time will awaken the monolith and begin the Anomaly quest endgame.

== Monolith endgame ==
After awakening the monolith, the '''monolith endgame''' starts. This is split into two stages, which can be advanced through by activating the [[void structure]]s that spawn.

The number of entities spawned is determined by [[raid points]]. Other [[major threat]]s cannot occur during the Anomaly endgame event. There will not be standard raids, infestation, or other major threats - not even [[quest]]s or the [[ship launch]] can start major threats.

===Stage 1===
After activating the monolith, the precursor to [[unnatural darkness]] will occur, setting the sun level to 0%, but colonists will not take damage from being in darkness.

Two [[void structure]]s will land on the map and [[metalhorror]]s will appear with them.

A [[fleshbeast attack]] event will occur.

Wandering singular [[noctol]]s and [[gorehulk]]s will appear from the map's edge in very low numbers. If these wandering entities are killed another will wander in from the map's edge. The map will constantly have low numbers of singular wandering entities.

Activating the two [[void structure]]s is required to progress to the next stage. After activating either void structure, an attack of assorted entities will appear from the map's edge. The attacks may consist of [[shambler]]s, [[gorehulk]]s, or [[devourer]]s.

===Stage 2===
After activating the second void structure, there will be a 2 day delay until the beginning of the second stage. A notification of Dimming Sky will be given via a yellow letter. 

Singular noctol and gorehulk wanderers will still continuously patrol about the map and new entities will be spawned if any are killed.

After the two day wait, three [[void structure]]s will appear on the map and be accompanied by [[metalhorror]]s. The sky will dim into full [[unnatural darkness]]. Any colonist in a dark area will be damaged.

Activating any of the three void structures will cause entity attacks from the map's edge. These entity attacks can consist of [[shambler]]s, [[gorehulk]]s, [[devourer]]s, [[sightstealer]]s, or [[noctol]]s. 

Once all 3 void structures are activated the monolith will begin to twist. After 4 hours, the "twisting" of the monolith will intensify, metalhorrors will appear around the monolith, and the monolith will become a portal to the [[metal hell]]. Activating the monolith one last time will send one colonist to the metal hell and allow the player to end the event by interacting with the [[void node]] inside.

== Preparations and strategy ==
Before attuning for the third time, you should stockpile food, weapons, drugs, and medicine for a week-long colony siege, at minimum. The entire map will be dark for the duration of the event, so outdoor farming will not be easy. A [[gravship]]{{OdysseyIcon}} should ideally be parked in a different tile so that it does not get damaged or contribute towards [[raid points]] during the event.

[[Metalblood serum]] is effective since [[fire]] is not a factor unless the player uses it. Crowd-control weapons like [[minigun]]s and [[psycast]]s{{RoyaltyIcon}} are good due to the large swarms of enemies; [[EMP]] is also effective against metalhorrors. [[Friendly mechanoids]]{{BiotechIcon}} can help due to their immunity to unnatural darknes

You technically have unlimited time during this event (if you can survive indoors), so retreat back to your base after destroying each structure to fight off enemies, heal, and regroup as needed. Use tools like [[flood light]]s, [[Disruptor flare pack]]s, and the [[solar pinhole]] [[psycast]]{{RoyaltyIcon}} to traverse the darkness easier and weaken any light-sensitive entities.

After you activate the last structure (for a total of 5), the monolith will become a portal to the [[metal hell]] that multiple colonists can enter, but only '''one''' can interact with the Void Node. '''The specific colonist you select is important, see below.'''

== Entering the Metal Hell ==
{{Quote|(name) has activated the monolith and been ripped from our spacetime into another place. (name) finds himself/herself in a room of jagged metallic spikes. Every surface is slick with dark fluid and the air vibrates with power. This metal hell is a connecting channel to an inhuman hyperintelligence.}}

After activating the void Monolith, the colonist will be teleported to the '''metal hell''', where the [[void node]] is located. ''Keep in mind that all other colonists are still subject to attacks on the outside map.''

Move the colonist over to the center and interact the void node, you will be prompted with two options to conclude the quest.

{{Quote|A writhing sphere of crackling psychic energy rises before (name). Its surface swells and twists, warping the space around it. (name) feels it inside his/her mind, deeper than any normal human sense. It defeats logic and sets him/her adrift in the void. He/She stands on the shoreline of an infinite black ocean of power, and it beckons him/her in. (name) shakes himself/herself back to reality. Nearby archotech devices support the sphere along fragile connections. '''(name) could disrupt them''', closing this link between worlds and putting an end to the terrifying phenomena that have plagued your home. Or, '''He/She could step into the sphere and merge with the void'''. It's unknown what lies beyond, or what dark powers could be gained from such an inhuman act.}}

When an option is chosen, these immediately happens:
* The unnatural darkness on your map lifts.
* All [[void structure]]s, void metal mass, void metal floors on your map are removed.
* All living [[entities]] on your map outside containment are instantly killed via the destruction of their brain part.
** This also instantly kills [[fleshmass nucleus]] and [[nociosphere]], dropping a large amount of materials.
** Void monolith, [[harbinger tree]], [[death pall]], [[pit gate]], [[fleshmass heart]], [[blood rain]], [[obelisk]]s, inactive [[unnatural corpse]], [[golden cube]] are not affected.
* The metal hell starts collapsing, leading to the chosen ending in few seconds.

There is no way to leave the metal hell without choosing an ending option.

=== Disrupt the link ===
{{Quote|The psychic flows destabilize and the archotech machinery begins to scream. The swirling void flickers. (name) feels it spilling beyond its limits and collapsing into itself, all at once. The metallic cavern begins to twist, reshaping like the chamber of a massive heart. Before (name) has a chance to scream, he/she blinks out of existence. '''(name) has rejected the void.''' His/Her story continues...|Endgame text after disrupt the link.}}

Once you confirm disrupt the link, you hear the decompression explosion sounds as the screen fades to white. The credits roll as the ending theme song plays.

After the credits screen display, the following things will occur:
* All factions that are not permanently hostile gain a one-time bonus of {{+|50}} goodwill.
* The colonist that disrupted the link gains a {{+|14}} '''Closed the Void''' moodlet for 60 days.
* All other colonists gain a {{+|8}} '''Void Closed''' moodlet for 30 days.
* The void monolith is named "collapsed monolith" and becomes dormant, random Anomaly events return to the same level as before it was awoken.
** The monolith can still be studied for advanced Anomaly research points.
* Spawn 10 [[shard]]s around the collapsed monolith.
* Spawn 3 [[monolith fragment]]s around the collapsed monolith.
** They provide additional sources of advanced Anomaly research points, which is useful since most Anomaly events are disabled. {{Check Tag|Detail needed|Confirm that Psychic Rituals function or do not function after disabling anomaly events.}}
* All colonists in the metal hell returns to your colony at the monolith site after a 5 to 10 seconds delay; items dropped in the metal hell are lost.

== Conclusion ==

The game continues normally after completing the monolith quest, allowing you to optionally pursue another ending.
