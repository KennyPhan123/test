'''Void metal''' is an unconstructable [[floor]] type added by [[Anomaly (DLC)]].

==Summary==
Void metal is an unobtainable [[floor]] found primarily in the [[metal hell]]. It can also be found surrounding the exit obelisk in the [[Labyrinth]], as well as [[void structure]]s spawned by the [[void monolith]]'s final phase.

Void metal flooring has a supreme [[cleanliness]] of +1, reducing the risk of contracting [[infection]]s while in metal hell.
