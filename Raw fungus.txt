== Summary ==
As a [[raw food]], raw fungus can be be eaten raw, with a flat {{Bad|2%}} chance of giving [[food poisoning]], or cooked into a [[meal]] with a food poisoning chance dependent on the skill of the cook and the [[cleanliness]] of the kitchen. [[Animals]] are immune to food poisoning from raw food but not from meals. When used in food recipes that require specific types of [[nutrition]], such as [[fine meal]]s, raw fungus is classified as a vegetable.

As with most raw food, eating raw fungus directly results in a {{Thought|desc=I had to eat raw food. We should be cooking that kind of food before eating it. We're not animals.|label=ate raw food|stack=1|value=-7|duration=1}}. This mood penalty is negated if the pawn has the [[ascetic]] trait or the [[Robust digestion]] {{BiotechIcon}} gene.

With the [[Ideology DLC]] enabled, all [[ideoligion]]s inflict additional moodlets when eating raw fungus or meals made from it depending on which [[Fungus (precept)|Fungus precept]] they have. Ideoligions with the [[Tunneler (meme)|Tunneler]] meme have [[Fungus (precept)|Fungus: Preferred]] and receive a {{Thought|desc=I ate some fungus and it was just what I like!|label=ate fungus|value=+3|stack=1|duration=1}} after eating raw fungus and a {{Thought|desc=I ate a meal with fungus. Is there any dish it doesn't complement?|label=ate cooked fungus|value=+3|stack=1|duration=1}} after eating it cooked into a meal. All other ideoligions have the [[Fungus (precept)|Fungus: Despised]] and receive a {{Thought|desc=That fungus smelled like damp rags and tasted no better.|label=ate fungus raw|value=-6|stack=1|duration=1}} after eating raw fungus and a {{Thought|desc=That whole meal smelled like a dead rat left in a stagnant pool.|label=ate fungus cooked|value=-3|stack=1|duration=1}} after eating it cooked into a meal. Note that in all cases, these mood bonuses and malus stack with any applicable mood effects from the form the food is eaten in.

== Analysis == 
In RimWorld Core, it cannot be farmed, making it at best a random, situational and strictly supplemental source of vegetables. 

With the [[Ideology DLC]], it can be be farmed, though all non-Tunnelers get a -3 mood debuff. Still, [[nutrifungus]] has the unique property of growing in darkness. This makes it possible to grow crops indoors (e.g. during the winter) without the need of a power-hungry [[sun lamp]]. Can also be grown if your colony prefers darkness ([[Combat in darkness|Combat in darkness: Preferred]] precept) or if a [[sun blocker]] is deployed. Note that, without a way of mitigating darkness penalties, colonists will work 80% as fast in the dark.

With the [[Odyssey DLC]], large numbers of [[Agarilux]] occur naturally in the [[glowforest]] [[biome]], making foraged raw fungus a possible main source of nutrition.
