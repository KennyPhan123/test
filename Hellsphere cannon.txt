The '''hellsphere cannon''' is an [[mechanoid]]-only ranged [[weapon]] added by the [[Biotech DLC]]. It is the main armament of the [[diabolus]]. 

This weapon cannot be independently obtained in normal play. It is provided here for comparisons sake.

== Acquisition ==
Hellsphere cannons spawn on all [[diabolus|diaboli]], including those gestated by a colony. However, it is not possible to obtain it directly in normal play. Pawns carrying it will not drop it on [[death]], [[downing]], or complete loss of [[manipulation]] like other weapons. It can be spawned in by the [[Development mode#Debug actions menu|Debug actions menu]] but, like all [[unobtainable items|unobtainable weapons]], will disappear after being dropped.

== Summary ==
The hellsphere cannon fires a projectile that explodes in a radius of 4.9.  The explosion has phenomenal power; it has a base damage of 800 [[Vaporize]] damage. For reference, an antigrain warhead has base damage of 550. Armor calculation is based on the heat armor stat.

The hellsphere cannon must target a tile on the ground, and cannot target a pawn directly like most other weapons. This means it can be avoided if you move quickly. Failure to do so results in vaporization.
