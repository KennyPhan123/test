'''Gear''' includes items that are worn, equipped, or carried. These include:
*[[Weapon]]s
*[[Utility]] items
*[[Apparel]] - [[clothing]] and [[armor]]
*Inventory items, such as [[drug]]s, [[medicine]], and [[meal]]s. Does not include [[haul]]ed items, but items packed by a [[caravan]] do count.
From the gear tab, you can drop items or use food. Dropping equipped items (weapons, utility, apparel) is not an instant process. Equipping apparel also has a delay. Multiple drops can be queued while holding {{Key|shift}} and clicking.

The gear tab also lists 2 stats:
*Mass carried.
*Comfortable temperature range, based on apparel worn
