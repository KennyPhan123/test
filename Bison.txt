'''Bison''', called '''bison calves''' as babies, are large, grazing [[herbivore]]s that provide [[bison wool|wool]] and act as [[pack animal]]s.

== Aquisition ==
Bison can be found in {{Habitats}}. They can either be tamed by a [[Work#Handle|Handler]] or self-tame in a random [[events|event]].

Bison can be bought and sold in other [[Trade#Faction base|faction bases]] and from [[Trade#Bulk goods trader 2|bulk goods traders]], [[Trade#Exotic goods trader 2|exotic goods traders]]. Bison purchased from traders will be already tamed.

== Summary ==
{{Pen Animal Note|Bison}}

Bison can be sheared for {{Icon Small|Bison wool}} {{P|Wool Amount}} [[bison wool]] ({{Icon Small|silver|16|{{#expr: {{Q|Bison wool|Market Value Base}}*120}}}}) every {{P|Shearing Interval Days}} days for {{Ticks|1700}} of work. Note that yield and shearing time are modified by the shearer's [[Animal Gather Yield]] and [[Animal Gather Speed]] respectively. 

Bison are also [[pack animal]]s, and will carry up to {{P|Pack Capacity}} kg of weight in a [[caravan]]. They cannot be ridden.

== Analysis ==
They provide a large quantity of leather compared to other creatures that spawn in the same biomes.  Killing two or three bison usually provides enough leather to make a parka as well as a generous amount of meat. When enraged, they can melee and can become a threat to colonists due to their high health. Unlike muffalo, bison are faster than a baseline human.

Bison are excellent [[pack animal]]s. While worse than [[horse]]s as they consume more food and cannot be ridden, a bison does its job well.

Bison stats are essentially equivalent to [[muffalo]] except they have a slightly higher market value and movement speed, and give [[plainleather]] instead of [[bluefur]]. When tamed they give [[bison wool]] instead of [[muffalo wool]], which is largely the same but has an inferior cold insulation - offering an insulation factor of +{{Temperature|{{Q|{{P|Wool Name}}|Insulation - Cold Factor}}||delta}} instead of +{{Temperature|{{Q|{{Q|Muffalo|Wool Name}}|Insulation - Cold Factor}}||delta}}. See [[Textiles#Comparison table|Textile Comparison Table]] for further details.
