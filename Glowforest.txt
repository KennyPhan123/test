'''Glowforest''' is a temperate [[biome]] in RimWorld added by the [[Odyssey DLC]].

== Summary ==
Glowforest biomes are permanently covered in smoke, preventing sunlight from reaching at all. This means that a number of fungi can grow freely, and [[darkness (meme)|Darkness]]-related [[precept]]s{{IdeologyIcon}} will always be active outdoors.

=== Events ===
The glowforest has two [[Events/Odyssey#Glowforest|unique events]]:
* [[Events/Odyssey#Biolumenescent Spores|Biolumenescent Spores]]: The glowforest's equivalent to an [[aurora]], this event grants a [[mood]] bonus for colonists that view it.
* [[Events/Odyssey#Boomshroom Sprout|Boomshroom Sprout]]: Causes [[boomshroom]]s to grow in the tile.

== Analysis ==
Glowforest is essentially a biome that's permanently night, although the fungal light will allow pawns to move and work at normal pace. [[Darkness (meme)|Darkness]]-related [[ideoligion]]s{{IdeologyIcon}} will benefit from the glowforest, with [[combat in darkness]] being a massive accuracy boost that's always active in a glowforest.

* '''Growing and Food: Moderate ([[Ideology]]{{IdeologyIcon}}) or Very Difficult (no [[Ideology]])''' - It is warm enough for crops to grow for long periods, animals are very frequent, and food can be foraged. However, no sunlight reaches the glowforest, meaning normal crops cannot grow without [[sun lamp]]s. With [[Ideology]] installed, [[nutrifungus]]{{IdeologyIcon}} can be grown in the darkness (and is not disrupted by fungal light), meaning food should not be an issue at the cost of the {{--|3}} ''Ate fungus cooked'' moodlet. Even then, other useful crops like [[healroot]] and [[cotton plant|cotton]] won't grow by default. Without Ideology, food must be foraged or hunted for until [[sun lamp]]s are available.

* '''Disease: Difficult''' - Disease occurs as often as a [[temperate forest]], but [[healroot]] cannot be grown at all until a [[sun lamp]] is installed. This limits the medicine supply until sun lamp growing is possible or until [[medicinemaker dryad]]s{{IdeologyIcon}} are available.

* '''Temperature: Easy to Moderate''' - Glowforest tiles are generally temperate or warm, generally not too warm outside of summer days and [[heat wave]]s.

* '''Fires: Easy''' - Despite the high density of plants, boomalopes, and boomshrooms, wildfires are not a threat as [[night grass]] has a much lower [[flammability]] than regular [[grass]] does, reducing the spread of outdoor fires greatly.

* '''Special:''' The [[anima tree]]{{RoyaltyIcon}} will generate after some time, but [[anima grass]] will eventually die without [[sun lamp]]s. The outdoors is always considered dark for the [[Darkness (meme)|darkness]]-related [[precept]]s.{{IdeologyIcon}}

Overall starting difficulty is difficult, although easier with Ideology as [[nutrifungus]]{{IdeologyIcon}} can secure food in the early game. Even with Ideology, the lack of medicine can be dangerous, especially for Tribal starts which won't establish sun lamps for a while.
