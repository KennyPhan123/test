'''Gauss cannons''' are big indestructible buildings described as defense structures against flying objects. They have no actual mechanics other than occupying space.

== Occurrence ==
Gauss cannons only appear at orbital platforms controlled by the [[Traders guild]] {{Check Tag|Detail needed|Mechhive?}}. They cannot be built by the player.
