Shambler Swarm Animals is a major threat from the Anomaly DLC. Shambler Swarm Animals can occur with a level 1 monolith or higher. 

==Summary==
A group of Shambler animals will appear nearby and wander across the map. Their behavior will emulate other non-initially aggressive Shamblers and not manhunter packs. Some [[Chimera]]s may be mixed in with the Shambler Animal swarm.
