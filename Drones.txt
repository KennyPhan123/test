'''Drones''' are hostile, [[mechanoid]]-like creatures introduced in the [[Odyssey DLC]] that can occasionally be found guarding ancient landmarks, defending them from [[mechanoids]] and most humanlikes. Unlike mechanoids, enemy drones operate as part of the [[Ancients#Hostile Ancients|Hostile Ancients]] faction, as opposed to the [[mechanoid hive]].

There are currently three types of drones, listed below:
* [[Sentry drone]]
* [[Wasp drone]]
* [[Hunter drone]]

== Summary ==
Drones are mechanical beings. They do not eat, do not feel [[mood]] or [[pain]], and are immune to [[toxic buildup]], [[vacuum exposure]], and temperature extremes - despite having a Comfortable Temperature range. They cannot be lit on fire but are not completely immune to flame or heat damage.

Drones are vulnerable to [[EMP]] damage and will adapt like mechanoids after being stunned.

All drones have a [[Psychic Sensitivity]] of 0%.
