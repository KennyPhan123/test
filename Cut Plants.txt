[[{{Q|Cut plants|Image}}|left]] Orders your colonists to take cut and remove [[plants]]. This will harvest any resources possible from the plant before destroying it. It is possible to click and drag this tool to order your colonists to remove plants from a large area. This tool can also be used to remove trees that are not fully grown and therefore are not able to be selected with the "chop wood" order. Trees that are not fully mature and are cut down using this tool will not yield the full amount of logs.

Only colonists assigned to [[Work#Plants|plant cutting]] will cut plants.
The speed of cutting plants depends on the colonist's [[plants (skill)|plants skill]].<noinclude>
