'''Bliss lobotomy''' is a surgical [[Doctoring#Operations|operation]] that makes the patient happier while rendering them incapable of intellectual and skilled labor.

==Acquisition==
A [[Gorehulk]] must be encountered before the Bliss Lobotomy research option is unlocked as a basic [[Research#Anomaly|void research]] project, which requires 25 points of void research to complete.

Once researched, the bliss lobotomy operation can be performed as a surgery. It requires a minimum medical skill of 4 and costs {{Icon Small|Bioferrite||30}} [[Bioferrite]]. The surgery has a base 100% Success Rate Multiplier and a 10% chance the patient pawn dies on failure.

==Summary==
[[File:BlissLobotomyPrisonBreakInterval.PNG|300px|right]]

Bliss lobotomy provides the following effects:
* {{Good|+20}} mood bonus
* [[Global Learning Factor]] {{Bad|-50%}}
* The following work types are disabled: [[Skills#Cooking|Cook]], [[Construct]], [[Skills#Plants|Grow]], [[Mine]], [[Skills#Plants|Plant Cut]], [[Skills#Crafting|Smith, Tailor, Craft]], [[Research]] and [[Research#Anomaly|Dark Study]]. Not even [[slave]]s{{IdeologyIcon}} can perform these tasks.
* [[Prisoner#Prison break|Prison Break Interval]] and [[Slave#Slave rebellion|Slave Rebellion Interval]]{{IdeologyIcon}} increased by x1000%.

Pawns that are bliss lobotomized will remain as such even if turned into [[ghoul]]s.

[[Healer mech serum]] and [[Unnatural healing]] can reverse a bliss lobotomy. The [[biosculpter]] bioregeneration cycle will not heal a bliss lobotomy.

== Analysis ==
Bliss lobotomized pawns can still serve as Wardens, Artists, Doctors, Animal Handlers, and Haulers/Cleaners. The procedure itself does not reduce [[Consciousness]], meaning that pawns will still work and move at the same speed as before, unlike installing a [[joywire]]. Having a dramatically reduced learning speed will restrict pawn growth, making Bliss Lobotomies more useful on pawns who already have high skill in unaffected work types, particularly [[Yttakin]] who have the [[Great animals]] gene for {{Good|+8}} to animals skill and the [[Aggressive]] gene which the mood bonus makes less severe. Alternatively, [[philophagy]] can be used to gain skill XP at the same rate even after a lobotomy.

A bliss lobotomy gives a net {{Good|+10}} mood bonus when used on a pawn with the [[Very unhappy]] gene,{{BiotechIcon}} which gives a hefty {{Good|+5}} metabolism. 

Bliss lobotomies might be attractive to players looking to cut down on the number of [[prison break]]s. The [[dead calm]] [[gene]]{{BiotechIcon}} is a stronger option for keeping prisoners, as it prevents prison breaks and violent mental breaks entirely, with mimial negative effect. However, installing dead calm requires a [[genepack]] and replaces any existing [[xenotype]], and it requires Biotech to be installed. Another alternative is installing [[peg leg]]s and removing them. This requires 4 surgeries prevents movement entirely, thus preventing prisoners from using a [[nutrient paste dispenser]] by themselves, but also prevents prison breaks and violent [[mental break]]s entirely. 

Bliss lobotomies also may be appealing to colonies with the [[Inhumanizing|Inhumanizing: Required]] meme, specifically on a pawn that isn't already inhumanized. Inhumanization causes a {{Bad|-12}} skill reduction on Animals, Social, and Artistic, which are some of the only worktypes that bliss lobotomy doesn't disable. Having the bliss lobotomy will make it less likely for a humanity break to occcur, ensuring your social pawn remains social. An already inhumanized pawn is a poor choice, as inhumanization is already great at regulating mood and nerfs the only skills available to a lobotomized pawn.

A wealthy colony may want to use a bliss lobotomy to improve the mood of a [[psychic harmonizer]]{{RoyaltyIcon}} pawn. It is recommended to combine this with a [[joywire]] and traits that boost mood, such as [[optimist]], and boosts to psychic sensitivity. This can keep a large population in a wealthy colony content without the need for individual bedrooms or high quality meals.
