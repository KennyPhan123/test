'''Slate''' is a natural structure that can be [[mine]]d to obtain a {{Icon Small|Slate block}} Slate {{Icon Small|Stone chunk}} stone chunk. Do note that this is not a guarantee.

== Summary ==
'''Slate''' tiles have 500 health each, being the middle point of health between rock tiles. Each mined block has ~25% chance base to yield a {{Icon Small|slate block}} slate {{Icon Small|Stone chunk}} stone chunk, each of which can be transform into {{Icon Small|slate block}} 20 slate blocks.

This tile can also be [[Structure#Smoothed_walls|smoothed]] to create [[Smoothed slate]].
