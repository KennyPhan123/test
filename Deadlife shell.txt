The '''deadlife shell''' is a type of [[mortar shell]] that, upon exploding, releases a cloud of [[deadlife dust]] in a radius around itself. Deadlife dust raises any nearby human or animal corpses as [[shambler]]s.

When fired from a [[mortar]], {{lc:{{P|Name}}}}s will linger and emit [[deadlife dust]] for ? ticks {{Check Tag|Duration?}}, releasing a cloud in a ~5 tile radius around itself. Deadlife dust raises nearby human, animal, and entity corpses as [[shambler]]s for 6 hours. Shamblers raised by player-fired deadlife shells are non-hostile towards the colony, but will attack enemies.

When damaged beyond 20% of its health, it will explode after {{Ticks|30}} to {{Ticks|60}}, releasing a cloud of [[deadlife dust]] in a ~9 tile radius around itself. Unlike other items which explode when damaged, but similar to [[smoke shell|smoke]] and [[tox shell|tox]]{{BiotechIcon}} shells, stack size does not have an effect on explosion radius.

== Analysis ==
It is generally better to use [[IED deadlife trap]]s rather than fire deadlife shells directly. This is because corpses already on the map can be moved to a desired location, after which IED traps are better due to being more accurate. Deadlife shells ''can'' be used to reanimate raiders that have just died, although mortars in general are inaccurate, have a hefty minimum range (possibly not covering the combat zone), and are likely too slow to make a real difference. 

It is possible to place singular deadlife shells in a desired location and set them off with explosives. Doing this is cheaper and has a greater radius than the IED trap, but is tedious to set up and will damage the corpses inside.
