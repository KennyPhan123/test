'''Animal flaps''' are [[structures]] similar to [[door]]s that [[pen animals]] can pass through.

== Acquisition ==
{{Acquisition}}

== Summary ==
{{Main|Door}}
[[File:Pen animal flap.png|thumb|left|250px|[[Door]]s and [[fence gate]]s block [[pen]]s. Animal flaps do not.]]
Animal flaps are largely equivalent to [[door]]s, supporting [[roof]]s, enclosing [[rooms]], and containing [[temperature]]. They differ primarily in that they do not enclose [[pen]]s, allowing tamed [[pen animals|penned animals]] to pass through them. A room enclosed by an animal flap will be included as part of a pen placed outside.

Animal flaps also have the same opening speed as a regular door.
<div style="clear:left></div>

== Analysis ==
The animal flap is useful for creating insulated, indoor areas for animals. Pen animals will naturally seek out food stored in an animal flap enclosed [[Cooler#Analysis|freezer]] and will seek out safe [[temperature]]s automatically in the event of temperature extremes. They will also seek out shelter from [[toxic fallout]], however an unenclosed [[roof]]ed area will also provide that benefit. [[Animal bed]]s can also be placed into a barn to protect them from raiders.

Animal flaps can also be used in place of doors to enclose rooms in [[biomes]] like the [[ice sheet]], where materials for traditional doors are in short supply.
