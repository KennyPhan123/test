'''Metal hell''' is a map generated once a colonist is selected to activate the fully awoken [[Monolith]].

== Summary ==
The Metal hell is generated within a 100 by 100 cell [[Pocket map]], classified as the MetalHell biome. It has a temperature of {{temperature|31}}, is considered underground, has a solid [[Void metal]] roof and floor, and call aid is disabled within the map. The map consists of a solid border of [[Void metal wall]]s, with a large carved out circular area defined with jagged edges, the edge of which is surrounded by scattered heaps of variably sized [[Void metal mass]]es. At the center is the [[Void node]], surrounded by [[Twisted archotech support]]s, with a swirling energy and fog masking the core of the metal hell. After activating the fully awoken Monolith and being sent here, a colonist within the metal hell can be selected to interact with the Void Node, and is met with two options: to embrace the void or disrupt the link; each achieving different [[endings#The Void|endings]].
