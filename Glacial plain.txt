== Summary ==
An open area scraped clean by the movement of glaciers. While vegetation and soil are sparse, a few hardy species have made their home here.

There are no [[geyser]]s in glacial plain maps. Frozen ruins are hidden in the glaciers on all maps, with much bigger ruins when a [[frozen ruins]] landmark is present. [[Anima tree]]s{{RoyaltyIcon}} will not appear even in a warm glacial plains map.

== Summary ==
Glacial plains are similar to [[boreal forest]]s or [[tundra]]s in terms of temperature and growing season length, but lack [[steam geyser]]s for heat and power, and have much less land to grow on.

* '''Food and Growing: Moderate to Difficult''' - Growing seasons can be similar in length to [[boreal forest]]s, but glacial plains do not have rich soil at all and regular soil is sparse. 

* '''Disease: Easy''' - Disease events are uncommon. [[Healroot]] still grows naturally during the summer, and healroot plants will survive (but not grow in) the cold.

* '''Temperature: Difficult''' - While the summers are fairly warm, the total lack of steam geysers and relative lack of [[tree]]s make temperature more difficult than the other cold biomes, namely for [[New Tribe]]s which don't start with Electricity.

* '''Fires: Easy''' - There is little plant life, and there are large stretches of ice between each patch of soil, meaning fires are not difficult to fight.

* '''Special:''' There are few/no [[ruin]]s outside of the ice, so the initial start may be harder. The [[anima tree]]{{RoyaltyIcon}} will not spawn.

Overall starting difficulty ranges from moderate to difficult. In warmer tiles and/or when playing with a developed start like [[Crashlanded]], the summer will last long enough for colonists to stablize and get a food supply established. Still, the colony will likely have to build in a sub-optimal location in order to be near a soil patch.
