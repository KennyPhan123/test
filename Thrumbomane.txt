'''Thrumbomane''' is a type of [[leather]] that can be acquired through the butchering of an [[alpha thrumbo]] at a [[butcher table]]. It is slightly better than [[thrumbofur]], making it one of the most protective and insulative textiles in the entire game.

== Analysis ==
{{Stub|section=|reason=Compare [[Thrumbofur#Analysis]]}}
Thrumbomane is one of the most powerful textiles in the game, but very rare. As such, you will likely be best spending it on highly protective clothing such as dusters and ceremonial hoods. It should be noted that it has worse heat and blunt armor than [[Hyperweave]]. Like [[Thrumbofur]] it is very good for cold environments. It is the most powerful textile that the player can produce infinitely.
