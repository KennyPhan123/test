}}{{Info|A plant that when harvested, yields [[hops]] that can then be used to produce [[beer]]. Hop plants can be planted in a [[growing zone]] or a [[hydroponics basin]].}}
Sowing hop plants requires a minimum [[plants (skill)|growing skill]] of {{P|Minimum Required Growing Skill}}, but any colonist capable of [[Work#Plant cut|plant cutting]] can harvest them.

Raw hops are not edible and are only used to make beer. [[Brewery|Brewing]] uses 25 [[hops]], and produces 5 [[wort]], which is then fermented to make 5 beer. Fermenting takes 6 days. When considering day/night cycles, hop plants take longer to grow than the barrel ferments, unless [[hydroponics]] are used.

==Growing==
{{Grow Table}}

Unlike most plants, the harvest yield is determined by the [[drug crop harvest yield]] stat of the harvester. This stat is still primarily determined by [[plant harvest yield]].

== Analysis ==
Beer is a useful and relatively safe drug. A beer per day gives {{+|10}} [[mood]] for 4.8 hours, fulfills some [[Recreation]], and will never get a colonist addicted. Make sure to set your [[drug policy]] to ''not'' take "For Recreation", as pawns will drink more than once a day.

Even if you haven't researched Beer Brewing, it may be worthwhile to grow the hop plants beforehand. Keep in mind that hops will spoil in 30 days if unrefrigerated.

===Production===
Each [[fermenting barrel]] can hold up to 25 wort, which means it can hold the equivalent of 125 hop items.

If you have a grower with Plants 8, are growing hops in regular soil, ''and'' are on [[difficulty|Strive to Survive]] difficulty:
* You will need 1 fermenting barrel per 15.62 hop plants if you want to ferment everything as soon as the harvest is done.
* You will need 1 fermenting barrel per 23.94 hop plants if you want to continuously ferment.

'''Remember that this is ''only'' true on the game conditions listed above.''' Higher Plants skill will increase hops yield ([[Plant Harvest Yield]]). Increasing difficulty decreases yield of all plants ([[AI Storytellers#Harvest yield|Harvest yield]]).
