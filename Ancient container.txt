'''Ancient container''' refers to one of three [[ruins]] with exactly the same name but different sizes and stats. Sizes are appended to differentiate them and are not present in-game:
* [[Ancient container (large)]]
* [[Ancient container (medium)]]{{OdysseyIcon}}
* [[Ancient container (small)]]{{OdysseyIcon}}

Note that despite being defined in Core, the large container will only appear in-game if either the [[Ideology]] and/or [[Odyssey]] DLC are enabled.
