The '''ship engine''' is a [[ship]] part, an antimatter-powered engine capable of launching a [[ship]] into orbit and on interstellar trajectories.

== Acquisition ==
{{Acquisition}}

They also appears as part of the ship featured in the [[ship to the stars]] quest.

== Summary ==
A [[ship]] needs three engines to be able to launch. It serves no other purpose. It can function under a [[roof]].

Note that despite appearing to have a vents of some sort on two faces, there are no restrictions to the placement of the engine and other parts. It can be surrounded on all sides and still safely launch.

== Analysis ==
You can delay building ship engines until the [[ship reactor]] is fully activated. This can reduce wealth, and therefore [[raid points]].
