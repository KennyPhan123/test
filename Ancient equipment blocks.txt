'''Ancient equipment blocks''' are [[ruins]] that are defined in Core but only appear when the [[Ideology DLC]] and/or the [[Odyssey DLC]] are enabled.

== Occurrence ==
If the Ideology DLC is enabled, they can be found in the [[ancient complex]].

If the Odyssey DLC is enabled, they can be found in [[Orbital scanner#Quests|satellites]].

If neither DLC is enabled, they will not appear in-game at all.
