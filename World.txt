The world overview page shows you an overview of the planet your colonists have landed on. Selecting a point on the map will provide the following information about that tile:

&#9642; Position in longitude/latitude

&#9642; Elevation in meters

&#9642; Average temperature

&#9642; Biome type

&#9642; Terrain type

&#9642; Rainfall in millimeters

The "Mode" button will configure the map to display a worldwide overview of:

&#9642; Biomes

&#9642; Elevation

&#9642; Rainfall

&#9642; Temperature

&#9642; All
