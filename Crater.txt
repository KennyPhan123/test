'''Craters''' are [[terrain]] blockers left behind by massive explosions.

Craters are created when an [[antigrain IED]] or [[Antigrain warhead|shell]] explodes on a cell. They can also be left behind after [[orbital debris]]{{OdysseyIcon}} impacts in [[orbit]].{{OdysseyIcon}}

== Summary ==
[[File:FillCrater.png|thumb|left|100px|Fill crater gizmo]]
Craters are like a mix of [[blast mark]]s and [[pit burrow]]s.{{AnomalyIcon}} Craters are temporary battle scars left from explosions that need to be cleared before the land they're on can be used to support [[buildings]]. If you attempt to set down a workbench on the edge, you will see a message stating "Interaction spot blocked by crater." While placing any building in the center will display the message "space already occupied." To remove a crater, use the "fill in" gizmo that appears when selecting the crater(s). After that, a pawn with [[construction]] work enabled will move over to begin filling in the crater.{{Check Tag|How much work?}}

After the crater has been filled in, it creates [[filth]] on the ground labeled "loose ground" in a cross formation.

You can lay flooring down under the crater, and it will not affect the floor tiles,{{Check Tag|Meaning| it allows them?  doesnt afgect speed? acts like a building?}} nor will filling in the crater remove the flooring underneath.

Work to fill depends on size:{{Check Tag|Stat?|Is this value affected by any stat?}}
* Small (2x2): {{Ticks|1250}}
* Medium (3x3): {{Ticks|2500}}
* Large (5x5): {{Ticks|6250}}
