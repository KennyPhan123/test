'''Void sculptures''' are [[sculpture]]s added by the [[Anomaly DLC]] that enhance the quality of [[psychic rituals]].

== Acquisition == 
{{Acquisition}}

Void sculptures can also drop from destroyed [[flesh sack]]s,{{AnomalyIcon}} with a ~ 1.17% chance of appearing.

== Summary == 
[[File:Psychic ritual spot radii.jpg|thumb|left|The sterile tile radius denotes all valid placements for void sculptures, note that it is not the same as the displayed radius]]
Void sculptures provide a bonus to the [[psychic ritual quality|quality]] of [[psychic rituals]] when placed within range of the [[psychic ritual spot]] used.

Up to 6 sculptures can be linked to a single spot, for a hypothetical limit of {{+|30%}} with all legendaries.

If more than 6 sculptures are nearby, the sculptures nearest to the psychic ritual spot will be selected, not the sculptures with the highest quality.

=== Meditation ===
Void sculptures have the Artistic mediation psyfocus type.{{RoyaltyIcon}} It shares similar mechanics to other [[sculpture]] types, where the highest quality sculpture is the focus and other sculptures add a small secondary bonus. However, void sculptures do not contribute towards the secondary sculpture bonus for void sculptures or regular sculptures, while regular sculptures always contribute towards the secondary bonus.

The highest quality sculpture is the primary source of psyfocus:

The maximum meditation focus strength is thus 44% for a legendary sculpture surrounded by 8 additional non-void sculptures of masterwork or legendary quality.

In addition, void sculptures contribute towards a [[psychic ritual spot]]'s Void meditation focus. Each void sculpture adds +2% meditation focus to the spot. for up to 6 sculptures, independent of sculpture quality. Note that void sculptures do not count towards Void focus on their own.

== Analysis ==
Void sculptures take a lot of work to create and have a fairly small impact on ritual quality, but since [[bioferrite]] is plentiful with sufficient [[containment]] facilities, it should be built once bioferrite and an artist's time is in surplus. Because a ritual spot can only have 6 void sculptures linked to it, it is important to maximize quality. Provided enough high-quality sculptures, it is possible to reach the cap of 100% ritual quality, dramatically improving the outcome of the ritual.

Note that traders will not buy void sculptures, so if you're going to mass-produce them in order to maximize quality, it is recommended to deconstruct low-quality sculptures to reduce bioferrite loss, or use them in other areas of the base.

For [[beauty]] purposes, it is typically better to make [[large sculpture]]s of other materials as large sculptures provide more beauty/tile. Void sculptures may have merit if non-bioferrite materials are not readily available, e.g., in a [[sea ice]] colony, as bioferrite is terrible for other sculptures.

=== Meditation ===
If building the base around the [[void monolith]], meditating towards that would provide a better Void meditation focus than a [[psychic ritual spot]] can even with the bonuses. Otherwise, a [[psychic ritual spot]] boosted by 4 [[shard beacon]]s and 6 void sculptures is the best Void focus available.

For pawns with both Artistic and Void focus types, using any type of sculpture as Artistic meditation offers a higher base and cap than a [[psychic ritual spot]], with a legendary sculpture equaling a fully maxed out [[psychic ritual spot]] on its own. If high enough quality sculptures can be made then standard Artistic is the better meditation focus.

Pawns with both Natural and Void focus types can use void focus in order to not have to walk so far away. The [[anima tree]], [[large nature shrine]], and [[animus stone]] offer greater base focus than a psychic ritual spot's maximum, assuming no artifical penalty and ignoring travel time, but the travel time spent can make void focus better for psyfocus gain.
