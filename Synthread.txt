{{Info|'''Synthread''' is a space age type of [[fabric]] only available through [[trading]] or through random drops.}}

== Acquisition == 
Synthread cannot be grown or manufactured, instead it is only available through [[trading]] or through the [[cargo pods]] event.

Additionally, the starting gear on any [[Scenario system|New Arrivals scenario]] is a synthread [[T-shirt]] and [[pants]], and occasionally a [[jacket]] or [[parka]] as well. Some [[raiders]] and outlanders will occasionally come wearing synthread apparel, as well.

== Analysis == 
The difficulty in sourcing sufficient synthread for use is not rewarded by its stats. It is the 9th most protective of the 31 [[textile]]s in the game against sharp damage, and the tied for second worst against heat damage, only beating [[patchleather]]. It has middling cold and heat insulation and provides the same item health as most [[leather]]s. Thus, if it should be found in the early game it is a decent candidate for [[duster]]s and other protective [[clothing]]. But it should be replaced by the better leathers or even [[devilstrand]] by the mid-game. 

For use in [[furniture]], it has a middle-of-the-road [[Property:Beauty Factor|beauty factor]]. It is not worth sourcing synthread just for furniture beauty. But as synthread becomes redundant for clothing, excess synthread can be used to improve furniture beauty.

Its one advantage over other textiles is that it, and its products, are 30% less flammable than normal textiles. It is still more flammable than [[hyperweave]] or devilstrand, which are both 60% less flammable than normal.
