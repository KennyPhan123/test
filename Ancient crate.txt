'''Ancient crate''' are [[ruins]] that are defined in Core but only appear when the [[Ideology DLC]] and/or the [[Odyssey DLC]] are enabled.

== Occurrence ==
If the Ideology DLC is enabled, they can be found among the debris scattered on maps.

If the Odyssey DLC is enabled, they can be found as debris in [[scarlands]] maps and in [[junkyard]]s, and outside [[Landmarks#Ruins|ancient ruins]], [[ancient warehouse]]s and [[ancient chemfuel refinery|ancient chemfuel refineries]].

If neither DLC is enabled, they will not appear in-game at all.
