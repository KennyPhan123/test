'''Smokepop''' is a level {{P|Psylink Level}} [[psycast]] that creates a cloud of [[blind smoke]].

== Acquisition ==
{{Acquisition}}

== Summary ==
[[File:smokepop radius.PNG|thumb|left|175px|Area of affect of Smokepop. Cast location has been marked in yellow, surrounding affected tiles in white, and unaffected tiles in red.]]
Upon casting, Smokepop creates a cloud of [[blind smoke]] for approximately 60 seconds within a circle of 3 tile radius, centered on the target tile. Valid targets include any tile within a 25 tile radius of the caster, respecting line of sight. While it persists, blind smoke multiplies the accuracy of all projectile attacks pathing through it by {{Bad|x70%}}, reducing their chance to hit their target by roughly one-third. Note that neither the shooter nor the target need to be within the smoke for this penalty to apply - as long as it exists between the shooter and target, the accuracy penalty will be applied. Additionally, blind smoke prevents [[turrets]] from targeting pawns within or behind it.

== Analysis ==
Unfortunately, Smokepop is noticeably less useful than psycasts of a equal or even lower level: blind smoke has a relatively limited utility, and the psycast competes with the [[smokepop pack]] and [[smoke launcher]], which serve a similar role. However, it can still see some use in combat, and can even prove invaluable in some encounters.

=== Comparisons ===
In both of these roles, smokepop occupies a near-identical role to the [[smokepop pack]] and the [[smoke launcher]]. Unlike the latter two items, the smokepop psycast does not take any equipment slots, making it best for repositioning, although it still requires psyfocus that could be used for other psycasts like [[Skip]]. 
* The smoke launcher takes a pawn's weapon slot, is inaccurate, and requires a staggering {{Ticks|210}} [[Aiming Time]] and {{Ticks|270}} [[Ranged Cooldown]]. The psycast only requires {{Ticks|{{P|Casting Time}}}} to deploy, is always accurate, and covers a 3-tile radius of smoke (1 tile more than the launcher). However, the launcher can still be great against mech clusters, where turrets are a known threat and smoke can be pre-fired. In addition, smoke launchers can make smoke indefinitely while the psycast is limited by heat and psyfocus. This makes the smoke launcher a strong substitute for fighting mech clusters. 
* The smokepop pack has an instant deployment time, and a larger radius, but can only deploy on the user's tile and takes the valuable utility slot. 

In addition, the launcher and pack are more consistent to obtain, only requiring one to research [[Gunsmithing]] and [[Research#Smokepop packs|Smokepop packs]], respectively. Additionally, [[Pirate]] and [[Outlander]] raiders can carry both. In contrast, smokepop requires a level 4 psycaster, and either obtaining it randomly or with a (relatively uncommon) [[psytrainer]]. However, the main advantage of the psycast - that it does not take any slots - makes it much more flexible, especially since blind smoke is a niche mechanic.
