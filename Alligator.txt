An '''alligator''' is a large carnivorous [[animal]] added by the [[Odyssey DLC]].

== Acquisition ==
Alligators can be found in {{Habitats}}. They can either be tamed by a [[Work #Handle|handler]] or self-tame in a random event. {{Check Tag|Trade?|Can they be purchased?}}
<!--Alligators can be bought and sold in other [[Trade#Faction base|faction bases]] and from [[Trade#Bulk goods trader 2|bulk goods traders]]. Alligators purchased from traders will be already tamed.-->

== Summary ==
A female alligator produces {{P|Eggs Per Clutch Average}} [[alligator egg]] every {{P|Egg Laying Interval}} day when fertilized. Alligators will not lay unfertilized eggs.

== Analysis ==
With no default trainability without a [[sentience catalyst]], they aren't good for much other than combat, however they are only middling at that. With less wildness, higher melee {{DPS}} and health scale, and faster move speed, [[grizzly bear|grizzly]] and [[polar bear]]s and [[warg]]s are better combat animals. Their advantages in the water are rarely relevant, and are almost always insufficient to make up for their deficiencies.

Like many other carnivores, alligators are more nutrient efficient than [[pig]]s for converting {{icon small|human meat}} human meat into animal meat. Alligators also mature faster than pigs, which is offset by needing to hatch from eggs, and have a greater tolerance for the cold, allowing a corpse freezer to be set to a lower temperature. Their significantly higher wildness means that they require colonist time to maintain.
