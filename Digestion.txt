It is lethal for [[human]]s and [[animals]] for Digestion to be reduced to 0%. Despite its name and description, Digestion has no effect on [[hunger]] rate.

{{Hover title|Version/1.4.3580|At the time of writing}}, the maximum possible Digestion capacity is [[Effective Maximum::1.325|132.5%]] with a healthy pawn high on [[luciferium]] with any of the artificial stomachs installed.
