'''Ancient terminal''' are [[ruins]] added by the [[Ideology DLC]]. Hacking it is required in many quests to find information of an [[ideoligion]]'s relic. Glows slightly.

A pawn must be capable of intellectual to be able to hack a terminal. Terminals in worshipful villages take 3 seconds to hack, while those found in ancient complexes can take significantly longer.

Deconstructing the terminal will instantly fail the quest, even if it has already been hacked. Doing so provides no resources and should be avoided.

== Version history ==
* [[Ideology DLC]] Release - Added.
* [[Version/1.3.3200|1.3.3200]] - Terminals in worshipful villages now take 3 seconds to hack.
