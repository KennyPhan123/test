A '''hermit crab''' is an [[animal]] added by the [[Odyssey DLC]].

== Acquisition ==
{{PAGENAME}}s can be found on coastal maps in {{Habitats}}. They can either be tamed by a [[Work #Handle|handler]] or self-tame in a random event. 

{{PAGENAME}}s can be bought and sold in other [[Trade#Faction base|faction bases]] and from [[Trade#Exotic goods trader 2|exotic goods traders]]. {{PAGENAME}}s purchased from traders will be already tamed.

== Version history ==
* [[Odyssey DLC]] Release - Added. 
* [[Version/1.6.4528|1.6.4528]] - Hermit crabs now hide in their shell when not moving. 
* [[Version/1.6.4566|1.6.4566]] - Hermit crabs no longer walk sideways. They also live considerably longer, life expectancy increased to 60 years from 12 years. These changes were likely made in direct response to a [https://www.reddit.com/r/RimWorld/s/HqOIXNimrS reddit post] that gained some traction and pointed out the inaccuracies with hermit crabs as initially implemented.
