'''Ancient comms console''' are [[ruins]] added by the [[Ideology DLC]]. Despite the description, it can be used to summon enemies or [[Cargo pods|supply drops]] at [[ancient complex]]es at which point it stops working.

== Summary ==
The ancient comms console can be hacked to trick an ancient supply satellite to deliver cargo pods. This only takes 4 hacking progress units, i.e. {{Ticks|200}} at 100% hacking speed. However the signal has a 50% chance to be detected by hostile factions and cause an enemy attack.

The drop pods arrive within {{Ticks|180}}. If there is a raid, it happens {{Ticks|300}} to {{Ticks|600}} later.

The ancient comms console can only be hacked once.
