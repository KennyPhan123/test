Shambler Swarm is a major threat from the Anomaly DLC. Shambler Swarm can occur even when the monolith is not investigated.

== Summary ==
A group of shambling, rotting corpses is approaching. They will now wander through the area. If they see a human, they will attack. If not, they will leave in a few days. The shamblers will drop as corpses after 2.5 to 3.5 days.
