The '''flak vest''' is a type of [[armor]] that protects well against sharp/piercing attacks (e.g. bullets, stabs, cuts, bites), and offer minor protection against blunt attacks, explosions and the ever-present threat of clubs, maces, and wild animals. 

== Acquisition ==
{{Acquisition}}

They can also be [[Trade|bought]] from orbital, [[Outlander]], or [[Empire]] {{RoyaltyIcon}} traders, as well as Outlander and Empire [[faction base]]s. Flak vests are also commonly found on Outlanders, [[Pirates]], [[Ancients]] and [[Empire#Pawns|Imperial Troopers]].{{RoyaltyIcon}}

== Analysis ==
Flak vests protect well against Sharp damage, which includes all bullets, at a low cost and at a very small decrease in movement. They are a very cost-effective measure for overall damage mitigation. Because flak vests have much higher armor values than [[flak jacket]]s and [[flak pants]], and as they protect most of the vital organs, vests should be prioritized over other armor items. 

Specifically, the vest covers the neck, torso, and the internal organs (heart, liver, lungs, kidneys), which are immediately fatal to lose / lose both of. The only vital organs a vest does ''not'' cover are the head and brain, which headgear serves to protect. 

As vests occupy the middle layer, they can be worn with overgarments, allowing excellent flexibility while still providing competitive protection. It can be combined with [[duster]]s or [[flak jacket]]s for even greater protection, dusters or [[parka]]s for insulation, [[eltex robe]]s{{RoyaltyIcon}} for [[psycast]]ers,{{RoyaltyIcon}} [[slave body strap]]s{{IdeologyIcon}} for [[slaves]],{{IdeologyIcon}} [[lab coat]]s{{AnomalyIcon}} for [[research]]ers, or [[heavy bandolier]]s{{BiotechIcon}} for a significant ranged {{DPS}} increase.

=== Comparisons === 
Despite being nominally more advanced, the [[recon armor]] provides worse Sharp armor than a flak vest ''alone'', only faring slightly better against Blunt. Recon does cover the arms and legs, and has no movement penalty. But, overall, the flak vest with any sort of outerwear is better for raw protection - deaths from limb damage (specifically, from [[blood loss]] or lowered [[consciousness]]) are considerably rarer than torso shots.

Flak vests can be worn with a [[flak jacket]], or a [[duster]] made from a protective [[textile]] such as [[devilstrand]], [[thrumbofur]], or [[hyperweave]]. With either overgarment, the flak vest is with [[marine armor]], [[cataphract armor]],{{RoyaltyIcon}} and their [[power armor|variants]]. Marine and cataphract tier armors still protect the limbs much better than any overgarment, but this comes at a significantly higher resource, research, and move speed cost. Meanwhile the low cost of flak vests and dusters allow for many of them to be crafted to in the hopes of getting a higher [[quality]] than is obtainable with power armor, they're cheaper to replace, and can be brought online much faster. 

Flak vests can thus be an effective armor choice all the way through the end game.
