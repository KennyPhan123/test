A '''morbid slab (medium)''' a floor covering added by the [[Ideology DLC]]. It is a smaller counterpart to the [[morbid slab (broad)]]. 

== Acquisition ==
{{Acquisition}}

== Summary ==
The morbid slab significantly increases the dominance of the {{P|Style}} style to those who see it, and is also moderately [[beauty|beautiful]]. As it is not an edifice, other buildings can be built on top of it. There are several possible varieties of texture available - the texture is determined randomly upon completion.

It has has no effect on [[move speed]] and [[cleanliness]]. For these, the stats of the [[floor]] or [[terrain]] beneath the rug is taken into account.
