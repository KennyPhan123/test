The '''death acidifier''' is an [[artificial body part]] that destroys all equipped [[weapons]] and [[apparel]] equipped by the installed pawn upon their [[death]].

== Acquisition ==
It cannot be crafted but can be purchased from traders and installed normally, although it is more or less useless for your colonists.

It is installed on all [[Empire#Pawns|Empire soldiers]]{{RoyaltyIcon}} but cannot be removed from them.

== Summary ==
{{stub|section=1|reason=Mechanics with how chemical burns are applied. Also chance of destruction on failed operation.}}
Upon [[death]], a death acidifier destroys held [[weapon]]s and worn [[apparel]] on a pawn. All other items carried in the dead pawn's inventory drop normally, including weapons and apparel not currently equipped. It does not prevent [[downed]] pawns from dropping their weapons or items, it only destroys weapons held and apparel worn at the time of death. It also does not prevent the stripping of apparel from a downed pawn.

It then applies chemical burns on the corpse, relative to where it destroyed items. It does not deal damage to anything else around the user on activation. 

It will still be triggered upon the death of pawns with [[death refusal]],{{AnomalyIcon}} but will not trigger on pawns saved from death by [[deathless]].{{BiotechIcon}}{{Check Tag|Verify}}

=== Installation ===
Installing the part requires {{ticks|2500}} of work, 2x medicine of [[Herbal medicine|Herbal]] quality or better, and a [[Skills#Medical|Medical skill]] of 5.

It cannot be removed once installed.

If the operation fails, the part will be destroyed.

== Analysis ==
The death acidifier merely serves as a way to prevent players from easily acquiring weapons and armor that would make the early game too easy. Installing the death acidifier on your pawns has no use, except for destroying equipment that could have been smelted for resources.

Downing a pawn does not cause the implant to activate. As long as the pawn is [[downed]] by [[blood loss]] or [[psychic shock lance]]s, so that the forced [[AI Storytellers#Enemy death on downed|death on down]] chance will not apply, the Empire's{{RoyaltyIcon}} gear can be recovered freely.

A [[biomutation lance]]{{AnomalyIcon}} will also not trigger the acidifier, and will cause the pawn to drop their equipment upon being converted into a [[fleshbeast]],{{AnomalyIcon}} allowing another route for circumvention.

If a pawn with a death acidifier carries [[wastepack]]s{{BiotechIcon}} and dies, the wastepacks will be destroyed without creating [[pollution]].

== Trivia ==
Animals with death acidifiers are immortal and cannot be killed by any means, including slaughtering, due to a bug that interrupts the death function. However without dev mode animals can never spawn with death acidifiers.
