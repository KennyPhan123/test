An '''Effigy''' is a consumable ritual focus added by the [[Ideology DLC]]. It serves as a focus for the [[Rituals#Symbol burning|Symbol Burning]] ritual.

== Acquisition ==
{{Acquisition}} Effigies can only be constructed by pawns with an [[ideoligion]] that has selected them as a building option. Only ideoligions with the [[Nudism (meme)|Nudism]], [[Raider (meme)|Raider]],  [[Flesh purity|Flesh Purity]], [[Pain is virtue|Pain Is Virtue]], [[Individualist]] or [[Human primacy|Human Primacy]] memes or the [[Ideoligion#Ideological|Ideological]] structure can select them.
