The '''large chemfuel tank''' is a [[building]] added by the [[Odyssey DLC]] that stores [[chemfuel]] and allows the fuel to be consumed by the [[gravship]] on which the tank is built.

It is the larger of the two such tanks, with the smaller being the appropriately named [[small chemfuel tank]].

== Summary ==
{{Stub|aection=1|reason=Mechanics including loading (who by, how, limits etc), unlowding, placmeent on ship, lack of plumbing needed etc. Ensure parity with [[Small chemfuel tank]]}}
{{Image wanted|section=1|reason= Gizmos}}
Large chemfuel tanks hold 750 units of chemfuel for use in gravship travel. Fuel extends the range of a gravship.
Chemfuel can be removed from this container by use of the "eject fuel" gizmo. 

Chemfuel tanks that hold chemfuel and are damaged to ~40% of their total [[hit points]] will hiss before dropping all their inventory as a chemfuel bomb. The size of the explosion is dependent on the amount of stored chemfuel.

== Analysis ==
{{Stub|section=1|reason=General- also specifically  placementand protection, how to decide which and how many to use, consider cribbing info/graphs/tables from [[fuel optimiser]] to show which you need when. ensure parity with [[Small chemfuel tank]]}}
A 3x3 building containing up to 750 chemfuel total, 83.33 chemfuel per square. This compares favorably with the small chemfuel tank which is a 2x2 building containing 250 chemfuel or 62.5 per square. 

If enough tanks are constructed to meet gravship range requirements but additional storage of chemfuel is required, [[Shelf|shelves]] are far more optimal for storage at 450 per square.
