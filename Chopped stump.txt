A '''chopped stump''' is what remains after a [[tree]] has been [[Chop wood|chopped]] down by a [[pawn]]. It blocks planting a new tree until it's removed, either by manually cutting it or waiting for it to naturally decay, an automatic process with a variable time of around 2-5 days.{{Check Tag|Anything more/less than 2-5?|These values observed in a dozen cut trees; a larger sample is needed.}} For the necessary {{Ticks|{{P|Harvest Work}} }} of work it yields only a comparably few {{P|Harvest Yield}} {{Icon Small|{{P|Harvest Product}} }}. Also it provides only relatively low {{%|{{P|Cover Effectiveness}} }} [[cover]].

Cutting them does not give a mood penalty to those with a precept against [[cutting trees]].{{IdeologyIcon}}

Despite not growing, it has growdays and lifespanDaysPerGrowDays values set. It is unclear what effect, if any, this has.
