'''Compacted steel'''  is a natural structure that can be [[mine]]d to obtain {{Icon Small|steel}} steel. 

== Summary ==
'''Compacted steel''' tiles have 1,500 health each, making them one of the faster ores to mine. They can be found in larger-sized clusters, ranging around 30+ tiles in size. Each mined block has a base yield of {{Icon Small|steel}} 40 steel, however this is modified by factors such as the [[Difficulty]] setting and the [[Mining_Yield|mining yield]] of the pawn.
