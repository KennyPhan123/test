A '''mechanoid ship chunk''' is a 2x2 sized piece of a destroyed mechanoid ship that has crashed onto the map. It cannot be hauled to a stockpile zone, it can be only disassembled at its location for 40 [[steel]] and 15 [[gravlite panel]]s. A number {{Check Tag|How many?}} fall from the sky with the [[grav engine]], and they can also be found in abandoned orbital platforms. {{Check Tag|Where else?}}

Like other [[chunk]]s, they can serve as a source of cover.
