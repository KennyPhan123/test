[[{{Q|Claim|Image}}|left]] Used to claim neutral [[structure]]s for the [[colony]], including [[ancient shrine]]s, existing [[ruins]], abandoned [[Raider|siege]] camps or destroyed [[faction base]]s. Claiming structures other than ones built by the player will automatically add them to your [[home area]]. Colonists will repair, maintain and extinguish fires in a claimed area or structure. This can be removed from the [[architect]] menu.<noinclude>
