Small-size '''ancient containers''' are [[ruins]] added by the [[Odyssey DLC]].

== Summary ==
These containers cannot be deconstructed. They can however be destroyed for {{Icon Small|Steel slag chunk||1}} [[steel slag chunk]]. 

Unlike the [[Ancient container|large variety]], they cannot be used as walls and only provide 30% cover.
