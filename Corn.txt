'''Corn''' is a vegetable [[raw food]] obtained by harvesting [[corn plant]]s.

== Acquisition == 
The corn plant can be sown by all pawns immediately. Each plant requires {{Q|Corn plant|Real Grow Days}} full days at optimal light and temperature range to reach maturity in normal, 100% fertile soil and yields {{Q|Corn plant|Harvest Yield}} units per plant, before modifiers for [[Difficulty]] and [[Plant Harvest Yield]].

== Summary ==
As a [[raw food]], corn can be be eaten raw, with a flat {{Bad|2%}} chance of giving [[food poisoning]], or cooked into a [[meal]] with a food poisoning chance dependent on the skill of the cook and the [[cleanliness]] of the kitchen. [[Animals]] are immune to food poisoning from raw food but not from meals. When used in food recipes that require specific types of [[nutrition]], such as [[fine meal]]s, corn is classified as a vegetable.

As with most raw food, eating corn directly results in a {{Thought|desc=I had to eat raw food. We should be cooking that kind of food before eating it. We're not animals.|label=ate raw food|stack=1|value=-7|duration=1}}. This mood penalty is negated if the pawn has the [[ascetic]] trait or the [[robust digestion]] {{BiotechIcon}} gene.

Corn takes {{P|Days To Start Rot Base}} days to rot without refrigeration, double that of most other vegetarian foods.

== Analysis ==
Corn is the least labor-intensive food crop per item of food produced. This is balanced by a long grow time, with the risks from raiders, events, and weather. This typically makes it useful late game where a stock of food is built up in case of famine, and where switching to corn frees up pawn time or means more pawns can be supported for the same labor. 

In terms of absolute food per tile per day, [[rice]] is slightly better in regular soil, and becomes much better in [[Hydroponics basin|hydroponics]], where corn can't be planted. However, this comes at the expense of massively more work - in most instances simply making a corn field bigger is a more efficient choice, with small fields of rice being used to make up any immediate needs for food.

With a shelf life of {{#show:corn|?Days To Start Rot Base}} days, corn is tied with [[toxipotatoes]] as the most resilient crop to store outside a freezer. While only relevant while a freezer is not available, this should give ample time to set up an appropriate storage facility, or even obviate the need for a freezer in the medium term.

With a nutrition of {{#show:Rice|?nutrition}} per unit and a deterioration rate of {{#show:Rice|?Deterioration Rate Base}}, there is no difference with other raw vegetables for the purpose of cooking meals. While direct consumption is possible, this is inefficient from a nutrition point of view compared to cooking a [[simple meal]] even without considering the mood penalty and food poisoning chance.

Feeding rice to [[herbivore]]s and [[omnivore]]s has no ill effects, but is inefficient in terms of work per nutrition compared to farming [[haygrass]] or making [[kibble]].
