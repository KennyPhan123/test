The '''mini-shotgun''' is an [[mechanoid]]-only ranged [[weapon]] added by the [[Biotech DLC]]. It is the main armament of the [[militor]]. 

This weapon cannot be independently obtained in normal play. It is provided here for comparisons sake.

== Acquisition ==
Mini-shotguns spawn on all [[militor]]s, including those gestated by a colony. However, it is not possible to obtain it directly in normal play. Pawns carrying it will not drop it on [[death]], [[downing]], or complete loss of [[manipulation]] like other weapons. It can be spawned in by the [[Development mode#Debug actions menu|Debug actions menu]] but, like all [[unobtainable items|unobtainable weapons]], will disappear after being dropped.
