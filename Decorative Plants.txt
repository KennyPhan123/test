__NOTOC__
'''Decorative plants''' are plants that can be planted by the player and which main purpose is to add [[Beauty]] to a room or area.

[[Rose]]s, [[daylily|daylilies]], [[dandelions]] and [[bonsai tree]]s{{IdeologyIcon}} may all be grown for decoration, providing [[Beauty]] indoors and out. Out of the four, only dandelions are unable to be planted in a [[plant pot|plant]] or [[bonsai pot]].{{IdeologyIcon}}

Roses, daylilies and bonsai trees only require 30% [[light]], which is attainable with a [[torch]] or [[standing lamp]].
