A '''Musical instrument''' may refer to one of several [[recreation]]al [[building]] types that provide the "Music" recreation type:  
*[[Harp]]
*[[Harpsichord]]
*[[Piano]]
*[[Drum]]
Each instrument requires its own [[research]] of the same name in order to be built.

A pawn must have a [[Hearing]] capacity above {{Bad|0%}} to get thoughts from listening to instruments, and a [[Manipulation]] capacity above {{Bad|0%}} to play any of them.
Nobles can put on concerts with the instruments, similar to a [[Events#Party|party]], giving a [[mood]] bonus to the noble and attendees.
