The '''monosword''' is a DLC [[Weapons#Melee Weapons|melee weapon]] that does a large amount of [[damage]], has a fairly short cooldown, and deals sharp damage. It also boasts one of the highest armor penetration values for melee weapons in the game.

It has a great advantage against armored targets and cause opponents to bleed out fairly quickly as the Monosword lops off limbs with fair ease.

== Acquisition ==
Monoswords cannot be crafted, instead they can only be [[trade|purchased]] from [[Outlander]] and [[Empire]] combat suppliers and [[faction base]]s, or received as a quest reward.

== Summary == 
Unlike other ultratech weapons, the monosword has no special mechanics. Instead it simply has excellent stats, coupled with an extraordinarily high {{AP}} for its Point and Edge attacks. Namely it has a base {{AP}} of {{P|Attack 2 AP}}%, as opposed to the {{#expr:{{P|Attack 2 Damage}}*1.5 round 0}}% that would be automatically calculated from weapon damage. The {{AP}} still scales with [[quality]] but does not scale with its wielder's [[Melee Damage Factor]].

== Analysis ==
With its exceptional {{AP}} and a high {{DPS}}, the monosword is a strong and versatile melee weapon without the gimmicks of a [[zeushammer]] or [[plasmasword]]. While the [[persona weapon]]s are stronger, and craftable weapons like [[longsword]]s are much easier to farm for higher [[quality]], a high quality monosword is a good weapon in its own right.

While it does have exceptional {{AP}}, armor pieces tend to be more protective against sharp than blunt. Thus, against high tier armors that aren't negated by the either weapon's {{AP}}, the monosword is more [[Apparel#Protection|affected]] by armor than the zeushammer of the same quality. For example, a normal [[quality]] zeushammer would have a higher effective DPS against a normal quality set of [[cataphract armor]] than a normal quality monosword. When the monosword {{AP}} is sufficient to outright negate the armor, such as against [[centipede]]s or any [[mechanoid]], then the superior optimal DPS of the monosword would win out.
