The '''pollution pump''' is a building added by the [[Biotech DLC]] that gradually clears pollution in a radius around itself when powered.

== Acquisition ==
{{Acquisition}}

== Summary ==
[[File:Pollution pump radius.jpeg|thumb|left|The radius of the pollution pump]]
When powered, pollution pumps turn [[pollution]] on the ground into [[toxic wastepack]]s. It takes 200 W of [[power]] to function. It has a radius of 10.9 tiles. 
It removes 1 tile{{Check Tag|Which?|How is the next tile cleaned selected? Is it random, radially outwards from centre, something else?}}{{Check Tag|Answer|Tile appears to be selected radially counter clockwise moving outwards from the pump, based on one users experience.}} of pollution every {{Ticks|15000}}, or 4 tiles per game-day.
Once 6 tiles of pollution have been removed, it creates a wastepack, or a wastepack every 1.5 days assuming sufficient pollution is in range.

The wastepacks then have to be managed as normal, lest they dissolve back into pollution.

Pollution pumps are not considered artificial for purposes such as the [[anima tree]].{{RoyaltyIcon}}
