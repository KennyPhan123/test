'''Large mech rechargers''' are [[buildings]] added by the [[Biotech DLC]] that allow the recharging of medium, heavy, and ultraheavy [[mechanoids]]. 

== Acquisition ==
{{Acquisition}}

== Summary ==
Large mech rechargers constantly consume {{P|Power Consumption}} W of direct, electrical [[power]], even when not actively recharging a mech. They are placed on the ground and can't be minified, or uninstalled.

Mech rechargers can recharge medium, heavy, and ultraheavy [[mechanoid]]s. This includes:
<div class="ul-column-width-150">
* [[Lancer]]
* [[Pikeman]]
* [[Scorcher]]
* [[Scyther]]
* [[Legionary]]
* [[Tesseron]]
* [[Centipede burner]]
* [[Centipede gunner]]
* [[Tunneler]]

* [[Diabolus]]
* [[Centurion]]
* [[War queen]]
</div>

A recharger gives the mechanoid 50% of their ''power need'' per day. This accumulates the recharger's waste meter. When a recharger is full of waste, a hauler can pick up 5 [[toxic wastepack]]s from the recharger. The exact amount of waste technically depends on the mechanoid. The existence of waste does not stop the recharger.

Suitable mechs will seek a recharger once reaching a configurable amount of charge, and a [[mechanitor|control group]] of mechs can be commanded to recharge (or avoid recharging) at will.

== Analysis ==
The majority of heavy mechs are designed for combat. You can keep these mechs dormant until an actual combat situation arises, which saves a lot of power. In practice, a mech that is never damaged and turned off diligently can survive for months and years at a time, just using the power from dormant self-charging. If mechs are dormant for most of the time, then you can keep the recharger off, too. 

If your mechs never get hurt, and you aren't using a [[tunneler]] for mining, you can delay building this recharger indefinitely.

== Trivia ==
* If the charger is destroyed while charging the mech, the mech will go berserk.
