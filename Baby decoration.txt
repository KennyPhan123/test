The '''baby decoration''' is a [[furniture]] item added by the [[Biotech DLC]]

== Acquisition ==
{{Acquisition}}

== Summary ==
[[File:Baby decoration radius.png|thumb|left|200px|Baby decoration area of effect radius.]]
The Baby decoration increases the amount of [[Baby#Play|Play]] need gained when an adult plays with a [[baby]] by 10%. The bonus is multiplicative and does not stack with other baby decorations. A baby decoration only provides this bonus when it is in the same room as the baby and less than 9.9 tiles away.

== Analysis ==
Baby decorations provide a passive bonus rather than being objects that pawns interact with. Placing them near [[crib]]s or in areas where babies are commonly played with makes it more likely that babies remain within range. The bonus can be combined with a [[toy box]]: a baby decoration and a toy box together provide 137.5% of the normal Play gain. Multiple baby decorations do not stack with each other.
