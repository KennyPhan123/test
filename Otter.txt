An '''otter''' is an [[animal]] added by the [[Odyssey DLC]].

== Acquisition ==
{{PAGENAME}}s can be found in ?{{Habitats}}. They can either be tamed by a [[Work #Handle|handler]] or self-tame in a random event. 

{{PAGENAME}}s can be bought and sold in other [[Trade#Faction base|faction bases]] and from [[Trade#Exotic goods trader 2|exotic goods traders]]. {{PAGENAME}}s purchased from traders will be already tamed.

== Analysis ==
An otter after a sentience catalyst is applied is the lowest combat power animal capable of hauling, making it the most raid point efficient altered animal for hauling. Otters are considered small animals so predators on the map will hunt otters making them more risky haulers outside base walls than larger animals or lifters. Compared to the Labrador, the otter has 30 combat power vs 70, so you can have 2 otter haulers for less raid points and less food consumption .24 x 2 (.48) vs .64 than a Labrador. Otters and Labradors have the same temperature range and filth ratings, however because Labradors have zero wildness they require no training maintenance once they are trained vs the otters 25% post sentience catalyst. Labradors are also significantly faster 5 vs 3.5, nuzzles, have comfort training options and do not require a sentient catalyst to be able to haul.
