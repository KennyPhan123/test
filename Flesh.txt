'''Flesh''' is a type of removable naturally occurring [[floor]] that accompanies [[fleshmass]].

== Summary ==
Flesh can be found generating in the [[Undercave]] of the [[Pit gate]] entity and the [[Distress signal]] quest. It is most often found underneath preexisting fleshmass wall, but can be commonly seen in smaller patches adjacent to the fleshmass. It has a move speed factor of {{P|Move Speed Factor}}, the same as most natural terrain.

== Analysis ==
It is very ugly and dirty, reducing mood and increasing chances of infection alongside the already great amount of filth produced by fleshmass. As it is flammable, this can be a way to expedite floor removal, although with the temporary nature of most encounters with flesh terrain, the advantages of such are limited.
