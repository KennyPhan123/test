'''Hermetic crate''' are lootable [[ruins]] that appear as part of some [[DLC]] content. Glows slightly, has contents that it ejects when opened. 

== Occurrence ==
Hermetic crates can be mostly found inside [[ancient complex]]es. They can also be found at some [[Orbit|orbital locations]]{{OdysseyIcon}}. If [[Ideology DLC]] is enabled, [[ancient danger]]s contain one crate that holds the most valuable item generated as part of the floor loot.
== Summary ==
{{Loot Container Note}}

Hermetic crates drop one stack of an item when opened.

=== Loot ===
{{Recode|section=1|reason=Loot pool at regular ancient complexes. See discussion. Ideally all loot pools would have tables.}}
The items inside a hermetic crate depend on the structure or room where they are generated. At ancient dangers they share the floor loot pool, while at orbital sites they share the pool of [[sealed crate]]s,{{OdysseyIcon}} both of which are covered in their respective articles.
At ancient complexes hermetic crates can have one of two pools depending on whether it is a [[Quests#Relic Hunt|Relic hunt]] subquest or not. Generally speaking, crates from relic related complexes yield less quantities from a smaller pool of items, mostly limited to basic resources.

==== Ancient danger crates {{IdeologyIcon}} ====
When the [[Ideology]] DLC is present, the most valuable item stack from the floor loot is placed in a hermetic crate. The amount of loot varies wildly, so any of the following [[ancient danger]] loot items can end up in the crate. Loot can also be absent, in which case there is no hermetic crate.
{{:Ancient shrine/Loot}}

==== Orbital site crates {{OdysseyIcon}} ====
These crates share the pool of [[sealed crate]]s.

==== Odyssey ruins crates {{OdysseyIcon}} ====
These crates are most often found in orbital wrecks but can also appear in the following Odyssey ruins: [[Landmarks#Ruins|ancient ruins]] and [[frozen ruins]] landmarks, as well as ruins found naturally in the [[scarlands]] and [[glacial plain]] maps. They can also be found at the site of the [[Quests#Ancient Reactor|ancient reactor]] quest.
