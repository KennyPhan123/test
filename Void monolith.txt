The '''void monolith''' is a structure that is the center point of the [[Anomaly DLC]]. It is used to enable in encounters with most [[entities]].

== Acquisition ==

The monolith appears on all new colony maps by default. If you have selected the "Ambient Horror" mode of the [[storyteller]] settings, the monolith '''will not be available'''.

If you choose the [[Scenario_system#The_Anomaly|Anomaly starting scenario]], the monolith will spawn right next to your crash landing spot, instead of its usual and distanced spot, allowing you to form a base around it easier.

If the monolith is enabled in storyteller settings, but there are no monolith on your maps for any reason (like move to a new map or add the DLC in the middle of another playthrough), you will receive a "Strange Signal" quest, which will spawn a monolith on your map when you accept it. The monolith's spawn point is random, and it may destroy existing structures when it arrives.

The table follows the in-game inspect text. The ''Embraced'' ending uses internal monolith level 5, but still displays "Level 4: Awakened"; the collapsed ''Disrupted'' state uses internal level 6 and has no numbered inspect text.

These stages are saved in the world; losing the monolith and getting the "Strange Signal" quest spawns a monolith of the same level.

The monolith must be attuned or investigated after each step to advance, allowing you to decide when to progress. If you chose the [[Scenario_system#The_Anomaly|Anomaly starting scenario]], the monolith will ''automatically'' move to Level 1 on its own a few days after you begin the game.

The monolith can be [[work|studied]] every two days as a source of either basic or advanced Anomaly tech tree research points.

The monolith separates rooms and provides total cover (like a wall), and it is invulnerable to all damage and fire. [[Raiders]] will ignore the monolith. When the monolith expands in size, it will uninstall, deconstruct or destroy any structures in the way. However, any connected power sources will continue to generate power until the power network is updated again. (This is almost certainly a bug.)

Depending on which path you select during the [[Monolith endgame|Anomaly ending quest]], the monolith will either collapse or remain awakened. In either case, it can still be studied indefinitely for advanced research points.

== Analysis ==
{{For|dealing with the monlith awakening at level 3|monolith endgame}}

The monolith is your only permanent source of Anomaly research points. However once the monolith study has been completed, you can research captured entities instead to avoid having to travel to the monolith. Studying the monolith or entities is done through the "Dark Study" job on the [[work|Work tab]].

You may want to build your base near the monolith to reduce the travelling time to it.

=== Meditation ===
The void monolith is the strongest [[Psyfocus#Void|Void]] [[meditation focus]],{{RoyaltyIcon}} providing a +30%/day [[Meditation Psyfocus Bonus|psyfocus bonus]]. Its focus-bearing center occupies 3×3 tiles (9 tiles) at every level. At level 1, two impassable side attachments add 6 tiles, filling a 5×3 rectangle (15 tiles). At levels 2–4 and in the ''Embraced'' ending (internal level 5), the top attachment and two front corners add another 5 tiles. The structure then spans a 5×5 bounding rectangle but occupies only 20 of its 25 tiles: 9 center tiles and 11 attachment tiles. The front center remains open.

The attachments added at levels 1 and 2 can block sight to the central building, which is the meditation focus. Because a [[meditation spot]] must have line of sight to its focus, the number of candidate tiles decreases as the monolith advances. On clear, standable ground, there are 68 candidate tiles at level 0, 34 at level 1, and 4 from level 2 through the ''[[Void Awakening (ending)#Embrace the Void|Embraced]]'' ending. Their locations are shown below.

[[File:Void_monolith_meditation_spot_tiles_by_stage.png|thumb|center|800px|Candidate meditation spot tiles by monolith stage on clear, standable ground.]]

The game's line-of-sight algorithm causes an asymmetric distribution of candidate tiles. On the southwest side of the level 2 monolith, one diagonal sight line passes through the front opening when it crosses a tile boundary. The mirrored line crosses the right-front attachment, so the mirrored tile is not a candidate spot. Other obstacles or inaccessible ground can reduce these counts.

===Theoretical infinite energy generation===
By placing power generators near the left or right side of the monolith, and power conduits adjacent to the left or rightmost side of the power generator. And activatting the monolith, one can achieve infinite power, in addition to that. The full cost of the generator in resources will also be returned. The power still remains in the network and can be used by various appliances, however the phantom power generators will disappear as soon as the power network is updated in any way, either by adding or removing buildings including conduits. This is almost certainly a bug.
