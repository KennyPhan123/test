'''Dandelions''' are a common [[beauty|beautiful]] flower that can be sown but also grows naturally in {{Habitats}}. 

== Acquisition == 
Dandelions can be sown by [[Work#Grow|growers]] in [[growing zone]]s. Sowing requires {{Ticks|{{P|Sow Work}}}} of work. Unlike [[rose]]s and [[daylilies]] they cannot be planted in [[plant pot]]s.

=== Animal feed ===
Dandelions have a niche as a fast-growing plant for animals to graze on inside [[pens]], for which they are almost strictly better than [[Rice plant|rice]]. However, dandelions should only be used if animal feed is needed urgently, as they are far inferior to [[Corn plant|corn]] and [[haygrass]] in terms of work per nutrition, and are inferior to haygrass in terms of nutrition per tile per day. Immature dandelions are worth less than the base {{P|Nutrition}} nutrition and will be eaten by animals just the same, lowering dandelion's work efficiency even further.

* A dandelion takes {{Ticks|{{P|Sow Work}}}} to sow and is worth {{P|Nutrition}} nutrition if allowed to grow to maturity, or {{Ticks|{{P|Sow Work}} / {{P|Nutrition}} round 2}} work per nutrition.
* [[Haygrass]] takes {{Ticks|{{Q|Haygrass|Sow Work}} + {{Q|Haygrass|Harvest Work}}}} to both sow and harvest, for {{#expr:{{Q|Haygrass|Harvest Yield}} * {{Q|Hay|Nutrition}}}} nutrition per plant, resulting in {{Ticks|({{Q|Haygrass|Sow Work}} + {{Q|Haygrass|Harvest Work}}) / ({{Q|Haygrass|Harvest Yield}} * {{Q|Hay|Nutrition}}) round 2}} work per nutrition.

Overall, haygrass takes {{Good|{{%|({{Q|Haygrass|Sow Work}} + {{Q|Haygrass|Harvest Work}}) / ({{Q|Haygrass|Harvest Yield}} * {{Q|Hay|Nutrition}}) / ({{P|Sow Work}} / {{P|Nutrition}}) round 3}}}} as much work as fully grown dandelions. [[Corn]] is even better for work efficiency. Some time is needed to haul the hay/corn to the pen, but extra travel time is also required for growers to come into the pen and constantly plant dandelions.

=== Beauty ===
Dandelions are modestly beautiful. While they provide comparatively little beauty per plant, each dandelion lasts for 20 days, which cuts down on work. If you have a big room with a dirt floor and open roof, they can be viable for raising the rooms beauty. In smaller rooms, the higher beauty per tile of roses or daylilies will be preferred.
