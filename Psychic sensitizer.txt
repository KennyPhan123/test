The '''psychic sensitizer''' is an implant in the [[Royalty DLC]] that gives a pawn that's been implanted with it a bonus of 25% [[Psychic Sensitivity]].

== Acquisition ==
Psychic sensitizers cannot be crafted. Instead they are only available as a [[Quest]] reward and through trade, specifically from [[Empire]] traders, Empire and [[Outlander]] [[faction base]]s, and Exotic Goods traders.

They can also be found implanted into some Imperial forces, namely [[Empire#Bestower|Bestowers]], [[Empire#Stellic warden|Stellic Wardens]], and [[Empire#Stellic defender|Stellic Defenders]]. If captured Alive, these pawns can be recruited or the implants removed to allow access to them.

== Summary ==
Once implanted in the brain, the psychic sensitizer additively increases the [[psychic sensitivity]] of the implantee by {{Up|+25%}}. That is, a normal pawn would have their sensitivity increase from 100% to 125%, while a [[Psychically hypersensitive]] pawn would have their sensitivity increase from 180% to 205%. 

In turn this increases the neural heat of the pawn.
{{Psychic Sensitivity Quality Table|0.25|noQuality=1}}

Psychic sensitivity also affects a number of other mechanics. For further details, see the [[psychic sensitivity]] page.

Unlike some other brain implants, it does not carry the risk of [[brain shock]] when the implantee is hit by [[EMP]] damage.

=== Installation ===
Installing the part requires {{Ticks|2500}} of work, 2x medicine of [[Herbal medicine|herbal]] quality or better, and a [[Skills#Medical|Medical skill]] of 5.

Removing the part requires {{Ticks|2500}} of work, 1x medicine of [[Herbal medicine|herbal]] quality or better, and a [[Skills#Medical|Medical skill]] of 5.

If the operation fails, the part will be destroyed.

== Analysis ==
The psychic sensitizer is most useful on psycasters, who can appreciate a small yet unique boost to neural heat limit. It is also useful on pawns with [[psychic harmonizer]]s. In addition, it has a tiny but non-zero effect on [[psychic reader]] and [[psychic ritual]]s{{AnomalyIcon}} effectiveness. Otherwise, psychic sensitivity is usually a downside due to the increased danger of psychic drones.

It is worth acquiring for psycasters at some point, but in the early and midgame, it should be a lesser priority than more tangible items (e.g., [[psytrainer]]s, [[doomsday rocket launcher]]s, [[component]]s). Harmonizers should be a lesser priority, with it only providing {{+|2}} mood before any sensitivity multipliers.

On a pawn that already has a [[psychic reader]] and 20 Social skill, the psychic sensitizer will increase [[Trade Price Improvement]] stat by flat {{+|0.75%}} before any multipliers. In order to make up the 1k market value, that pawn would need to buy/sell goods worth a total of {{Icon Small|silver}} 133333 [[silver]] (133k). By the time that is possible, 1k is a drop in the bucket, so it does not make any real impact. [[Bioferrite]]{{AnomalyIcon}} equipment can multiply the boost, but the broader point remains true.
