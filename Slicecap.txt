'''Slicecaps''' are a form of [[clothing]] added by the [[Ideology DLC]]. A form of headwear, it covers less and provides very little protection and insulation compared to other options.

== Acquisition ==
{{Acquisition}}

They can also be acquired through [[trade]].

== Analysis ==
The slicecap is lackluster in nearly every way. It has abysmal protection - with even high [[quality]] [[plasteel]] slicecaps offering practically negligible armor. Its insulative ability is similarly bad. Notably, it also covers the least of any headwear item, only covering the head while even hats also cover the ears. Its one strong point is cost, but even there it is tied for cheapest headwear for a given [[stuff]] by the superior-in-every-way [[visage mask]].

There is essentially no reason to ever use the slicecap unless it is required by an [[ideoligion]] or for aesthetic reasons.
