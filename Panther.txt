'''Panthers''' are medium-sized [[predator]]s, and good guardians when [[tame]]d.

They are effectively identical to the [[cougar]], only differing in appearance, habitat, and comfortable temperature range.

== Acquisition ==
{{PAGENAME}}s can be found in {{Habitats}}. They can either be tamed by a [[Work #Handle|handler]] or self-tame in a random event. 

{{PAGENAME}}s can be bought and sold in other [[Trade#Faction base|faction bases]] and from [[Trade#Exotic goods trader 2|exotic goods traders]]. {{PAGENAME}}s purchased from traders will be already tamed.

== Analysis ==
Panthers and cougars can make for good hauling animals and decent attack animals, if you can keep them fed. While they are more wild than a [[husky]], meaning they create more [[filth]] and need to be retrained, panthers will not be attacked by other predators. You can feed them off meals, and when using meals, they are actually more efficient than huskies when it comes for hunger rate. However, you'll need raw meat or [[kibble]] to train them.

== Trivia ==
* In real life, panthers are not a distinct species, and [[Wikipedia:Black panther|black panthers]] are simply melanistic anomalies of the ''[[Wikipedia:Panthera|Panthera]]'' genus, usually in leopards or jaguars.
