'''Electric inhibitors''' are buildings added by [[Anomaly DLC]] that improve the [[containment]] of [[holding platform]].

== Acquisition ==
{{Acquisition}}

== Summary == 
{{Stub|section=1|reason=Detail, Positioning, order of operation for containment etc}}
[[File:Electric_inhibitor_positioning.png|thumb|left|Showing electric inhibitor effective distance and blocking. Note that the inhibitor on the left is too far from the holding platform and the one in the top right is blocked by the inhibitor in front of it.]]
Each electric inhibitors increases [[containment]] of a [[holding platform]] by {{+|10}} up to a maximum of {{+|60}} with 6 inhibitors.

== Analysis ==
{{Stub|section=1|reason=Largely missing anaylsis}} 

Requiring a 1 tile buffer zone to be placed and being 2 tiles long usually will mean a containment area that has to be either redesigned or designed with the placement of the inhibitors in mind. However despite the large space requirement they are a relatively cheap and easy way of containing higher level entities. 

For basic entities, they usually aren't  needed as a basic wooden and lit room with a holding platform are able to meet the containment difficulty (Exceptions being a [[Gorehulk]] and [[Toughspike]]). However Inhibitors will be necessary for [[Bioferrite]] harvesting, with 2 being enough to offset it and provide a net gain of {{+|5}} Strength.
