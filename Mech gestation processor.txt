'''Mech gestation processor''' are [[artificial body parts]] added by the [[Biotech DLC]] that increase [[Mech Gestation Speed]] of a [[mechanitor]]. 

== Acquisition ==
{{Acquisition}}

== Summary ==
Each mech gestation processor increases [[Mech Gestation Speed]] of the implanted [[mechanitor]] by {{+|33%}}. Mech gestation processors have a [[max install count]] of six, meaning up to six processors can be installed in each mechanitor's brain. Each processor stacks additively as if it were {{+|33.3&#773;3&#773;%}}, with the end result rounded down.

=== Installation ===
{{Stub|section=1|reason=See other bionics}}
Any Mechanitor with Mechlink installed, can install the Mech gestation processor by themselves, it takes roughly 3 seconds to implant, no surgery needed.

== Analysis ==
Mech gestation processors offer a significant benefit to mech creation and revival, allowing spare signal chips to be used effectively. [[Bandwidth pack]]s are likely the most important priority for signal chips due to their large and permanent boost to bandwidth. [[Mechcommander helmet]]s and [[diaboli]] also deserve unique consideration. Afterwards, the use of signal chips will depend on the colony's chokepoints and needs - mech gestation processors help if the colony needs to make new mechs or revive them, while [[control sublink (standard)|control sublinks]] if the colony needs more mech workspeed.

As mechanitors in your colony can exchange mechanoids with each other, it makes sense to give one mechanitor all the gestators, then have that mechanitor gestate all the mechs in the colony. The other mechanitors can take control once gestation is done.

Best case scenario for the processors: Diabolus has the most cycles at {{Q|Diabolus|Gestation Cycles}}. At crafting skill 0 that that takes {{#expr: 2 * {{Q|Diabolus|Gestation Cycles}}/0.75}} days. With 6 processors, it takes {{#expr: 2 * {{Q|Diabolus|Gestation Cycles}}/2.75 round 2}} days. It is significant if you are losing mechs often, even with a highly skilled crafter and multiple gestators running in parallel. However, if you're not, you should spend your signal chips on all the other upgrades they unlock first, with the possible exception of the [[mechcommander helmet]] which might already be outmoded by the [[mechlord helmet]] before you get to it.
