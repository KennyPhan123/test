A '''{{PAGENAME}}''' is an [[animal]] added by the [[Odyssey DLC]].

== Acquisition ==
{{PAGENAME}}s can be found in {{Habitats}}. They can either be tamed by a [[Work #Handle|handler]] or self-tame in a random event. 

== Summary ==
A female swan produces {{P|Eggs Per Clutch Average}} [[swan egg]] every {{P|Egg Laying Interval}} day when fertilized. Swans will not lay unfertilized eggs.
