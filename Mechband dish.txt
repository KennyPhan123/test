A '''mechband dish''' is a [[building]] added by the [[Biotech DLC]] that allows a [[mechanitor]] to summon the [[apocriton]].

== Acquisition ==
{{Acquisition}}

== Summary ==
{{Stub|section=1|reason=How long until it arrives. Activation method etc.}}
A mechband dish can be activated by a [[mechanitor]] to summon the hostile [[apocriton]], which will drop a [[nano structuring chip]] when defeated. Once activated, it will self destruct after {{Ticks|120}} leaving {{Icon Small|steel slag chunk||5}} [[steel slag chunk]]s.

It cannot be used in the time between another mechanoid boss being summoned and that boss arriving. In addition, there is a 2 day cooldown between summoning any mech boss.
