'''Burrow wall''' is a natural wall created upon map generation to form the walls of hives in [[infested settlement]]s and [[insect lair]]s.

== Summary ==
The Burrow wall itself is relatively weak and flammable, where in destroying one wall will also destroy multiple nearby and connected chunks of wall at a time. The wall is also quickly targetable via melee or ranged weapon, and the associated pawn(s) will continue to attack nearby burrow wall after the initial target is destroyed. This leads to less initial micromanagement but may also result in unwanted wall destruction or pawns getting 'distracted' from your intended target. 

They are created upon map generation and remain static, and serve no real threat other than acting as a physical barrier that can conceal insectoids within unrevealed areas.
