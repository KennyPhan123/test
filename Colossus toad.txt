A '''colossus toad''' is an [[animal]] added by the [[Odyssey DLC]].

== Acquisition ==
{{PAGENAME}}s can be found in {{Habitats}}. They can either be tamed by a [[Work #Handle|handler]] or self-tame in a random event. 

{{PAGENAME}}s can be bought and sold in other [[Trade#Faction base|faction bases]] and from [[Trade#Exotic goods trader 2|exotic goods traders]]. {{PAGENAME}}s purchased from traders will be already tamed.

== Summary ==
{{Stub|section=1|reason=needs at least mention of lack of eggs and traditional amphibian lifecycle}}
Despite the description mentioning the colossus toad being covered in unpleasant slime they do not cause any debuff on your colonists.

== Analysis ==
{{Stub|section=1|reason=Analysis stub}}
A large breed of Toad that has been bioengineered for domestic farming/ranching. They are easy to tame with a minimum handling of 2.
They do not require a pen and only an area zoned for them to roam in.

They make very useful tames in the glow forest for raising for meat or to be trained as attack dogs, having modest attack and are somewhat durable but can often be used in large numbers to overwhelm enemies even in the early game due to how fast they breed and mature.
