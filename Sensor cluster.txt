The '''sensor cluster''' is a [[ship]] part that is necessary for the ship to launch. It serves no other purpose.

== Acquisition ==
{{Acquisition}}

It also appears as part of the ship featured in the [[ship to the stars]] quest.

== Analysis ==
You can delay building the sensor cluster until the [[ship reactor]] is fully activated, which can reduce wealth, and therefore [[raid points]].
