The '''mini-flameblaster''' is an [[mechanoid]]-only ranged [[weapon]] added by the [[Biotech DLC]]. It is the main armament of the [[scorcher]]. 

This weapon cannot be independently obtained in normal play. It is provided here for comparisons sake.

== Acquisition ==
Mini-flameblasters spawn on all [[scorcher]]s, including those gestated by a colony. However, it is not possible to obtain it directly in normal play. Pawns carrying it will not drop it on [[death]], [[downing]], or complete loss of [[manipulation]] like other weapons. It can be spawned in by the [[Development mode#Debug actions menu|Debug actions menu]] but, like all [[unobtainable items|unobtainable weapons]], will disappear after being dropped. It can also be selected as one of the items to migrate with when selling colony and proceeding to take your allowed 7 items with you and can then be used. (inconclusive information regarding drop disappearance when acquired this way, pawn carrying it was downed while traveling and it did disappear but not sure if it would have when dropped on map, if someone knows this edit this out)

== Summary ==
Has one of the shortest ranges in the game, at 4.9 tiles. However, this weapon can be fired in melee combat, a mostly unique property for ranged weapons. Creates [[fire]] on pawns.
