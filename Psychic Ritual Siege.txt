A group of [[Horax cult]]ists arrive nearby and will attempt to use a [[psychic ritual]] to either abduct a colonist or spawn [[fleshbeasts]]. They may attempt to perform multiple rituals.

This event is fired as a major threat, requires at least 200 [[Raid points]] and can be fired before interacting with the monolith. The event requires Anomaly DLC

== Summary ==
The cultists will choose a spot nearby to cast their ritual. Only the caster is required to perform the ritual and all other cultists will wander around and defend. Damage or interruption to the caster will interrupt the ritual and cause the cultists to attack directly. 

The cultists can perform either 4 abductions or 4 fleshbeast summons. After performing four rituals the cultists will attack like a standard raid. The cultists do not attempt to kidnap those abducted with skip abduct. The ritual can fail for unknown reasons and cause the cultists to attack at seemingly anytime. The ritual can even fail before the cultists setup their first ritual.

The summon fleshbeast ritual always summons fleshbeast near a colonist. The fleshbeasts summoned by the ritual are hostile to the Horax cultists.

== Strategy ==
The cultists will attack as soon as the leader performing the ritual gets hurt. Therefore the ritual can be interrupted by firing mortars at the leader or hurting them by other means (psycasts etc.) The defending cultists will attack approaching colonists making sniping a rather risky option. It can also be easier to set up formation close to the ritual and lure and kill some cultists during the ritual in order to make the amount of attackers at the same time more manageable.
