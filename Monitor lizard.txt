A '''monitor lizard''' is an [[animal]] added by the [[Odyssey DLC]].

== Acquisition ==

{{PAGENAME}}s can be found in {{Habitats}}. They can either be tamed by a [[Work #Handle|handler]] or self-tame in a random event. 

{{PAGENAME}}s can be bought and sold in other [[Trade#Faction base|faction bases]], [[Trade#Exotic goods trader 2|exotic goods traders]] or [[Trade#Bulk goods trader 2|bulk goods traders]]. {{PAGENAME}}s purchased from traders will be already tamed.
