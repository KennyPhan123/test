The '''Shard inhibitor''' is a shard of archotechnology being utilized to increase the [[containment strength]] of your [[Holding platform]]s.

== Acquisition ==
{{Acquisition}}

== Summary ==
{{Stub|section=1|reason=general summary info}} 
Increases containment by {{+|20}} for all [[holding spot]]s and [[holding platform]]s within a 5 tile radius - the middle cell of the holding spot/platform needs to be within the radius. Works through walls, a well placed shard beacon can cover multiple holding platforms in multiple cells.

== Analysis ==
Since a [[security door]] and stone walls are usually sufficient for containment needs, and a shard inhibitor uses a valuable [[shard]], they are not a very big priority to build. Still, they can be used in the early game before security doors are available, or when containment is especially needed (such as against [[metalhorror]]s).
