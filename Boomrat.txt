{{Info|'''Boomrats''' are small, omnivorous [[animals]] that create fire-starting explosion whenever they die, including when they are slaughtered.}}

== Acquisition ==
{{PAGENAME}}s can be found in {{Habitats}}. They can either be tamed by a [[Work #Handle|handler]] or self-tame in a random event. 

{{PAGENAME}}s can be bought and sold in other [[Trade#Faction base|faction bases]] and from [[Trade#Exotic goods trader 2|exotic goods traders]]. {{PAGENAME}}s purchased from traders will be already tamed.

{{Starting Pet Note}}

== Summary ==
On death, boomrats explode, doing 10 [[Damage Types#Flame|Flame]] damage in a 1.9 tile radius. This explosion does not itself set fires nor create [[Filth#Chemfuel puddle|chemfuel puddles]] to burn but the Flame damage might ignite surrounding objects and pawns.

Boomrats have 90% [[Toxic Environment Resistance]] rendering them highly resistant to environmental effects such as [[rot stink]] and [[toxic buildup]] from non-attack sources.

== Analysis ==
They are a smaller version of the [[boomalope]] and spawn in areas where the ground is mostly flammable. Like the boomalope, they are a punishment for being in an area with a long or year round growing season, and are very dangerous to settle near for a [[permadeath]] settlement as a [[manhunter]] pack of them can create a devastating fire with no hope of being stopped, that will eventually melt through steel. It can be very hard for a colony to put the fire out until it rains. They will also eat [[Domesticated Plants|crops]] if given the chance.

Consider avoiding them, and keeping them healthy. Boomrats are best hunted in the [[rain]], which will extinguish the fire. Alternatively, they can be tamed and sold, or slaughtered in an area with no risk of fire, such as lake, or in a room of stone floors and walls. 

=== As attack animals ===
Tamed boomalopes are considered [[pen animals]], meaning that raiders won't attack them. Boomrats, however, are not.

They can be used as an explosive weapon, especially when combined with [[transport pod]]s. Load a pod with some boomrats, some stronger explosives (like [[mortar shell]]s or a proper boomalope), and land it on top of stationary enemies. [[Siege]]s are prime targets, as you can set their own shells on fire.

=== As organic mines ===
You can create a new area where a raid appears and assign some of your boomrats to that location. They'll quickly move to their new assigned area and will get attacked by the raiders, making them explode and causing chain reactions if you sent enough.

Due to their relatively quick reproductive cycle, any lost boomrats will be quickly replenished. However, keeping enough rats for them to be viable will noticeably affect your food supply, make sure you have enough food to afford doing this, and to cull the population whenever it starts outstripping your food production.

== Lore ==
<onlyinclude>The origin of the boomrat has varied over time. In the initial release of the [[Lore#Longsleep_Revival_Briefing_.28Obsolete.29|Revival Briefing]], which is now considered non-canon, they were described as originating as a biological weapon that survived and bred after wars on the worlds they now inhabit. The [[Lore#Cryptosleep_Revival_Briefing|current version]] of the Revival Briefing states that they, along with the [[boomalope]], were originally engineered as a primitive renewable fuel source. While this holds true for boomalopes, boomrats cannot and could never be milked for [[chemfuel]] in-game.  Meanwhile, their current in-game description also disagrees with the current briefing and supports the now-obsolete original version, stating that their explosive tendencies are either a result of deliberate weaponization or intended as a defense mechanism.

Boomrats are likely one of the chemfuel-producing animals mentioned in the [[boomshroom]]'s description as being engineered from the boomshroom's genetic material.

Boomrat pizza is briefly mentioned in a backer pawn backstory. <ref>[[List of Player-created Pawns#Robert Krondorfer|Gnugfur]]</ref></onlyinclude>
