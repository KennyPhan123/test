A '''lava snail''' is a slow, armored [[animal]] added by the [[Odyssey DLC]].

== Acquisition ==
{{PAGENAME}}s can be found in {{Habitats}}. They can either be tamed by a [[Work #Handle|handler]] or self-tame in a random event. 

{{PAGENAME}}s can be bought and sold in other [[Trade#Faction base|faction bases]] and from [[Trade#Exotic goods trader 2|exotic goods traders]]. {{PAGENAME}}s purchased from traders will be already tamed.

== Summary ==
{{Pen Animal Note|Lava snails}}

== Analysis ==
Their low hunger rate makes them superior to pigs in term of meat per feed. Their optimal meat output exceeds their food requirement.

However, their slow speed makes roping them between the grazing area and the gravship quite time consuming, and their temperature requirements mean they must be kept in a heated enclosure anywhere that it goes below {{Temperature|0}}. Conversely, they are very resilient to high temperatures, making them a viable addition to even extreme desert colonies where other animals would require cooling to survive- their maximum comfortable temperature is so high that starvation will be an issue before heatstroke, as plants will stop growing before they begin to suffer. 

Additionally, they do not produce any leather or other animal products, limiting their utility compared to other farm animals.
