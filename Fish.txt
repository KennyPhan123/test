'''Fish''' is a type of [[raw food]] obtained through [[fishing]] in bodies of water. It counts as [[meat]] for purposes of cooking [[meals]] and the [[Meat eating]] precept.{{IdeologyIcon}}

== Acquisition ==
Fish can be obtained by fishing at fishing zones after the [[Research#Fishing|Fishing]] research project is completed.
The specific species of fish available are displayed when hovering the cursor over a body of water.
