The '''salvagers''' are a [[Tech level|spacer tech level]] hidden [[faction]] added by the [[Odyssey DLC]]. Like the [[Traders guild]], they operate entirely within the space layer.<br>
They also function similarly to [[pirates]] - their [[goodwill]] is locked at {{Bad|-100}}; they are permanently hostile.
== Lore ==
<blockquote><small>''A ruthless faction of orbital scavengers who scour space for derelict ships and valuable wreckage. Salvagers operate entirely in space.'' - '''In Game Description'''</small></blockquote>
