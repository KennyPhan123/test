The '''ship structural beam''' is a [[ship]] part that connects all other parts, transmits [[power]], and hosts the [[ship cryptosleep casket]]s. 

== Acquisition ==
{{Acquisition}}

They cannot be built under a [[roof]], that is at least 5 out of the 12 squares it occupies must be uncovered. However, a roof can be constructed afterwards.

They also appears as part of the ship featured in the [[ship to the stars]] quest.

== Summary ==
Ship structural beams allow the building of [[ship cryptosleep casket]]s. Each beam can support up to 8 caskets. They can also connect other ship parts together, though are not required to do so.

== Analysis ==
At least one beam is required to host the ship cryptosleep caskets. In order to conserve resources when building the ship:
* Keep as much open space for caskets. Only 1 tile needs to be connected to the rest of the ship. Using both tiles of the width will waste a casket spot, which is relevant if you are planning to send more than 7 colonists up to space.
* Directly connect as many of the ship parts to each other as possible. The [[ship reactor]] in particular has enough surface to connect all the other ship parts together. Unless terrain is a restriction, there is no need to place beams for any other ship part.

Like the other ship parts, you can activate the [[ship reactor]] before the rest of the ship is completed. This both saves on [[raid points]], as material gathering and construction adds to [[wealth]], and limits what has to be protected during the raids.

Beams are also necessary if ship caskets are wanted for uses outside of the ship building, such as using its auto-eject feature to store colonists.
