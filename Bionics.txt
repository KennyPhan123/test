[[File:Health item bionic.png|frame|right|The icon used for bionic-tier artificial body parts.]]{{For|all types of body part replacement|artificial body parts}}'''Bionics''' may refer to one of several different [[body parts|body part]] replacements unlocked by the [[Research#Bionic replacements|Bionic replacement]] research project. These are:
* [[Bionic arm]]
* [[Bionic eye]]
* [[Bionic ear]]
* [[Bionic heart]]
* [[Bionic leg]]
* [[Bionic spine]]
* [[Bionic stomach]]
* [[Bionic jaw]]
* [[Bionic tongue]]

They are equal or superior to normal body parts, and provide a bridge between the inferior-to-natural [[prosthetics]] and the superior but uncraftable [[archotech]] parts. Not all body parts are represented in every tier.

'''Bionics''' is also used informally to refer to [[artificial body parts]] generally, but is not used as such in-game.
