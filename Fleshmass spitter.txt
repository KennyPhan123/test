'''Fleshmass spitter''' acts as a medium range mortar that defends the [[Fleshmass heart]].

== Occurrence ==
One Fleshmass spitter spawns with the initial growth spurt of the fleshmass heart, with an additional one spawning each time a new [[Nerve bundle]] spawns.
