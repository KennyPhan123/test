The '''traders guild''' is a [[Tech level|spacer tech level]] [[faction]] added by the [[Odyssey DLC]]. It has bases only in orbit.

Because their faction bases only have hostile actions and [[transport pod]]s cannot be sent to them, they cannot be made non-hostile via gifts, and instead other options to improve faction relations like [[quests]] and releasing their prisoners are required.{{Check Tag|Detail and Verify}}

== Lore ==
<blockquote><small>''A loose coalition of orbital traders. Together they form an orbital marketplace that buys and sells goods from around the planet. Orbital traders will also buy goods from the occasional interplanetary trading vessel, giving them access to rare technologies.<br>These merchants are rarely found on the planet itself, preferring to live in the safety of their orbital platforms.'' - '''In Game Description'''</small></blockquote>

== Faction bases ==
{{Stub|section=1|reason=Missing faction base section}}

Traders guild faction bases only appear in [[orbit]]. It is impossible to trade with or gift items to a traders guild faction base; they can only trade through [[trade ship]]s. Attacking their faction base requires a [[gravship]] with a [[signal jammer]]. Additionally, polluting asteroids will count as asteroid pollution and anger the faction.
