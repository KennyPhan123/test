'''Snow''' refers to two intrinsically linked mechanics - weather of that name and the terrain covering that it creates. Snow on the ground is created on [[roof|unroofed]] areas during snowy weather, i.e. "rain" when the [[temperature]] is freezing. It will cover solid [[terrain]] but will not cover water of any type, including marsh.

It can be removed by [[fire]], [[steam geyser]]s, [[lightning]], [[colonist]]s when designated with the [[Zone#Snow clear area|Snow Clear Zone]], and by temperatures above {{Temperature|0}}.

Colonists may build [[snowman|snowmen]] as a [[recreation]]al activity when there is snow on the ground.

== Weather ==
There are two types of snowy [[weather]]: gentle snow and hard snow. These can occur at temperatures of {{Temperature|-0.5}} and below.

== Melting ==
The melting rate of snow from ambient temperature is found using the following table:

{| class="wikitable"
! Temperature range
! Melting rate (depth %/tick)
|-
| {{Temperature|0}} and below
| 0
|-
| Between {{Temperature|0}} to {{Temperature|10}}
| 0.058% of temperature²
|-
| {{Temperature|10}} and above
| 0.58% of temperature
|}

As a result, snow will not be melted by ambient temperatures of {{Temperature|0}} or less, and the higher the temperature the faster the snow will melt.
