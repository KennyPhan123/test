'''Grav engines''' are buildings added by the [[Odyssey DLC]] that are required to create a [[gravship]]. Only one grav engine is obtainable at a time per playthrough.

== Acquisition ==
A singular grav engine is obtained from a mechanoid ship that crashes onto the map after accepting a quest, alongside corpses of various mechanoids, [[mechanoid ship chunk]]s, and a large quantity of [[Gravlite panel|gravlite panels]]. Alternatively, one is included in ''The Gravship'' [[starting scenario]].

If the initial grav engine is lost, stolen, or destroyed, the [[Quest#Gravship Wreckage|gravship wreckage quest]] provides a map location in which a replacement may be fought for.

== Summary == 
[[File:Grav engine substructure radius.png|240px|thumb|left|Grav engine substructure radius visualized.]]

A grav engine is necessary to build a [[gravship]]. The player must place [[gravship substructure]] under and around the grav engine to construct the frame of the gravship, upon which other buildings can be constructed. Buildings on top of substructure connected to the grav engine will be taken with the gravship when it launches.

By default, a grav engine allows up to 500 substructure tiles to be placed in a radius of 18.9 cells. Installing [[grav field extender]]s will increase the substructure count limit by 250 tiles each, up to a maximum of 6 grav field extenders. Each extender has its own radius of 16 cells and must be placed inside the initial radius of the grav engine.

A grav engine cannot be deconstructed, only uninstalled and re-installed. If the grav engine lands outside the buildable area of the map, it can be uninstalled to be moved inside the buildable area.

If installed on a temporary map, it will prevent the map from being abandoned if all colonists leave or are downed.

== Analysis ==
A grav engine is trivially easy to obtain, as the original quest to obtain it appears early on and has no requirements. This allows the player to quickly build a [[gravship]], since a grav engine is necessary to build one. However, a player is only allowed to have one grav engine at a time, and thus, one gravship at a time. Future quests to obtain a grav engine will not generate as long as the player has a grav engine.
