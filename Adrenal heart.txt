'''Adrenal hearts''' are [[ghoul]] exclusive [[artificial body parts]] added by the [[Anomaly DLC]]. They replace the organic heart and provide the ability to go into a frenzy with increased combat ability, at the expense of making its user require more food. 

== Acquisition ==
{{Acquisition}}

It can sometimes spawn on hostile [[ghoul]]s, however they cannot be harvested from them.

== Summary == 
[[File:Gizmo_adrenal_heart_ghoul_frenzy.png|left|thumb|The gizmo used to activate "Ghoul Frenzy"]]
{{Quote|This creature's blood is saturated with stress hormones and liquid energy, permitting it to move and attack at incredible speeds for a short time.|Ghoul frenzy hediff description}}

The adrenal heart is a [[ghoul]] exclusive body part. It has a part efficiency of 100%. This fully replaces the functionality of a normal heart but provides no statistical enhancement - when coupled with the heart body part having a [[Blood Pumping]] importance of 100%, it results in a directly proportional increase in blood pumping when replacing a heart with impaired efficiency. Blood pumping, in turn, affects [[Moving]]. Moving, in turn, affects the following [[stats]]: {{#ask: [[Moving Importance::+]]}}. For further details, see those pages.

Pawns with the heart installed have access to the "ghoul frenzy" ability while [[draft]]ed. The frenzy must be manually initiated by the player through use of a gizmo available while the ghoul is drafted and selected. Colonist ghouls will never independently use the frenzy. NPC ghouls will automatically use the ability in combat.
The frenzy lasts for {{Ticks|900}} and provides the following effect:
* [[Melee Cooldown (Pawn)]] Factor: {{Good|×70%}}
* [[Move Speed]] Offset: {{+|4 {{CS}}}}

The frenzy has a cooldown of {{Ticks|1800}} before it can be used again.

The adrenal heart inflicts a {{++|15%}}  [[hunger rate]] offset, increasing the rate at which ghouls get hungry, and therefore the amount they eat. This applies at all times, not just while the ghoul frenzy ability is active.

=== Installation ===
The adrenal heart can only be installed in [[ghoul]]s. Note that despite the description noting [[pain]] as the reason it cannot be installed in other pawns, methods of pain nullification such as [[painstopper]]s do not allow the hearts installation into other pawns.

Installing the part requires {{ticks|2500}} of work. It doesn't [[Anesthetic|anesthetize]] the ghoul, requires no medicine, cannot fail, but requires medical skill of 4.

== Analysis ==
The {{++|15%}} hunger rate is a small price to pay in exchange for ghoul frenzy, especially for ghouls with [[nuclear stomach]] who already consume very little food. After unlocking the [[metalblood serum]], this should be the main heart upgrade for your combat ghouls.

The frenzy duration of 15 second is a bit less than 0.4 hours, half of the cooldown. For the aforementioned cost of hunger, you can keep your ghoul in frenzy half of the time, all day. On average, this will completely cancel the move speed penalty of all other ghoul-specific body parts.
