== Analysis ==
Mastodon leather is very similar to [[elephant leather]] in its statistics - trading away some of the textile's beauty for more cold insulation.

Like the elephant leather, it ties with [[Bearskin]] and [[Hippo leather]] for 9th most protective textile against sharp and blunt, and 3rd most protective textile against heat, beaten only by [[Devilstrand]] and [[Hyperweave]]. As such, they are good canditates for [[duster]]s and other protective clothing - unlike elephant leather however, they can also be used in colder biomes. Mastodon leather is as good as [[Heavy fur]] for cold insulation, being tied for 6th most insulating textile against cold.

Despite its better-than-average rankings, this textile should be replaced by better materials once you can afford them. The aforementioned heavy fur protects more against sharp-based attacks such as gunshots, claws, and bites, without being worse than mastodon leather in any other statistics. Other textiles like [[Devilstrand]] protect significantly more against sharp-based attacks, but aren't as insulating against the cold. [[Thrumbofur]] and [[Thrumbomane]] are strictly better than mastodon leather, but are more difficult to come by.

For use in [[furniture]], it only has a slightly improved [[Property:Beauty Factor|beauty factor]] than other common textiles. More importantly though, [[wool]]s are superior for beauty and reasonably common, especially considering [[Mastodon wool]] which comes from the same animal. Thus, you should consider using wool for furniture instead of leather, which requires killing the mastodon.
