== Analysis ==
The revolver is clearly an early game weapon. [[Crashlanded]] starts with a revolver, and they are very easy to acquire through other means, as early game raiders often use them and they are cheap to both research and make.

For early colonies, the revolver works as a short-medium ranged weapon. It deals decent damage with single shot, and has a high enough stopping power to stagger advancing [[human]]s or [[Lancer|human-]][[Scyther|sized]] [[mechanoid]]s. Despite having a decent range, the revolver isn't particularly suited to medium-range engagements, although it will be better than an [[autopistol]] or [[recurve bow]].
* An [[autopistol]] of equal quality fires faster, meaning it is more likely to get at least one hit in, and is better at short ranges. The revolver is better at longer ranges and can stagger humans. The differences are relatively small, so the higher quality weapon will most likely perform better.
* A [[bolt-action rifle]] is clearly better at long distance combat, and the revolver is better for close ranged combat - when combat happens < 12 tiles away.
* A [[recurve bow]] of equal quality is worse than a revolver at all ranges, but a large quality disparity (e.g., awful revolver vs normal bow) can make the bow worth using.

A very high quality revolver, such as from an early [[Inspired creativity|inspiration]], has surprising longevity. A legendary revolver's {{DPS}} is competitive with that of an excellent [[Heavy SMG]] at all but very close range, while offering better single point damage, range, and {{AP}}. They should not be specifically farmed for - don't spend an inspiration on a revolver when you can make a HSMG, but any that are acquired should be considered even until either masterworks become common or true end-game weapons are acquired.

Since the revolver's warmup time is so low, [[Aiming Time]] reductions like [[trigger-happy]] or [[shooting specialist]]{{IdeologyIcon}} have a relatively small effect on a revolver, but a [[heavy bandolier]]{{BiotechIcon}} has a relatively greater impact.

=== Stats ===
Against an unarmored human, a revolver can kill with 4 hits to the torso, 3 hits to the head, 2 hits to the neck or 1 hit to the brain; cripple or destroy limbs in 3 hits; and cripple or destroy eyes and digits with a single hit. It'll generally take around 6 hits (~15.5 seconds average) to incapacitate that human from pain shock.
