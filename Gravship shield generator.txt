The '''gravship shield generator''' is a [[building]] added by the [[Odyssey DLC]], that projects a shield that protects against both ground-level and overhead projectiles around a [[gravship]].

== Acquisition ==
Acquired as a quest reward. You can also find them from sealed crates in structures.

== Summary ==
The 3x3 building requires 200W power.  It can be activated to create a shield vs ground and aerial projectiles which has 500HP and lasts 100 seconds (2.4 hours rimworld) or until EMP'd.  The shield ability has a radius of 25 tiles from the center of the building, and a cooldown of 4 hours (2 minutes, 46 2/3 seconds real time).

When deconstructed, it provides 4 advanced components, 40 gravlite panels and 100 steel.

Gravship shield generators must be placed on [[gravship substructure]], and to function, must be connected to the [[grav engine]] via substructure and be within a 19-tile radius of it; being within a [[grav field extender]]'s radius is not enough. Up to 2 shield generators may be connected to the grav engine.

== Analysis == 
Two shield generators can be used in sequence and cycled regularly to maintain a continuous shield around a gravship, with a few seconds of time during which they will overlap.

Can be combined with physical defences such as walls and barricades to create an embrasure equivalent and improve the performance of both. Place the walls such that the shield covers the gaps in the walls for firing through, but the walls themselves cover the shield. This ensures that only shots that would have gone through the gap and risked hitting a colonist are blocked by the shield, while others are absorbed by the wall and don't consume shield HP. This also provides a some protection against EMP projectiles breaking the shield.
