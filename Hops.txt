{{Info|'''Hops''' are inedible flowers harvested from [[hop plant]]s, a single harvest yields 8 crops at 100% growth. They are processed by [[Work#Cook|cooks]] in bundles of 25 at a [[brewery]] to produce a batch of 5 [[wort]]. Wort is carried to [[fermenting barrel]]s by [[Work#Haul|haulers]] to ferment into [[beer]].}}
