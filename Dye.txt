'''Dye''' comes from the [[tinctoria]] plant. It can be used to paint walls and other buildings {{RimworldIcon}} or dye [[apparel]] and hair at the [[styling station]]{{IdeologyIcon}}

== Acquisition ==
Dye is obtained by harvesting [[tinctoria]] plant. Each fully grown plant yields {{Q|Tinctoria|Harvest Yield}} before modifiers for [[AI Storytellers#Harvest yield|Storyteller Harvest Yield]] and the harvesting pawn's [[Plant Harvest Yield]]. Note that by default, Storyteller Harvest Yield varies by difficulty.

== Analysis ==
With [[Ideology]], dying a pawn's clothes to match either the pawn's favorite color or the color associated with their ideoligion will a {{+|1}} [[mood]] buff, stacking to {{+|2}} if they are the same color. While the actual impact of dye is minimal, using dye only requires a tiny amount of work, as clothes are not replaced often. A small field of tinctoria is all that's needed, and only a stack of dye or two kept in storage is sufficient. Therefore, the only reasons ''not'' to dye clothes would be if the colony does not have the growing space to do so, such as on an [[ice sheet]], or if the cost of the [[styling station]] cannot be afforded.

Otherwise, there is no mechanical benefit to dye beyond its selling value, although it can help make pawns and buildings easier to identify. As a cash crop, tinctoria is outclassed by [[cotton plant]]s or any of the drug plants, which take longer to grow, but take much less time to sow/harvest and provides a much better yield.
