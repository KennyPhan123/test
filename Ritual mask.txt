== Summary == 
In addition to minor head protection, a ritual mask increases the [[pain shock threshold]] of its wearer by {{+|15%}}, allowing its wearer to take more [[pain]] before being [[downed]].

{{Hover title|Version/1.6.4630|At the time of writing}}, the ritual mask is the only item with the "Bioferrite" [[stuff]] tag. This technically makes the mask stuffable but forces only one option for the crafting material. This is done to allow the mask to benefit from the following psychic buffs afforded to stuffable items made of bioferrite:

Equipped items stuffed from bioferrite including the mask apply a multiplier to the wearer's [[psychic sensitivity]]. These multipliers stack multiplicatively with one another and with other bonuses from [[eltex]]{{RoyaltyIcon}} items {{Check Tag|Prestige?|Prestige armor implements it bonus differently, check how it stacks}}

== Analysis ==
{{Rewrite|section=1|reason=Cleanup, expansion}}
The ritual mask serves as an excellent early game full-head-coverage armor piece. It provides the same protection as a [[visage mask]]{{IdeologyIcon}} with an added increase to [[pain shock threshold]]. A [[simple helmet]] has better armor ratings but, unlike the ritual mask, doesn't cover the entire head.

For a [[psycast]]er,{{RoyaltyIcon}} it's a cheap and highly efficient alternative to [[eltex]]{{RoyaltyIcon}} headgear, especially for its competent protection.

In terms of armor, if your colony is in possession of enough [[devilstrand]] or [[hyperweave]], it's recommended that you instead use a [[ceremonial hood]], although the ceremonial hood comes without the increased pain shock threshold and psychic sensitivity.
