A pawn that is '''stunned''' cannot move or act. Abilities that have zero warmup time, such as [[firefoam pop pack]]s, can still be used while stunned, but all abilities with warmups are disrupted until the stun ends. Certain forms of stun will cause adapatation, where further stuns are not possible.{{Check Tag|Different source adapt?|If a mech is EMP'd, can the psycast or disruptor flares still work?}}

Stun be inflicted by:
* [[Blunt]] melee damage type
* [[EMP]] damage on [[mechanoid]]s, [[turret]]s, certain [[entities]],{{AnomalyIcon}} and [[drone]]s{{OdysseyIcon}}
* [[Stun (psycast)|Stun psycast]]{{RoyaltyIcon}} on any pawn with non-zero psychic sensitivity
* [[Nerve spiker]]{{AnomalyIcon}} on living targets
* [[Disruptor flare]]{{AnomalyIcon}} on any pawn with non-zero psychic sensitivity
