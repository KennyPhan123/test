A '''{{PAGENAME}}''' is an [[animal]] added by the [[Odyssey DLC]].

== Acquisition ==
{{PAGENAME}}s can be found in {{Habitats}}. They can either be tamed by a [[Work #Handle|handler]] or self-tame in a random event. 

{{PAGENAME}}s can be bought and sold in other [[Trade#Faction base|faction bases]] and from [[Trade#Exotic goods trader 2|exotic goods traders]]. {{PAGENAME}}s purchased from traders will be already tamed.

== Summary ==
A female flamingo produces {{P|Eggs Per Clutch Average}} [[flamingo egg]] every {{P|Egg Laying Interval}} day when fertilized. {{PAGENAME}} will not lay unfertilized eggs.

== Analysis ==
Flamingos are not good animals for food production. They produce roughly {{#vardefineecho:egg_nutr_per_day|{{#expr:{{P|Eggs Per Clutch Average}}/{{P|Egg Laying Interval}}*{{Q|Flamingo egg (fert.)|Nutrition}}}}}} nutrition per day (assuming the eggs will be fertilized as soon as possible), and flamingos are not pen animals, meaning they must be re-tamed. On top of that, their meat production is low compared to livestock animals.

The only real use for tame flamingos is to trade them, as they are easier to tame compared to other animals. Even then, they won't fetch a high profit.

With a low heath scale and a fairly good meat yield, wild flamingos are good targets for food hunting, though the leather they provide is mediocre.
