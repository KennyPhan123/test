The '''toxbomb launcher''' is a ranged [[weapon]] added by the [[Biotech DLC]]

== Acquisition ==
{{Acquisition}}

== Summary ==
[[File:Tox launcher blast.png|thumb|left|325px|AOE of a single toxbomb shot. Shots land within the yellow area. Blue area is the expected gas explosion outside. Actual gas distribution can vary, especially in cramped areas.]]
Creates [[tox gas]]. [[Quality]] seems to have little or no impact to the amount gas created.

<div style="clear:left></div>

== Analysis ==
[[File:Tox grenade launcher comparison.png|thumb|left|300px|Gas created when tox weapons are fired constantly outside. <br>Left: [[Tox grenades]]. Right: Toxbomb launcher (normal)]]
Compared to [[tox grenades]], toxbomb launchers deploy tox gas faster, as the launcher has higher shot velocity and detonates instantly on impact. They also have greater range. However, tox grenades create more gas over time, due to its faster overall firerate. This makes the launcher relatively better as a in-field support weapon, and grenades for your gas-related kill tunnels.
