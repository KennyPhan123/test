== Analysis ==
Camelhide is one of many [[textiles]] with stats almost identical to [[plainleather]], only diverging in having better heat [[insulation]] and slightly higher market value and [[Property:Beauty Factor|beauty factor]]. In this way, it can be considered the heat focused counterpart to [[Bluefur]].

Considering that camelhide is the 11th most protective [[textile]] in the game (part of a 6-way tie), it is largely irrelevant for protective [[clothing]] after the very early game. That said, camelhide has very high heat insulation - the second best against heat (in a 3-way tie). Because of this, camelhide can remain useful in hot biomes into the mid-game. Later on, [[devilstrand]] is equally insulative and significantly more protective. Therefore, growing devilstrand should be made a priority in hot biomes. If heat is not an issue, then other leathers should be used whenever possible before devilstrand becomes available.

For use in [[furniture]], it only has a slightly improved [[Property:Beauty Factor|beauty factor]] compared to other common textiles. Despite this, the fact that camelhide becomes obsolete for clothing during the mid-game makes it a decent choice for improving furniture [[beauty]]. However, because [[wool]]s are superior for beauty and are reasonably common, the long-term utility of camelhide is limited.
