== Acquisition ==
{{Main|Monument}}

A monument marker is delivered to the colony when accepting a [[monument]] [[quest]].

== Summary ==
A monument marker can be placed to designate an area to build a monument, with the marker at the center. Once placed, selecting the marker will allow the player to automaticlaly place blueprints of the required structures. Once all required strucutres and floors are built, the quest will be completed. The marker can be moved after being placed.
