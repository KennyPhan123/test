'''Fleshbulbs''' are a bioluminescent light source of the [[Fleshmass]] family.

== Occurrence ==
Fleshbulbs are generated within [[Fleshmass]] infested areas, namely the [[Quests#Distress_Call|Distress call quest]] and the [[Undercave]] created by the [[Pit gate]] entity/event. They spawn dispersed among the other fleshmass, providing moderate illumination around fleshmass growths.

== Summary ==
Fleshbulbs have a 50% chance of dropping 4-8 pieces of [[Twisted meat]] when destroyed.

It cannot be [[claim]]ed, moved, or deconstructed. 

=== Lighting ===
[[File:Fleshbulb illumination area.png|300px|thumb|left|The white area (1 tile out) is 50% illumination, required for best [[Surgery Success Chance Factor|surgery success chance]]; the yellow (2nd-5th tiles) represents 30% or better, adequate for all general labor, avoiding [[mood]] penalties, and for growing [[Decorative Plants|decorative plants]] indoors in [[plant pot]]s.]]
A fleshbulb produces [[light]] in a 9 tile radius, with the nearest {{P|Light Radius}} tiles at or above 30% light level and considered "[[Light|Lit]]", to a maximum of 50%. The provided light in this area is bright enough to prevent work speed penalties, grow decorative plants, and prevent the debuff [[Thoughts_list#In_darkness|"In darkness"]]. However, fleshbulbs are not bright enough to grow most [[crop]]s indoors, which require 51% illumination. Light does not "stack", so no amount of fleshbulbs will ever give more than 50% illumination (e.g. for [[rice plant]]s, etc).

== Analysis ==
The player is unlikely to significantly interact with fleshbulbs outside of niche circumstances such as playing with [[Combat in darkness]] ideoligions.{{IdeologyIcon}} They cannot be meaningfully farmed or moved, and they only provide meager light. In almost all cases, constructed lighting options are a much better idea.  As such, the most common player interaction is simply destroying them for additional [[twisted meat]] before leaving the area.
