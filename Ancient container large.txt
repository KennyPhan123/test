'''Ancient containers''' are [[ruins]] that are defined in Core but only appear when the [[Ideology DLC]] and/or the [[Odyssey DLC]] are enabled.

== Occurrence ==
If the Ideology DLC is enabled, they can be found among the debris scattered on maps.

If the Odyssey DLC is enabled, they can be found in [[ancient warehouse]]s and [[ancient chemfuel refinery|ancient chemfuel refineries]], plus occasionally in [[Landmarks#Ruins|ancient ruins]] and in [[scarlands]] ruins.

If neither DLC is enabled, they will not appear in-game at all.

== Summary ==
Ancient containers are cumbersome ancient structures that cannot be deconstructed. They can however be destroyed for {{Icon Small|Steel slag chunk}} 3 [[steel slag chunk]]s. 

They can be used as walls, both to provide cover in a fight and for room building.
