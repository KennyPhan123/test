'''Cataphract''' can refer to a number of different things, including:

* [[Empire#Cataphract|Empire cataphracts]]: Heavily armored [[Empire]] troops
* [[Cataphract armor]]: The heavy power armor worn by Cataphracts and named for them.
** [[Prestige cataphract armor]]: A [[noble]] and [[psycast]]er focused variant of the above
* [[Cataphract helmet]]: The heavy power armor helmet worn by Cataphracts and named for them.
** [[Prestige cataphract helmet]]: A [[noble]] and [[psycast]]er focused variant of the above

Though it does not bear the name, [[phoenix armor]] is another variant of cataphract armor with an integrated incendiary launcher and a focus on protection from on fire.
