'''Equipment''', currently consisting only of [[weapons]], are items that can be bought, stolen, or produced by your own colonists. Trade ships and trade caravans usually have many weapons to sell, but at a high cost. Killing pirates can give you many weapons, but they are more likely to be of poor [[quality]].

Equipment can be assigned to a [[colonist]] by clicking with the right mouse button on the item while a colonist is selected. An equipped item can be dropped by using the right mouse button again on the colonist carrying it and selecting <tt>"Drop (item name, e.g. Charge rifle)"</tt>. Equipment can also be dropped by clicking the arrow on the colonist's Gear tab.
