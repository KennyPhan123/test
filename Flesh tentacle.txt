A '''flesh tentacle''' is a [[body part]] added by the [[Anomaly DLC]] that acts as a replacement for a pawn's natural arm, increasing [[Manipulation]] at the cost of [[Beauty (Pawn)|beauty]] and [[mood]].

== Acquisition ==

The [[unnatural healing]] ability from creepjoiners have a chance to give the recipient a flesh tentacle in place of an arm.

[[Horax cult]] pawns can also spawn with one, as can pawns from [[The Anomaly]] scenario.

{{Mutated Body Part Aquisition}}

== Summary ==
Flesh tentacles replace the user's organic arm up to, and including, the shoulder. They are universal and can replace either the left or right. The flesh tentacle has a part efficiency of {{Good|120%}}. This not only replaces almost all of the functionality of a normal arm - when coupled with the arm body part having a [[Manipulation]] importance of 50%, it results in an increase in manipulation of {{+|10%}} over natural for each flesh tentacle. Manipulation, in turn, affects a great many [[stats]]. For a full list of affected stats, see [[Manipulation#Affected stats|Manipulation]].

The tentacle also inflicts a {{--|1}} [[Beauty (Pawn)|beauty]] and completely removes the natural fist attack of this body part.

Finally, each tentacle inflicts {{Thought|desc=Get this thing off of me!|label=Flesh tentacle|value=-6|stack=2}} on its bearer unless they are [[inhumanized]], have the [[body modder]] trait, or [[Body modification|Body modification: Approved]] precept.{{IdeologyIcon}}

=== Combat ===
Because the flesh tentacle removes the hand, it also removes the natural fist attack of the user, as well as the claw attack of any [[ghoul]]s. However, unlike other similar artificial body parts, it does not replace it with another attack. The practical effect of this will depend on the other melee verbs available to the pawn, but it will typically have minimal or no effect unless the other hand is also removed or replaced.

Unlike other artificial body parts, flesh tentacles can [[bleeding|bleed]] from wounds, however these wounds won't cause any [[pain]].

== Analysis ==
While flesh tentacles provide slightly less [[Manipulation]] than a [[bionic arm]] would, flesh tentacles do not cost any resources, and do not increase colony [[wealth]] at all. This makes flesh tentacles ideal for [[wealth management]] strategies. Unlike bionic arms, there is no mechanism to choose which side the flesh tentacle to replace, so replacing a lost arm with a flesh tentacle is harder than using bionic arms. 

For healthy pawns, the increased manipulation is best used for the [[Doctoring]], [[Crafting]], or [[Skills|Construction]]; doctors tend faster and with higher quality, while crafters and constructors make objects faster (quality is unaffected). Manipulation is one of the few ways to increase [[general labor speed]], so it is also good for skill-less crafting tasks like [[stonecutter's table|stonecutting]] and [[electric smelter|smelting]]. A single flesh tentacle will also increase the research, hacking speed, entity studying rate by the maximum amount manipulation can give. 

For combat, tentacles will increase [[Melee Hit Chance]], but they only increase [[Shooting Accuracy]] if Manipulation is below 100%. While manipulation may go below 100% in combat, [[bionic eye]]s are superior and should be your main priority.

A flesh tentacle is useful when combined with [[power claw]] for a [[ghoul]],{{AnomalyIcon}} as the tentacle removes an inferior [[melee verb selection|melee verb]] from the ghoul's natural hand and claw attacks so that attacks are more likely to be power claw, but without the further lower [[moving]] like a second power claw would. Keep in mind that a [[wooden hand]] does the same, is significantly cheaper and easier to acquire, and retains compatibility with the [[elbow blade]],{{RoyaltyIcon}} but obviously is statistically inferior in terms of manipulation.
