'''Fennec foxes''', called '''fennec fox puppies''' as babies, are small, carnivorous [[animals]]. They are one of several different types of [[fox]].

== Acquisition ==
{{PAGENAME}}s can be found in {{Habitats}}. They can either be tamed by a [[Work #Handle|handler]] or self-tame in a random event.

{{Starting Pet Note}}

== Analysis ==
With a body size of 0.55 they are just large enough to avoid being staggered by weapons with a stopping power of 0.5, such as the [[charge rifle]] and [[assault rifle]].
Foxes compared to all other animals add the most DPS per Raid Points of any animal. Foxes closest competition is the [[bog hound]], [[monkey]], [[colossus toad]], [[arctic wolf]]/[[timber wolf]].
Foxes have the second highest wildness after wolves.
Foxes have the second lowest health after monkeys.
Foxes are unable to graze unlike monkeys and toads.
Foxes are not immune to tox gas like the boghound. 
Foxes are third fastest after boghounds and wolves.
Foxes do eat the lowest amount of food compared to these other animals.

The main niche for foxes as combat animals is situations where food is scarce and/or labor intensive.
=== Attacks ===
Fennec foxes have two attacks: scratch (paw) and bite (teeth). In addition to the base damage, both attacks apply a stun for {{ticks|20}}.
