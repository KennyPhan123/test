'''Gauranlen pod''' is [[plant]] that when harvested provides a single [[gauranlen seed]]. 

== Acquisition == 
There are three methods of having a gauranlen pod sprout.
* Gauranlen Pods can be spawned via a [[Events#Gauranlen_pod_sprout|natural event]], similar to ambrosia sprouts. These occur roughly every 60 [[time|days]], in every [[biome]] except Ice Sheets and Sea Ice.
** [[Tree connection]] will reduce the amount of time for a gauranlen pod sprout event to occur to approximately every 30 days.
* Gauranlen Pods can be spawned via a [[Rituals|Ritual]]
** A Gauranlen pod has a 100% of spawning if the outcome of the ritual is fun or unforgettable.
** A Guaranlen pod ritual can only be done by Ideoligions with [[nature primacy]] or [[tree connection]]
* 3 [[Gaumaker dryad]]s from an already existing [[Gauranlen tree]] can merge to create a new pod nearby
** this dryad caste is only available to Ideologies with [[nature primacy]] or [[tree connection]]

== Trivia ==
Despite the description, it *is* possible to get multiple seeds from a single pod. A pawn with a particularly high [[Plant Harvest Yield]] stat can harvest a second seed from one pod, so it is recommended that you only use your Plants Specialist and / or best farmer to harvest pods whenever available.
