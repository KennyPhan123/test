A  '''Mechanoid Hive''' is a permanently hostile [[faction]] composed entirely of [[mechanoids]]: deadly autonomously intelligent robots.

== Summary ==
All [[mechanoid]]s from a mech hive are permanently hostile to the colony and every other non-mech hive faction. They do not eat and are immune to temperature extremes, despite having a Comfortable Temperature range. They are also immune to [[pain]], [[fire]], [[toxic buildup]], [[smokeleaf]] clouds from an [[autobong]], [[EMI_dynamo|Emi Dynamo]], [[Solar_flare|solar flares]], and [[Rot_stink|rot stink]]. 

As machines, they are vulnerable to [[EMP]] attacks, though will "Adapt" for {{ticks|2200}} (regardless of source), making them temporarily immune to all further EMP strikes. Hostile mechanoids do not flee, are unable to feel [[pain]] and will not seek [[cover]].

Mechanoids can be found sealed in ancient ruins. They may also attack the player's base through [[events]] like [[raids]], [[Crashed Ship Part#Poison Ship|poison ships]], [[Crashed Ship Part#Psychic Ship|psychic ships]], and [[mech cluster]]s.{{RoyaltyIcon}} They may sometimes appear dormant in Ancient Complex and guarding any relic with [[Ideology DLC]].{{IdeologyIcon}}

Hostile mechanoids are not bound by [[power]] limits. While [[mechanitor]]s can create their own [[allied mechanoid]]s in the [[Biotech DLC]],{{BiotechIcon}} they are unable to tame or convert hostile ones. Friendly mechs may become feral and join a mech hive if left Uncontrolled (such as having too little [[bandwidth]]) for too long, and can't be re-converted.

=== Raids ===

Mech Raids generally consist of fewer total enemies than human raids but each individual mechanoid is generally more dangerous than an individual human raider.

The Mech Hive can attack with standard raids, breach raids, and water emerge raids. Notably, the Mech Hive cannot Sapper Raid, Siege, "Drop right on Top of you", nor "scattered drop across the area". Water Emerge and Breach raids are Immediate Attack raids, all other Mech Hive raids are stage then attack. 

Centipede Burners and Centipede Gunners only appear in all Centipede raids, Mech Clusters, and Mechanitor Boss Waves. Centipedes in all other raids will always be Centipede Blasters.

== Analysis ==
Mechanoid forces are specialized by their unit types, with each unit performing a single role. Basics summaries are available here, see the individual pages for further details and analysis.

* [[Scyther]] - The melee boogeymen of Mechanoids. Fast and extremely dangerous up close, large numbers can and will overwhelm single enemies. Melee blocking, use of chokepoints, and focus fire while they close in are useful tactics. Mechanoid raids consisting entirely of large numbers of scythers are fairly common.
* [[Lancer]]- Armed with [[charge lance]]s, they have medium range and modest overall DPS. However, their high shot damage presents a modest, but serious risk of one-shot-killing pawns by destroying body parts. Therefore, exposure should be limited and lancers prioritized as threats. Like many non-scythers, melee combat is very effective if a pawn can get close enough.
* [[Pikeman]] - Acting as the sniper, their [[needle gun]] doesn't do too much damage. Their main role is to allow mechanoids to engage at range - use bends in chokepoints to force them into range, or simply advance and kill them.
* [[Centipede]] - Centipedes are tanks and crowd controllers. They are incredibly durable, but rather slow. They can wield [[heavy charge blaster]]s or [[minigun]]s for raw, inaccurate damage, or [[inferno cannon]]s to light fires and force ignited pawns out of cover. Mortar Fire and melee combat are decent options for actually damaging the centipede due to it's slow movement speed and large size, and avoid bunching against any of their weapons. Alternatively, a [[psychic insanity lance]] against a centipede turns a danger into a significant asset, though note that other mechanoids are immune to inferno cannons.
* [[Termite]] - While not particularly dangerous on their own, their thump cannon can destroy most walls in a single shot, leaving your colonists open to damage from other mechanoids. Depending on the exact strategy employed against the raid as a whole, these might be the highest priority, or of negligible concern.
* [[Militor]]{{BiotechIcon}} - A small combat mechanoid armed with a low power, 12 range mini-shotgun. One of the weakest mechanoids, they serve as earlygame threats and support other mechanoids. They can be scary through sheer numbers - large mechanoid raids comprised only of militors occasionally spawn.
* [[Scorcher]]{{BiotechIcon}} - A mechanoid that specializes in incendiary attacks. Its flame burst attack has little reach, but once it closes on a group of defenders it can ignite and disrupt them with blasts of searing flame.
* [[Legionary]]{{BiotechIcon}} - A combat support mechanoid with a wide range bullet shield and long-range needle gun. Designed to support other mechanoids from long range, the legionary is vulnerable to anyone who can get inside its shield.
* [[Tesseron]]{{BiotechIcon}} - A medium-range combat mechanoid. The tesseron's sweeping beam attack pierces shields, ignites pawns, and deals great damage, but can't be fired up close.
* [[Centurion]]{{BiotechIcon}} - An incredibly durable ultra-heavy mech, with a large shield capable of defending allies. It also has point-defense turret, which can fire while moving.
* [[Tunneler]]{{BiotechIcon}} - A large, somewhat durable melee mechanoid with a natural shield and smokepop. While mostly made for mining, some [[apocriton]] summon groups will arrive with tunnelers. Does not seem to appear in regular mech raids.
* [[Diabolus]]{{BiotechIcon}} - An ultra-heavy mechanoid with an ultra-powerful hellsphere cannon. It takes time to charge up, but devastates everything including buildings and walls in a large radius. Also has a flame burst for use against nearby foes, and a general purpose turret.
* [[War queen]]{{BiotechIcon}} - An ultra-heavy mech with a built-in mech gestator. Fed with appropriate resources, the war queen can form small [[war urchin]] combat mechs within its massive carapace and deploy them into combat. 
* [[ Apocriton]]{{BiotechIcon}} - A mechanoid commander designed to coordinate and motivate other mechs during long extermination campaigns. Its most obvious power is its ability to resurrect recently-killed mechs by supercharging their self-repair processes. Less obviously, it is intelligent and psychically present, radiating hatred into the minds of anyone in a wide radius.  While all mechanoids have a dim psychically-present intelligence, only the apocriton and a few others truly feel hatred for their victims and understand the suffering they inflict.

== Trivia ==
* Although all mechanoid-only weapons are 'Spacer' in terms of tech level; the hidden mechanoid faction itself is actually 'Ultra' tech level - one above 'Spacer', and the second highest tech level in the game, below 'Transcendent'.
