The '''flamebow''' is a ranged [[weapon]] added by the [[Biotech DLC]] that spreads fire.

== Acquisition ==
Flamebows cannot be crafted. Instead, they can be [[Trade|purchased]] from any traders,{{Check Tag|Which?}} or obtained from raiders from [[Tribes|savage impid tribes]].

== Analysis ==
{{Stub|section=1|reason=Value proposition, acquisition strategies, hotbox uses etc}}
Can be deadly in the hands of the enemy if you're unprepared. 

Great for fueling ovens, with a high throughput on fire compared to both [[molotov cocktails]] and [[incendiary launcher]]s due to RoF and fuel splash. 

It's also a decent area denial tool, if you can afford to burn your map. Be careful around crops and wooden structures, or put out the fire before it spreads.
