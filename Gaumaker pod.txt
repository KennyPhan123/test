A '''gaumaker pod''' is the result of 3 [[gaumaker dryad]] from a [[Gauranlen tree]] merging, It is used to create new [[Gauranlen pod]]s. It takes 4 [[time|days]] to grow to maturity and create a harvestable pod. They cannot spawn on buildings.{{Check Tag|Non-edifice|Can it spawn on rugs? Floors? Etc?}}
