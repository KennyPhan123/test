'''Statues''' are artistic [[buildings]] similar to [[Small sculpture|small]] [[sculpture]]s that depict a [[human]] or individual [[colonist]].

== Acquisition ==
{{Acquisition}}{{Check Tag|Trade? Quests?|True for small sculptures}} 

== Summary ==
Functionally similar to small sculptures, statues have 60 base beauty, 10 more than small sculptures, cost 15 more materials to make, and always recreate the likeness of the subject human or colonist at the time the statue was finished. Statues will match the appearance of a living human, including their clothing, equipment, hairstyle,{{IdeologyIcon}} genes,{{BiotechIcon}} and body parts like [[bionic eye]]s and [[flesh tentacle]]s.{{AnomalyIcon}}{{Check Tag|Verify|Health capacity? Standing? Only living humans?}}{{Check Tag|Beauty|State whether pawn or equipment beauty affects final object - likely not, but should be outright stated regardless}} Their subject is always included in a tale associated with the sculpture's description - for further details, see the [[Sculptures#Story generation|Sculptures]] page.
Unlike other beauty-focused artistic buildings, statues do not have a 110% trade price improvement stat.

=== Meditation ===
{{Royalty|section=1}}
Pawns with the [[Psyfocus#Artistic|Artistic]] meditation focus type can use statues in a 10-tile radius to increase psyfocus. This includes all pawns who are not Tribal or [[Ascetic]]. 
Statues are functionally equivalent to other 1x1 sculptures for the purposes of meditiation. 

The highest quality statue or sculpture is the primary source of psyfocus:

== Styles ==
Statues do not have distinct styles but reflect the features and appearance of their subject, which will include [[style]]d clothes, [[gene]]s, body parts and injuries as they were when the statue was finished.
