A '''large nature shrine''' is a type of [[furniture]] added by the [[Royalty DLC]] at which a pawn can meditate for recreation and to regain [[psyfocus]].

== Acquisition ==
{{Acquisition}}

== Summary ==
{{Stub|section|reason=Raider avoidance mechanic - ensure parity with all other similar items}}
Large nature shrines are a natural [[meditation focus]]. They have a base meditation focus strength of '''30%'''. Each [[animus stone]] within 9.9 tiles increases the meditation effectiveness by {{+|2%}}, up to a total of four stones for a maximum of {{+|8%}}.

Like other natural focus objects, its effectiveness is reduced by artificial buildings within a 34.9 tile radius. Note that this does not include [[floor]]ing and [[torch lamp]]s. The reduction is set by the following curve:

The maximum meditation focus strength is thus '''38%''' for a large nature shrine with no artificial buildings within 34.9 tiles and surrounded by four [[animus stone]]s.

Large nature shrines also give {{+|1%}} psyfocus per day to other nearby natural foci, for a total of {{+|4%}} with four nature shrines.

== Analysis == 
{{Stub|section=1|reason=Better comparison of nature options, and when to use this if ever. Some mention of other focus also valuable }}
As a natural meditation focus, the large nature shrine has +2% psyfocus / day compared to the [[anima tree]], but does not have the [[Anima tree#Linking ritual|anima tree linking ritual]]. Therefore, the anima tree is usually better for daily meditation.

The large nature shrine can be built when the anima tree is too far for natural pawns to be conveniently able to meditate at, and when the superior [[animus stone]] is unavailable. For boosting the strength of an anima tree, the [[small nature shrine]] is cheaper and gives the same amount of extra psyfocus.
