{{Info|A '''psychic insanity lance''' is a two-use [[utility]] [[artifact]] that can be equipped by pawns in the [[utility]] slot. Manually activated, it can cause a target to go [[berserk]].}}

==Acquisition==
Psychic insanity lances cannot be crafted. Instead they must be purchased from Exotic Goods [[trade]]rs or [[faction base]]s.

== Summary ==
When equipped, a psychic insanity lance is worn in the waist [[Utility|utility slot]]. Pawns that are [[incapable]] of violence can equip, but not use, the lance. Once the wearer is [[draft]]ed, it can be manually activated by the player to target a pawn within {{P|Range}} tiles and within [[line of sight]]. The lance has 2 charges and cannot be recharged - when both charges are expended, the item disappears completely and no resources can be salvaged from it. 

After a {{Ticks|{{P|Aiming Time Base}}}} warm-up, the target will instantaneously go [[berserk]] - this berserk state is essentially identical to the mental break with the same minimum recovery time of {{ticks|40000}} and the same end condition of unconsciousness. The warmup time before firing is affected by the [[Aiming Time]] stat of the wearer. There is also a 30% chance that the target will be set [[Fire|alight]] and given 1 to 5 {{HP}} of brain damage in the form of a burn scar. When targeting colonists, this 30% chance is multiplied by the Friendly Fire Chance in the [[Difficulty]] settings.  

Pawns with 0% [[psychic sensitivity]], such as those with the [[psychically deaf]] trait, cannot be targeted by the lance. Sensitivity does not otherwise modify the effect. The three [[mechanoid commander]]s{{BiotechIcon}} and the [[dreadmeld]]{{AnomalyIcon}} are also immune to psychic lances. [[Revenant]]s{{AnomalyIcon}} are not technically immune, but the berserk condition has no effect on their behavior.

If used on a non-hostile [[faction]]'s pawn, it will decrease [[goodwill]] with that faction by {{--|200}} points.

{{Utility Note}}

== Analysis ==
When used against a group of enemies, psychic insanity lances are an effective way to eliminate a single threat and distract the group for a short while. As they occupy the utility slot, they are a flexible option for when big and/or dangerous enemies show up.

It is especially useful for dealing with pesky [[triple rocket launcher]] and [[doomsday rocket launcher]] raiders, as the lance outranges (42 vs 36 tiles) and has a shorter warmup (132 ticks vs 270 ticks) than either rocket launcher. Targeting the user directly will guarantee the rocket won't be used against your colony. Targeting another nearby pawn won't guarantee that (the rocket launcher must not continue aiming at colonists, and the berserked must survive long enough), but can cause massive collateral damage if it does.

It can also be exceptionally useful when used on [[centipede gunner]]s and [[centipede blaster|blasters]] - not only does it distract the mechanoids around it, it will continue to attack its compatriots until it or the others are dead. Depending on the composition of the mechanoid forces, this can result in anything from just the target centipede dying to the destruction of all but one mechanoid. As all mechanoids are immune to fire, [[centipede burner]]s are less effective choices as they cannot harm their ex-comrades. However, they still make a good distraction and, sometimes more importantly, the centipede itself will die in the process. 

Insanity lances will not work on sleeping targets, even if the target is awoken by using the insanity lance. Therefore, dormant mechanoids must be woken up before targeting them with the lance.

===Comparisons===
[[Psychic shock lance]]s are useful against lone enemies or tiny groups, e.g., a solo pawn [[caravan]] fighting against small ambushes, while insanity lances are not. Psychic shock lances are also better for capturing pawns alive, and with their armor intact. Against large groups, insanity and shock lances perform a very similar role in combat. Insanity lances are better as they'll cause distractions and friendly fire, but against large raids, the targeted pawn will usually die quickly. Therefore, prefer psychic shock lances if their capturing is required or could be useful.

[[Shard insanity lance]]s{{AnomalyIcon}} have a similar effect but have a smaller range, so the psychic insanity lance is a much stronger item overall.
