'''Fleshmass neural lump''' is an item dropped by the [[Nerve bundle]] upon its destruction.

== Summary ==
A colonist can be selected to analyze the item, deleting the item after analysis is complete and providing 1/3 progress towards finding a way to eliminate the [[Fleshmass heart]].
