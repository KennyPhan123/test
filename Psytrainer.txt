The '''psytrainer''' is a rare item added by the [[Royalty DLC]] that gives the user a single [[psycast]].

== Acquisition ==
{{Stub|section=1|reason=Acquisition stub. Is it in any other trader inventories? What factions offer it for quests? How are the psycasts selected - are all psycasts as common as each other? fishing reward etc}}
Psytrainers cannot be crafted. Instead they must be received as a reward from [[quest]]s, or [[trade|bought]] from [[Empire#Faction bases|empire bases]], orbital exotic goods traders, or outlander exotic goods traders.

== Summary ==
Psytrainers can be used to learn a specified [[psycast]]. After use, the psytrainer is consumed. Each psytrainer is attuned to one specific psycast, which cannot be changed.

In order to actually use the psycast, a pawn must have a [[psylink]] high enough to use it. For example, a pawn can only use Neuroquake, a level 6 psycast, at psylink level 6. 

The psytrainer can be used before the user has the psylink level to actually use the psycast. In this case, the pawn will not receive the random psycast upon reaching the psylink level of the gained psycast
For example, a pawn with level 5 psylink that has used a psytrainer to get [[Neuroquake]] will not receive another psycast upon reaching level 6. Pawns with no psylink cannot use psytrainers.

The market value depends on the level of the psycast; higher level psycasts are more valuable. This follows the equation of ''100 + 150*Level''.

== Analysis ==
Many psycasts are very powerful tools, and psytrainers are the only way to collect the options a psycaster didn't randomly select on level up. Psytrainers ensure that a pawn will have access to any psycast, ''eventually''. 

Psytrainers are rare items and as such when a psytrainer of a psycast you know you will want appears for sale, it is often best to just buy them even if you don't have a psycaster of a high enough level to use it.

They can be good for [[wealth management]]. Having a psycast does not increase colony wealth, so buying and using a psytrainer will both lower wealth and be useful on their own.

Trainers should not be used prior to getting the requisite level to cast the contained psycast. There is basically never a valid reason to miss out on the free psycast, even the wealth they add while you're waiting to gain psylink levels isn't sufficient to justify it.
