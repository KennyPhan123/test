A '''gibbet cage''' is a building added by the [[Ideology DLC]]. It stores [[human]] [[corpse]]s to inflict [[terror]] on nearby pawns. 

== Acquisition ==
{{Acquisition}}

They can also be from certain raiding quest maps.

== Summary ==
{{Stub|section=1|reason=Inadequate summary and mechanics description}}
If the cage contains a corpse then +25% [[Terror]] in a 5 tile radius, otherwise does nothing.  Similar to shelves in that stored corpses don't give viewed a corpse thoughts.

Gibbet cages will prevent their occupants rising from a [[death pall]]{{AnomalyIcon}} or [[deadlife dust]].{{AnomalyIcon}}

== Analysis ==
=== Compared to other terror sources ===
With a similar effectiveness but higher cost compared to [[skullspike]]s and a lower terror value than [[terror sculpture]]s, gibbet cages have little to argue for them beyond aesthetics, even should a player want to use terror over slave apparel.
