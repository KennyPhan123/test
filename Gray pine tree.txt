'''Gray pine trees''' are trees added by the [[Biotech DLC]] that can be found in {{Habitats}}. Like the [[Witchwood tree]] or [[Polux tree]], they can survive in polluted terrain. They grow naturally, but the player can also sow them. These trees look ugly and don't yield as much wood as other trees do, but they require much less fertile soil to grow.
