'''Nutrifungus''' grows on lichen-covered soil and must be planted in and stay in complete darkness. So long as the darkness requirement is met, it can be grown in any [[terrain]] that is sufficiently fertile to support a [[growing zone]]. This includes, but is not limited to, [[fungal gravel]] and [[hydroponics basin]]s. Due to its low fertility sensitivity, it takes fewer penalties from poorer soils than more sensitive plants, but also benefits less from being in hydroponics. Nutrifungus is immune to [[blight]]. An interesting exception to the rule that Nutrifungus can only grow in darkness is when planted around other mushroom-like emitters of light, such as Bryolux, Agarilux, or Glowstool.

Note that most [[ideoligion]]s hate eating [[raw fungus]].

== Analysis ==
As an actual food crop, nutrifungus is nearly identical to [[potato plant]]s. Nutrifungus takes {{#expr:100*6/5.8 round 1}}% the time to grow for the exact same yield as potatoes, and their yield per work is identical. Nutrifungus also has low fertility sensitivity, so it grows slightly faster than potatoes if in poor soil.

If the colony has an ideoligion with the [[Tunneler (meme)|Tunneler meme]], it is almost a no-brainer to grow nutrifungus, as raw fungus provides a {{+|3}} mood boost and other plant food provides a {{--|3}} moodlet. Tunneler colonies may want to grow [[rice plant]]s at the ''very'' beginning, since rice grows faster and doesn't require roofing, but should switch to fungus quickly.

For other colonies, there are two main downsides of growing nutrifungus:
* Nutrifungus must grow in darkness. A baseline pawn works {{Bad|80%}} as fast in the dark, unless this penalty is negated by the [[Lighting (precept)|Lighting: Darklight Preferred]] precept or [[Dark vision]] gene,{{BiotechIcon}} making nutrifungus take more time to sow/harvest. In addition, since nutrifungus is generally grown inside, dirt's [[beauty]] penalty applies often causing the need to drop to zero for pawns farming it without sculptures nearby, this includes the {{--|4}} beauty raw fungus as an item creates.
* Non-[[Tunneler]] ideoligions dislike eating [[raw fungus]], with a {{--|3}} moodlet when cooked. This is a minor penalty but one that can be easily avoided by growing other crops.

Therefore, potatoes are generally a superior crop for standard colonies. The main reason why a non-Tunneler colony would grow nutrifungus over other crops is that it can grow indoors without needing a [[sun lamp]], thus providing a cheap and easily accessible way to grow food in the winter, permanent darkness, or under a mountain - see below for details.

Nutrifungus will not die when exposed to very cold temperatures (similar to [[healroot]]), though it still requires temperatures above {{Temperature|0}} to grow. 

=== Indoor farming ===
Even without the Tunneler meme, fungus can be worthwhile to sow. This is because nutrifungus can be grown in darkness, which lets you grow indoors without needing a [[sun lamp]]. Being indoors, in turn, lets you use [[heater]]s to control the temperature, and grow crops during the winter without needing a [[power]] hungry [[sun lamp]]. Therefore, it is a cost effective way to grow year-round, and it can allow pre-Electricity tribals to grow year-round too. Also since it grows indoors or in caves, it is considerably harder for raiders to set alight than other chemfuel feedstocks.

It can also be grown freely if you are forced to (or [[Darkness (meme)|want to]]) live under a [[sun blocker]]{{RoyaltyIcon}} or in a [[glowforest]]{{OdysseyIcon}} biome. With a sun blocker, you'll still need to place a [[roof]] over the growing area for colonists to actually plant the crop.
