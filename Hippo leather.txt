== Analysis ==
Hippo leather ties with [[bearskin]] for 6th most protective textile against sharp and blunt, and 5th against heat. They are very good candidates for [[duster]]s and other protective clothing - especially in temperate biomes. 

Hippo leather should be replaced by better materials, like [[devilstrand]], once available. [[Duster]]s and other protective apparel receive priority; using the best textiles on dusters has the most impact on protection. If limited on better material, you can use excess hippo leather for [[pants]] or [[button-down shirt]]s.

For use in [[furniture]], hippo leather is comparable to fox fur [[Property:Beauty Factor|beauty factor]]. But more beautiful textiles, like [[thrumbofur]], are in high demand as clothing. So, once hippo leather is made redundant for clothing, it is a decent choice for furniture [[beauty]].

Breeding hippos for leather is a questionable choice despite its relatively high market value.
