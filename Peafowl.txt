'''Peafowl''', called '''peacocks''' when male and '''peahens''' when female, are [[animal]]s added by the [[Odyssey DLC]]. Males of the species can be distinguished by their vibrant blue coloration and their large, green, fanned tails, while females, called peahens, are largely brown, with a dark green neck and lack any pronounced tail.

== Acquisition ==
Peafowl can be found in {{Habitats}}. They can either be tamed by a [[Work #Handle|handler]] or self-tame in a random event. 

Peafowl can be bought and sold in other [[Trade#Faction base|faction bases]] and from [[Trade#Exotic goods trader 2|exotic goods traders]]. {{PAGENAME}}s purchased from traders will be already tamed.

== Summary ==

A peahen produces {{P|Eggs Per Clutch Average}} egg every {{P|Egg Laying Interval}} day when fertilized. Peahens will not lay unfertilized eggs.
