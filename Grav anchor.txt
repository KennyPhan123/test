A '''grav anchor''' allows [[gravship]]s to launch without permanently destroying the tile it is on.

== Acquisition ==
{{Acquisition}}

== Summary ==
When installed in a map, grav anchors prevent the tile from being destroyed by [[gravship]] launches. It also prevents temporary maps, i.e., [[quest]] sites, [[camp]]s, and all areas in [[orbit]], from being abandoned when there are no non-[[downed]] colonists or [[slave]]s{{IdeologyIcon}} present.

The map will still remain loaded even after all pawns leave. If the anchor is destroyed, there is no [[grav engine]] present, the map hasn't been settled yet, and there are no non-downed colonists/slaves present, the map will be lost.

== Analysis ==
Grav anchors are essential for having a permanent colony and a gravship at the same time. They are also essential for a permanent colony in [[orbit]], whether on an asteroid or on one of sites generated by an [[orbital scanner]], as they cannot be settled normally.

In addition, anchors could be used to return to an unfinshed quest site if a gravship won't stay there. For example, if you enter a satellite and the [[uranium slug turret]]s kill an important colonist or two, the anchor will let you retreat and return. However, this requires bringing the materials to the quest site, spending said materials, having enough time to build the anchor, and not having the anchor be destroyed, so this is unlikely to be practical.

If the anchor is destroyed, no gravship is present, and it is not a permanent settlement, the map will be lost if there are no colonists or [[slave]]s{{IdeologyIcon}} there. Animals, buildings, items, and [[friendly mechanoids]]{{BiotechIcon}} will be abandoned, so it is very important to keep the anchors safe to not lose everything there.
