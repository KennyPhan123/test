'''Horax cults''' are semi-hidden [[Tech level|spacer tech level]] [[faction]]s added by the [[Anomaly DLC]] - each rimworld will have only one Horax cult, and it will be named '''The Servants of Horax'''. 

== Lore ==
Horax cultists worship Horax, the archotech anomaly behind the entities of the Anomaly DLC. Their ideology refers to its members as "cultists". They have their own unique "Horaxian" style for their ideology. Other than that, the cultists are a mystery. It is unknown whether Horax is actually communicating with the cultists, or if they only think Horax is.

== Summary ==
{{Stub|section=1|reason=Summary lacking, also needs links to raid types.}}

Horax Cultists will primarily appear in two [[major threat]] types: [[Psychic Ritual Siege]] and [[Hate chanters|Hate Chanters]]. Horax Cultists are hostile to all other factions including [[Insectoids]] and [[Entities]]. They are also hostile to factionless humans. It is not possible to improve relations with the Horax cult.

Horax Cultists always fight to the death and possibly beyond via [[Death refusal]]. They never retreat, withdraw, kidnap, nor steal: even during Psychic Ritual siege abductions. All cultists are [[Inhuman]], have a high prisoner resistance of 55 to 65, and can be studied for [[Research#Anomaly|Basic Anomaly Research]] when captured.
