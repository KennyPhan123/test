== Analysis ==
{{Stub|section=1|reason=Analysis}}
Mink fur is one of the least protective [[textiles]] in the game, 4-way tied for 18th most protective. However, it also has exceptional cold insulation, market value, and [[Property:Beauty Factor|beauty factor]]. It is quite similar to [[Chinchilla fur]], but it trades market value, beauty factor, and heat insulation for cold insulation.
