A '''bionic tongue''' acts as a replacement for a removed tongue. It has 100% part efficiency, perfectly replacing but not exceeding the capabilities of a natural tongue. 

== Acquisition ==
{{Acquisition}}

They can also be [[Trade|bought]] from traders.{{Check Tag|Which?}}{{Check Tag|Quest rewards?}}

== Summary ==
{{Rewrite|section=1|reason=Format standard}}
Bionic tongues fully replace an organic tongue. Without a tongue, pawns have 0% [[Talking]] and are unable to:
* Initiate or respond to relationship requests, become lovers, or propose marriage.
* Engage in conversations, good or bad.
** Rebuff or be rebuffed by others.
** Trigger [[social fight]]s by insulting others.
** Increase [[opinion]] of an existing romantic partner.
* Try to convert others to their [[ideoligion]].{{IdeologyIcon}}
* (to be checked): Issue role commands.{{IdeologyIcon}}

In addition, a pawn without a tongue is considered [[disfigured]], giving {{--|15}} [[opinion]] from others. They will also have a {{Thought|desc=I am missing my tongue. It is so frustrating not being able to speak!|label=Missing Tongue|value=-8|stack=1}} until the tongue is replaced.

Installing [[denture]]s or a [[bionic jaw]] will also replace a missing tongue, as well as removing any previously installed bionic tongues upon installation. It is impossible to install a bionic tongue on a colonist with a bionic jaw.

=== Installation ===
Installing the part requires {{Ticks|2500}} of work, 2x medicine of [[Herbal medicine|herbal]] quality or better, and a [[Skills#Medical|Medical skill]] of 5.

Removing the part requires {{ticks|2000}} of work, 1x medicine of [[Herbal medicine|herbal]] quality or better, and has no [[Skills#Medical|Medical skill]] requirements.

If the operation fails, the part will be destroyed.

== Analysis ==
As a bionic tongue has the same stats as a regular tongue, installing one on a healthy pawn serves no physical purpose. It can replace a missing tongue, although installing a [[denture]] will replace a missing tongue at partial effectiveness and a [[bionic jaw]] will replace it at {{Good|125%}} effectiveness. Alternatively, if [[Ideology DLC]] is installed, the [[biosculpter pod]]'s [[bioregeneration cycle]] {{IdeologyIcon}} can be used to directly restore the tongue at the cost of {{Icon Small|glitterworld medicine}} 2 instead.

If a pawn's tongue is missing, installing a denture is usually the more cost-effective option. A denture does have a [[Talking]] and [[Eating]] penalty, but unless that pawn uses the [[talking]] stat (wardening, trading, taming), the penalty is small enough to be tolerable. Alternatively, not replacing the tongue will prevent all negative converstations, which can help with [[trait]]s like Abrasive, but removes relationship penalties. Note that the [[healer mech serum]] can heal a missing tongue in place of other ailments, so replacing the tongue may allow healer mech serum to target another condition.

[[Bionic jaw]]s replace the tongue, offer better Eating and Talking (although no stat benefits from Talking over 100%), and are not much more expensive. Therefore, jaws are a better option for wardens as the extra Talking can help in case of injury.

Body modders and [[transhumanist]]s{{IdeologyIcon}} will be happier when using any artificial body part. Among the {{hover title|RimWorld base content without DLC|core RimWorld}} artificial parts that don't have any downsides, bionic tongues require the fewest [[advanced component]]s. This should be compared to [[denture]]s and [[cochlear implant]]s, which only gives a penalty to jobs using the Social or Animals skills. With the [[Royalty DLC]], [[venom fangs]],{{RoyaltyIcon}} [[knee spike]]s,{{RoyaltyIcon}} and [[elbow blade]]s{{RoyaltyIcon}} can provide a similar benefit without requiring advanced components and [[xenogerm]]s{{BiotechIcon}} are effectively free, even a purely cosmetic gene will satisfy them.
