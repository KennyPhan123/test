{{Biotech}}
'''Mechanoid commanders''' are three powerful [[mechanoids]] that are distinguished by their unique abilities, ability to be summoned by a colony separate to normal raids, and the dropping of chips used to research and build a number of Biotech exclusive buildings and items, primarily focused on improving [[mechanitor]]s. They are:
* {{Icon Small|Diabolus}} [[Diabolus]] - Summoned with the [[comms console]] and dropping the {{Icon Small|Signal chip}} [[Signal chip]].
* {{Icon Small|War queen}} [[War queen]] - Summoned with the [[mechband antenna]] and dropping the {{Icon Small|Powerfocus chip}} [[Powerfocus chip]].
* {{Icon Small|Apocriton}} [[Apocriton]] - Summoned with the [[mechband dish]] and dropping the {{Icon Small|Nano structuring chip}} [[Nano structuring chip]].

The term mechanoid commanders is used in the promotional material and version histories put out by Ludeon. In-game, the term is used in the [[apocriton]]'s description and messages/items relating to it.
