The ''filth rate'' is set by the property of the pawn in question and has no further modifiers. It therefore effectively ranges from '''[[Effective Minimum::1]]''', the default value, up to '''[[Effective Maximum::24]]''' for [[elephant]]s and [[megasloth]]s.

Note that as mentioned above how much filth is actually deposited also depends on the [[filth multiplier]] of the [[floor]], however straw matting is the only floor that has a value other than 100%.
