== Summary ==
{{Stub|section=1|reason=Base door open speed. Note: value in in-game infobox is not absolute door speed but rather its value relative to the base of its model)}}
{{Main|Door}}
Fence gates are largely equivalent to [[door]]s, enclosing [[rooms]] (including [[prison]]s), and containing [[temperature]]. Despite the description, animals cannot walk over fence gates. Also, fence gates have a faster opening speed, at base 115% compared to a regular door's 100%.

== Analysis ==
Fence gates are mostly identical to regular doors. They have the same material cost, amount of work, and cover as regular doors. The main differences are that fence gates open faster and do not support roofs but have much less HP. In areas where door HP doesn't matter, such as the interior of a base, you should use fence gates.

[[Autodoor]]s still open faster, so once you can afford them, use those where needed.
