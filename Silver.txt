{{info|'''Silver''' is a precious, uncommon [[materials|material]]. It is recognized as a [[trade]] currency which all traders will readily accept.}}

== Acquisition ==
Tiles of silver ore can be found on any map, but must be [[mine]]d before they can be used. Once you've completed the [[Research#Deep drilling|research]] necessary to construct a [[deep drill]], it can also be found in midsize deposits underground.

All traders carry a significant amount of silver on them, and will happily trade it for your goods. Non-tribal [[raiders]] may carry silver with them when they attack.

== Ore ==
[[File:Silver_ore.png|frameless|left|Silver ore]]

'''Silver ore''' tiles have 1,500 health each, making them one of the faster ores to mine. They can be found in midsize veins, ranging from 4 to 12 tiles in size. Each mined block has a base yield of 40 silver, however this is modified by factors such as the [[Difficulty]] setting and the [[Mining_Yield|mining yield]] of the pawn. 

Despite being literally made of RimWorld's currency, silver ore has the smallest monetary yield per tile of any ore. All other ores except [[Component#Ore|compacted machinery]] have the same base yield of 40 per tile of ore but produce resources worth more than {{Icon Small|silver||1}} each, while compacted machinery makes up for its much smaller yield with a significantly more valuable resource. However, silver as a currency is accepted by all traders at 100% value, and is lighter than most other metals.

== Summary ==
Silver is the primary trade currency, also represented by the dollar symbol ($).

In the [[trade]] menu, it will always appear as the topmost item, separated from the rest of the items. It cannot be bought or sold directly, but buying other items will cost silver and selling items will give the colony silver. Traders will always carry a finite amount of silver, and if they owe more than they have, any excess items are given for free.{{Check Tag|Goodwill?|Is the goodwill boost for overtrading equal that of regular gifitng?}}

== Analysis ==
{{For|advice on acquiring silver|Money making guide}}

For trading, silver is useful as a light and universal currency. For example, not all traders buy [[duster]]s, but you can sell dusters for silver at a [[faction base]] and use that to buy goods. Certain items like [[gold]] are also universally accepted while being more valuable, but since there is a [[trade price improvement|penalty for buying and selling]], it would be more economical to keep already obtained silver than to buy gold. Silver specifically is also required to create [[sterile tile]], to pay for ransoms and [[persona core]] locations over a [[comms console]], and less importantly, to create [[silver tile]].

In extreme [[difficulties]] (Losing is Fun and higher), hoarding silver just because you ''might'' need it to buy a rare item is unwise, as the extra [[wealth]] will increase the difficulty of raids. This applies to all money making items, not just silver itself - see [[wealth management]] for details.

=== As a material ===
Silver is beautiful as furniture and art, but in most cases, it is more efficient to use [[jade]] instead. Jade buildings require half the market value (each jade is worth {{Q|Jade|Market Value Base}}{{Icon Small|Silver}}, but requires 10x less materials) and are more beautiful than silver. Even when considering the [[Trade Price Improvement|colony trading disadvantage]], buying jade for building is cheaper than using silver directly. Jade is heavier and not always available to buy, but if making silver buildings, it is likely that the colony is advanced enough to not worry about that.

[[Silver tile]]s do have the niche of taking up no space and having increased [[cleanliness]], making them a strong option to boost room impressiveness when space is limited. They are also a [[fine floor]]{{RoyaltyIcon}} and competitive in that role. Compared to [[fine stone tiles]],{{RoyaltyIcon}} silver tiles take less work, are more beautiful and cleaner, and are faster to clean. Compared to [[fine carpet]],{{RoyaltyIcon}} silver tile is similar wealth while being nonflammable and faster to clean.

For weapons and armor, silver is equal to or worse than [[steel]] while being significantly more expensive.

When starting out on the [[sea ice]], silver can be used to build [[wall]]s to save on [[steel]].
