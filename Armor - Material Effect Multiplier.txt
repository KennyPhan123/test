It is the base value for an item of [[apparel]]. This is multiplied by the [[Stuff]]'s Armor Factor to get the final armor rating.

The game uses one stat for all armor types, but the wiki uses separate properties for Sharp, Blunt and Heat:
* [[Armor - Material Effect Multiplier]] × [[Property:Armor - Sharp Factor|Armor - Sharp Factor]] to get the final [[Armor - Sharp]] rating
* [[Armor - Material Effect Multiplier]] × [[Property:Armor - Blunt Factor|Armor - Blunt Factor]] to get the final [[Armor - Blunt]] rating
* [[Armor - Material Effect Multiplier]] × [[Property:Armor - Heat Factor|Armor - Heat Factor]] to get the final [[Armor - Heat]] rating
