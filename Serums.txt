{{Anomaly}}
'''Serums''' are [[drug]]-like consumables that alter or enhance a [[pawns]] natural capabilities.  Serums can be crafted at a [[serum lab]] once the respective research projects are completed. These projects require [[dark study]] to unlock. Unlike drugs, serums are typically long lasting, do not have a severity-based high, have no addiction, tolerance, or withdrawal mechanics, and do not count as any kind of drug for the purposes of the [[Chemical (need)|Chemical need]], the [[chemical interest]], [[chemical fascination|fascination]], or [[teetotaler]] traits, or the [[Drug use]] precepts.{{IdeologyIcon}}
