The '''meditation spot''' is a spot where your pawns will [[Meditation|meditate]].

== Acquisition ==
Meditation spots do not require a colonist to construct or [[deconstruct]], nor is there a cost, build time, or [[skill]] requirements for placing them. Instead, they are simply selected from the menu and placed directly by the player.

== Summary ==
Meditation spots designate where pawns meditate. A pawn can use a compatible focus object if at least one of its tiles is within the spot's 3.9-tile focus search radius and the object is visible from the spot. Different pawns can use different focus objects from the same spot.

An unassigned meditation spot will be used by pawns if unoccupied and if the spot is not ineligible for meditation, preferring spots that would provide better meditation focus. An assigned meditation spot will be used by that pawn if able. A pawn may only be assigned to one meditation spot at a time, although a pawn may be assigned to a [[meditation throne]] and spot at the same time.

Meditating colonists train the [[Intellectual|Intellectual skill]]. Meditation provides 18% as much base Intellectual skill experience as doing research for the same amount of time.

== Analysis ==
While not strictly necessary, meditation spots can help ensure that pawns won't wander too far away when trying to meditate. They can also be useful to command pawns to meditate at an [[anima tree]] despite not having a [[psylink]] yet.

If pawns choose to meditate at a different place than the assigned spot, it will be for one of these reasons:
* The meditation spot is in another pawn's [[bed]]room or [[prison]].
* The [[temperature]] is unsafe.
* It is not inside the pawn's [[allowed area]].
* It is in [[vacuum]].{{OdysseyIcon}}
