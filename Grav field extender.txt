A '''grav field extender''' increases the tile limit and building radius of a [[gravship]].

== Acquisition ==
{{Acquisition}}

== Summary ==
The gravship field extender increases the maximum amount of tiles that can be connected to a [[gravship]] by 250 tiles each. In addition, any tile within 16 tiles of a field extender will be connected to the gravship, thus increasing the distance that connected buildings can be placed. However, extenders must be placed in the range of the [[grav engine]], so daisy-chaining extenders is not possible.

A gravship can have up to 6 extenders at once.

== Analysis ==
For gravship-focused playthroughs, some amount of gravship field extenders are practically a must in order to give the gravship more space, and should be the first priority for [[gravcore]]s. In a colony where the gravship is only used for travel, they are less necessary but still helpful for added storage. In either case, once enough space is available, or if 6 extenders have already been built, [[gravcore power cell]]s can be made instead to provide more [[power]].

For the largest building radius, extenders should be placed far from the grav engine.
