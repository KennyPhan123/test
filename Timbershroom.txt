'''Timbershroom''' is a [[tree]] that spawns in caves and [[glowforest]]s{{OdysseyIcon}}. Cutting them does not give a mood penalty to those with a precept against [[cutting trees]].

== Occurrence ==
Timbershroom is a tree that can't be planted (except in glow forests), but spawns in caves &/or on Fungal gravel.
It may be more gainful than fibercorn, given space and '''luck'''.

In "soil", Fibercorn produces 0.18 wood/day of growth and Timbershroom produces 0.68 wood/day
