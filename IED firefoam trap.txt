'''IED firefoam traps''' are landmines that harmlessly explode, spreading [[firefoam]] in a radius when tripped.

== Acquisition ==
{{Acquisition}}

== Summary ==
[[File:Firefoam ied radius.png|thumb|left|The radius of the IED firefoam trap]]

{{IED Note}}

When triggered, the trap will begin to spark and emit a hissing sound for {{ticks|15}} before exploding, spreading [[firefoam]] in a 9.9-tile radius, extinguishing [[fire]]s and covering both the floor and nearby pawns in firefoam, preventing re-ignition. For a full list of effects, see [[Firefoam]].

Non-drafted pawns will attempt to run from an activated IED trap, even if it wouldn't affect them.{{Check Tag|Verify|verify this fact}}

== Analysis ==
{{Stub|section=1|reason=General use, value prop, strats etc.}}
Extinguishing your enemies isn't a particularly useful combat tactic. However, they can be useful for protecting your colony from fire.

IED firefoam traps are cheaper than [[firefoam popper]]s, but must be manually triggered.{{Check Tag|Damage?|What about direct damage from fire?}} [[Firefoam pop pack]]s are manually triggered and completely mobile, but have a smaller radius. Therefore, the firefoam trap is a cheaper alternative to protect large, stationary areas from fire. For dousing colonists, the pop pack is cheaper and more convenient to use.

They do have a rare combat niche for [[Sightstealer]]{{AnomalyIcon}} swarms and [[Revenant]] hunting, IED Firefoam traps can be used preferably in conjunction with [[Proximity detector]]s to reveal them, do note that Sightstealers and Revenants do not trigger traps so the IED must be shot.
