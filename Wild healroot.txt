'''Wild healroot''' grows in {{Habitats}}. Each healroot plant yields 1 herbal medicine when fully grown. Harvesting it early results in a chance of failing to receive anything from the harvest, even though it is not marked as failed. Any colonist capable of plant cutting can harvest wild healroot. Once the basics of a base is set up, harvesting all healroot near your base is advised.

Wild healroot is statistically identical to normal [[healroot]], except it cannot be cultivated manually. It has a grow time of 10 days, but harvesting wild healroot will reduce its growth percentage to 30% - so effectively grows for the same time.
