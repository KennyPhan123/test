The '''scattergun''' is an [[drone]]-only ranged [[weapon]] added by the [[Odyssey DLC]]. It is the main armament of the [[sentry drone]]. 

This weapon cannot be independently obtained in normal play. It is provided here for comparisons sake.

== Acquisition ==
Scatterguns spawn on all [[sentry drone]]s, however, it is not possible to obtain it directly in normal play. Pawns carrying it will not drop it on [[death]], [[downing]], or complete loss of [[manipulation]] like other weapons. It can be spawned in by the [[Development mode#Debug actions menu|Debug actions menu]] but, like all [[unobtainable items|unobtainable weapons]], will disappear after being dropped.

== Analysis ==
Scatterguns are essentially worse [[pump shotgun]]s, dealing less damage and taking longer to cooldown. In the open, [[sentry drone]]s are vulnerable to [[kiting]], and in buildings, they can be ambushed in melee.
