The '''tactical turret''' is a temporary short range, low power [[turret]] deployed from the [[turret pack]].

== Acquisition ==
It is deployed by the [[turret pack]].

== Summary ==
[[File:Tactical_turret_widget_gizmos.png|thumb|left|Battery power tooltip: This turret has a limited battery life. Once it runs out, it will stop working entirely.]]

Once deployed, it lasts for {{Ticks/gametime/hours|12500}}, and it remains in an unpowered state once the battery is depleted.

It is forbiddable, breakdownable, stunnable by [[Stun]] and [[EMP]] damage, and has a 50% chance to explode from damage while below 20% health, taking {{Ticks|240}} to explode, with a radius of 3.9 cells of [[Bomb]] damage.
