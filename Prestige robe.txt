The '''prestige robe''' is a clothing item added by the [[Royalty DLC]]. [[Titles|Nobles]] of Archon rank and above require these, a [[cape]], an [[eltex robe]], or prestige [[power armor]] to consider themselves properly dressed and avoid a negative moodlet.

== Acquisition ==
{{Acquisition}}

It can also be found on [[Empire#Pawns|Imperial Nobles]], for sale at [[traders]] and as a [[quest]] reward. All [[Empire#Bestower|Imperial Bestowers]] spawn with a red [[hyperweave]] prestige robe of Excellent [[quality]].

== Summary ==
{{Apparel Suppression Note|-0.20}}

When combined with suitable headgear, a [[formal shirt]], and a [[formal vest]] / [[corset]], a [[noble]] of the Archon rank will be properly dressed and not count as a moodlet. Alternatively, a [[cape]], any [[eltex]] bodywear, prestige power armor, or [[mechlord suit]]{{BiotechIcon}} fulfills the need for shirt, formal vest, and robe at the same time.

Prestige robes provide {{+|20%}} [[Toxic Environment Resistance]] even if the [[Biotech DLC]] is disabled.

== Analysis ==
As an actual piece of clothing, a prestige robe is nearly identical to a regular [[robe]], the only difference being the prestige robe's +20% Toxic Environment Resistance. Compared to [[cape]]s and [[duster]]s, prestige robes have identical coverage, two-thirds as much armor, and much less heat insulation, but slightly better cold insulation. [[Parka]]s have the same armor value and much better cold insulation, but don't cover the legs.

Overall they are outclassed by [[cape]]s in warm environments, as capes provide more armor and do not require wearing a [[formal shirt]] or a [[formal vest]]/[[corset]]. Prestige [[power armor]] sets are more expensive, but provides massive cold insulation as well as protection and a [[psychic sensitivity]] boost. For non-nobles, regular [[robe]]s will suffice unless toxic resistance is especially needed. 

They do provide a source of Toxic Environment Resistance even without the [[Biotech DLC]], and are the only body apparel to do so, but the better protection of other armors is typically more important.
