'''Impids''' are a [[Xenotypes|xenotype]] of fire-breathing devilish desertpeople. They are adapted well to the desert, but sacrifice ability with melee fighting, plants, and animals, to accomplish this. They're recognizable by their horns, bright skin, and fire-breathing attacks.

== Lore ==
<onlyinclude>Made for desert life, impid cultures live on many [[Lore#Rimworld|rimworlds]] and dry [[Lore#Deathworld|deathworlds]]. Outlanders call them dustrunners because of their speed over open terrain and resistance to heat. Some tribes call them devils because of their horns and because they burn their enemies.

Impids are extremely fast-moving, easily tolerate high temperatures, and can spew fire from their mouths. Though they are masters on the sand, impids struggle in other environments.<ref name="Impid xenotype long desc">[[Impid]] [[xenotype]] description</ref> They are terrible with farming,<ref name="Impid xenotype long desc"/><ref name="Impid xenotype short desc">[[Impid]] [[xenotype]] short description</ref> and have weak immune systems.<ref name="Impid xenotype short desc"/> They fight well at range, but in close combat they do little damage<ref name="Impid xenotype short desc"/> and their physical weakness becomes apparent.<ref name="Impid xenotype long desc"/> Impid tribes' combat tactics usually center on rapid-approach incendiary attacks using both [[Flamebow|handheld fire weapons]] and their fire spew ability.<ref name="Impid xenotype long desc">[[Impid]] [[xenotype]] description</ref> 

This is reflected in the particular tribe/s encountered in-game - they have no intention of coexisting peacefully with anyone else and their warriors are experts at using their natural speed and flame attacks to burn the towns and children of other peoples. <ref name="Impid faction desc">[[Savage impid tribe]] [[faction]] description</ref>They have been on the planet for thousands of years<noinclude><ref name="Biotech Preview 4">[https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/294100/view/3319740412995643281 Biotech Preview #4] Steam Announcement</ref></noinclude><includeonly><ref name="Biotech Preview 4"/></includeonly>, and the origins of this tribe are lost in myth. They might descend from a group of settlers who lost their technology, or an impid community in some long-ruined civilization.<ref name="Impid faction desc"/>

Note that this places the origin of the xenotype to approximately 3500 at the latest, as it is "thousands of years" prior to the 5500 date of the game.</onlyinclude>

As raiders, impids have a x1.2 [[raid points|combat power]] multiplier, meaning they count as 1.2 of a raider. A raid consisting of all impids would be at most {{%|1/1.2 round 2}} as large as the same raid with [[baseliner]]s. An Impid raid will likely be even smaller than {{%|1/1.2 round 2}} because many enemies have special Impid versions that cost more than their standard tribal counterparts.

== Summary ==
Impids generally live in the desert and their genes reflect this. They have a mixed bag of physical abilities, but their fire spew ability is their main talent. 

=== Visuals ===
Impids differ from regular baselines in two major ways: their skin color (which can vary from orange all the way to red) and the mini-horns which protrude from their head. These both offer no advantage in combat and are purely cosmetic. They also have blond hair and no beards.

== Acquisition ==
You'll meet many impids when you get attacked by the Savage Impid Tribe: a vicious tribe with every intention of burning your base down. There is a slight chance that impids will show up with various [[Pirates|pirate gangs]], but it appears impids do not attempt to peacefully cohabitate in regular tribes and unions. They will also appear in transport pod crashes, as is the same for almost every xenotype.

== Analysis ==
Impids are threatening enemies due to the combination of Very Fast Runner and Fire Spew. Impids are hard to [[kiting|kite]], due to their speed. Their [[fire]] can cause pawns to run around wildly, removing them from [[cover]]. Care should be taken in indoor areas as the fire can quickly spread. [[Mechanoid]]s are a decent counter against their fire-breathing; mechs are immune to fire, but enemies will waste their fire spew regardless. Weak melee damage encourages melee tactics.

[[File:impid_immolation.png|thumb|A group of impids spewing fire at a berserked impid]]
The berserk psycast and insanity lances are effective tools to exhaust the fire spews from Impid raids. A single berserked Impid at a chokepoint is likely to bait a dozen fire spews, as nearby Impids use it on the berserked pawn, burning other Impids in the process.

As colonists, impids main benefit is very fast runner's movement speed. Fire Spew has a 5 day cooldown, but it can be useful to disengage from melee enemies. They are poor at Plants and Animals, but are reasonably good at most other tasks, depending on the specific colonist. Impids are naturally resistant against heat and weak against cold, though [[parka]]s and/or [[heater]]s should keep impids warm even in the coldest of [[biome]]s. High speed, weak melee, and slow wound healing encourage them to be ranged fighters.

It is not recommended to use impids as slaves. The depressive gene leads to frequent nervous breakdowns, and during uprisings, impids can set fire to valuable or explosive objects. On the other hand, weak close combat allows two impids to stun rebellious colonists without harm to health.

=== Gene extraction ===
Some of the more valuable genes impids have to [[gene extractor|extract]] are:
* Poor <Skill> - If a pawn won't use these skills, poor skill genes are a net positive.
* [[Very fast runner]] - While metabolically expensive, [[move speed]] is very useful and the more reasonably priced [[Fast runner]] relies on catching rare [[sanguophages]] or finding one for sale by chance.
* [[Fire resistant]] - The reduction in the chance to be set on fire makes this a very useful gene on frontline fighters, especially when facing impids themselves. The reduction in Flame damage is just a bonus.
