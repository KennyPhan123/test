'''Rescue''' is an [[Order]] that can be performed on any [[Downed]] [[Animals]], [[Human]]s, and [[Mechanoid]]s. Note that Mechanoids cannot normally be downed, and instead will always die upon being downed.

[[Animal]]s require a reachable, un-reserved [[animal sleeping spot]] in safe [[temperature]].
[[Human]]s require a reachable, un-reserved [[sleeping spot]], [[bed]], or [[hospital bed]] in safe [[temperature]]. Note that [[Animal]]s and [[Human]]s may [[Death|die]] of any [[Ailment]]s even in the hands of a rescuer.

For rescue purposes, temperature is considered safe if it is within {{Temperature|90||delta}} of the rescuer's [[Comfortable Temperature]] range. The comfortable range of the pawn being rescued is not taken into account.

Rescuing pawns entails no [[moving|Movement]] Penalty.

== Manual Rescue ==
[[Animal]]s and [[Human]]s can be manually [[carrying|carried]], and ordered to be placed into any sleeping spot or bed by [[draft|Drafted]] pawns. Pawns carrying other pawns can use [[jump pack]] to traverse terrain easier.
