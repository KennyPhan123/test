'''Terror sculpture''' are a type of [[sculpture]] added by the [[Ideology DLC]] that inflict [[terror]] to help keep [[slave]]s suppressed. 

== Acquisition ==
{{Acquisition}} Unlike most crafted items, the quality of the sculpture depends on the [[artistic]] skill of the sculptor.

== Summary ==
[[File:Terror sculpture radius.png|thumb|left|200px|Area of effect radius of a terror sculpture]]

Terror sculptures cause slaves to feel [[terror]], inside an 5 tile radius, decreasing [[suppression]] loss rate.

The terror induced by a terror statue depends on their [[quality]]. Material has no effect.

== Analysis ==
Terror statues are quite [[beauty|ugly]]. This beauty penalty should be considered if using terror sculptures, but investing in high beauty materials to offset the penalty is not worth it versus using those materials to add other sources of beauty. 

The sources of terror are skull spikes, gibbet cages, and terror statues. For skull spikes you'll need to harvest skulls from corpses, for gibbet cages you'll need to put corpses into them. Terror sculptures allow a terror causing building option, with no skull or corpse requirement.
