A '''small nature shrine''' is a type of [[furniture]] added by the [[Royalty DLC]] at which a pawn can meditate for recreation and to regain [[psyfocus]].

== Acquisition ==
{{Acquisition}}

== Summary ==
{{Stub|section|reason=Raider avoidance mechanic - ensure parity with all other similar items}}
Small nature shrines are a natural [[meditation focus]]. They have a base meditation focus strength of '''22%'''. Each [[animus stone]] within 9.9 tiles increases the meditation effectiveness by {{+|2%}}, up to a total of four stones for a maximum of {{+|8%}}.

Like other natural focus objects, its effectiveness is reduced by artificial buildings within a 34.9 tile radius. Note that this does not include [[floor]]ing and [[torch lamp]]s. The reduction is set by the following curve:

The maximum meditation focus strength is thus '''30%''' for a small nature shrine with no artificial buildings within 34.9 tiles and surrounded by four [[animus stone]]s.

Small nature shrines also give {{+|1%}} psyfocus per day to other nearby natural foci, for a total of {{+|4%}} with four nature shrines.

== Analysis == 
{{Stub|section=1|reason=Better comparison of nature options, and when to use this if ever. Some mention of other focus also valuable }}
Small nature shrines can be used to boost the psyfocus gain of the [[anima tree]]. If you can obtain them, [[animus stone]]s are superior, but animus stones aren't a common item.

They can also be used for psyfocus if the anima tree is unavailble. The [[large nature shrine]] gives +8% psyfocus for only {{Icon Small|stuff}} 150 [[wood]] / [[stone]] / [[steel]] more. If you cannot justify the cost of the large nature shrine, then the small shrine is a subsitute.
