The '''sarcophagus''' is a building used to store a [[corpse]] and is an alternative burial method to the [[grave]]. 

== Acquisition ==
{{Acquisition}}

Sarcophagi made of [[steel]], [[granite blocks|granite]], [[limestone blocks|limestone]], [[marble blocks|marble]], [[sandstone blocks|sandstone]] and [[slate blocks|slate]], can also spawn at map generation alongside other [[ruins]].

== Summary ==
{{Stub|section=1|reason=burial mechanics, recreation mechanics, include Ideology effects.}}

The storage tab above the inspect pane can be configured to set what types of pawns can be buried in the selected sarcophagus. The default setting is colonists only.

The command icons (Copy settings, Paste settings) can be used to copy storage settings between sarcophagi and graves. Settings can also be copied to other containers like stockpile zones and dumping stockpile zones; however, settings that a sarcophagus doesn't have (i.e. food) will be copied as disallowed. The copy function can take settings from one container and apply them to many containers, when shift-click is used to select multiple.

A body can be removed from a grave with the 'Open' command, and will be done by a colonist assigned to [[hauling]]. A [[Unnatural corpse]]{{AnomalyIcon}} that was buried will automatically get out on its own when offscreen.

The sarcophagus will gain an art piece related to the dead pawn on it {{Check Tag|Unnatural corpses?}} once a pawn has been interred into the sarcophagus. A room with sarcophagi will be classified as a [[Room roles|tomb]].

As sarcophagi can be placed in freezers to preserve their inhabitants, they are a good method for protecting the corpses of colonists waiting for [[resurrector mech serum]] from hungry [[animals]] and overly efficient [[human resources|butchers]].

Organic corpses in sarcophagi will still be raised as a [[shambler]]{{AnomalyIcon}} if the sarcophagus is exposed to [[deadlife dust]],{{AnomalyIcon}} opening and deposing the corpse next to the sarcophagus to undergo the usual raising process. The [[Corpse#Rotten corpses|decay level]] of the corpse, or the type of organic pawn it was in life has no effect on whether it can successfully exit the sarcophagus. Corpses inside sarcophagi will '''not''' be raised by a [[death pall]]{{AnomalyIcon}} however.

=== Meditation ===
Sacrophagi can be used as a Morbid [[meditation focus]].{{RoyaltyIcon}} It has a base strength of +10% psyfocus per day, increased by +10% for being filled, and increased by another +10% if it contains a pawn with a relationship to the meditator. It is further increased by being withing 10 tiles of up to 4 [[grave]]s or sarcophagi (+1% if empty, +2% if full), for a maximum psyfocus of 38%.

== Analysis ==
There are few mechanical benefits for using a sarcophagus over a [[grave]]. Sarcophagi are better as a Morbid [[meditation focus]].{{RoyaltyIcon}} They can also be built on constructed [[floor]]s and stone floor while graves can't, slightly improving the [[cleanliness]] and [[beauty]] of a room. Otherwise, a grave fulfils the same purpose but cheaper. Of course, a sacrcophagus can be built for sentimental reasons or to make a favorite colonst's corpse easier to distinguish. Sarcophagi provide no beauty unless made of a material like [[marble]] or [[jade]], and even then the pre-[[quality]] beauty is equal that of a [[wall]] or [[fence]] of that material.

If wanting to preserve a colonist's corpse so that it can be revived by [[resurrector mech serum]], place a sacrophagus or grave inside of a freezer. If corpse preservation is critical, the following setup would provide the most safety to a corpse:
* Build a dedicated freezer containing a sacrophagus or [[grave]].
* Have [[cooler]]s set to as low as possible, well below freezing to keep temperatures low in case of a [[solar flare]].
* Have a double walled room with an airlock of multiple doors, all foribidden. A [[vac barrier]]{{OdysseyIcon}} should be included if possible.

== Notes ==
Despite the mood bonus for burying a colonist in a sarcophagus being removed, it still exists in the game code. This is unlike other obsolete content which is usually removed. It is currently unclear if it is simply a bug that it doesn't occur, if its lack of removal is an oversight, or if there are simply unknown requirements to trigger it in-game.
