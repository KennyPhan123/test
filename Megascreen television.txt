A '''megascreen television''' is a [[recreation]] item that provides "{{P|Recreation Type}}" recreation to [[colonist]]s in an area in front of it. It is functionally similar to other types of [[television]].

== Acquisition ==
Megascreen televisions cannot be constructed. Instead, it can only be bought from traders ({{Traders}}), found in [[ancient shrine]]s, or obtained from a quest.

== Summary ==
{{Television Summary|7|6}}
[[File:Megascreen tv.jpg|thumb|center|A megascreen television and its area of effect.]]

== Analysis ==
It has a 6×7 area of effect on the other side of a 1 space gap in front of the TV. The seats do not necessarily have to face the television.

These TVs can be placed in front of many [[hospital bed]]s to entertain the patients. The patients will be entertained whether they face the TV or not, as long as the head part of the bed is within the TV's area of effect.

Because of its huge size, it may be hard to find a good position for it.

== Trivia ==
Despite being uncraftable, there is a partial recipe defined for the megascreen television in the game files. Namely, {{icon Small|steel}} 260 [[steel]] and {{icon Small|Component}} 22 [[component]]s. It is possible this is used to calculate the amount deconstruction returns, or it may simply be cut content.
