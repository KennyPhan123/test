'''Ancient exostrider leg''' are [[ruins]] added by the [[Biotech DLC]].

== Summary ==
The ancient exostrider leg spawns in a set of three, along with 1 [[ancient exostrider midsection]], 1 [[ancient exostrider head]], and 1 [[ancient exostrider cannon]].

Destruction will yield 2 [[steel slag chunk]]s.
