== Calculating Melee Cooldown ==
The base cooldown time of a melee attack is multiplied by [[Melee Cooldown|Melee Cooldown (Stuff Multiplier)]] if applicable, and then multiplied by [[Melee Cooldown (Pawn)]]:

<code>Final Cooldown Time = Base Cooldown Time × Melee Cooldown (Stuff Multiplier) × [[Melee Cooldown (Pawn)]]</code>

For hediffs, stat offsets are applied first and factors second. Although with the current usage of this stat, no instances of offsets and factors both applying at once would arise.
