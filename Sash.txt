The '''sash''' is a piece of [[apparel]] added by the [[Biotech DLC]]. 

== Acquisition ==
{{Acquisition}}

== Summary ==
{{Apparel Nudity Note}}

Beyond this, the sash is otherwise a typical item of clothing, just with a below average coverage and statistical effect.

== Analysis ==
The sash gives a slight boost to insulation; the armor bonus is small and is usually negated completely by weapons' armor piercing values. The [[formal vest]]{{RoyaltyIcon}}/[[corset]]{{RoyaltyIcon}} is strictly superior in both respects, and [[flak vest]]s are far more protective, so for most colonists it only has the niche use of "well, it's better than nothing" before they can make better clothes.

The main use case is for [[nudist]]s, who otherwise ''would'' in fact wear nothing. It's one of the few nudist-friendly ways to improve their comfortable temperature range, if only slightly. Additionally, it will not interfere with the movement speed increase from being "unclothed" caused by the [[Naked speed]] gene, hence the tendency of [[Yttakin]] to appear wearing only a sash, and perhaps a hat or [[heavy bandolier]].
