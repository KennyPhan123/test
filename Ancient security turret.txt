'''Ancient security turret''' are [[ruins]] added by the [[Ideology DLC]]. They can be found on the colony map and at [[ancient complex]]es. They strongly resemble [[mini-turret]]s that can be constructed by a colony, however they are all visibly destroyed and are colored differently.
