A '''Sacrificial flag''' is a [[ritual]] focus added by the [[Ideology DLC]]. It represents a colony's enemies and gives a focus for the symbol burning ritual.

== Acquisition ==
Constructing a sacrificial flag requires {{Required Resources}} and {{Ticks|{{P|Work To Make}}}} of work. They require an ideoligion with a symbol burning ritual in order to be constructed. They must also be listed as a building on the ideoligion to be built.

== Summary ==
{{Stub|section=1|reason= Missing summary - Mechanical detail of which rituals use it, how they're used, what mechanically happens etc.}}
Adding a symbol burning festival without giving your [[Ideoligion]] access to either the [[pyre]], sacrificial flag, or [[effigy]] will result in the ritual being impossible to complete. Despite being lit on fire as part of the symbol burning ritual, it has a flammability score of 0, and can't set it's surroundings on fire.

{{Pyromaniac Building Note}}

== Analysis ==
{{Stub|section=1|reason= Missing analysis - Needs comparison to other burnables, comparison of its rituals to other choices etc.}}
Compared to other objects for ritualistic burning, it can be hard to obtain due to its need for cloth. Since it can't spread fire, it is safe to put in your temples for the room impressiveness bonus (although it does create a lot of filth). Symbol burning, compared to other rituals such as social festivals, can be sub-optimal as it consumes resources.
