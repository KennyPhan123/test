'''Omnivorous grazers''' are creatures that will eat [[plants]] or [[meat]]. Unlike their [[Omnivore|non-grazing counterparts]], they can also eat live plants. They usually rely on wild plants for sustenance but will eat any [[food]] left lying about like raw [[meat]], [[vegetables]], or prepared [[meals]].
