'''Control sublinks (standard)''' are [[artificial body parts]] added by the [[Biotech DLC]]. Each [[control sublink]] installed increases the number of [[Mechanitor#Mechanoid Control|control groups]] available to a [[mechanitor]] and increase the work speed of their mechs. 

== Acquisition ==
{{Acquisition}}

== Summary ==
Each standard and [[Control sublink (high)|high control sublink]] increases the number of [[control group]]s available to a mechanitor by {{+|1}} and the work speed of their mechs by {{+|6%}}, up to a maximum of {{+|6}} and {{+|36%}}. Both sublink types have a [[max install count]] of three, meaning up to three of each type of sublink can be installed into a mechanitor's brain. At least three standard sublinks must be installed before any high sublinks can be installed.

As all non-[[Tunneler]] laborer mechs have a base Global Work Speed of 50%, this boost is relatively higher than it seems. For example, 1 sublink with no other factors turns 50% work speed into 56% workspeed; a {{Good|x112%}} increase.

=== Installation ===
{{stub|section=1|reason=Written from memory. Verification, expansion, and clarity needs work}}
Unlike most other artificial body parts, the control sublink is not installed via an [[operation]]. Instead, the selected mechanitor can be selected to use the device on themselves, consuming the item and installing it.

== Analysis ==
Typically, you may want to use the [[signal chip]]s the sublinks require on a [[bandwidth pack]] first. The extra mechs from the (worst-case) 30% increase in bandwidth will have a much larger impact on both combat and economic potential than {{+|12%}} work speed. Best-case, it is a 150% ''increase''. The extra mechs do come at cost of additional outlay, [[power]], and [[pollution]] however.

Eventually, you'll want to install all 6 control sublinks - more for the additional work speed, rather than the control groups. A few extra control subgroups can be helpful, but you'll rarely need more than 4.
