'''Sealed containers''' are structures added by the [[Odyssey DLC]]. Glows slightly, has contents that it ejects when opened. 

== Occurrence ==
There are two types of sealed container depending on where they spawn, with different contents. 

The first type contains a [[gravship]] upgrade and has a 33% chance to spawn on the following [[Quests#Odyssey|gravship quest]] sites: ancient reactor, abandoned orbital platform, crashed mechanoid platform, and mechanoid platform. It can occasionally spawn on the abandoned platform quest sites found by the [[orbital scanner]].

The second type has more general content and spawns on satellite quest sites found by the [[orbital scanner]].
