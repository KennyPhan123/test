'''Ancient nav beacon''' are [[ruins]] added by the [[Ideology DLC]]. They will often spawn in the corners of partially incomplete, 11x11 areas of [[concrete]], with anywhere between 1 and 4 ancient nav beacons being present - presumably representing [[ship landing beacon|ship landing zones]].
