'''Daylilies''' are a decorative [[plant]] that have relatively high [[beauty]], but need to be replanted often. They can be grown indoors, simply using the light from a [[torch lamp|torch]] or [[standing lamp]].

== Acquisition ==
Daylilies can be sown by [[Work#Grow|Growers]] in [[plant pot]]s or in [[Growing zone]]. Sowing requires {{Ticks|{{P|Sow Work}}}} of work. 

== Analysis ==
[[File:Daylilies_dining_room.png|thumb|left|300px|Daylilies turn a simple 13x13 dining room from ''Mediocre'' to ''Very Impressive'' at the cost of work.<br>All buildings placed are of normal [[quality]].]]
Daylilies are a quick and cheap way to increase a room's [[beauty]], and therefore, [[impressiveness]]. A [[plant pot]] is unnecessary; simply keeping a section, or even all, of the floor as dirt saves on having to build a pot in the first place. 

In essence, they are a way to turn work time into positive moodlets, before you can build [[sculpture]]s for a more permanent mood increase. Daylilies take more work to maintain than [[rose]]s. However, daylilies have a higher beauty. With a higher beauty per time sowing, they are better if you are growing multiple flowers in a single, centralized area. (Roses would be better if you have to plant flowers in separate areas, as they reduce walking time).

As shown in the image to the left, planting daylilies can have a large impact. The increase in room impressiveness results in a net {{+|10}} mood from the room bonuses alone - {{+|5}} each for the room as a dining room and as a rec room. This is in addition to the mood bonus from a full [[beauty]] need, which can be as high as {{+|15}} at 100%. Note that [[filth]] would quickly lower this room back down to "Somewhat impressive" but further improvements such as the addition of [[dining chair]]s will provide enough of a impressiveness buffer to prevent small specks of filth from lowering it further.

Later on, you will want to switch to [[sculpture]]s, as they don't require constant maintenance, can be placed on regular floor, and are significantly better at filling the beauty need. Sculptures increase [[wealth]] but this amount is trivial outside of 500% difficulties - see [[Wealth#Practical_examples|Wealth]] for a more detailed analysis. Sculptures also unlock the "Viewing Art" solitary [[recreation]] activity, however the effect of this is usually minor.
