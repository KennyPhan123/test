A '''{{PAGENAME}}''' is an [[animal]] added by the [[Odyssey DLC]].

== Acquisition ==

{{PAGENAME}}s can be found in {{Habitats}}. They can either be tamed by a [[Work #Handle|handler]] or self-tame in a random event. 

{{PAGENAME}}s can be bought and sold in other [[Trade#Faction base|faction bases]] and from [[Trade#Exotic goods trader 2|exotic goods traders]]. {{PAGENAME}}s purchased from traders will be already tamed.

== Analysis ==
The Macaw and the crow are the two trainable animals with the lowest combat power and thus raid points. The Macaw is more valuable at 90 market value making it the second most raid point efficient option for a bonded animal. In comparison to the Crow the Macaw eats slightly more (.19 vs .15). They both have a wildness of 25% and a min handling skill of 2. The Macaw has 2x the health scale (20% vs 10%) though still likely to die in any combat. Macaws have a vastly longer life span 50 years vs the crow's 4 making Macaws a more suitable choice for longer running games if you don't want your bonded animal dying of old age before the run finishes. If sentient catalysts are taken into account, Sparrows have even less combat power at 20 Vs Macaws and Crows 25. A catalysted Sparrow also has a modified min handling skill of 3 and a wildness of 35% requiring more a more skilled pawn and more pawn time to keep trained. a similarly catalysted Macaw or crow have a min handling skill of 1 and a wildness of zero which almost any pawn has enough skill to train.
