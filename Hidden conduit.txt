A '''hidden conduit''' transmits [[power]] from generators or [[batteries]] to appliances up to six squares away. Conduit doesn't block the placement of other structures and isn't blocked by them, so conduit can be placed wherever it's needed, even in walls, except unsmoothed mountain rocks and ores. Hidden conduits differ from power conduits in that they cannot be attacked, are invisible, and are immune to [[short circuit]]s.

== Acquisition ==
{{Acquisition}}

== Summary ==
Hidden conduits transport power. There must be a direct connection to another conduit, [[power switch]], battery, or power source for a conduit to be able to transport power. Electric appliances, such as an electric stove, can link up to a conduit, battery, or generator up to 6 tiles away. In other words, anything with the "Reconnect" gizmo can attach to a distant conduit, but conduits can't attach to a distant conduit.

Hidden conduits occupy the same layer as regular [[power conduit]]s and can be placed under [[wall]]s, but they are invisible on the ground and do not contribute any [[beauty]] penalty. They cannot be targeted or attacked by any pawn, nor can they be lit on [[fire]] or destroyed by any type of explosion. The only ways that hidden conduits can be removed are:
* An [[overhead mountain]] [[roof collapse]]. Standard roof collapses will not damage hidden conduits.
* If they are constructed on a [[bridge]] and the bridge is destroyed.
* If deconstructed by a pawn.

Unlike regular power conduits, hidden conduits cannot [[short circuit]].

== Analysis ==
Because of their practical invincibility, lack of negative impact to [[beauty]], and resistance to [[short circuit]]s they are superior to normal power conduits in every way, though cost double the steel and take 8 times longer to build. Unless truly strapped for steel or labor, they are always preferable.

In the early game, it is possible to connect appliances directly to a generator or battery to avoid having to build any conduits.
