* Def Name:<br />PsychicEntropy
* Label:<br />neural heat
| Despite being called "heat", neural heat is not a measure of temperature. It refers to a type of chaotic psychic energy in the mind which builds up when using psychic powers and dissipates over the course of a few seconds or minutes.<br /><br />If neural heat is pushed past normal limits, it may cause temporary psychic breakdown and unconsciousness due to psychic shock. These conditions don't cause permanent damage.
