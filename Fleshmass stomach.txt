The '''fleshmass stomach''' is a [[body part]] added by the [[Anomaly DLC]] that replaces a human [[stomach]] and provides immunity to [[food poisoning]].

== Acquisition ==
{{Mutated Body Part Aquisition}}

== Summary == 
The fleshmass stomach replaces the user's organic stomach. The fleshmass stomach has a part efficiency of 100%. This fully replaces the functionality of a normal stomach but provides no direct enhancement - when coupled with the stomach body part having a [[Digestion]] importance of 50%, it results in an increase in digestion of up to 50% when replacing a missing or totally inoperable Stomach. Digestion, in turn, affects a pawn's [[Rest Rate Multiplier]]. 

A fleshmass stomach also renders the pawn immune to [[food poisoning]] and will prevent a colonist from contracting [[gut worms]]. It will also cure a pre-existing case of gutworms.

A fleshmass stomach inflicts {{++|8%}} [[pain]] on its bearer. With no other sources of pain, this is insufficient to inflict any [[Consciousness]] penalties but does inflict a {{--|5}}/{{+|5}}/{{+|3}} [[mood]]let for normal, [[masochist]], and [[Pain (precept)|Pain Idealizing]]{{IdeologyIcon}} pawns respectively. These thoughts do not stack, with the most positive taking precedent. 

Finally, for [[psycast]]ers,{{RoyaltyIcon}} pain also adds a multiplier of <code>100% + 3 × Pain percentage</code> to [[Neural Heat Recovery Rate]], or {{Good|x{{#expr:100 + (3*8)}}%}} assuming no other sources of pain besides the stomach.

Note that the effects of pain on mood and consciousness are not strictly linear, and the values may be different for pawns with other sources of pain. See the [[Pain]] article for details. 

Note that the fleshmass stomach has no item form.

== Analysis ==
{{Stub|section=1|reason=Lots to compare to, see other stomach pages but wb sterilizer stomach specifically/especially?}}
In terms of effectiveness, a Fleshmass Stomach is a side grade to the [[Bionic stomach]]. Offering no chance for food poisoning over the increased efficiency from the bionic. However, the Fleshmass Stomach is effectively a free upgrade, requiring only to wait for the cooldown and patience with the RNG. For colonists with the masochist trait or a colony with the Idealized Pain Precept, the pain that comes from the Fleshmass Stomach might even make it preferable to a Bionic Stomach.
