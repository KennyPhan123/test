{{Biotech}}
'''Subcore''' refers to one of three different crafted resources used in the creation of mechanoids. These are:
* [[Basic subcore]], created at a [[subcore encoder]].
* [[Standard subcore]], created from a human subject at a [[subcore softscanner]].
* [[High subcore]], created by killing a human subject at a [[subcore ripscanner]].
Each player-created mechanoid requires one subcore of the correct type, with the exception of the [[war urchin]].
