The '''eltex staff''' is a [[Weapons#Melee Weapons|melee weapon]] added by the [[Royalty DLC]] that improves [[psychic sensitivity]] and makes [[psycast]]ers more effective.

It is one of a set of [[eltex|eltex items]] with similar effects.

== Acquisition ==
Eltex staffs cannot be crafted. Instead can be purchased from any combat supplier or war merchant or obtained as a quest reward. 
Additionally, [[Empire#Bestower|Imperial Bestowers]] always spawn with an eltex staff.

== Summary ==
Eltex staves improve the wielder's [[neural heat recovery rate]] by {{+|0.083}} heat/second, and increases [[Psychic Sensitivity]] by an additive amount dependent on [[quality]]. The increase in Psychic Sensitivity also increases the neural heat limit. The increases are shown in the table below:

{{Psychic Sensitivity Quality Table|0.50|0.083}}

Note that eltex staves are unable to be wielded by pawns incapable of violence.

== Analysis ==
Eltex staves are clearly meant for psycast focused characters, allowing for more casts. Despite its description claiming that "it is a solid melee weapon", the actual attack is weaker than a piece of [[wood]].

Compared to [[bioferrite]]{{AnomalyIcon}} weapons, eltex staves will always offer better [[neural heat recovery rate|neural heat recovery]]. [[Neural heat limit]] is more complicated: bioferrite multiplies psychic sensitivity instead of adding it, meaning bioferrite can provide a higher limit when modifiers are stacked. For reference, a legendary bioferrite weapon provides the same increase to sensitivity as a legendary eltex staff when equipped by a pawn at 375% senstivity. Bioferrite equipment gives an additional additive offset to neural heat limit, so assuming level 6 psylink, a legendary bioferrite weapon would give the same neural heat limit increase as a legendary eltex staff at only 263% sensisitivity. Also, bioferrite weapons are effective as actual melee weapons, and are easier to acquire in high quality due to being craftable.

With a full psycaster set ([[eltex robe]], [[eltex shirt]], [[eltex vest]], [[eltex skullcap]], [[bioferrite]]{{AnomalyIcon}} [[slave collar]]{{IdeologyIcon}}), your pawn will have little armor. It's a good idea to keep them in the back as a "support" pawn. You can also wear a [[shield belt]], [[face mask]],{{BiotechIcon}} and [[devilstrand]] [[pants]]/[[flak pants]] for added protection without impacting psycasts. An alternate set using bioferrite [[plate armor]] and [[flak helmet]]s can be used instead for more protection.
