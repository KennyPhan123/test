== Analysis ==
Boreal forest is a step up in difficulty from [[temperate forest]], but not much harder than a temperate forest tile with a winter. The growing season will be shorter, meaning more food will need to be produced and stored in the early game, there are less animals and plants, and winters will be colder, but gameplay is otherwise similar.

*'''Growing: Moderate''' - Growing seasons are shorter, but soil is plentiful and warmer boreal forests can have growing seasons of 30 days.

* '''Disease: Easy''' - Diseases happen less frequently than in warm biomes, and [[wild healroot]] grows naturally. Medicine may run dry during the winter if not enough is grown or bought.

* '''Temperature: Moderate''' - The temperature can reach dangerous levels during the winter or [[cold snap]]s. [[Heat wave]]s may occur during warmer tiles in the summer, but are fairly rare.

* '''Fires: Easy-Moderate''' - There are plenty of trees and wild plants around to catch fire, but less than a temperate or tropical biome.

* '''Predators: Moderate''' - Animals in general are less common than in warm biomes, but predators are still common and are more likely to get hungry during the winter.

Overall starting difficulty is moderate, with the main challenge coming from the short growing season, thus forcing early game colonies to prepare food, [[cloth]], and [[medicine]] in advance. Once [[greenhouse]]s are established so that the colony can grow crops year-round, the difficulty is similar to most other biomes, with less disease than warm biomes, but winters forcing colder clothing.
