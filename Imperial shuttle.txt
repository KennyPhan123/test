'''Imperial shuttles''' are transport vehicles that can carry pawns and items. When landed on the ground, they are treated mechanically as a type of [[building]].

== Occurrence ==
Imperial shuttles are used to transport pawns for [[Royalty (DLC)]] quests from [[Lore#Tech levels|Industrial]]-tech level or higher factions. Despite the name, they are used in non-Empire quests as well. They are also called by the [[Titles#Call transport shuttle|Call Transport Shuttle]] permit.

Empire [[faction base]]s will also always have an imperial shuttle sitting on a floored landing zone defined by four [[ship landing beacon]]s — the shuttle will fly away if a player pawn is at least 30 tiles next to it, but the shuttle can be destroyed if action is taken fast enough. {{Check Tag|How fast?}}

If the player colony has a functional landing pad, imperial shuttles will attempt to land there if possible.

== Summary ==
An imperial shuttle called by permit will transport [[colonists]], items, and [[animals]] on a one-way trip to a valid tile within 70 world tiles. The shuttle can hold a total of 2,000 kg when loaded on a colony map, but as of 1.2.2900, it ignores this weight limit when called for a caravan on the world map.{{Check Tag|Quest behavior?|How does the weight limit work when used in quests? Both when collecting NPCs and when shuttling player pawns to offsite combats}}

Damaging or destroying a shuttle does not create a [[crashed shuttle]] in its place. {{Check Tag|Detail|Does the shuttle attempt to flee when it's damaged? How much goodwill is lost?}} Imperial shuttles will only yield a {{icon Small|Shuttle engine}} [[shuttle engine]] when destroyed if the [[Odyssey DLC]] is active.

== Analysis ==
As imperial shuttles fly to their destination tile directly, they are far faster than a caravan and can transport more mass at once than a transport pod. Imperial shuttles have a higher carrying capacity of 2,000 kg than a player-built [[passenger shuttle]], {{OdysseyIcon}} which can only hold up to 500 kg. However, since an imperial shuttle called by permit is only valid for one trip, a single imperial shuttle cannot do a round trip unlike a caravan or passenger shuttle. It is possible to use two permits for a round trip, or to travel to the destination some other way and travel back with the imperial shuttle.
