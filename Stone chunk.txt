A '''stone chunk''', also referred to in places as a '''rock chunk''', is the general term for is any of the several types of stone chunk namely '''granite chunk, limestone chunk, marble chunk, sandstone chunk, slate chunk''' or '''vacstone chunk'''{{OdysseyIcon}}.  Each chunk can be cut at the [[stonecutter's table]] to yield 20 [[Resources#Materials|stone blocks]] of the same kind. This takes {{Ticks|1600}} of work. Rock chunks cannot be sold without first being turned into blocks.

== Acquisition == 
Stone chunks can either be found naturally throughout the world or produced through mining.

A typical flat 250x250 [[World_generation#Landing_site|map]] will contain around 600 to 900 chunks of each stone type on maps with two stone types, or 400 to 600 chunks of each stone type on maps with three stone types. Chunks can also be produced through mining [[Structure#Natural_walls|natural stone]], with each tile having a ~25% chance to spawn a chunk of that stone type.

Infinite chunks can also be produced by a [[deep drill]] in an area that contains no underground ore deposits. The type of chunk produced will be displayed on the deep drill's tooltip. It can be beneficial to place the drill right next to the stonecutter's table to remove the need for hauling, but this does put the stonecutter at risk of being attacked by [[Events/Incidental#Too_Deep:_Infestation|deep drill infestations]], in addition to the drill operator.  

Stone chunks must be manually designated by the player to be hauled using the [[Haul things]] option in the [[Orders]] tab or by selecting the stone chunks and clicking the Haul things gizmo. They can be hauled to any [[stockpile]] with the appropriate settings, with [[dumping stockpile zone]]s including stone chunks by default.

Another way to prompt pawns to haul chunks is by placing a stockpile zone underneath the chunks and excluding that type of chunk from the item list. The chunks will be moved to a stockpile that accepts them. This works for chunks that get produced by deep drilling, as well as any other chunks that happen to be dropped into that stockpile later on.

== Analysis ==
Despite the objects made of the resultant stone blocks having different HP based on which stone type is used, all stone chunks have the same amount of HP. However, they do have different masses:
{|width="550"
|
* [[Granite]]: 25 kg
* [[Marble]]: 25 kg
|
* [[Limestone]]: 22 kg
* [[Sandstone]]: 20 kg
|
* [[Slate]]: 18 kg
* [[Vacstone]]:{{OdysseyIcon}} 18 kg
|}
Thus, if stone chunks must be transported, such as when transporting stone to a colony in a [[biome]] with low stone supplies like Ice Sheets, it can be advantageous to chose Slate over other stone types, despite the inferior stats of the [[slate blocks|end product]]. Note that stone chunks weigh the same as the blocks they are cut into, so, barring fractional calculations to maximize [[pack animal]]s' load, they are just as efficient to transport in either form. However, practically speaking, it may be advantageous to do the stonecutting at the colony where preexisting shelter, [[stonecutter's table]]s, [[tool cabinet]]s, [[armchair]]s, [[light]], [[food]], [[bed]]s, [[recreation]] and other production benefits can more easily be provided. If you do wish to create (some) stone blocks, it would be wise to take a pre-constructed stonecutter table with you, as it only weighs 20kg when minified, and you can bring it back when you're done.

Chunks provide decent [[cover]] (50% as opposed to a tree's 25% or sandbags' {{#expr: {{Q|Sandbags|Cover Effectiveness}}*100}}%). Together with the fact they are omnipresent on natural maps, they are useful in firefights and will be used by the AI. Considering they can be easily hauled, it is a good idea to remove them from places the enemy could use and form your own makeshift barricades with them - whether when attacking enemy settlements or defending against raiders.

The [[chunk skip]] [[psycast]] {{RoyaltyIcon}} can be beneficial for both positioning cover for yourself or to remove it from enemies, as well as an easy way to move chunks a short distance without tying up a hauler. It skips the nearest 5 stone chunks or [[Steel slag chunk]]s to the target location. It is a level 1 psycast that costs 4% psyfocus and 14 points of neural heat.
