The '''sealed crate''' is a structure added by the [[Odyssey DLC]]. It glows slightly and has contents that it ejects when opened. 
== Occurrence ==
Sealed crates are generated in a variety of map features and Odyssey quests. They appear in larger numbers in orbital sites such as abandoned and mechanoid platforms.{{Check Tag|Add Details}}

There are 4 types of sealed crates, with different contents and different spawning locations.

==== Mortar shell crates ====
Sealed crates containing [[mortar shell]]s spawn exclusively in [[ancient garrison]] landmarks and quest sites.

==== Gravlite panel crates ====
A sealed crate containing [[gravlite panel]]s can be found in a dedicated loot room in the following [[Quests#Odyssey|gravship quest]] sites: frozen terraformer, ancient reactor, crashed mechanoid platform, orbital ancient platform, orbital mechanoid platform. Except in the case of the terraformer, the room also has a 33% chance of containing a [[sealed container]] with a gravship upgrade.

This crate also has a 10% chance of spawning on abandoned platforms discovered with the [[orbital scanner]].

Most of these sites can also have regular sealed crates in other rooms.

==== Archean seed crates ====
Any [[archean tree]] found on an asteroid comes with a sealed crate containing at least one [[archean seed]].

==== Regular crates ====
Regular crates with high value content spawn in many places:
* [[landmarks]] and features: [[ancient warehouse]], [[landmarks#ruins|ruins]], [[frozen ruins]], [[infested settlement]], [[landmarks#stockpile|stockpile]]
* non-landmark ruins occurring naturally in the [[scarlands]] and [[glacial plain]] biomes
* some [[Quests#Odyssey|gravship quest]] sites: ancient stockpile, frozen terraformer, ancient reactor, crashed mechanoid platform, orbital mechanoid platform, plus the [[Endings#The_Mechhive|Mechhive]]
* [[orbital scanner]] quest sites: satellite, mechanoid platform, orbital item stash, abandoned platform, orbital wreck (all except asteroid)

== Summary ==
{{Loot Container Note}}

Sealed crates drop one stack of an item when opened.

=== Loot ===
==== Mortar shell crate loot ====
Crates in ancient garrisons will always contain 2-5 [[mortar shell]]s (excluding firefoam, antigrain and deadlife dust shells).

==== Gravlite panel crate loot ====
These crates always contain {{Icon Small|gravlite panel||40-60}} [[gravlite panel]]s.

==== Archean seed crate loot ====
Crates that spawn on an asteroid next to an [[archean tree]] always contains {{Icon Small|archean seed||1-2}} [[archean seed]]s.
