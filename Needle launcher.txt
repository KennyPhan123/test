The '''needle launcher''' is an [[mechanoid]]-only ranged [[weapon]]. It is the main armament of the [[legionary]].

This weapon cannot be independently obtained in normal play. It is provided here for comparisons sake.

== Acquisition ==
Needle guns spawn on all [[legionaries]], including those gestated by a colony. However, it is not possible to obtain it directly in normal play. Pawns carrying it will not drop it on [[death]], [[downing]], or complete loss of [[manipulation]] like other weapons. It can be spawned in by the [[Development mode#Debug actions menu|Debug actions menu]] but, like all [[unobtainable items|unobtainable weapons]], will disappear after being dropped.

== Analysis ==
{{Stub|section=1|reason=Missing analysis}}

The Needle Launcher is statistically identical to the already weak [[Needle_gun|Needle Gun]] in all aspects except for it's range, which is 16 tiles or ~28% shorter. This makes it a poor choice for direct combat.
