'''Voidsight serum''' is a consumable added by the [[Anomaly DLC]] that provides long-term buffs to [[Study Efficiency]] and [[Psychic Sensitivity]] in exchange for [[mood]] penalty. 

== Acquisition ==
{{Acquisition}}

== Summary == 
Voidsight serum is a serum that enhances psychic ability and dark study at the cost of mood. Unlike drugs, serums do not have a severity-based high, have no addiction, tolerance, or withdrawal mechanics and do not count as any kind of drug for the purposes of the [[Chemical (need)|Chemical need]], the [[chemical interest]], [[chemical fascination|fascination]], or [[teetotaler]] traits, or the [[Drug use]] precepts.{{IdeologyIcon}}

=== Upon injection ===
{{Quote|"This person has ingested a drug which enhanced their perception of dark psychic phenomena. They will gain more knowledge when studying dark psychic phenomena and entities. Their psychic sensitivity is also increased."|Voidsight hediff description}}
A pawn taking the serum will do so right where they stand without looking for a place to sit first. The serum is taken by injection, taking {{Ticks|{{P|Ingestion Time}}}}. Pawns can be ordered to inject the serum through the usual means, but it can also be ordered from the item itself. This option allows [[ghoul]]s to take serums without using an operation bill.

The serum applies the "'''voidsight'''" hediff with the following effects for {{Ticks|900000}} or 15 in-game days:
* {{Good|x125%}} [[Study Efficiency]] 
* {{Good|x125%}} [[Psychic Sensitivity]]
* {{Thought|desc=The serum makes me see so much more. Connections everywhere, emotions flowing. Faces in the shadows. So much... |label=voidsight|value=-4|stack=1}}. This mood penalty is nullified by the [[inhumanized]] hediff.

== Analysis ==
{{Stub|section=1|reason=Psychic harmonizer (on yunners or on a moody pawn) given 15 day duration also synergy with psychic reader. Also could use more depth on actual bonus when stacking for both effects given multiplicative nature}}
The downsides of using voidsight serum are fairly negligible, a minor mood penalty that can be easily balanced out in any established colony. However, the benefits are also not so impressive that you'll necessarily want to produce it.  
The boost to psychic sensitivity can be a blessing on [[psycast]]ers,{{RoyaltyIcon}} but it also renders the recipient more vulnerable to negative psychic effects such as [[psychic drone]]s. If you intend to make use of psycasts aggressively, keeping some on hand as a buff for your psycasters might make sense.{{RoyaltyIcon}} The multiplicative nature of the bonus means that without any other buffs, the bonus is modest but can make for significant differences in neural heat when stacked onto a proper psycaster build.

The study efficiency bonus is much more clearly a positive, increasing the amount of research produced while studying entities.

Otherwise, you will likely have better uses for your bioferrite and neutroamine, and should use any voidsight serum you acquire to buff research rather than hoard it.

== Trivia ==
This is likely a reference to the mysterious drug known as "soy sauce" from the novel ''[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Dies_at_the_End John Dies at the End]''; both are black, squirming liquids that open a person's ability to perceive horrific otherworldly beings.
