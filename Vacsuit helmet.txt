The '''vacsuit helmet''' is an item of [[apparel]] added by the [[Odyssey DLC]] that provides significant [[vacuum resistance]], for use in the [[vacuum]] of [[Orbit|space]]. It forms a set with the [[vacsuit]].

== Acquisition == 
{{Acquisition}}

Vacsuit helmets are practically ubiquitous along NPC factions. Most humanoid NPCs like civilians and [[Raider|raiders]] can spawn wearing them, even if they are otherwise completely unarmored.

== Summary ==
A vacsuit helmet increases the [[vacuum resistance]] of its wearer by {{+|{{%|{{P|Vacuum Resistance Offset}}}}}}. [[Quality]] does not affect this resistance.

Vacsuit helmets increase [[Toxic Environment Resistance]] by {{+|80%}}, causing the effects of [[rot stink]] and [[toxic buildup]] from environmental sources like [[tox gas]], [[toxic fallout]], or [[pollution]] to be 20% effective. It also prevents the user from being affected by [[tox gas exposure]]. It does not protect from [[disease]]s, nor does it prevent [[toxic buildup]] from attacks such as the [[cobra]]'s bite. The helmet's [[quality]] does not affect this resistance.

== Analysis ==
===Vacuum use===
The [[vacsuit]] and vacsuit helmet set provides a complete {{+|100%}} Vacuum Resistance, enabling pawns to survive in the vacuum of [[orbit|space]] until the armor breaks. In addition, the vacsuit set is the cheapest and earliest unlocked set to allow safe orbit travel.

Any [[power armor]] helmet will provide better combat protection and slightly less Vacuum Resistance. Still, their vacuum resistance is usually ''enough'' for extended trips, especially with an oxygenated room available.
* Even a [[recon armor]] and a [[recon helmet]] still provides {{+|95%}} Vacuum Resistance, enabling airless survival for as long as {{ticks/gametime/days|60000}}. 
* Better helmets or a [[vacsuit]] will improve the safety time further, with a [[cataphract helmet]]{{RoyaltyIcon}} + vacsuit even providing 100% resistance. 

Therefore, power armor helmets can be suitable for general use in orbit. However, power armor helmets require more research and are more expensive, so vacsuit helmets can be good before power armor is craftable. In addition, if there is no oxygenated room available, e.g., if using a [[drop pod]] or [[shuttle]] to enter orbit, then having 100% vacuum immunity is more valuable.

If there is another source of vacuum resistance (the [[vacskin gland]],{{RoyaltyIcon}} the [[Vacuum resistant]]/[[Breathless]] genes,{{BiotechIcon}}) then any of the power armor helmets alone will cause pawns to reach 100% Vacuum Resistance, so the power armor helmets become better cost notwithstanding.

===Combat===
The vacsuit helmet is roughly one quality level behind to a [[steel]] [[flak helmet]] in terms of armor, e.g., a normal vacsuit helmet ≈ a poor steel flak helmet. However, the vacsuit helmet has multiple advantages beyond Vacuum Resistance:
*The vacsuit helmet is cheaper, not requiring [[plasteel]] to create.
*The vacsuit helmet protects a pawn's eyes, ears, and jaw while the flak helmet doesn't. The eyes are useful body parts, and even the ears are useful as they can take hits in place of the main head.
*The vacsuit helmet provides great Toxic Environmental resistance and an immunity to [[tox gas]],{{BiotechIcon}} making them great when fighting [[waster pirates]].{{BiotechIcon}} Note that the flak helmet can be worn with a [[face mask]] for some Toxic Environmental protection, but it doesn't fully prevent the tox gas hediff.

The vacsuit helmet completely outclasses steel [[simple helmet]]s of the same quality and [[gas mask]]s,{{BiotechIcon}} providing more protection and with an identical toxic resistance to the gas mask. [[Plasteel]] and [[uranium]] simple helmets are more protective, but are prohibitively more expensive. [[Bioferrite]]{{AnomalyIcon}} simple helmets are arguably cheaper, still a bit more protective, and boost [[psycast]]ing{{RoyaltyIcon}}, but lack the aforementioned benefits of the vacsuit helmet.

===Children===
The vacsuit helmet is one of the better helmets that [[children]]{{BiotechIcon}} can wear. For Sharp protection, it is beaten only by high quality material helmets, including: 
*A [[kid helmet]] made from [[plasteel]], [[uranium]], or [[bioferrite]].{{AnomalyIcon}} Plasteel and uranium are rather expensive, though, and bioferrite is cheap but slow to obtain in the early game.
*A [[ceremonial hood]]{{AnomalyIcon}} made of [[devilstrand]] or [[hyperweave]]. Devilstrand is reasonably obtainable once a greenhouse is established or in a temperate biome.

Both options lack toxic or vacuum resistance, although the kid helmet can be worn with a [[face mask]] for some toxic protection.

Regardless, since children have lower HP and should be avoiding combat anyway, upgrading their combat armor is a very low priority. On a child, vacsuit helmets should mainly be considered if going to orbit or the colony is rich enough to easily afford them. Devilstrand and [[bioferrite]] have the advantage of being easier to farm in-colony, whilst components are locked behind Fabrication and steel behind [[deep drill]]ing.
