'''Ancient uplinks''' are structures added by the [[Odyssey DLC]], as a part of the [[landmarks]] feature. They can be hacked to discover a quest in orbit.

== Occurrence ==
The ancient uplink can only be found on a [[landmark]] with the [[Landmarks#Ancient uplink|ancient uplink]] feature.

== Summary ==
An ancient uplink can be hacked to discover a quest in orbit. This requires an [[intellectual]] skill of 6 and takes 100 hacking progress, which translates to {{Ticks|6000}} at 100% [[hacking speed]]. Detection during a hacking attempt locks out the uplink for 6-8 hours.

The ancient uplink gives the same quests as the [[orbital scanner]].

Once hacked, an ancient uplink can be deconstructed for {{Icon Small|Steel}} 50 [[steel]]. Alternatively it can be destroyed for {{Icon Small|Steel}} 35 [[steel]] and {{Icon Small|steel slag chunk}} 2 [[steel slag chunk]]s.

== Analysis ==
Ancient uplink hacking is an alternative to building and manning an [[orbital scanner]], suitable for a nomadic play style with the gravship.
