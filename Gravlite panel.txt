'''Gravlite panels''' are a resource added by the [[Odyssey DLC]] and used for the construction of [[gravship]]s and related structures.

== Acquisition == 
{{Acquisition}}

They can also be found via Gravcore quests, looted from [[sealed crate]]s, obtained from deconstructing mechanoid ship chunks, from the transport pod event, and rarely bought from space bulk goods traders. [[Starting scenario|The Gravship]] scenario starts with gravlite panels scattered on the initial map.

== Analysis ==
Gravlite panels are required to build and expand a [[gravship]], as well as to build [[grav anchor]]s. Until they can be crafted, gravlite panels are a valuable resource. If planning to keep a given map, enough panels should be saved in advance to make an anchor.

As the amount of panels obtained from the initial grav engine quest scales with colony wealth,{{Check Tag|confirmation required}} the player should consider postponing this quest in order to grow their colony first. This allows the player to obtain enough panels to build the gravship, the anchor, as well as some spare ones.
