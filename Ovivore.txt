'''Ovivores''' are [[animals]] that will eat [[animal products]]. Being an ovivore would also enable them to eat [[meal]]s, [[processed foods]] and [[kibble]], if they could not already. There are no pure ovivores; all ovivores are also either [[omnivore]]s or [[carnivore]]s
