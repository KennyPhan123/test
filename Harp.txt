A '''Harp''' is a [[musical instrument]] that is a source of High Culture [[recreation]] and is a requirement for specific [[titles]].

== Acquisition ==
{{Acquisition}}

== Summary ==
{{Stub|section=1|reason= 1) Has almost no information about unique mechanics 2)  Titles information}}
Nobles can put on concerts with the harp, similar to a [[Events#Party|party]], giving a [[mood]] bonus to the noble and attendees.
Anyone listening to or playing the harp receives a {{+|2}}  thought for as long as the harp is being played. It can be heard within a radius of 12 tiles. Pawns with {{Bad|0%}} [[Hearing]] do not get the mood bonus.  
{{Recreation Power Quality Table}}

The harp cannot be played if the pawn has a [[manipulation]] capacity of 0%.

== Analysis ==
Since the harp provides its own recreation type (Musical) and costs only 150 wood and 500 research points to build, it is a good way to increase recreation in a colony. In addition, it is only 1 tile in size, so it fits neatly into an already established rec room.
