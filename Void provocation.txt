'''Void provocation''' is a [[psychic ritual]] that triggers an [[Entity]] related event to occur, but with a chance to render the invoker comatose.

* Causes an entity event to occur between 2 to 4 hours, or {{Ticks/gametime|5000}} and {{Ticks/gametime|10000}}, after completion of the ritual. If possible, void provocation will cause an undiscovered entity to appear. If there are no possible undiscovered entities to be sent, a random already discovered but possible entity will be sent. == Analysis == 
Void provocation allows the colony to advance through Anomaly content faster than the natural [[storyteller]], both by simply making events more frequent and ensuring that novel entities are chosen, if possible. To maximise this effect, ensure that the requirements of at least one unknown entity are met. See the [[Anomaly#Anomaly event list|Event list]] for these requirements.

Additionally, most Anomaly [[major threat]]s have a chance to give [[shard]]s, so this ritual is a decent way to get extra shards, and it's available very early into the Anomaly research tree.

Since the void provocation essentially adds another major threat, there is a risk of a standard [[raid]] coming in just after an entity attack has severly damaged the colony, especially when playing with [[Randy Random]]. Therefore, discretion should be used before provoking the void. If the colony has been doing more than fine overall, then it may be safe to provoke as often as possible. Note that some Anomaly events are not immediately threatening, such as [[obelisk]]s, [[unnatural corpse]]s, and [[golden cube]]s.
