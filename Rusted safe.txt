'''Rusted safes''' are structures added by the [[Odyssey DLC]]. Drops items when opened. 
== Occurrence ==
Rusted safes are generated in a variety of map features and Odyssey quests, usually on planet locations.{{Check Tag|Add Details}}

== Summary ==
{{Loot Container Note}}

Rusted safes drop one stack of an item when opened. The pool of possible rewards is considerably smaller than that of sealed crates.
