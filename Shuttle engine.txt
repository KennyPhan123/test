'''Shuttle engines''' are items added by the [[Odyssey DLC]], used exclusively for the construction of [[passenger shuttle]]s.

== Acquisition == 
One shuttle engine will be obtained from deconstructing or destroying manned shuttles with the Odyssey DLC active, which are the [[imperial shuttle]], [[crashed shuttle]], and [[passenger shuttle]]. The [[spacedrone]] {{IdeologyIcon}} and [[crashed mechanitor ship]] {{BiotechIcon}} will not yield a shuttle engine when deconstructed or destroyed.

The shuttle engine can also be acquired by [[Trade|trading]] with exotic goods traders, combat suppliers, or [[faction base]]s — as long as the trader or base is of the [[Traders guild]] faction. As that faction is only in orbit, the colony will need have a [[comms console]] to purchase one. Only one shuttle engine can be in stock at a time. Shuttle engines can also be obtained as a [[quest]] reward.

== Summary ==
A single shuttle engine is used in the creation of a [[passenger shuttle]].

== Analysis ==
{{See also|Passenger shuttle#Analysis}}

[[Passenger shuttle]]s are a powerful late-game alternative option for transportation, which makes acquiring a shuttle engine highly desirable.

The easiest method is to accept the quests [[Quests#Sanguophage_Transport|Sanguophage Transport]] {{BiotechIcon}} and [[Quests#Shuttle_Crash|Shuttle Crash]],{{RoyaltyIcon}} which create a [[crashed shuttle]] even if the latter quest is failed. Any quest involving shuttle transport from the [[Empire]]{{RoyaltyIcon}} will spawn an imperial shuttle, which can be destroyed for one shuttle engine. By refusing to load the necessary pawns onto the shuttle, you may anger the faction, but [[goodwill]] can be regained. Also the [[Titles#Call_transport_shuttle|call transport shuttle permit]]{{RoyaltyIcon}} can be used to summon a shuttle, allowing you to destroy it for a shuttle engine without harming relations with the Empire.

Without [[Royalty]] or [[Biotech]] installed, shuttle engines are more inconsistent to obtain. Crashed shuttles can be found in certain locations in [[orbit]]. In addition, shuttle engines can be bought via orbital [[trade ship]]s or generate as [[quest]] rewards, although they are worth a lot.

Shuttle engines are quite valuable, although outlander and tribal settlements will not buy shuttle engines sold by caravans.

== Trivia ==
* Despite [[imperial shuttle]]s being entirely self-sufficient with [[uranium]] powered fuel cells, the shuttle engine extracted from them automatically converts into being powered by [[chemfuel]] after being installed into [[passenger shuttle]]s.
