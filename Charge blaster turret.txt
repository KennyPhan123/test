The '''charge blaster turret''' is an [[mechanoid]]-only ranged [[weapon]] added by the [[Biotech DLC]]. It is the main armament of the [[centurion]] and [[war queen]], and is also carried by the [[diabolus]]. 

This weapon cannot be independently obtained in normal play. It is provided here for comparisons sake.

== Acquisition ==
Charge blaster turrets spawn on all [[centurion]]s, [[diabolus|diaboli]], and [[war queen]]s, including those gestated by a colony. However, it is not possible to obtain it directly in normal play. Pawns carrying it will not drop it on [[death]], [[downing]], or complete loss of [[manipulation]] like other weapons. It can be spawned in by the [[Development mode#Debug actions menu|Debug actions menu]] but, like all [[unobtainable items|unobtainable weapons]], will disappear after being dropped.

== Summary ==
* Unlike other unobtainable weapons, it does not appear on the gear screen.
* Can be fired while moving. 
* Automatically aimed. Players can turn their mech's turret on or off, but can't choose a target.
* Same damage, range, and accuracy as [[needle gun]], but fires about 80% faster and has less armor penetration. Zero aiming time.
* While fairly weak for such large machines, each mechanoid has their own unique properties on top of the turret.
