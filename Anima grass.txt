'''Anima grass''' grows from [[anima tree]]s when it is meditated under by a pawn with the natural [[psyfocus]]. Unlike anima trees, anima grass is ''not'' immune to [[toxic fallout]], [[fire]] or rot from the continued absence of [[light]]. 

Once twenty anima grass has grown, a pawn may use the tree to gain a [[psylink]] level, consuming some or all of the grass. See [[Anima tree]] for details. If unused, the grass will die after {{P|Lifespan}} days.

Unlike normal grass, anima grass is inedible, so there is no danger of it being grazed on by animals.
