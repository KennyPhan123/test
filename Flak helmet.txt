The '''flak helmet''' is a step up in protection from the [[simple helmet]]. It's a mid-tier [[Armor#Headgear|helmet]] that is relatively easy to acquire and suitable for most colonists.

== Acquisition ==
{{Acquisition}}

Alternatively, the helmet can be received as a quest reward, purchased from [[traders]], or found on [[raiders]].

== Analysis ==
Compared to the [[simple helmet]], flak helmets provide significantly more protection while requiring an additional 2 components and 10 plasteel. A [[steel]] flak helmet outclasses even a [[plasteel]] simple helmet and is often cheaper to manufacture. 

All power armor helmets, even the [[recon helmet]], outclass the flak helmet in protection to the head and ears as well as covering the pawn's eyes, nose, and jaw, but require the much more expensive [[advanced component]] to craft. Keep in mind that while a plasteel flak helmet has a significantly larger item HP, lasting over twice as long, it also requires more plasteel than both the recon and [[marine helmet]]s and as much as a [[cataphract helmet]] {{RoyaltyIcon}} while also being less protective. Accordingly, steel flak helmets are generally the standard headgear of most colonies in the mid-game until they are replaced by [[power armor]] helmets in the late-game. 

A [[ceremonial hood]] is comparable in protection to a flak helmet, while also providing eye coverage and cold insulation. Ceremonial hoods become available much earlier in the game, even before [[Research#Complex clothing|Complex Clothing]] is researched, and can therefore be crafted on a [[Crafting spot]]. A [[devilstrand]] hood has slightly less sharp protection than a steel flak helmet, while a [[hyperweave]] hood matches the sharp protection of a plasteel flak helmet. Hyperweave is very rare, so a hood is unlikely to be available for every colonist, but when both hyperweave and plasteel are being purchased from traders, the materials for a hyperweave hood are several times cheaper than those for a plasteel flak helmet. Leftover hyperweave from [[duster]]s can also be used for hoods, which require only 20 hyperweave compared to 80 for a duster.

The protection offered by a flak helmet depends on its [[quality]] and [[material]]. It has a material effect multiplier of 70% for all types of damage. See [[Apparel#Materials|apparel]] for material protection values. Despite the description, flak helmets actually offer a decent amount of protection from blunt damage.

A [[bioferrite]]{{AnomalyIcon}} flak helmet is among the best headgear for [[psycast]]ers.{{RoyaltyIcon}} Bioferrite-[[stuff]]ed items offer excellent buffs to [[psychic sensitivity]], [[neural heat limit]]{{RoyaltyIcon}} and [[neural heat recovery rate]]{{RoyaltyIcon}} and the flak helmet provides the best protection of any bioferrite-stuffable headgear. This makes it the balanced option when compared to protection-focused [[prestige armor|prestige helmets]], and the psycasting focused [[eltex]]{{RoyaltyIcon}} headwear or bioferrite [[torture crown]].{{IdeologyIcon}} Also keep in mind that with a sufficiently high psychic sensitivity from other sources, a bioferrite item can outperform its eltex equivalent.

== Version history ==
* '''Advanced helmets''' were added in Alpha 17, replacing the '''Kevlar helmet''' from previous versions. The major change was that advanced helmets can be made out of any material, requires 5 plasteel to craft and have increased protection across the board.
* Beta 19 - Material cost was decreased from 70 to 40, but plasteel cost increased from 5 to 10, and materials now affect armor rating. It also protects against heat.
* 1.1 - Renamed from '''Advanced helmet''' to '''Flak helmets'''
* [[Ideology DLC]] Release - Received styles via new [[Ideoligion]] [[style]] system.
