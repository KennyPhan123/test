'''Grizzly bears''' are huge solitary omnivores found in {{Habitats}}. 

Although infamously bad-tempered, as wide-ranging creatures that will eat anything, grizzlies rarely are hungry enough to attack your colonists or their livestock without provocation. If they do, they present a problem even to industrial era-equipped colonies thanks to their wall of health and savage amounts of damage. If successfully hunted though, they provide large amounts of meat and valuable [[bearskin]]. 

They are identical to a [[polar bear]], except for their appearance, habitat, comfortable temperature range, and wildness.

== Acquisition ==
{{PAGENAME}}s can be found in {{Habitats}}. They can either be tamed by a [[Work #Handle|handler]] or self-tame in a random event. 

{{PAGENAME}}s can be bought and sold in other [[Trade#Faction base|faction bases]] and from [[Trade#Exotic goods trader 2|exotic goods traders]]. {{PAGENAME}}s purchased from traders will be already tamed.

== Analysis ==
If they can be successfully obtained despite their {{#expr:{{P|Turn Manhunter Chance When Taming}}*100}}% [[manhunter]] chance on failed taming attempts, bears make good haulers and melee blockers.

=== Attacks ===
Grizzly bears have two attacks: scratch (paw) and bite (teeth).  Both attacks apply a stun for 7 seconds on their first strike when hunting.
