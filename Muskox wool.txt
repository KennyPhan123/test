'''Muskox wool''' is a type of [[fabric]] gathered by [[Skills#Animals|animal handlers]] by shearing tamed [[muskox]]en. Muskox wool is a very good insulator, and is therefore recommended in clothing like [[parka]]s and [[tuque]]s.

== Acquisition ==
Muskox wool is acquired by shearing tamed [[muskox]]en - each muskox can be sheared every {{Q|Muskox|Shearing Interval Days}} days for {{Q|Muskox|Wool Amount}} wool, for an average of {{Q|Muskox|Daily Wool Average}} per day. Note that the amount of wool sheared scales with the [[Animal Gather Yield]] of the shearer. 

Shearing takes {{Ticks|1700}}, regardless of the amount of wool produced, and only modified by the [[Animal Gather Speed]] of the handler.

It can also be purchased from [[trade]]rs.
