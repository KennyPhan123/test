'''Components''' are materials required to build and repair electrical devices, as well as produce [[Weapons#Ranged Weapons|firearms]], [[armor]], and [[ship]] parts.

== Acquisition ==
{{Acquisition}}

They can also be obtained by:
* [[Mine|Mining]] from [[compacted machinery]]:
** Distant lumps using the [[long-range mineral scanner]]; each lump yields 100+.
** Establishing additional "quarry" [[Colony#Multiple colonies|colonies]] to support your primary colony.
* Deconstructing [[ship chunk]]s, [[crashed shuttle]]s, [[mech node]]s,{{RoyaltyIcon}} and buildings that require components.
* Destroying [[crashed ship parts]].
* Defeating [[mechanoid]] and [[outlander]] raiders, who sometimes carry components as loot.
* Purchasing them from [[trader]]s.
* Obtaining them from component [[work site]]s.{{IdeologyIcon}}

== Ore ==
[[File:Compacted_machinery.png|frameless|left|Compacted machinery in game]] 

'''Compacted machinery''' tiles have 2,000 health each, making them slower to mine than stone or many ores, but still significantly faster than [[uranium]] and [[plasteel]] ore. They can be found in small veins, ranging from 3 to 6 tiles in size. Each mined block has a base yield of 2 components, however this is modified by factors such as the [[Difficulty]] setting and the [[Mining_Yield|mining yield]] of the pawn.

Components are also used for repairing component [[breakdown]]s. Every so often, electrical devices may become dysfunctional, until they are replaced with a new component. See [[Breakdown#Affected buildings|List of affected buildings]] for the full list.

== Analysis ==
For large portions of the game, before [[fabrication bench]]es are researched, components will be limited. The main sources of components in the early game are [[ship chunk]]s and compacted machinery, which can run out, and [[trade]], which is reliable but requires a way to generate money and someone to trade with. Almost ''every'' electrical device in the game uses components, so they present a very real bottleneck to most colonies. In the late game, components remain in high demand because they are used to create [[advanced component]]s. With Fabrication, a colony can still be limited by the [[steel]] required to make components.

It is recommended to always keep a couple components (3-5) in reserve, in case devices [[breakdown]]. If a [[geothermal generator]] breaks down, for instance, the colony will be out of power until a new component is found.

=== Acquisition analysis ===
* '''Conserve your components'''. Often, the simplest way to have components is to not use them. Don't build unnecessary power generators. [[Hydroponics]] are unnecessary if you can just use [[sun lamp]]s and soil. "Rerolling" weapons for [[quality]] consumes a lot of components.

* '''ABC - (Always) Buy Components'''. Traders are, for a significant part of the game, the only renewable source of components. Excess food, leathers, drugs, and other resources can be used to buy components. Even if a large stash is available ''right now'', a component supply can run out surprisingly quickly. Even when Fabrication is researched, trade can convert excess and otherwise useless resources into useful components.

* '''Research Fabrication quickly'''. Fabrication is an important and useful technology, but requires Microelectronics and Multi-analyzer before it is available. It is also necessary to buy or obtain 2 [[advanced component]]s before the actual fabrication bench can be constructed, and steel is needed to craft components. While Fabrication may not be an immediate priority - defensive research like Gas Operation or Mortars may be even more important - it is still worth researching.

:The preferred way to get steel is using a [[ground-penetrating scanner]] and [[deep drill]]s. Once a ground scanner is available, deep drills, and a fabrication bench, obtaining components should be much less of an issue. Note that the process of crafting components takes a chunk of time on its own.

* '''Long-range mineral scanner'''. Unlike the ground-penetrating scanner, the [[long-range mineral scanner]] can find components directly. Every scan brings 100 - 140 components (before [[Mining Yield]] and [[difficulty]]), ready to be used. Note that the long-range scanner requires its own research and 2 advanced components to make. In addition, colonists must travel to and from this deposit; the weight of components can be an issue.

* '''Raiding'''. Raiding a [[faction base]] or [[Quest#Bandit Camp|bandit camp]] can net a few components by deconstructing the [[solar generator]]s, [[batteries]], and [[turret]]s in the area, although raiding these places just for components is not particularly worthwhile. Component [[work site]]s{{IdeologyIcon}} are a good source if they show up.

* '''New Settlement.''' Settling on a new tile, either by enabling multiple colonies in the settings or using a [[gravship]],{{OdysseyIcon}} will generate fresh ores, although at a 50% rate.
