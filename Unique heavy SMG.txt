{{Info|The '''unique heavy SMG''' is the [[unique weapon]] variant of the [[heavy SMG]]: a moderately heavy, multi-shot ranged [[weapon]] that fires 3-shot bursts of rounds which deal a moderate amount of damage each; a moderate delay between each shot; a moderate time between bursts; slightly shorter range and moderate overall accuracy. Recommended for low skill shooters.

Each unique heavy SMG comes with a combination of traits that make it different from other heavy SMGs.}}

== Acquisition ==
Unique weapons can be obtained through [[quests]], found in loot boxes like [[sealed crate]]s, [[sealed container]]s, or [[ancient safe]]s, found while [[fishing]], or gifted by [[visitor]]s. Unique weapons can also rarely be dropped by [[raider]]s.
Ancient Mercenary and Orbital Item Stash quests will always contain at least one unique weapon, typically masterwork or legendary [[quality]].

Weapons depot and [[ancient garrison]] map features do NOT contain unique weapons, nor do [[ancient danger]]s.

== Summary ==
Before traits, unique heavy SMGs are statistically identical to [[heavy SMG]]s. The stats provided in this article are for base model before traits, except where specified otherwise.

Unique heavy SMGs have the following namerLabels for use in their name generation:
* heavy SMG
* SMG
* chugger
