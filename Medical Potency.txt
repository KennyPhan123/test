== Use ==
When a [[Medicine (disambiguation)|medicine]] is used to provide [[Treatment|treatment]], its Medical Potency is used to calculate the outcome of the [[Medical Tend Quality]]. See [[Medical Tend Quality]] for further information on how this is calculated.

When a [[Medicine (disambiguation)|medicine]] is used in an [[operation]], its Medical Potency is used to calculate the probability of success of the operation. See [[Operation|Operations]] for further information on how this is calculated.
