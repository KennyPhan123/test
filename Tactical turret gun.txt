The '''tactical turret gun''' is a [[turret]]-only ranged [[weapon]]. It is the main armament of the [[tactical turret]], which is spawned by the [[turret pack]]. It fires a 2-round burst that deals modest damage with a long cooldown.

This weapon cannot be independently obtained in normal play. It is provided here for comparisons sake.

Notably, with the exception of range and graphical size, it is identical to a [[mini-turret gun]].

== Acquisition ==
Tactical turret guns spawn on all [[tactical turret]]s. However, it is not possible to obtain it directly in normal play. Buildings do not drop it upon destructions and pawns carrying it will not drop it on [[death]], [[downing]], or complete loss of [[manipulation]] like other weapons. It can be spawned in by the [[Development mode#Debug actions menu|Debug actions menu]] but, like all [[unobtainable items|unobtainable weapons]], will disappear after being dropped.
