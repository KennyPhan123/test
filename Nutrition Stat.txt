== Stat Parts ==
=== Body Size ===
<code>'''Nutrition''' = '''Nutrition''' × '''Body Size'''</code>

'''Body Size Factor''' is determined by the lifestage of a pawn. This value is 1 by default for most adult / fully formed pawns, or for those with only one applicable lifestage: resulting in no impact to nutrition.

'''Base Body Size''' is determined by the race of a pawn: [[Human]]s have a base body size of 1.

These are the 2 factors for calculating the resulting pawn's body size: 

<code>'''Body Size''' = '''Body Size Factor''' × '''Base Body Size'''</code>

=== Natural Not Missing Body Parts Coverage ===
<code>'''Nutrition''' = '''Nutrition''' × '''Coverage of Intact Natural Body Parts'''</code>

<code>'''Coverage of Intact Natural Body Parts''' = '''Coverage of Body Parts''' - '''Coverage of Missing Natural Body Parts'''</code>

=== Plant Growth Nutrition Factor ===
<code>'''Nutrition''' = '''Nutrition''' × '''Nutrition Factor From Growth'''</code>

'''Plant Growth''' is a value ranging from 0~1: by default it is 0.15, or 15%.

'''Nutrition Factor From Growth''' is a multiplier on nutrition gained from plants, based on '''Plant Growth'''.

For sowable plants:

<code>'''Nutrition Factor From Growth''' = '''Plant Growth'''</code>

For non-sowable plants:

<code>'''Nutrition Factor From Growth''' = 0.5 + 0.5 × '''Plant Growth'''</code>

=== Is Flesh ===
<code>'''Nutrition''' = '''Nutrition''' × '''Is Flesh'''</code>

'''Is Flesh''' is determined by whether or not the flesh type of a pawn's race is organic.

If the flesh type is organic:
<code>'''Is Flesh''' = 1</code>

If the flesh type is not organic:

<code>'''Is Flesh''' = 0</code>

In other words: organic pawns have nutrition, and non organic pawns do not. Organic flesh types include '''Normal''', '''Insectoid''', '''Entity Flesh''' ,{{AnomalyIcon}} and  '''Fleshbeast''' {{AnomalyIcon}}: i.e. [[Human]]s, all [[Animals]], [[Insectoids]], and [[Fleshbeasts]] ,{{AnomalyIcon}} [[Sightstealer]]s ,{{AnomalyIcon}} [[Noctol]]s ,{{AnomalyIcon}} {{Plural||Fleshmass nucleus|link=true}} ,{{AnomalyIcon}} [[Gorehulk]]s ,{{AnomalyIcon}} [[Devourer]]s ,{{AnomalyIcon}} and [[Chimera]]s {{AnomalyIcon}}. Non organic flesh types include: '''Mechanoid''' and '''Entity Mechanical''' {{AnomalyIcon}}: i.e. all [[Mechanoids]], and [[Nociosphere]]s ,{{AnomalyIcon}} [[Metalhorror]]s ,{{AnomalyIcon}} and [[Revenant]]s {{AnomalyIcon}}.

=== Is Corpse Fresh ===
<code>'''Nutrition''' = '''Nutrition''' × '''Is Corpse Fresh'''</code>
'''Is Corpse Fresh''' is determined by the rot stage of a corpse. 
The three stages are:
* Fresh
* Rotting
* Dessicated
If the rot stage is fresh:

<code>'''Is Corpse Fresh''' = 1</code>

Otherwise:

<code>'''Is Corpse Fresh''' = 0</code>

If a corpse is not rottable, then it is always considered fresh (e.g. [[Unnatural corpse]]s {{AnomalyIcon}}).

In other words: fresh corpses have nutrition, and rotting or dessicated corpses do not.

== Calculating Nutrition ==
For Plants:
<code>'''Nutrition''' = '''Base Nutrition''' × '''Nutrition Factor From Growth'''</code>

For Corpses:
<code>'''Nutrition''' =  × '''Body Size''' × '''Coverage of Body Parts''' × '''Is Flesh''' × '''Is Corpse Fresh'''</code>
