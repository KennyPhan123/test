The '''cannibal platter''' is a [[ritual]] focus used by cannibals.

== Acquisition ==
{{Acquisition}} They require an ideoligion with the cannibal feast ritual to be constructed.

== Summary ==
A cannibal platter is used for the [[Rituals#Cannibal feast|cannibal feast]] [[ritual]]. Once the ritual has completed, the platter is consumed.

Participants will have [[Filth#Blood|blood]] spawn around them and their [[saturation]] filled, and get any associated thoughts from eating [[human meat]].

== Analysis ==
From a pure gameplay perspective, there is little reason to pick the cannibal feast over another ritual that doesn't consume items. Depending on how many pawns participate, it can be an incredibly messy ritual, significantly lowering the room's [[beauty]] and taking extra work to clean; and it requires a room [[impressiveness]] of 120 to reach full quality.

If you are unable to preserve human meat with a [[cooler]], then a cannibal platter can be used to preserve human meat. Note that this building can only be consumed via ritual.
