'''Fortified walls''' are a type of impassable [[structure]] found in maps from the [[Odyssey DLC]]. They have high HP and so are hard to destroy.

== Acquisition ==
Fortified walls cannot be crafted or moved. If the player wishes to use a fortified wall for their base, they will have to set up base on a map that generates with fortified walls. Orbital platforms and some ancient ruins from the [[Odyssey DLC]] will generate with fortified walls. {{Check Tag|Which ruins?|I don't know which ones to list as their pages haven't been made yet.}}

== Summary ==
Fortified walls act the same as normal [[wall]]s, bounding [[rooms]], blocking movement, and holding up [[roof]]ing. Unlike regular walls  they cannot be deconstructed or claimed, and do not act as a [[psyfocus]].{{RoyaltyIcon}}

== Analysis ==
Fortified walls have the highest HP of any wall, at 7,500 HP. This makes them hard to demolish if the player is trying to get into a structure. Fortified walls can be destroyed quickly with [[breach axe]]s, or demolished with three [[ancient mining charge]] {{OdysseyIcon}} detonations. [[Frag grenades]] or player-controlled [[diaboli]]{{BiotechIcon}} are make reasonable breaching options.

Because of their high HP, fortified walls can be used to contain entities from the [[Anomaly DLC]], since they provide a high [[containment strength]]. However, the player will have to find a map with the walls to build their containment cell in, since fortified walls cannot be built.
