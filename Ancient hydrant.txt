'''Ancient hydrants''' are [[ruins]] that are defined in Core but only appear when the [[Ideology DLC]] is enabled.

== Occurrence ==
If the Ideology DLC is enabled, they can be found among the debris scattered on maps.

Otherwise they will not appear in-game at all.
