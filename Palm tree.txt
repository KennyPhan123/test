'''Palm trees''' are a [[tree]] found only in [[tropical rainforest]]s. They do not produce human-edible [[food]]. Palm trees can be chopped for 18 [[wood]] at full growth. Like any other tree, they provide 25% cover effectiveness.  It takes 600 work to harvest, and 4000 to sow once [[Tree sowing]] has been researched. They provide {{P|Nutrition}} nutrition to [[dendrovore]]s.

== Lack of coconuts ==
{{Humour}}
[[Empire|The Empire]] has banned all [[Trade#By inventory|import]] of coconut bearing palm trees to the rim due to safety concerns.

Coconuts are well known for their predatory behavior, killing more people a year[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Death_by_coconut] than [[Dogfish|sharks]].
