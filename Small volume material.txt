'''Small volume material''' refers to two specific [[metals]]:

* {{Icon Small|silver|24}} [[Silver]]
* {{Icon Small|gold|24}} [[Gold]]

For [[stuff]]able items, like [[wall]]s, [[sculpture]]s, and [[melee weapons]], a small volume material would require ×10 as many items. If a wall would take {{Icon Small|stuff}} 5 [[stuff]], a golden wall would take {{Icon Small|gold}} 50 [[gold]].

== Version history ==
* [[Alpha 17]] - [[Jade]] is no longer a small volume material.
* [[Version/1.0.0|1.0.0]] - [[Uranium]] is no longer a small volume material.
