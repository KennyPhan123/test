{{Info|'''Smokeleaf leaves''' are harvested from a [[smokeleaf plant]]. They can be processed by [[Work#Craft|crafters]] at a [[crafting spot]] or [[drug lab]] to produce [[smokeleaf joint]]s.}}

== Acquisition ==
Smokeleaf leaves are obtained by harvesting [[smokeleaf plant]] plant. Each fully grown plant yields {{Icon Small|Smokeleaf leaves||{{Q|Smokeleaf plant|Harvest Yield}}}} before modifiers for [[AI Storytellers#Harvest yield|Storyteller Harvest Yield]], the harvesting pawn's [[Plant Harvest Yield]], and the [[Drug use|Drug use: Essential]] precept.{{IdeologyIcon}} Note that by default, Storyteller Harvest Yield varies by [[difficulty]].
