'''Entropy link''' was a level 2 [[psycast]] that caused "entropy", now called [[neural heat]], from psycasting to be shared evenly between the caster and target. It was replaced with [[neural heat dump]].

== Acquisition ==
{{Acquisition}}

== Summary ==
So long as the caster and target stayed within 10 tiles of each other, any entropy gained would eb shared evenly between them, allowing the caster to cast more without negative effects at the cost of inflicting the side effects on another pawn.

Additionally, note that Neural heat dump is considered a harmful ability, and will incur a [[goodwill]] penalty of {{Bad|-15}} when cast on pawns from a neutral or allied [[faction]].
