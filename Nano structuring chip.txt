'''Nano structuring chips''' are a resource added by the [[Biotech DLC]]. 

== Acquisition ==
A single nano structuring chip will be dropped by a hostile [[apocriton]] upon death, regardless of whether the [[apocriton]] is summoned by a [[mechband dish]] or spawned naturally as part of a [[raid]]. It is not relevant who kills the apocriton - an apocriton killed by other factions, wild animals, or berserk mechanoids still drop the nano structuring chip.

== Usage ==
Nano structuring chips are used to create various mechanitor items, and studying one at a [[research bench]] is required to research [[Ultra mechtech]] and [[Research#Wastepack atomizer|Wastepack atomizer]]. The chip is not consumed in the process of studying it.

== Analysis ==
[[Wastepack atomizer]]s provide an entirely clean way of reducing pollution, improving quality of life. Still, in a lot of cases, it is easy to freeze wastepacks, let them pollute far away tiles of the current map, or dump them on a tribal faction. The benefits of atomizers have no recurring material cost, require no manual involvement, and do not invite any threats at all. If these are important, or if clean disposal is desired for personal reasons, then the wastepack atomizer can be justified.

The [[mechlord helmet|mechlord]] [[mechlord suit|apparel]] is essentially [[recon armor]] with slightly less armor, a penalty to combat, and a massive [[bandwidth]] boost. They make sense to prioritize for a [[mechanitor]]-heavy colony, particularly where the mechanitor does not expect directly particpate in combat much. When deciding which piece to build first, the mechlord helmet has less item HP but is less likely to be hit in combat, so the helmet is best to make first for a combat focused mechanitor, and vice versa for the suit.

The [[war queen]] is the most durable mech in the game, and can further boost its distracting power by creating [[war urchin]]s, but its damage output is lacking. If a tanky distraction is necessary, the war queen is the best in class, although it competes with the [[centipede]] variants which require less resources and 1 less [[bandwidth]].

The [[repair probe]] offers a minor but downside-less buff to a mechanitor. They should not be built over mechlord suits or war queens if either of those are desired, but if there are no other uses for these chips and [[advanced component]]s, they might as well be installed.

Finally, they can be sold, which should be done if mechlord armor and war queens are not desired, and the player does not have the resources to spend on atomizers or repair probes.

The chip closely resembles Cortana’s chip from the Halo series.
