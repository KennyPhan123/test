'''Advanced components''' are a late game crafting material used for high-tech crafting and construction, such as building the [[ship]] to escape the planet, or making [[charge rifle]]s and [[marine armor]]. 

== Acquisition ==
{{Acquisition}}

Advanced components can also be collected by destroying [[crashed ship parts]] or by purchasing them from [[traders]]. Advanced components can only be bought from [[orbital trader]]s, [[outlanders]], and the [[Empire]].{{RoyaltyIcon}} They can also be found in [[ancient shrine]]s.

== Summary ==
Damage to an advanced component's HP does not affect their [[market value]].

== Analysis ==
Like with regular [[component]]s, advanced components are often a bottleneck to a colony. It is highly recommended to keep 2 advanced components for when you can build a [[fabrication bench]], which allows you to make both types of component yourself. You may also want to grab one to make a [[ground-penetrating scanner]] or [[long-range mineral scanner]]. Both can scan for ores indefinitely; the metal that they find can be another bottleneck towards development.

Before Advanced Fabrication, the most reliable way to get these is via trade. Outlander [[faction base]]s often have a few advanced components on sale. If you are unable to trade this way, both [[Trade#Exotic goods trader|exotics goods traders]] and [[comms console|orbital trade ships]] are able to sell some. If the colony is [[money making guide|rich enough]], a steady supply of caravans can be called in using the [[comms console]], providing a stream of advanced components (gifting to pay for the [[goodwill]] cost of each caravan). Alternatively, a [[passenger shuttle]]{{OdysseyIcon}} can quickly hop from faction base to faction base to buy them directly; this is cheaper than using the comms console, but each base has a 30 day cooldown.

Fabrication is slower than trade, but once available, crafting advanced components is cheaper and helps to amass the large amount of components required in the late game. A large amount of time - {{Ticks|{{P|Work To Make}}}} of work per component - is required, so building multiple fabrication benches and 1-2 [[tool cabinet]]s can help speed up production. Of course, if crafting is too slow, it is possible to use both trade and fabrication.

Note that you will need both [[plasteel]] and [[gold]] to create advanced components, so plan ahead accordingly.
