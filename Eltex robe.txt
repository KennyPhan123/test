The '''eltex robe''' is an item of [[clothing]] added by the [[Royalty DLC]] that improves [[psychic sensitivity]] and makes [[psycast]]ers more effective.

It is one of a set of [[eltex|eltex items]] with similar effects that cover different parts of the body.

== Acquisition ==
Eltex robes cannot be crafted. Instead, they can only be purchased through [[trade]] or received as a [[quest]] reward. 

They also spawn on [[Empire#Pawns|Imperial Nobles]].

== Summary ==
The {{lc:{{PAGENAME}}}} improves a pawns [[psychic sensitivity]] by a [[quality]]-dependent amount, and improves the pawn's base [[neural heat recovery rate]] by {{+|0.083}} heat per second, though this will be modified by their psylink level.
To consider themselves properly dressed and avoid a {{--|4}} [[Mood|''"Want <TITLE>-specific apparel"'']] moodlet, [[Titles|nobles]] must wear rank-appropriate clothing. A {{lc:{{PAGENAME}}}} satisfies all non-headwear related requirements of all nobles. Note that any quality requirements must still be met to avoid the separate and stacking a {{--|4}} "''Want <TITLE>-quality apparel''" moodlet.

== Analysis ==
{{Stub|section=1|reason=General analysis - e.g. value propositio, worth seeking out, worth not using a duster for, wb prestige armor etc.}}
Compared to a [[duster]] it is readily apparent that this is only for exclusively psychic focused pawns, with the same coverage and slot usage but with worse protection than even a cloth duster.
The eltex robe also appeases the outer layer torso requirements of all [[Titles|nobles]], replacing the [[Prestige robe]] or [[Cape]].
